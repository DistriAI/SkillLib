# Grant Proposal Skill

Draft a grant proposal based on: **$ARGUMENTS**

## 1. Determine Grant Type & Inputs

- **Academic Grants (KAKENHI, NSF, NSFC, ERC, etc.):**
  - Identify the specific agency and sub-type (e.g., NSF CAREER, NSFC Youth).
  - Required: Research direction, validated ideas, preliminary data.
  - Language: Auto-detect (KAKENHI→Japanese, NSF→English, NSFC→Chinese).
- **Generic/Charitable Grants:**
  - Required: Funder name, grant amount, project description, organization track record, funder stated priorities, word limits, deadline.

## 2. Research & Validation (Academic Track)

If the grant is academic:
1. **Literature Survey:** Run `/research-lit` to survey the field.
2. **Novelty Check:** Run `/novelty-check` to verify the research gap.
3. **Structure Design:** Define aims and a research matrix.

## 3. Drafting the Proposal

### Academic Structure
- **KAKENHI:** Research Objective, Plan & Methods, Preparation Status, Ethics.
- **NSF:** Project Summary, Project Description (Aim-based), Broader Impacts, Budget Justification.
- **NSFC:** Rationale, Content, Objectives, Plan, Feasibility, Innovation, Expected Outcomes.

### Generic Structure
1. **Project Summary:** 200-300 words, written last.
2. **Problem Statement:** Specific, evidenced, aligned with funder priorities.
3. **Objectives:** 3-5 SMART objectives.
4. **Methodology:** Phased approach with key activities.
5. **Impact & Outcomes:** Table of outputs, short/medium/long-term outcomes.
6. **Evaluation Plan:** Who, how, when, what.
7. **Budget Narrative:** Justified line items.
8. **Organisational Capacity:** Track record and governance.
9. **Risk Register:** Likelihood, impact, and mitigation.

## 4. Review & Revision

- **Academic:** Run `/research-review` for panel-style feedback. Revise up to 2 rounds.
- **Generic:** Check against funder priorities and word limits.

## 5. Final Output

- Save to `grant-proposal/GRANT_PROPOSAL.md`.
- Ensure all sections explicitly reference funder/agency priorities.
- Verify budget justification and sustainability/feasibility.
