# App Store Optimization (ASO)

## 1. Keyword Research
1. **Define Scope**: Identify target audience, core functions, and competitive category.
2. **Generate Seeds**: Use app features, user language, and store autocomplete.
3. **Expand List**: Add modifiers (free, best), actions (track, create), and audiences (for teams).
4. **Evaluate & Score**: 
   - **Relevance** (35%): Does it describe the app?
   - **Volume** (25%): Estimated monthly searches.
   - **Competition** (25%): Quality of ranking apps.
   - **Conversion** (15%): Transactional intent.
5. **Prioritize**: Rank by score. Primary keywords go to Title/Keyword Field; Secondary to Subtitle/Short Description.

## 2. Metadata Optimization
1. **Title**: Use `[Brand] - [Primary Keyword]` or `[Keyword] - [Brand]`. Max 30 chars (iOS) / 50 chars (Android).
2. **Subtitle/Short Description**: Communicate primary value proposition. Max 30 chars (iOS) / 80 chars (Android).
3. **Keyword Field (iOS)**: 
   - Max 100 UTF-8 bytes.
   - Comma-separated, no spaces after commas.
   - No duplicates from Title/Subtitle.
   - No plurals (Apple indexes both).
   - No category names (Apple adds automatically).
4. **Description**: 
   - **Hook**: First 1-3 lines (pre-fold). Strongest benefit.
   - **Features**: 4-6 bullets (use Unicode •). Focus on outcomes.
   - **Social Proof**: Awards, press, user counts.
   - **CTA**: Clear next step.
5. **Validation**: Check character counts. Ensure no keyword stuffing (>5% density). Verify no duplicate terms across indexed fields.

## 3. Competitor Analysis
1. Identify top 10 direct and indirect competitors.
2. Extract keywords from their titles, subtitles, and first 100 words of descriptions.
3. Build a keyword matrix to find gaps (keywords with <40% competitor coverage).
4. Analyze visual assets (icons, screenshots) for messaging patterns.

## 4. Advanced Strategies (Apple)
- **Custom Product Pages**: Create variants for different ad campaigns or audiences.
- **In-App Events**: Promote new features or seasonal content to boost visibility.
- **Review Prompts**: Use `RequestReviewAction` at moments of user success.

## 5. Analytics & Tracking
- Track keyword rankings, impression-to-install conversion, and download velocity.
- Calculate an ASO health score based on metadata quality, ratings, and conversion metrics.
