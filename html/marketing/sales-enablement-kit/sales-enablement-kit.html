# Sales Enablement Kit

Build a sales enablement kit for a product, feature, or launch. The kit can be internal (sales/support/CS enablement for a launch) or rep-facing (a one-pager reps can use live on a call). Choose the scope from the user's request; if unclear, ask.

## Required Inputs

Ask for these if not provided:

- **What's being sold** — product, feature, or launch, and who it's for (segment, persona, buyer vs user)
- **The core value** — the problem it solves and the measurable outcome
- **Proof** — customers, metrics, case studies, or a demo environment
- **Top competitors / named alternatives** and the main objections reps hear today
- **Pricing/packaging** basics and any constraints on what reps can say
- **The motion** — inbound, outbound, PLG-assist, partner
- **Source of truth** (internal scope) — the message house and PR-FAQ spine, or a path to them
- **Embargo / launch date** (internal scope) — the authoritative date/stage/embargo record

Mark anything unknown as `[to confirm]` rather than inventing claims or metrics.

## Process

1. **Anchor on the buyer** — name the persona, their pain, and the outcome they buy.
2. **Compress positioning** — one sentence a rep can say, plus 3 value pillars with proof. For internal scope, derive this from the message house; do not add new external messaging facts.
3. **Write discovery** — questions that make the pain vivid and qualify fit.
4. **Build the talk track** — what to say at each stage; lead with value, land features as evidence. For internal scope, derive the talk track from the PR-FAQ spine.
5. **Map the demo** — the shortest path that shows the "aha," not every screen.
6. **Arm for resistance** — the real objections and crisp, honest counters; competitive traps and how to reframe. For internal scope, produce one battle card per named alternative with where we win, where they win, and trap questions.
7. **Close the loop** — the CTA, next step, and links to assets. For internal scope, produce an internal launch announcement keyed to the embargo lift, an internal FAQ, and CS macros.

## Quality Checks

- [ ] The 10-second pitch is one sentence and jargon-free (rep-facing)
- [ ] Every value pillar has a proof point, or is marked `[to confirm]`
- [ ] Discovery questions surface pain, not product features
- [ ] The demo flow is the shortest path to the payoff, not a tour
- [ ] Objection responses are honest — they don't over-claim
- [ ] A new rep could run a call from this kit alone (rep-facing)
- [ ] Every battle card fact is traceable or marked `[needs source]` and submitted as a candidate (internal)
- [ ] The talk track contains no fact absent from the message house / PR-FAQ spine (internal)
- [ ] The internal announcement is keyed to the embargo lift (internal)

## Anti-Patterns

- [ ] Do not list every feature; reps sell outcomes, not spec sheets
- [ ] Do not invent metrics, logos, or case studies — mark them `[to confirm]`
- [ ] Do not write objection answers that dodge the real concern
- [ ] Do not make the demo a full product tour; find the one "aha"
- [ ] Do not bury the CTA — reps need to know exactly what to ask for next
- [ ] Do not originate a fact in the internal kit; every card, response, and macro must trace back to the message house, claims ledger, or a named competitor source
- [ ] Do not write the external message house or PR-FAQ; derive from them only
- [ ] Do not track competitors or build outbound sequences within this skill; hand off to the appropriate skill
