# Content Repurposer

Atomize one long-form source (blog post, article, video transcript, or newsletter) into a complete pack of platform-native derivatives. Each derivative must be re-written for its platform, not merely truncated or re-flowed.

## Inputs
- **Source**: Paste the content, provide a URL, or state the core idea.
- **Platforms**: Default to the union of all supported platforms below; user may select a subset.
- **Tone/Voice**: Extract from source or a brand kit; define 3-5 tone descriptors, vocabulary to use/avoid, and sentence style.
- **CTA/Goal**: Subscribe, follow, buy, comment, or reply.

## Process
1. **Analyze source**: Identify the core message (one sentence), key points, and the 2-3 most compelling hooks.
2. **Define voice profile**: Tone descriptors, vocabulary list, sentence style, and per-platform formality adjustments.
3. **Extract hooks**: Use hook formulas (contrarian, question, number, challenge, story, result) to craft a distinct first-line hook for each platform.
4. **Produce derivatives**: Write each platform-specific piece using the specifications below.
5. **Score and verify**: Apply the scoring rubric and quality checks before delivery.
6. **Deliver package**: Include the source analysis, voice profile, all derivatives, publishing cadence, and automation note.

## Platform Specifications

### LinkedIn
- Length: 1,300-1,600 characters optimal.
- Hook: First 140 characters (before "See more").
- Format: Short paragraphs, line breaks, emoji bullets optional.
- Hashtags: 3-5, placed at end.
- CTA: Soft (comment/share) or link in comments.

### Twitter/X Thread
- Length: 70-100 characters optimal per tweet.
- Thread format: Number tweets (1/7), create curiosity gaps.
- Hook: First tweet must stop scroll.
- Hashtags: 1-2 max per thread.
- End: CTA + repost request.
- Tweets: 5-10, each carrying one point.

### Email/Newsletter
- Teaser: 2-3 sentence summary.
- Bullets: 3-5 key takeaways.
- Featured insight: 1 quotable moment.
- CTA: Link to full piece.
- Subject line: 1-2 options.
- Preview text: 1 line.
- Body: 150-250 words with a clear takeaway and exit link.

### Ad Hooks (Paid Social)
- 2-3 hooks, each 5-10 words.
- Angles: Attention grabber, alternative angle, question format.

### Short Video Script (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
- Length: 15-45 seconds (50-150 words spoken).
- Hook: 0-3 seconds must grab attention.
- Structure: Hook → Value → CTA.
- Style: Conversational, direct address.
- Include on-screen text cues.

### Instagram/LinkedIn Carousel
- Slide 1: Hook.
- Slides 2-6: One point each (≤12 words per slide + one sentence of body).
- Final slide: CTA.
- Provide on-screen text and caption.

## Hook Writing Formulas
| Type | Formula | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Contrarian | "Stop doing [common practice]" | "Stop posting content every day" |
| Question | "Ever wonder why [problem]?" | "Ever wonder why your posts get 12 likes?" |
| Number | "5 things I wish I knew about..." | "5 things I wish I knew about LinkedIn" |
| Challenge | "Most people get [topic] wrong" | "Most people get content repurposing wrong" |
| Story | "3 years ago, I was [situation]" | "3 years ago, I was posting into the void" |
| Result | "This one change got me [result]" | "This one change got me 10x engagement" |

## Scoring Rubric (0-40)
Score any output before delivery; 32+ is quality for sending.

| Dimension | 0 | 5 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Platform naturalness** | Same paragraph pasted five times with different line breaks | Formats differ but prose is identical underneath — re-flowed, not re-written | Each draft is written for how it reads on that platform: thread points stand alone, LinkedIn breathes, carousel slides fit, script sounds spoken |
| **Hook strength** | Openings are explanatory ("Here are some thoughts about…") | Hooks exist but are interchangeable between platforms and could headline any post on the topic | Each piece opens with a distinct first-impression line that stops the scroll and wins the next second on *that* platform |
| **Core idea coherence** | Pack is five weakly related posts without a core idea established | Core idea is established, but some drafts drift toward secondary points that dilute it | One core idea leads the pack and every draft returns to it — a reader hitting any single piece gets the full insight |
| **CTA and cadence fit** | CTAs missing, identical everywhere, or fighting the platform (hard sell in a thread); no publishing plan | CTAs present and roughly match the goal, but cadence is an afterthought ("post when you want") | Each CTA matches the established goal *and* platform norms, and the publishing order/cadence sequences the pack deliberately over the week |

## Quality Checks
- [ ] Each platform draft is genuinely *re-written* for that platform (length, format, tone), not the same text re-flowed.
- [ ] Each piece has a distinct and strong hook in its first line.
- [ ] All pieces return to the single core idea.
- [ ] CTAs match the established goal and platform norms.
- [ ] Carousel slides fit the word limit and include on-screen text and caption.
- [ ] No invented statistics or examples without marking them as *(assumed — replace)*.
