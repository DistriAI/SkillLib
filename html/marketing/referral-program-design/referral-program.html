# Referral Program Strategy

## 1. Initial Assessment
- **Context**: Read project context files (e.g., `contextus.md`, `app-marketing-context.md`) if present.
- **Product Fit**: Determine if the product has network effects, natural sharing moments, or high LTV. If weak fit, consider UGC or retention strategies instead.
- **Economics**: Identify CAC, LTV, and Gross Margin. 
  - *Sizing Rule*: Max Reward ≤ (LTV × Margin) - Target CAC. 
  - *Benchmark*: Typically 10-30% of price or $10-$50 for B2C.

## 2. Program Design
- **Type**: Choose between Customer Referral (existing users) and Affiliate (partners/creators). Hybrid is common.
- **Incentive Structure**:
  - **Double-sided**: Both referrer and referee get rewards (highest conversion).
  - **Tiered**: Rewards increase with volume (motivates power users).
  - **Type**: Cash, credits, free months, or status/cosmetics.
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Web/SaaS**: Unique links, codes, or in-product share buttons.
  - **Mobile**: Deep links with deferred handling, system share sheet, and one-tap share.

## 3. Implementation & Tracking
- **Attribution**: Use first-touch attribution. Set windows (30-90 days for web, 180 for subscription).
- **Tools**: 
  - *Self-build*: URL params/cookies + fraud logic.
  - *Third-party*: Cello, Viral Loops, ReferralCandy (E-com), Impact (Enterprise), Branch/AppsFlyer (Mobile).
- **UX**: One-click share, clear rules, dashboard with referral stats, and instant reward notification.

## 4. Fraud Prevention
- **Self-referral**: Detect same device/IP/payment.
- **Bot/Fake accounts**: Require email/phone verification or qualifying actions (e.g., purchase, retention).
- **Caps**: Limit referrals per user (e.g., max 10-50).
- **Mobile Specifics**: Check for Family Sharing signals and IDFV/Android ID.

## 5. Optimization & Measurement
- **KPIs**: Referral rate, conversion, CAC, LTV of referred users, and K-factor (for mobile/viral loops).
- **A/B Testing**: Test incentive amounts, share copy, and trigger moments (e.g., after 'aha' moment vs. onboarding).
- **Communication**: Use email sequences for launch and nurture (Day 7, 30, 60 reminders).
