# Google Ads

## Strategy and Setup (from kostja94/marketing-skills)

### Two Modes
- **PMF testing**: Pre-PMF; validate idea before building. Budget $47–500; start small. Simple landing page with headline, benefits, problem solved, CTA. Metrics: CTR, sign-up rate, bounce rate. Allow 4–6 weeks for PMax learning phase.
- **Conversion-driven**: PMF validated; commercialization. Scale; ROAS target. Full funnel; ad-to-page alignment. Metrics: ROAS, CAC, conversion rate.

### Campaign Structure
- Account → Brand (Search), Non-Brand (Search), Competitor (Search, optional), Retargeting (Display), Performance Max.
- Naming: `GOOG_[Objective]_[Audience]_[Offer]_[Date]`.

### Performance Max Optimization
- Run at least 6 weeks for algorithm ramp-up.
- Asset groups organized by audience intent.
- Per asset group: ≥5 images (include 1200×1200), ≥5 text assets (4 headlines, 5 descriptions), video when possible.
- Add remarketing lists and Customer Match signals.
- Weekly health check: flag brand terms >30% of conversions, unexpected geo conversions, any placement >15% of total spend, asset group performance below "Good."

### Keyword Strategy
- Brand: protect brand terms; exclude from non-brand campaigns.
- Negative keywords: build weekly; add support terms (login, forum, pricing, help).
- Match types: Broad → Phrase → Exact.

### Quality Score
- Target ≥6. Improving from 5 to 7 can reduce CPC by 30–50%.
- Levers: expected CTR, ad relevance, landing page alignment.

### Bidding Strategy
- <30 conversions/month: Manual CPC.
- 30–50: Target CPA.
- 50–100: Target CPA.
- 100+: Target ROAS.

### Tracking
- Enhanced Conversions, offline conversion imports, UTM parameters for GA4 cross-check.
