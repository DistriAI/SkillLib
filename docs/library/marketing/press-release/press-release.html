It uses an inverted pyramid structure, includes embargo dates, and focuses on news value and media contact details. Because they serve different stages of the product lifecycle (planning vs. launch) and have conflicting structural requirements (e.g., 'not for launch day' vs. 'ready to send to journalists'), crowning one would hide a valid alternative use case.

## Purpose
Create a press release to either define customer value before building (Working Backwards) or to announce a product to the media (Launch).

## Input
- **Mode:** Planning (Working Backwards) or Launch (Media Release).
- **Product/Feature:** What is being announced or planned?
- **Customer/Persona:** Who is it for?
- **Problem/News Hook:** What problem is solved or what news is being shared?
- **Key Benefits/News Details:** Specific outcomes or facts.
- **Company Info:** Name, mission, boilerplate.
- **Media Contact:** Name, email, phone (for Launch mode).

## Step 1: Determine Mode
- **Planning Mode:** Use when the product is not yet built. Goal is to test value and align stakeholders.
- **Launch Mode:** Use when the product is ready or launching. Goal is to inform journalists and the public.

## Step 2: Draft Structure

### If Planning Mode (Working Backwards)
1. **Headline:** Benefit-focused, specific (e.g., "[Product] Aims to [Benefit]").
2. **Dateline:** City, State, Date.
3. **Introduction:** What is being launched, who it's for, key benefit.
4. **Problem:** The customer problem solved.
5. **Solution:** How the product addresses the problem (outcomes, not features).
6. **Quote:** Vision and customer commitment from a leader.
7. **Details:** Supporting benefits or data.
8. **Boilerplate:** Company background.
9. **Call to Action:** How to learn more.
10. **Media Contact:** Press contact.

*Constraint:* Do not use launch-specific headers like "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE". Focus on customer value, not features.

### If Launch Mode (Media Release)
1. **Header:** "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" or "EMBARGOED UNTIL: [Date]".
2. **Headline:** Active voice, specific, <10 words. Do not start with company name.
3. **Subheadline:** The "why" in one sentence.
4. **Dateline:** City, Date.
5. **Lead Paragraph:** Who, What, When, Where, Why in 2-3 sentences. Must stand alone.
6. **Significance:** Why this matters to the industry/customers.
7. **Quote:** Specific, human, adds context not in the facts.
8. **Supporting Details:** Data, customer names, context.
9. **Next Steps:** What happens next, launch date, how to access.
10. **Boilerplate:** "About [Company]" (factual, 3-4 sentences).
11. **Media Contact:** Name, Title, Email, Phone, Timezone.

*Constraint:* Use inverted pyramid. Most important info first. No promotional fluff.

## Step 3: Quality Checks
- **Planning:** Is the value proposition clear? Would a customer want this? Is it feature-free?
- **Launch:** Is the headline <10 words? Does the lead stand alone? Is the quote human? Is the boilerplate factual?

## Anti-Patterns
- **Planning:** Do not write it as a final marketing copy. Do not list specs.
- **Launch:** Do not bury the news. Do not use superlatives. Do not omit embargo/contact info.
