# eBay Product Research

Research profitable products to sell on eBay by analyzing completed listings, sell-through rates, average selling prices, competition density, seasonal demand, and sourcing opportunities.

## Inputs
- Category (e.g., electronics, vintage watches)
- Keyword (e.g., wireless earbuds)
- Optional: seller ID for competitor analysis
- Optional: price and cost for profit calculation
- Optional: marketplace/site (US, UK, DE, AU)

## Steps
1. Identify the target category and keyword.
2. Analyze completed listings to determine real selling prices and average selling price.
3. Calculate sell-through rate and compare against category benchmarks.
4. Assess competition density for the category and keyword.
5. Identify seasonal demand patterns and trending products.
6. If a seller ID is provided, perform competitor analysis for that seller.
7. If price and cost are provided, calculate profit margin including eBay fees for the selected marketplace.
8. Identify sourcing opportunities (arbitrage, wholesale, thrift) where applicable.
9. Produce structured recommendations with actionable steps, benchmarks, and platform-specific guidance.

## Output
- Structured product recommendations
- Sell-through rate and price benchmarks
- Competition density assessment
- Seasonal demand and trend notes
- Profit margin estimate (when price/cost provided)
- Sourcing opportunity notes
