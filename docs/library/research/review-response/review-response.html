# Review Response

This skill handles two distinct types of review responses. Determine the context to select the appropriate workflow.

## Workflow A: Customer Review Response (E-commerce)

**Use when:** Responding to public customer reviews on platforms like Google, Amazon, or Trustpilot.

1. **Analyze the Review:** Identify sentiment, the specific issue, and whether it is fixable.
2. **Determine Strategy:**
   - **Positive:** Thank the customer, echo specific praise, add brand warmth, invite them back.
   - **Negative/Mixed:** Acknowledge the specific problem, take ownership without excuses, apologize sincerely, state the remedy, and move resolution to a private channel.
3. **Draft the Response:**
   - Address the reviewer by name.
   - Keep it calm and brief (audience is the next shopper).
   - Do not argue facts or expose private data.
4. **Provide Templates:** Offer reusable patterns for common cases (shipping, product fault, etc.).

## Workflow B: Academic Rebuttal (Research)

**Use when:** Responding to peer reviewer comments for academic papers.

1. **Classify Comments:** Parse comments into Major, Minor, Typo, or Misunderstanding.
2. **Develop Strategy:** For each comment, choose a response type: Accept, Defend, Clarify, or Experiment.
3. **Write the Rebuttal:**
   - Acknowledge strengths first.
   - Provide evidence-based justifications for experimental setups or claims.
   - Address clarity issues by expanding explanations or moving details to appendices.
   - Maintain a professional, respectful, and non-defensive tone.
4. **Tone Check:** Ensure the response is polite, complete, and addresses all points.

## Common Principles

- **Audience Awareness:** Tailor the tone to the specific audience (future shoppers vs. academic peers).
- **Specificity:** Address the specific points raised in the review/comment.
- **Professionalism:** Avoid defensiveness, sarcasm, or arguing facts in public/peer forums.
