mobisys-author-response

separate

Use when drafting a MobiSys rebuttal after round-2 reviews — working within the scope limit (correct factual errors, answer specific reviewer questions), adding only directly-responsive new results, promising camera-ready work honestly, staying double-blind, and giving the area chair a clean rationale for a mobile-systems accept decision.

When to use

  • correcting factual errors in reviews
  • answering specific reviewer questions
  • adding directly-responsive new results
  • promising deliverable camera-ready work
  • maintaining double-blind anonymity
  • providing rationale for mobile-systems accept decision

When not to use

  • revising the paper
  • rearguing the paper
  • adding non-responsive new experiments
  • making undeliverable promises
  • revealing identity or institution
