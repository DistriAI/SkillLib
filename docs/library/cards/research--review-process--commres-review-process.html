commres-review-process

separate

Use to understand how Communication Research (CR) evaluates a manuscript — double-anonymized review, editorial screening for quantitative/theory fit, external review, and the requirement that two independent positive reviews back a Revise or Accept decision. Sets expectations and shapes the paper to survive review; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • Stress-test fit, rigor, and anonymization before submitting
  • Deciding if contribution is quantitative and theory-testing enough
  • Interpreting a decision letter and setting expectations
  • Understanding what reviewers are instructed to weigh
  • Shape the paper to pass editorial and external review
  • Review-risk pass for Communication Research
  • Reading a 'major revisions' signal

When not to use

  • Contacting editors
  • Submitting qualitative or descriptive papers to a quantitative venue
  • Polishing format if a sibling journal owns the contribution
