jcr-data-analysis

separate

Use when running and reporting the evidence for a Journal of Consumer Research (JCR) manuscript — process evidence (mediation/moderation) for behavioral experiments, or trustworthy interpretation for Consumer Culture Theory (CCT) work — plus robustness and the transparency reporting JCR requires. Executes and reports; it does not design the studies (jcr-methods).

When to use

  • analyzing and reporting JCR manuscript evidence
  • process evidence for behavioral experiments
  • trustworthy interpretation for Consumer Culture Theory (CCT) work
  • robustness and transparency reporting
  • bootstrapped indirect effects and simple-slopes
  • spotlight/floodlight analyses
  • defending trustworthiness of interpretive evidence

When not to use

  • designing the studies
