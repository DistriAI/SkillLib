revedres-organizing-framework

separate

Use when turning a screened, coded corpus into an analytic spine that advances theory or policy for a Review of Educational Research (RER) manuscript. Builds the conceptual contribution beyond a tally; it does not run the search (revedres-literature-synthesis) or appraise robustness/risk-of-bias (revedres-comprehensiveness-and-balance).

When to use

  • turning a screened, coded corpus into an analytic spine
  • building the conceptual contribution beyond a tally
  • advancing theory or policy for a Review of Educational Research (RER) manuscript
  • creating section architecture that mirrors the framework
  • reconciling apparent contradictions in the literature
  • generating a theory, research, or policy agenda from empty cells

When not to use

  • running the search
  • appraising robustness
  • appraising risk-of-bias
