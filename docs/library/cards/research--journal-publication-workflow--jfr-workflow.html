jfr-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jfr-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from fit positioning through rebuttal for 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research). Routes — does not replace — the specialized skills, and cross-refs sibling journal packs.

When to use

  • Deciding which jfr-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from fit positioning through rebuttal
  • Resolving ambiguity between macro-finance and micro-corporate finance lines
  • Addressing unclear financial channels in empirical findings
  • Responding to R&R external review comments

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized jfr-* skills
  • Handling clean causal inference and theory for economic-research
  • Handling frontier econometrics and theoretical empirics for china-economic-quarterly
  • Handling industrial upgrading rather than financial mechanisms for china-industrial-economics
  • Handling management theory or case studies for nankai-business-review
  • Handling accounting information, auditing, or earnings quality for accounting-research
