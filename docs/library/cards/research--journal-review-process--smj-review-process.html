smj-review-process

separate

Use when interpreting the Strategic Management Journal (SMJ) review process and a decision letter, and planning the revision strategy. Explains the process and triages the decision; the actual response letter is drafted in smj-rebuttal.

When to use

  • understanding what happens after submission
  • reading a decision letter correctly
  • deciding whether and how to revise
  • managing the action editor relationship
  • weighing conflicting reviewer comments
  • planning the revision strategy

When not to use

  • drafting the actual response letter
