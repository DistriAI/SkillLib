law-of-figure-ground

separate

Apply the Law of Figure-Ground — establish which layer is foreground and actionable versus background. Use when designing modals, overlays, and depth. For emphasising one element among peers, use `von-restorff-effect`.

When to use

  • designing modals
  • designing overlays
  • designing depth
  • establishing foreground and background layers
  • controlling figure-ground relationships
  • defining surface stacks
  • validating scrims
  • managing elevation and shadow

When not to use

  • emphasising one element among peers
