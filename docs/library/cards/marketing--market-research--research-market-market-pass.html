research-market-market-pass

separate

Step of the research-market playbook: the worker brief for researching the market itself, including category, size, changes, pricing norms and demand. Run research-market instead; not for direct use.

When to use

  • researching the market a brand sells into
  • identifying market category and positioning
  • estimating market size top-down and bottom-up
  • analyzing market changes in the last 12 months
  • researching pricing norms and demand

When not to use

  • direct use
  • competitor research
  • audience research
  • loading other skills or playbooks
  • starting subagents or workflows
