finman-referee-strategy

separate

Use when pre-empting the objections a Financial Management (FM) referee is likely to raise, before pressing submit — red-teaming the paper from the perspective of an applied-finance reviewer who weighs relevance, rigor, and the "so-what." Anticipates pushback; it does not run the submission preflight (finman-submission) or draft a response to an actual report (finman-rebuttal).
