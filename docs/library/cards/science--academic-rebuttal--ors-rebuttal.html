ors-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to an Operations Research (OR) decision letter — writing the point-by-point response, closing proof gaps, adding baselines/instances, and passing the ORJournal code/data reproducibility review across a revision cycle. Drafts the revision and response; it does not parse the decision in the first place (ors-review-process) or run the final preflight (ors-submission).

When to use

  • writing the point-by-point response
  • closing proof gaps
  • adding baselines/instances
  • passing the ORJournal code/data reproducibility review
  • preparing the code/data deposit for the ORJournal pull-request review
  • addressing the editor's synthesis
  • repairing proofs and stating assumption usage
  • weakening assumptions or justifying necessity

When not to use

  • parsing the decision in the first place
  • running the final preflight
