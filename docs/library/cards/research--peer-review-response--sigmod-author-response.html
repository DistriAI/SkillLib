sigmod-author-response

separate

Use when writing SIGMOD author feedback during a PACMMOD round or the revision letter after a major/minor-revision verdict, covering the mid-round feedback window, the four-page letter limit, per-reviewer change highlighting, the extra content page, and anonymity that must hold through resubmission.

When to use

  • writing SIGMOD author feedback
  • writing SIGMOD revision letter
  • PACMMOD round feedback
  • major or minor revision response
  • mid-round feedback window
  • per-reviewer change highlighting
  • extra content page planning
  • anonymity checks

When not to use

  • adding new contributions during revision
  • arguing requirements are unreasonable in feedback phase
  • promising future experiments as done in feedback phase
