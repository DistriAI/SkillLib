jue-writing-style

separate

Use when the prose of a Journal of Urban Economics (JUE) manuscript buries the spatial mechanism, or the intro/abstract do not land for an urban-economics audience. Sharpens the writing to JUE house voice; it does not run analysis or build exhibits. Late-stage — invoke after identification and the mechanism settle.

When to use

  • Sharpening JUE manuscript writing
  • Improving intro and abstract for urban-economics audience
  • Ensuring spatial mechanism is clear
  • Applying JUE house voice
  • Threading policy implications
  • Checking abstract fit test

When not to use

  • Running analysis
  • Building exhibits
  • Early-stage identification
  • Setting up mechanism
