jfm-referee-strategy

separate

Use when anticipating the microstructure referee's objections for a Journal of Financial Markets (JFM) manuscript — a pre-submission pre-mortem and, post-decision, a triage of the report. Anticipates and triages; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • pre-submission pre-mortem
  • triage of the report
  • surface objections a microstructure insider will raise
  • triage which comments are fatal, fixable, or push-back
  • estimate whether the asks are within an R&R's reach
  • read the signal in the editor's letter
  • plan for microstructure specialist and broader-finance reader profiles

When not to use

  • invent evidence
  • invent citations
