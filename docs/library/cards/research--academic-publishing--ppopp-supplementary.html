ppopp-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what belongs in a PPoPP paper's 10 reviewed pages versus the artifact or appendix, splitting content by decision-criticality so proofs, full core sweeps, and correctness arguments that determine acceptance stay legible in the body, while bulk data and extended runs move out.

When to use

  • deciding content placement for PPoPP papers
  • splitting content by decision-criticality
  • identifying body content for 10 reviewed pages
  • identifying artifact or appendix content
  • checking if acceptance-deciding content is in the body
  • compressing core sweeps into figures
  • moving exhaustive tables to artifact

When not to use

  • writing the full formal proof
  • generating raw logs or per-run tables
  • creating Dockerfiles or build scripts
  • evaluating the artifact post-acceptance
