research-competitors

separate

Competitor research: what every main rival is running, launching, charging and saying now, what works for them, and what that means for you. Use when the marketer asks for competitor research or a teardown, "what ads are my competitors running", "how do they price", "find gaps and angles", "what changed this week" (a weekly watch), or "X just launched / shut down / cut prices" (react now). Also builds a sales battlecard, checks a competitor page's tools/pixels, or studies who engages with their LinkedIn posts. Delivers a readable report saved to the brand, so the next run reports what changed. Never copies their words or disparages them. Not for what people say about you or your market — use research-market; not for who your buyers are — use research-audience.
