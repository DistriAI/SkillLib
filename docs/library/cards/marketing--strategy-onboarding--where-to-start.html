where-to-start

separate

First-move advice for a broad growth or marketing ask with no job named: looks at what GooseWorks already knows and at the site, asks a few short questions in one message, names the one thing in the way, and sends the marketer to the right next job. Use when the marketer says "help me grow my brand", "I need to grow my business", "how do I get more customers / sales", "help me with marketing", "where do I start", "sales are flat", "what should I focus on this month", or "I'm back, what's next?". Recommends only; never runs the work. Not for a named job like "make me ads" or "grow my Instagram" (go straight to it); not for a campaign with a launch, budget or date — use plan-campaign; not for why this week's numbers moved — use analyze-performance.

When not to use

  • Routing direct asks — they go straight to their playbook. Why this week's numbers moved — analyze-performance.
  • Running any playbook, or starting one without a yes.
  • A marketing plan, budget split, calendar or committed channel mix — plan-campaign.
  • Research beyond the short look (competitors, reviews, ad libraries, search data) — the research playbooks.
  • Brand setup, a full claims audit or voice sign-off — not covered yet (the claim lines a job needs are approved inside that job).
  • Investment, fundraising or financial advice.
