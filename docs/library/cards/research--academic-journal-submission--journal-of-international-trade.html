journal-of-international-trade

separate

Use when targeting 《国际贸易问题》(Journal of International Trade — 对外经济贸易大学主办、国家社科基金首批资助、不收费的国际贸易核心刊) or deciding whether a Chinese trade/open-economy manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, fee-free policy, online-only submission, house style, official re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《国际贸易问题》
  • deciding if a Chinese trade manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese economics manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit for trade policy or FDI topics

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting via third-party agencies
  • domestic policy evaluation without open-economy focus
  • macro open-economy theory (use journal-of-world-economy)
  • financial openness (use studies-of-international-finance)
