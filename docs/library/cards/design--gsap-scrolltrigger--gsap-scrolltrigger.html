Conflict

The two copies overlap on core ScrollTrigger usage (registerPlugin, trigger/start/end, scrub, toggleActions, pin) but each contains material steps the other lacks. The greensock/gsap-skills copy is the official, more precise reference: it documents clamp(), refreshPriority, endTrigger, containerAnimation, snap, markers, once, id, and the full config table, plus ScrollTrigger.create() and batch(). The freshtechbro copy adds a broader GSAP foundation (tweens, timelines, position parameter) and common patterns (fade-in, pin, horizontal scroll, parallax, scroll-triggered timeline) that the official copy does not include. Crowning one would hide a real alternative: the official reference for precise config vs. a broader tutorial-style skill. They do not materially conflict on the shared parameters; they differ in scope and emphasis.
