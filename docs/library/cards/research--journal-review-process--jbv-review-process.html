jbv-review-process

separate

Use when understanding how the Journal of Business Venturing (JBV) review process works — Co-EIC / area-editor routing, double-anonymized review by a minimum of two reviewers, desk-reject risk for off-phenomenon work, and reading a JBV decision letter. Explains the process and reads the letter; the response is drafted with jbv-rebuttal.

When to use

  • Understanding JBV review process
  • Interpreting JBV decision letters
  • Gauging desk-reject risk
  • Identifying handling editor priorities
  • Sorting reviewer points
  • Naming integrative concerns
  • Calibrating revision effort

When not to use

  • Drafting the response to the decision letter
