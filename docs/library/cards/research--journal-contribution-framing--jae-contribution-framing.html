jae-contribution-framing

separate

Use when articulating the contribution of a Journal of Accounting and Economics (JAE) manuscript — stating explicitly what the paper teaches about accounting's economic role (information content, contracting, disclosure, standards) and why an economics-based result advances the positive-accounting literature beyond an incremental correlation.

When to use

  • articulating JAE manuscript contribution
  • stating economic understanding of accounting phenomena
  • identifying mechanisms for why accounting matters economically
  • resolving economic tensions between competing explanations
  • framing clean identification of causal economic results
  • informing standards or regulation positively
  • quantifying economic magnitude of results
  • closing contribution in discussion with implications

When not to use

  • documenting new associations without economic understanding
  • framing contribution as 'first to study X'
  • presenting mechanism-free results as theory advances
  • overclaiming causality beyond identification design
  • normative policy prescriptions instead of positive consequences
  • significance without economic interpretation
