gec-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Global Environmental Change (GEC) manuscript against the relevant literatures. GEC is interdisciplinary, so the paper must engage more than one body of work — environmental social science, governance, the relevant domain literature — and speak across them. Shapes the framing and citations; it does not write the literature review.

When to use

  • Positioning GEC manuscripts against relevant literatures
  • Drafting or revising the introduction and gap
  • Reconciling domain and social-science literatures
  • Choosing debates for a broad policy-relevant audience
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on missing key literatures
  • Reframing disciplinary papers for GEC fit

When not to use

  • Writing the literature review
