aaag-research-design

separate

Use when defending the research design of an Annals of the American Association of Geographers manuscript — spatial/quantitative analysis and GIScience, remote-sensing and physical-environmental methods, qualitative human-geography inference, or nature-society mixed methods. The Annals judges each tradition on its own terms. Strengthens the design; it does not write code.

When to use

  • Defending research design for Annals of the American Association of Geographers
  • Spatial and quantitative analysis design
  • GIScience methodology
  • Remote-sensing and physical-environmental methods
  • Qualitative human-geography inference
  • Nature-society mixed methods
  • Specifying identification, sampling, case selection, or measurement
  • Addressing reviewer questions on spatial autocorrelation, scale, MAUP, or validation

When not to use

  • Writing code
