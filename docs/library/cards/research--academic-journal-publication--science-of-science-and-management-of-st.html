Use when targeting 《科学学与科学技术管理》(Science of Science and Management of S&T — 天津市科技发展战略研究院主办、中国科学学与科技政策研究会会刊, 1980 年创刊) or deciding whether a Chinese science-of-science/S&T-management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/keyword house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《科学学与科学技术管理》
  • deciding if a manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a manuscript for this journal
  • listing desk-reject risks
  • checking journal fit and framing
  • verifying official submission requirements

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • using second-hand submission website formats
  • submitting to rural-economy
  • submitting to science-and-technology-progress-and-policy
  • submitting to science-research-management
  • submitting to scientific-decision-making
