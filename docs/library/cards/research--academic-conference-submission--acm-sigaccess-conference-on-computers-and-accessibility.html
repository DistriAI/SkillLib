acm-sigaccess-conference-on-computers-and-accessibility

separate

Use when targeting ACM SIGACCESS Conference on Computers and Accessibility (ASSETS) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for accessibility.

When to use

  • targeting ACM SIGACCESS Conference on Computers and Accessibility (ASSETS)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for accessibility
  • manuscript in accessible computing needs a conference-fit read before being formatted or submitted
  • re-framed from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis for this venue
  • accessible computing, assistive technology, disability studies, inclusive design, and user-centered evaluation
  • disability, accessibility, assistive technology, inclusive design, or access barriers are the research object

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • generic CS paper until the introduction names the exact subcommunity, contribution type, and proof or empirical standard
  • tangible artifacts to TEI and pervasive sensing deployments to UbiComp unless the primary contribution is an accessibility outcome, participatory process, or disability-centered system
