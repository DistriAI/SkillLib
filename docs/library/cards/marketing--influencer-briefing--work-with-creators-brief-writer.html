work-with-creators-brief-writer

separate

Writes a one-page brief for one creator you have already picked: hook ideas in their format, at most three approved must-say lines, never-says, the disclosure for their platform, deliverables, timeline, the usage-rights ask and two examples. Adds a short first-message draft (gift and paid versions) for you to send. Use alone when you already have the creator, their best fitting post and the goal; otherwise run work-with-creators.

When to use

  • Writing creator briefs
  • Drafting first messages
  • Defining must-say lines
  • Defining never-say lines
  • Specifying deliverables and timeline
  • Adding disclosure lines
  • Selecting examples
  • Creating UGC-for-ads or seeding variants

When not to use

  • Selecting creators
  • Sending messages
  • Inventing offers, claims, codes, or prices
  • Rewriting creator content with brand voice
