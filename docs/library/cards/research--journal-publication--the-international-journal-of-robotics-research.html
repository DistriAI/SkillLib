the-international-journal-of-robotics-research

separate

Use when targeting The International Journal of Robotics Research (IJRR) or deciding whether a robotics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the longer-form conceptually-framed-and-general contribution bar, depth-and-generality rigor, the IJRR-vs-T-RO routing, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for IJRR manuscripts
  • re-framing results into general methods or principles
  • choosing between IJRR and IEEE Transactions on Robotics
  • checking IJRR depth and generality bar
  • applying desk-reject heuristics
  • foundational methods in planning, control, estimation, learning, and perception
  • manipulation, locomotion, and navigation with generalizable principles
  • algorithmic and theoretical robotics with formal treatments

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
  • single working system or demo with no generalization
  • incremental results better suited to other venues
