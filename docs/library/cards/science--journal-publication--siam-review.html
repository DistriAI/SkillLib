siam-review

separate

Use when targeting SIAM Review (SIAM Rev.) or deciding whether an applied- or computational-mathematics manuscript fits this expository, broad-readership SIAM venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, expository-and-rigor bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting SIAM Review
  • deciding manuscript fit for SIAM Review
  • writing authoritative surveys
  • writing Research Spotlights
  • preparing SIGEST reprints
  • preparing Education section material
  • understanding SIAM Review sections
  • understanding expository bar and rejection reasons

When not to use

  • narrow primary research without broad expository value
  • specialized SIAM journal submissions
