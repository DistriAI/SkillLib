Use when targeting Asian Conference on Computer Vision (ACCV) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for computer vision regional flagship.

When to use

  • targeting Asian Conference on Computer Vision (ACCV)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks
  • computer vision methods and applications with strong Asia-Pacific community visibility
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • generic CS paper without naming exact subcommunity, contribution type, and proof or empirical standard
  • application-forward winter-cycle vision work
  • flagship global vision work
  • biomedical imaging when domain validation is central
