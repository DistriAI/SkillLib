journal-of-economic-theory

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Economic Theory (JET) or deciding whether a pure economic theory manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Economic Theory
  • deciding if pure economic theory manuscript fits
  • choosing among theory venues for general theoretical results
  • re-framing applied-theory or modeling papers
  • identifying JET desk-reject risks
  • finding credible theory or general-interest alternatives

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • providing empirical or quantitative evidence
  • handling econometric or identification theory
  • handling behavioral or experimental-adjacent theory
  • handling quantitative macro mechanisms
  • handling IO-flavored strategic theory with market application
