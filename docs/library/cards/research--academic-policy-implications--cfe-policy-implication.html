cfe-policy-implication

separate

Use when drafting or strengthening the policy-implications section of a Journal-of-Finance-and-Economics manuscript. Frames implications at the significance / institutional level rather than the actionable / ministry level used by Management-World.

When to use

  • drafting policy-implications section
  • strengthening policy-implications section
  • Journal-of-Finance-and-Economics manuscript
  • framing implications at institutional level
  • framing implications at macro level
  • framing implications at principle level
  • translating evidence into institutional language
  • calibrating policy implications to journal sections

When not to use

  • actionable / ministry level policy advice
  • Management-World style policy suggestions
  • specific operational details for ministries
  • predicting market trends
  • naming specific industries to support
  • general international situation discussion
  • management advice for single enterprises
  • policy claims not supported by design
