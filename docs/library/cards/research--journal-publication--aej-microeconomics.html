aej-microeconomics

separate

Use when targeting American Economic Journal: Microeconomics or deciding whether a microeconomic theory manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting American Economic Journal: Microeconomics
  • deciding whether a microeconomic theory manuscript fits this venue
  • micro theory, game theory, mechanism and market design, and theoretical IO
  • desk-reject risks and credible top-5 / theory-field alternative list
  • fit / venue-selection / re-framing tool

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • empirical or general-interest venue framing
  • macro or applied empirics
