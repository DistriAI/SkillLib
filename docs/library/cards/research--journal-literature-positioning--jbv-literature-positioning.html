jbv-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Business Venturing (JBV) manuscript in the entrepreneurship literature — joining a live conversation, problematizing assumptions instead of gap-spotting, and engaging across economics, psychology, and sociology. Positions the paper; it does not derive the mechanism (jbv-theory-development) or state the contribution claim (jbv-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • Positioning a JBV manuscript in entrepreneurship literature
  • Problematizing assumptions instead of gap-spotting
  • Engaging across economics, psychology, and sociology
  • Naming the specific entrepreneurship debate
  • Identifying the delta from closest papers
  • Signaling the home domain for field-editor routing
  • Distinguishing entrepreneurship literature from general management

When not to use

  • Deriving the mechanism
  • Stating the contribution claim
  • Gap-spotting as the sole hook
  • Citation dumping
  • Single-silo positioning for multidisciplinary phenomena
  • Straw-manning debates to manufacture novelty
