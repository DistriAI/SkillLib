arsoc-comprehensiveness-and-balance

separate

Use when calibrating completeness vs. selectivity and ensuring fairness across theoretical schools, methods, authors, and debates in an Annual Review of Sociology (ARSoc) review — including not over-promoting the author's own work. Audits coverage and even-handedness; it does not design the framework (arsoc-organizing-framework) or write prose (arsoc-writing-style).

When to use

  • calibrating completeness vs. selectivity
  • ensuring fairness across theoretical schools
  • ensuring fairness across methods
  • ensuring fairness across authors
  • ensuring fairness across debates
  • auditing coverage and even-handedness
  • preventing over-promotion of the author's own work
  • tiering the literature corpus

When not to use

  • designing the framework
  • writing prose
