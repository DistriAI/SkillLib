acm-interactive-surfaces-and-spaces

separate

Use when targeting ACM Interactive Surfaces and Spaces (ISS) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for interactive surfaces.

When to use

  • targeting ACM Interactive Surfaces and Spaces (ISS)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference-fit read before formatting or submission
  • re-framing from journal or arXiv style into CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis
  • interactive surfaces, tabletops, spatial interfaces, touch, tangible displays, collaborative interaction

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • broad human factors (route to CHI)
  • UI systems techniques (route to UIST)
  • tangible embodied interaction (route to TEI)
  • visual-interface or visualization claims (route to AVI/VIS)
  • human computation and crowdsourcing workflows (route to HCOMP)
  • accessibility-first outcomes (route to SIGACCESS)
