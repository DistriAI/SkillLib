smr-empirical-illustration

separate

Use when building the real-data empirical illustration for a Sociological Methods & Research (SMR) paper — a demonstration that the method changes a substantive conclusion, not a decorative example. Designs the illustration; does not derive properties or design the Monte Carlo.

When to use

  • building the real-data empirical illustration
  • demonstrating that the method changes a substantive conclusion
  • designing the illustration
  • showing where incumbent and new method diverge
  • choosing a dataset in the failure regime
  • reporting side-by-side results
  • stating the substantive stake

When not to use

  • deriving properties
  • designing the Monte Carlo
  • decorative examples
  • full substantive studies
  • ASR/AJS-style papers
