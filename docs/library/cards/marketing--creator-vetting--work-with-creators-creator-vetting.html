work-with-creators-creator-vetting

separate

Vets creators you have already named from their public pages. Covers the median views of their last 12 to 20 posts (sponsored and organic apart), a read of their comments, how often they post, audience fit with how sure it is, about 12 months of brand-safety checks, rival partnerships and rules for regulated categories. Gives a verdict with dated numbers for each one. Use alone when you already have the creator handles and the brand's goal; otherwise run work-with-creators.

When to use

  • Vetting named creators
  • Analyzing median views of last 12-20 posts
  • Reading visible comments
  • Assessing audience fit and confidence
  • Conducting brand-safety checks
  • Checking rival partnerships
  • Regulated category rules
  • Providing verdicts with dated numbers

When not to use

  • Finding creators when handles are unknown
  • Guessing hidden data like views or comments behind login
  • Inferring ethnicity, age, gender, skin tone, or health
  • Business-contact lookups
