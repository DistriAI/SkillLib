critical-care-medicine

separate

Use when targeting Critical Care Medicine or deciding whether an intensive/critical-care study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's ICU-focused fit, the clinical-evidence and translational bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, SCCM house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • ICU, sepsis, resuscitation, or organ-support study fit check
  • Re-framing critical-care studies around intensive-care management questions
  • Choosing between Critical Care Medicine, AJRCCM, and The Lancet Respiratory Medicine
  • Reviewing reporting-guideline, registration, and ICU-trial expectations
  • Checking SCCM house style and structure
  • Pre-submission self-check for Critical Care Medicine

When not to use

  • Clinical or regulatory advice
  • Replacing the journal's current instructions for authors
