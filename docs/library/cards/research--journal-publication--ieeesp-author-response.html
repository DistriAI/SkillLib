ieeesp-author-response

separate

Use when drafting the IEEE S&P (Oakland) rebuttal for second-round papers or the response plan for a Revise decision, including triaging reviews under the ~5-day window, answering adaptive-attack and threat-model objections, handling ethics questions with documentation, and writing against the Revise expectations summary.

When to use

  • drafting IEEE S&P rebuttal
  • drafting Revise response plan
  • triaging reviews under 5-day window
  • answering adaptive-attack objections
  • answering threat-model objections
  • handling ethics questions with documentation
  • writing against Revise expectations summary

When not to use

  • first-round submissions
  • other venues not S&P
