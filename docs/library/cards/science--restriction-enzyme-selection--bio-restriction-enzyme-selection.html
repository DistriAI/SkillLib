bio-restriction-enzyme-selection

separate

Select restriction enzymes by criteria using Biopython Bio.Restriction. Find enzymes that cut once, don't cut, produce specific overhangs, are commercially available, or have compatible ends for cloning.

When to use

  • selecting restriction enzymes for cloning
  • selecting restriction enzymes for analysis
  • finding enzymes that cut once
  • finding enzymes that don't cut
  • finding enzymes with specific overhangs
  • checking commercial availability
  • finding compatible enzyme pairs
  • filtering by overhang type
