colt-experiments

separate

Use when deciding whether a COLT (Conference on Learning Theory) paper needs numerical content at all — COLT has no experiments requirement — and, when numerics genuinely help, designing small illustrative simulations that visualize a proved bound, a separation, or a phase transition without diluting the theory.

When to use

  • deciding whether to include numerics in a COLT paper
  • designing small illustrative simulations
  • visualizing a proved bound
  • visualizing a separation
  • visualizing a phase transition
  • creating log-log rate check figures
  • plotting separation between models
  • plotting threshold phenomena

When not to use

  • decorative benchmark tables
  • empirical evaluation for non-COLT venues
  • compensating for proof gaps
  • simulating outside the proved regime without labeling
