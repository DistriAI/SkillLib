gcb-study-design

separate

Use when designing the study behind a Global Change Biology (GCB) manuscript — manipulative experiments, observational/gradient studies, or process modelling of biological responses to global change. GCB reviewers probe scale, replication, realism, and causal inference. Guides design choices; it does not collect or simulate data.

When to use

  • Designing warming, eCO2, drought, or N-addition experiments
  • Setting up gradient or observational studies
  • Setting up process-model or species-distribution-model experiments
  • Justifying scale, replication, controls, and manipulation realism
  • Addressing confounding, pseudoreplication, or extrapolation concerns
  • Designing evidence synthesis or meta-analysis protocols

When not to use

  • Collecting data
  • Simulating data
  • Data analysis
