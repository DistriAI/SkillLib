eer-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a question is a fit for the European Economic Review (EER) — general-interest across all of economics — rather than a field journal, JEEA, or The Economic Journal. Diagnoses fit and reframes for breadth; it does not build the identification or the model.

When to use

  • deciding whether a question is a fit for EER
  • diagnosing fit for general-interest economics
  • reframing narrow questions for breadth
  • choosing among EER, JEEA, The Economic Journal, or field journals
  • assessing if a contribution is broad-interest or field-internal

When not to use

  • building the identification
  • building the model
