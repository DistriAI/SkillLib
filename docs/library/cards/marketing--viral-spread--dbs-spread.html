传播心理解码。给定一段内容，用 5 个经典传播学理论解码它为什么能引起受众共鸣，分析受众情绪底层和有效立场，输出聊天室的讨论方向。 触发方式：/dbs-spread、「传播心理解码」、「为什么这个能火」、「受众想听什么」、「这个内容打中了什么情绪」 Transmission psychology decoder. Given a piece of content, analyze the psychological mechanism that makes it resonate, identify the emotional core and effective stance, and output direction for chatroom discussion. Trigger: /dbs-spread, "why does this resonate", "what emotion does this hit", "decode this content"

When to use

  • Decoding content psychological resonance mechanisms
  • Analyzing effective stance and entry angle
  • Identifying audience emotional core
  • Determining what audience wants to hear next
  • Providing chatroom discussion directions
  • Analyzing why content resonates with audience

When not to use

  • Predicting platform algorithm performance
  • Predicting completion rate or click-through rate
  • Predicting recommendation volume
  • Predicting if content will go viral
  • Giving good or bad ratings to content
  • Evaluating content transmission power in isolation from platform
