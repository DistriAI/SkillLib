joap-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing a Journal of Applied Psychology (JAP) manuscript to fit APA 7th-edition style, the I-O contribution-forward narrative arc, and the article-type length norms (Feature Article commensurate with contribution; tighter Research Report). Tightens prose, structure, and style; it does not invent content.

When to use

  • Drafting the Introduction or Discussion
  • Doing final polish
  • Writing the structured abstract
  • Aligning the manuscript to APA 7th edition
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on buried contributions
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on over-claims
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on wandering writing

When not to use

  • Generating claims
  • Inventing content
