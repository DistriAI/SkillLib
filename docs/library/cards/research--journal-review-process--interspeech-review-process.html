interspeech-review-process

separate

Use when reasoning about how INTERSPEECH review actually works — area-based assignment inside ISCA's scientific program, multi-reviewer scoring and meta-review on CMT, what the rebuttal can and cannot move, oral/poster allocation, ISCA best student paper selection, and reading a decision letter for next-cycle strategy.

When to use

  • reasoning about INTERSPEECH review mechanics
  • understanding area-based reviewer assignment
  • analyzing multi-reviewer scoring and meta-review
  • assessing what rebuttals can and cannot fix
  • understanding oral and poster allocation
  • reading decision letters for next-cycle strategy
  • calibrating expectations by review phase
  • interpreting ISCA best student paper selection

When not to use

  • writing the actual rebuttal text
  • designing experiments for the paper
  • selecting the paper topic
  • optimizing submission for awards
