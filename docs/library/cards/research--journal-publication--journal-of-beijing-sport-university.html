journal-of-beijing-sport-university

separate

Use when targeting 《北京体育大学学报》(Journal of Beijing Sport University) — a CSSCI comprehensive sport-science journal hosted by Beijing Sport University, China's flagship sport academy. Accepts the full breadth of sport science: exercise physiology/biomechanics/sports medicine, sports training & competitive sport, sport humanities & social science, and school PE. Best for substantial, well-evidenced studies across any sub-field; route narrowly applied/competitive pieces to 《中国体育科技》 and theory-defining work to 《体育科学》.
