journal-of-beijing-sport-university

separate

Use when targeting 《北京体育大学学报》(Journal of Beijing Sport University) — a CSSCI comprehensive sport-science journal hosted by Beijing Sport University, China's flagship sport academy. Accepts the full breadth of sport science: exercise physiology/biomechanics/sports medicine, sports training & competitive sport, sport humanities & social science, and school PE. Best for substantial, well-evidenced studies across any sub-field; route narrowly applied/competitive pieces to 《中国体育科技》 and theory-defining work to 《体育科学》.

When to use

  • Exercise physiology/biomechanics/sports medicine
  • Sports training & competitive sport
  • Sport humanities & social science
  • School PE
  • Substantial, well-evidenced studies across any sub-field
  • Comprehensive, wide-scope sport science CSSCI journal
  • Mid-to-high quality manuscripts with standard methods
  • Uncertain whether to submit to comprehensive or specialized journals

When not to use

  • Narrowly applied/competitive pieces
  • Theory-defining work
  • Manuscripts with insufficient weight or rough design
  • Economics/management or clinical papers where sport is only a case
  • Sports history or traditional ethnic sports
  • Specialized school physical education journals
