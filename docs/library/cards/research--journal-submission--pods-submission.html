pods-submission

separate

Use when auditing an ACM PODS research submission for EasyChair readiness, covering the chosen cycle's abstract-then-paper deadlines, the acmsmall[review,anonymous] format and 15-page budget, the at-submission proof appendix, lightweight double-anonymity, the declared conflicts of interest, PACMMOD-track publication, and desk-risk triage before the AoE cutoff.

When to use

  • auditing ACM PODS research submission
  • checking EasyChair readiness
  • verifying abstract-then-paper deadlines
  • checking acmsmall[review,anonymous] format
  • verifying 15-page budget
  • checking at-submission proof appendix
  • verifying lightweight double-anonymity
  • checking declared conflicts of interest

When not to use

  • non-PODS submissions
  • two-column sigconf style
  • IEEE template
  • online or external appendices
