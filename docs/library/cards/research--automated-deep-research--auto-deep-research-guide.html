auto-deep-research-guide

separate

Automated deep research tool for thorough topic investigation

When to use

  • thorough topic investigation
  • comprehensive literature reviews
  • grant proposals requiring background knowledge
  • advising students on adjacent topics
  • researchers entering a new field
  • iterative search-analyze-refine cycles
  • structured understanding of topic history and debates

When not to use

  • simple literature search
  • returning a list of papers
