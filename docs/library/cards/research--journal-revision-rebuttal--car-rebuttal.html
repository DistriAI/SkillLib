car-rebuttal

separate

Use when a Contemporary Accounting Research (CAR) revise-and-resubmit arrives — planning the revision and drafting a point-by-point response to two reviewers and the subject Editor, including any new analyses, robustness, and updated Data Integrity/code-sharing materials. Drafts the response; it does not run the new estimation (car-data-analysis) or the final preflight (car-submission).

When to use

  • Planning CAR revise-and-resubmit revisions
  • Drafting point-by-point response to reviewers and Editor
  • Addressing new identification tests or robustness requests
  • Updating data availability and code-sharing materials
  • Managing CAR code-sharing and data-availability requirements

When not to use

  • Running new estimation
  • Final preflight submission
