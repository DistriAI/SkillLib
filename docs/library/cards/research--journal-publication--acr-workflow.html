acr-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which acr-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from fit positioning through rebuttal for 《会计研究》 (Accounting Research). Routes — does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which acr-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from fit positioning through rebuttal
  • Determining if a manuscript fits Accounting Research or Financial Research
  • Identifying the current weakest accounting link in a manuscript
  • Routing based on symptoms like missing mechanism or identification issues

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized acr-* skills
  • Handling financial pricing mechanisms
  • Handling corporate governance management theory
  • Handling clean causal inference with economic theory
  • Handling macro or industrial effects of tax/industrial policy
