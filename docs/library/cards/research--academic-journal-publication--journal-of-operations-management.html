journal-of-operations-management

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Operations Management (JOM) or deciding whether an empirical / survey-based / behavioral operations-and-supply-chain manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Operations Management (JOM)
  • deciding if empirical OM/SCM manuscript fits JOM
  • survey-based research with rigorous construct development
  • behavioral operations experiments and field studies
  • theory-driven empirical work using secondary data
  • re-framing modeling-heavy papers around empirical theoretical contribution
  • identifying JOM desk-reject risks
  • providing credible OM/MS alternative list

When not to use

  • analytical OM theory and models
  • broad OM with society audience
  • broad management insight or empirics across subfields
  • OR methodology or optimization for its own sake
  • computational operations research
  • manuscripts for the management Journal of Management
