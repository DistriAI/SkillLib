jmf-workflow

separate

Use as the entry point for any Journal of Marriage and Family (JMF) manuscript. Routes to the right JMF sub-skill based on where you are in the lifecycle and whether the work fits a full article or a brief report. JMF is the interdisciplinary flagship of family science; the router's first job is to confirm a genuine family-science contribution. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new JMF paper
  • Deciding between full article and brief report
  • Routing mid-project to the next applicable skill
  • Handling decision letters for rebuttal
  • Confirming family-science contribution fit
  • Executing a review pass with evidence gates
  • Comparing against sibling journals for re-routing

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Treating JMF as a general sociology or psychology journal
  • Padding a focused study into a full article
  • Skipping data availability statements until the end
