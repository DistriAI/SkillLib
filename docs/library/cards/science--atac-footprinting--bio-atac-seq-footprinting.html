bio-atac-seq-footprinting

separate

Detect transcription factor binding sites through footprinting analysis in ATAC-seq data using TOBIAS. Use when identifying TF occupancy patterns within accessible regions, as TF binding protects DNA from Tn5 cutting.

When to use

  • Identify TF binding footprints in ATAC-seq data
  • Detect protected DNA regions within accessible chromatin
  • Compare TF binding between two conditions
  • Identify regulators with differential activity
  • Extract and visualize aggregate ATAC-seq signal around predicted TF binding sites
  • Scan for motifs in peaks
