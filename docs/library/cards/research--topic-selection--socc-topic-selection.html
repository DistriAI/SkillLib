socc-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a cloud-computing project belongs at ACM SoCC or should be routed to OSDI, NSDI, EuroSys, USENIX ATC, SOSP, or a data venue (SIGMOD/VLDB), leaning on SoCC's unique joint SIGMOD+SIGOPS identity, its welcome of measurement and experience papers alongside systems-building, and the model-swap and re-label tests.

When to use

  • deciding if a cloud-computing project belongs at ACM SoCC
  • routing projects to OSDI, NSDI, EuroSys, USENIX ATC, SOSP, or SIGMOD/VLDB
  • applying model-swap and re-label tests
  • evaluating fit for systems-and-data intersection contributions
  • assessing measurement, experience, or cloud-economics papers
  • checking evidence maturity for submission readiness
  • performing cheap reconnaissance on SoCC programs and citations

When not to use

  • drafting the paper
  • writing code or implementing systems
  • conducting experiments or collecting traces
  • submitting to conferences
