jfr-mechanism

separate

Use when the mechanism section of a 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) manuscript stops at "promotes / inhibits" — to pin the effect to a concrete financial channel (credit / risk-taking / balance-sheet / information) with channel-specific evidence rather than a generic mediation regression.

When to use

  • pinning effects to credit channels
  • pinning effects to risk-taking channels
  • pinning effects to balance-sheet channels
  • pinning effects to information channels
  • distinguishing competing financial channels
  • validating channel magnitude compatibility

When not to use

  • generic mediation regression without channel evidence
  • mechanisms stated only as 'promotes' or 'inhibits'
  • mismatched macro/micro channel lineages
