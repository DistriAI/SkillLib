atc-workflow

separate

Use when planning an ATC (ACM SIGOPS Annual Technical Conference, formerly USENIX ATC) campaign end to end — running the year backward from the early-June deadline through the two review rounds, the rebuttal, conditional-acceptance shepherding, the post-acceptance artifact evaluation, and the ACM Open Access camera-ready for the November Hong Kong conference.

When to use

  • planning ATC campaign backward from deadline
  • managing two-round extended-abstract review
  • preparing rebuttal and conditional-acceptance shepherding
  • coordinating post-acceptance artifact evaluation
  • preparing ACM Open Access camera-ready
  • scheduling system freeze and experiment finalization
  • hardening two-page extended abstract
  • managing double-blind submission and HotCRP fields

When not to use

  • planning OSDI/NSDI/EuroSys/FAST campaigns
  • writing full paper prose without ATC style constraints
  • designing new experiments after submission
  • managing non-ATC conference logistics
