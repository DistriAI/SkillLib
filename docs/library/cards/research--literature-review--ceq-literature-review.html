ceq-literature-review

separate

Use when positioning a 《经济学(季刊)》 (China Economic Quarterly, CEQ) manuscript's contribution against the literature — benchmarking the precise method/result delta versus specific field papers (English + Chinese), not piling citations. Use after the topic is set and before/alongside identification.

When to use

  • Positioning CEQ manuscript contribution against literature
  • Benchmarking method or result delta versus specific papers
  • Writing precise contribution statements
  • Integrating English and Chinese literature
  • Identifying methodological source citations
  • Distinguishing identification, mechanism, or new parameters

When not to use

  • Piling citations without precise positioning
  • Writing generic contributions like 'enriching research'
  • Listing literature chronologically
  • Citing only application papers without methodological sources
  • Claiming 'first in China' as the primary contribution
