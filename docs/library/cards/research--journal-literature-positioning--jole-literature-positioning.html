jole-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Labor Economics (JOLE) manuscript against the labor literature — staking the contribution against the closest papers under Chicago author-date citation norms, not writing a standalone survey. Positions the paper; it does not run analysis.

When to use

  • Positioning a JOLE manuscript against labor literature
  • Staking contribution against closest papers
  • Correcting Chicago author-date citation style
  • Ordering in-text citations chronologically then alphabetically
  • Identifying the gap in existing literature
  • Connecting paper to adjacent labor subfields
  • Rebutting 'this has been done' concerns

When not to use

  • Writing a standalone survey
  • Running analysis
  • Blinding self-citations
  • Padding literature to look thorough
