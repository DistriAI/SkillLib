dbs-deconstruct

separate

dontbesilent 概念拆解。用维特根斯坦 + 奥派经济学的方法，把模糊的商业概念拆到原子级别。 触发方式：/dbs-deconstruct、/拆概念、「帮我拆解这个概念」「这个词到底什么意思」 Concept deconstruction using Wittgenstein + Austrian economics framework. Trigger: /dbs-deconstruct, "deconstruct this concept", "what does this really mean"

When to use

  • deconstructing vague business concepts
  • analyzing concept usage in different contexts
  • detecting pseudo-concepts
  • calibrating concepts with Austrian economics
  • distinguishing Questions from Problems
  • performing deep ontological analysis with 7 tables

When not to use

  • explaining concepts with more complex concepts
  • providing analyses that are empty or superficial
  • pretending to understand things that are not understood
