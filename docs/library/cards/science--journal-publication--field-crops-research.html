field-crops-research

separate

Use when targeting Field Crops Research or deciding whether an agronomy / crop-physiology manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the field-scale multi-environment and replication bar, data-reporting expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a field-agronomy or crop-physiology paper
  • framing results for generalizability across environments
  • choosing between Field Crops Research, agronomy-for-sustainable-development, and agriculture-ecosystems-and-environment
  • desk-reject heuristics around replication and field validity
  • crop yield and yield-component determinants under field conditions
  • resource-use efficiency: water-, nitrogen-, and radiation-use efficiency
  • cropping systems and management evaluated in the field
  • crop simulation modeling calibrated and validated against field data

When not to use

  • single site-year, unreplicated trial presented as a general agronomic finding
  • pot/glasshouse-only study generalized to the field with no field validation
  • explicit sustainability framing
  • review/meta-analysis of cropping systems
  • environmental fluxes (GHG)
