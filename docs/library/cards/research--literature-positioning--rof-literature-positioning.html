rof-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Review of Finance (RoF) manuscript against the finance frontier — citing the right top-three work, honoring RoF's strict author-responsibility citation norm, and staking the contribution without a standalone survey.

When to use

  • Positioning a Review of Finance manuscript
  • Citing top-three finance work
  • Staking contribution without a standalone survey
  • Mapping the finance frontier
  • Bridging empirical and theoretical finance
  • Documenting search protocol for RoF

When not to use

  • Writing a standalone survey
  • Padding the bibliography
  • Citing only one's own subfield
  • Omitting close papers
