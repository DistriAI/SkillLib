jbes-identification-strategy

separate

Use when the methodological core of a Journal of Business & Economic Statistics (JBES) paper is the bottleneck — assumptions, regularity conditions, asymptotic theory, and Monte Carlo design for a new estimator, test, or algorithm. Stress-tests the method's validity, not a causal design.

When to use

  • assumptions and regularity conditions
  • asymptotic theory
  • Monte Carlo design
  • new estimator validity
  • new test validity
  • computational algorithm correctness
  • identification of target parameter
  • finite-sample evidence

When not to use

  • causal design
