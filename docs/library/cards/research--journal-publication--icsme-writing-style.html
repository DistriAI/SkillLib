icsme-writing-style

separate

Use when revising an IEEE ICSME paper for a maintenance/evolution contribution stated on the first page, research-question contracts, a threats-to-validity section that argues rather than recites, evidence proportional to the claim, double-anonymous wording, and disciplined use of the IEEEtran two-column 10-page budget.

When to use

  • revising an IEEE ICSME paper
  • stating a maintenance/evolution contribution on the first page
  • defining research-question contracts
  • writing a threats-to-validity section that argues rather than recites
  • ensuring evidence is proportional to the claim
  • maintaining double-anonymous wording
  • managing the IEEEtran two-column 10-page budget

When not to use

  • greenfield systems demos
  • generic ML results with a maintenance title glued on
