journal-of-the-acm

separate

Use when targeting the Journal of the ACM (JACM) or deciding whether a theoretical computer science manuscript fits this most-prestigious broad CS-theory venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, proof-and-rigor bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting the Journal of the ACM
  • deciding if a theoretical CS manuscript fits JACM
  • preparing a complete journal version of a breakthrough conference paper
  • assessing significance and rigor bar for JACM
  • identifying common rejection reasons for JACM
  • framing foundational or definitive CS theory results

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • narrow or incremental results
  • non-theoretical computer science topics
