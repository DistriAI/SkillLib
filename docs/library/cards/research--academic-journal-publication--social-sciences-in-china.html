social-sciences-in-china

separate

Use when a manuscript targets 《中国社会科学》(Social Sciences in China) — the CASS flagship and the single most prestigious cross-disciplinary social-science journal in China. Apply to judge fit (思想性 over 技术炫技), to reframe a paper around a 重大问题 + 原创理论 + 中国自主知识体系, or to polish toward its grand-but-rigorous house style. Not for narrow empirical notes.

When to use

  • judge fit for 《中国社会科学》
  • reframe paper around 重大问题 + 原创理论
  • polish toward grand-but-rigorous house style
  • determine if manuscript qualifies for top social science platform
  • refine theoretical proposition layer
  • assess cross-disciplinary narrative fit

When not to use

  • narrow empirical notes
  • pure regression papers without theoretical propositions
  • business or management empirical applications
  • policy evaluation without theoretical elevation
