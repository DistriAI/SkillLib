mobicom-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or revising a MobiCom paper for house style — putting the wireless/mobile mechanism and its operating regime on the first page, pairing every claim with over-the-air measurement, replacing superlatives with percentiles and intervals, and fitting the argument into the double-column 12-page body.

When to use

  • drafting a MobiCom paper
  • revising a MobiCom paper
  • structuring the first page arc
  • naming the operating regime
  • pairing claims with evidence
  • replacing superlatives with percentiles
  • fitting content into 12 double-column pages
  • applying prose discipline

When not to use

  • writing non-MobiCom papers
  • designing wireless mechanisms
  • conducting over-the-air measurements
  • formatting supplementary material
