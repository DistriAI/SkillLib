sw-genre-anatomy

separate

Genre anatomy from John Truby's The Anatomy of Genres (类型解剖 / 类型节拍 / 混合类型 / 超越类型) — genres as beat systems with a life philosophy underneath; three rules (hit every beat of your genre, mix three or four genres, transcend the primary genre), the ladder and families of twelve genres (Horror 恐怖, Action 动作, Myth 神话, Memoir and Coming-of-Age 回忆录与成长, Science Fiction 科幻, Crime 犯罪, Comedy 喜剧, Western 西部, Gangster 黑帮, Fantasy 奇幻, Detective and Thriller 侦探与惊悚, Love 爱情), each with its hero/opponent/world/plot-shape template, beat list, hard rules with failure conditions, subgenres and transcendent paths, plus a genre-mixing worksheet and a diagnostic. Kept beside sw-truby-anatomy and the Field/Snyder page maps. Use when choosing or confirming a script's genre, when a genre story feels generic or misses the beats its audience expects, when mixing genres, when a horror lacks pressure, an action lacks plot, a love story lacks opposition or a detective story lacks reveals.

When to use

  • Choosing or confirming a script's genre
  • Fixing generic genre stories that miss expected beats
  • Mixing two to four genres
  • Diagnosing existing genre script drafts
  • Transcending the primary genre to avoid mediocrity
  • Applying genre-specific beat lists and hard rules

When not to use

  • Operatic projects
  • Chekhov or Ozu-style anti-climax projects
  • General story skeleton without genre focus
