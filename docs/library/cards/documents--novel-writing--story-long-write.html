story-long-write

separate

长篇网文规划与写作。支持只讨论结构、只写大纲或指定细纲，明确要求正文后再写章节。触发方式：/story-long-write、/写长篇、「帮我开书」「定设定」「出卷纲」「规划剧情」「写大纲」「补细纲」「日更」「续写」「继续写」「修改第X章」「回炉」「重写第X章」。

When to use

  • long-form web novel planning
  • story structure discussion
  • outline and volume planning
  • detailed chapter plotting
  • writing specified chapters
  • daily updates and continuation
  • revising or rewriting chapters
  • setting and character definition

When not to use

  • short story writing
  • non-fiction writing
  • automatic full book generation without confirmation
  • writing body text without explicit authorization
  • expanding scope beyond requested planning level
