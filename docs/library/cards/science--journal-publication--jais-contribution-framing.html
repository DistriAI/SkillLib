jais-contribution-framing

separate

Use when sharpening the theoretical contribution of a Journal of the Association for Information Systems (JAIS) manuscript — stating what the paper adds to IS knowledge, matching the contribution claim to the chosen category, and making it legible to a pluralistic, theory-forward reviewer pool. Frames and tests the contribution; it does not build the theory (jais-theory-development) or run the analysis (jais-data-analysis).
