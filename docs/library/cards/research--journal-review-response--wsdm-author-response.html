wsdm-author-response

separate

Use when planning author-side communication for WSDM, a venue that historically runs no rebuttal - covers preemptive objection engineering inside the submitted PDF, the narrow legitimate channels to chairs after submission, reading reviews you cannot answer, and converting a rejection into a stronger next-cycle or sibling-venue submission.

When to use

  • preemptive objection engineering inside the submitted PDF
  • identifying legitimate channels to chairs after submission
  • reading reviews you cannot answer
  • converting a rejection into a stronger next-cycle or sibling-venue submission
  • building an objection inventory before submission
  • triaging reviews for the next version
  • planning the rejection fork

When not to use

  • scripting a rebuttal
  • arguing merit to chairs
  • adding results to chairs
  • lobbying chairs
  • disagreement dressed as misconduct
