ssc-fit-positioning

separate

Use when judging whether a manuscript is on-target for 《中国社会科学》 (Social Sciences in China) before investing in revision — screening for 思想分量 over technical sophistication, big-question level, cross-disciplinary reach, and original theory — and to recommend a better venue when fit is low. The most common save: stopping a niche empirical paper from being mis-submitted.
