plg-fundamentals

separate

Help users master the transition from sales-led to product-led growth by leveraging the product itself as the primary vehicle for customer education and revenue generation.

When to use

  • Assessing product viability for self-serve motion
  • Auditing data and experimentation infrastructure
  • Mapping user journey for automation
  • Prioritizing funnel levers
  • Implementing workflow-integrated value sampling
  • Automating repetitive sales tasks
  • Aligning marketing, sales, and engineering for PLG

When not to use

  • High-complexity products requiring extensive customization
  • Targeting only a handful of large companies
  • Products where users cannot reach aha moment without manual services
