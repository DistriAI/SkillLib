journal-of-xian-physical-education-university

separate

Use when targeting 《西安体育学院学报》(Journal of Xi'an Physical Education University) — a CSSCI comprehensive sport-science journal hosted by Xi'an Physical Education University, covering the full breadth of sport science (training & competitive sport, exercise science, sport humanities & social science, school PE) with a solid northwest-China all-round profile. Best for well-framed, well-evidenced studies needing a comprehensive CSSCI sport venue; route to specialist venues when a sharper home exists.

When to use

  • 运动训练与竞技体育
  • 运动人体科学
  • 体育人文社会学
  • 学校体育
  • 综合性、规范的体育学 CSSCI 刊
  • 方法规范、证据扎实的稿件
  • 不确定该投综合学报还是专门刊

When not to use

  • 体育史 / 民族传统体育
  • 强理论体育社会科学
  • 运动人体科学实验偏重
  • 应用 / 竞技
  • 学科理论引领
  • 学校体育
  • 体育只是案例的经管 / 临床稿
