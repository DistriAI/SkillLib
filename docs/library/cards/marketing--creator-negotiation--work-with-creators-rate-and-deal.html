work-with-creators-rate-and-deal

separate

Prices a creator you have already vetted. Gives a low and a target fee from median views and the platform's typical cost per 1,000 views, a walk-away ceiling from your goal and margin, and usage rights priced on their own. Checks against real quoted rates and recommends gift, paid, affiliate or a mix, with one line showing the math. Use alone when you already have the creator's recent view numbers, your goal and your unit cost; otherwise run work-with-creators.

When to use

  • Pricing a vetted creator
  • Calculating low and target fees
  • Determining walk-away ceiling
  • Pricing usage rights
  • Recommending deal structure
  • Checking against real quoted rates

When not to use

  • Vetting creators
  • Running work-with-creators when view numbers are missing
