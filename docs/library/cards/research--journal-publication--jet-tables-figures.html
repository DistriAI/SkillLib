jet-tables-figures

separate

Use when designing the exhibits in a Journal of Economic Theory (JET) paper — mostly schematic theory figures (best-response/phase diagrams, lattices, timing trees, mechanism flow) and small illustrative tables, kept consistent with the body's notation and rendered as vector graphics. Light for a theorem-proof journal; numerical tables only when grounded in theory.

When to use

  • designing schematic theory figures
  • creating best-response or phase diagrams
  • drawing lattices or Hasse diagrams
  • constructing timing or extensive-form trees
  • making mechanism or information flow diagrams
  • creating small illustrative tables
  • formatting exhibits for elsarticle submission

When not to use

  • large numerical tables presented as results
  • decorative exhibits that do not reduce cognitive load
  • raster screenshots or low-resolution diagrams
  • figures introducing notation conflicting with the proof
