comparative-economic-and-social-systems

separate

Use when targeting 《经济社会体制比较》(Comparative Economic and Social Systems — 中央党史和文献研究院主管、中央编译出版社主办的 CSSCI 双月刊, 1985 年创刊, 不收版面/审稿费) or deciding whether a Chinese social-science/econ/management manuscript fits this journal. Applies the journal's fit, comparative framing, abstract/keyword house-style, official-submission-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《经济社会体制比较》
  • deciding manuscript fit for the journal
  • rewriting general Chinese econ/management manuscripts for the journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking official submission requirements
  • comparative economic and social systems research

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • manuscripts that only list policy documents without academic questions
  • industry background or local case studies without comparative framing
  • pure reform policy without economic problem awareness
  • political economy theory without mechanism explanation
