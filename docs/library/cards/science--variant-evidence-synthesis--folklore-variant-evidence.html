folklore-variant-evidence

separate

Retrieves ClinGen gene-disease validity assertions for a public gene or disease, and reviews source-linked public evidence and literature for one supported GRCh38 germline nuclear SNV or simple indel through Folklore Clinical Variant Interpretation MCP. Used when a scientific agent must branch deterministically on resolved, ambiguous, not-found, invalid, unsupported, or unavailable variant outcomes; chain a resolved public variant into related literature or publication details; or preserve evidence provenance without accepting patient, phenotype, family, segregation, or private case data.

When to use

  • Retrieving ClinGen gene-disease validity assertions
  • Reviewing source-linked public evidence for one variant
  • Handling resolved, ambiguous, not-found, invalid, unsupported, or unavailable variant outcomes
  • Chaining a resolved public variant into related literature or publication details
  • Preserving evidence provenance
  • Retrieving structured public variant evidence
  • Automated variant-level ACMG/AMP decision support

When not to use

  • VCF annotation
  • Batch processing
  • Somatic variants
  • Structural variants
  • Polygenic scores
  • Patient-specific interpretation
  • Accepting patient, phenotype, family, segregation, or private case data
