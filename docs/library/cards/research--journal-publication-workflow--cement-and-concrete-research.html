cement-and-concrete-research

separate

Use when targeting Cement and Concrete Research or deciding whether a cementitious-materials manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's materials-science fit, the mechanism-plus-characterization bar, the distinction from mix-design optimization, characterization rigor, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for cement-chemistry manuscripts
  • re-framing mix performance into mechanism stories
  • deciding between mechanism and proportioning emphasis
  • characterization-rigor bar for cementitious work
  • desk-reject heuristics for cementitious materials
  • venue selection for durability and microstructure papers

When not to use

  • mix-design or proportioning optimization with no mechanism
  • stand-alone technique demonstration
  • short or purely applied results
  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
