torchdomainauctionbot

separate

Domain lending protocol on Solana. Domains become tokens. Tokens become collateral. Top holder controls the domain. Borrow SOL against your position -- but get liquidated and you lose the domain. Built on torchsdk v3.7.23 and the Torch Market protocol.

When to use

  • Domain lending protocol on Solana
  • Launching domains as tokens with bonding curves
  • Borrowing SOL against domain token positions
  • Monitoring active lending positions
  • Liquidating underwater loans through a Torch Vault
  • Managing domain lease rotation upon liquidation

When not to use

  • Autonomous invocation without explicit user initiation
