competitor-social-research

combined

Use when the user wants to research competitors' social media strategy, compare brands or creators, find what content is working in a niche, identify content gaps, or produce a practical social strategy brief from public social data. Compare competitors' social content across platforms to identify recurring topics, formats, hooks, audience reactions, distribution patterns, and credible content opportunities for a brand.

When to use

  • compare competitors on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Threads, or other social platforms
  • find what content is working in a niche
  • benchmark posting frequency, formats, topics, and engagement
  • identify content gaps or opportunities
  • build a social strategy from competitor research

When not to use

  • comparing raw views between different brand sizes without context
  • mixing platforms without platform context
  • assuming profile matches from ambiguous company names
  • presenting inferred strategy as fact
