journal-of-ecology

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Ecology (J Ecol) or deciding whether a full-length plant-focused or broader ecology manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Ecology
  • deciding if a full-length plant-focused manuscript fits
  • deciding if a broader ecology manuscript fits
  • choosing between Journal of Ecology, Ecology Letters, and Functional Ecology
  • framing a study with solid empirical depth for a high-impact full-length journal
  • checking Journal of Ecology scope boundaries
  • checking data-archiving conventions
  • checking desk-reject criteria

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official guidelines
