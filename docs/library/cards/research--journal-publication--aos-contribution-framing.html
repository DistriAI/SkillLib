aos-contribution-framing

separate

Use when articulating what an Accounting, Organizations and Society (AOS) manuscript contributes — converting findings into a claim that changes how the field understands accounting's social, organizational, behavioral or institutional operation. Frames the payoff; it does not produce new analysis (aos-data-analysis).

When to use

  • articulating AOS manuscript contributions
  • converting findings into conceptual claims
  • framing the payoff of accounting research
  • drafting falsifiable-in-spirit contribution sentences
  • structuring the discussion section for AOS
  • identifying required revisions for prior papers
  • arguing transferability through theory or process

When not to use

  • producing new analysis
  • aos-data-analysis
  • creating tables and figures
  • providing managerial or consulting advice
