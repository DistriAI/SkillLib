Conflict

The two copies overlap on the core Ansoff 2x2 structure and the risk gradient, but they disagree on the procedure and output. phuryn/pm-skills is a self-contained, prescriptive template that lists concrete strategies and examples per quadrant and ends with a prioritized roadmap. deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills is an evidence-backed research workflow that enforces a question budget, a search-plan gate, Fact/Inference/Assumption labels, a strict output schema, and a risk-rated sequence. Crowning one would hide a real alternative: a quick strategic template versus a rigorous, evidence-gated research run.
