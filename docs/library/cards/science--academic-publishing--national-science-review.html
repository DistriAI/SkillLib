When to use

  • targeting National Science Review (NSR)

  • navigating between NSR, the-innovation, pnas, nature-communications, and science-advances
  • reframing manuscripts for NSR
  • assessing fit for physical, life, earth, and energy sciences
  • evaluating synthetic reviews of fields with China-based research depth
  • checking method and evidence bar for NSR submissions
  • reviewing house style and structure for NSR

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
