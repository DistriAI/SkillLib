modern-economic-research

separate

Use when targeting 《现代经济探讨》(Modern Economic Research — 江苏省社会科学院主管主办的综合性经济理论月刊, 1982 年创刊, 匿名审稿、不收版面费) or deciding whether a Chinese econ/management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, fee-free/anonymous-review policy, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《现代经济探讨》
  • deciding if a Chinese econ/management manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a generic Chinese econ/management manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks and alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to other journals like 《管理科学》 or 《管理世界》
