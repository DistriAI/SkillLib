webconf-experiments

separate

Use when designing or auditing the empirical section of a Web Conference (WWW) paper — matching evidence to the claim's scale, choosing datasets with provenance and freshness, blocking temporal and popularity leakage, running honest baselines from the sibling circuit, and deciding when live-platform or user-study evidence is required.

When to use

  • designing empirical sections for WWW papers
  • auditing claim-evidence match
  • selecting datasets with provenance and freshness
  • blocking temporal and popularity leakage
  • running honest baselines from sibling circuits
  • deciding when live-platform or user-study evidence is required
  • preempting predictable reviewer objections

When not to use

  • writing non-empirical sections of WWW papers
  • designing experiments for non-Web Conference venues
