respol-contribution-framing

separate

Use when the one-sentence "so what" is the bottleneck for a Research Policy (RP) manuscript — converting solid results into an explicit advance for innovation theory/evidence that usually carries a policy or managerial implication. Frames the claim; it does not build the mechanism (respol-theory-development) or polish prose (respol-writing-style).

When to use

  • Converting solid results into an explicit advance for innovation theory
  • Framing the contribution claim
  • Stating policy or managerial implications
  • Aligning abstract, intro, and discussion around the claim
  • Calibrating claim strength to evidence

When not to use

  • Building the mechanism
  • Polishing prose
  • Creating tables or figures
