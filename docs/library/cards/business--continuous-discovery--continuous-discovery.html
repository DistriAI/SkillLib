continuous-discovery

combined

Help users build a sustainable habit of regular customer interaction to ensure product development is driven by real-world needs rather than internal assumptions. Build a weekly cadence of customer touchpoints using Opportunity Solution Trees, assumption mapping, and interview snapshots. Use when the user mentions "continuous discovery", "opportunity solution tree", "weekly interviews", "assumption testing", "discovery habits", "product trio", "outcome-based roadmap", "how do I talk to customers regularly", "we keep building things nobody uses", or "connect research to the roadmap". Also trigger when setting up regular customer feedback loops, prioritizing which experiments to run, or tying discovery insights to delivery work. Covers experience mapping, co-creation, and prioritizing opportunities. For interview technique, see mom-test. For team structure, see inspired-product.

When to use

  • Audit current proximity
  • Define discovery rituals
  • Structure the opportunity space
  • Accelerate evidence gathering
  • Distinguish needs from solutions
  • Remove layers between builders and users
  • Integrate discovery into execution
  • Prioritize high-signal informal contact

Skill page

Conflict

The two skills cover the same domain but use different primary mechanisms. The wondelai skill is a structured, diagnostic framework centered on Opportunity Solution Trees, experience mapping, and a 10-point scoring system. The RefoundAI skill is a qualitative, principle-based guide derived from podcast insights, focusing on removing layers between builders and users, forward-deployed engineering, and informal high-signal contact. Neither is a reproduction of the other; they offer complementary but distinct approaches to establishing a discovery cadence.
