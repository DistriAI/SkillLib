jru-robustness

separate

Use when a Journal of Risk and Uncertainty (JRU) result may be sensitive to specification, incentive frame, sample, or inference. Organizes robustness by the threat to the risk/uncertainty parameter; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • Functional-form dependence of risk parameters
  • Utility-weighting confounds
  • Elicitation-device artifacts
  • Random-incentive or isolation failures
  • Stake or hypothetical-bias sensitivity
  • Subject heterogeneity masked by pooling
  • Multiple comparisons across menus or treatments
  • Optimistic inference or clustering issues

When not to use

  • Inventing evidence or citations
  • Specification search without pre-commitment
  • Reporting only the functional form with the cleanest number
  • Treating EU-only robustness as sufficient for non-EU claims
  • Pooling heterogeneous subjects without addressing heterogeneity
  • Reporting significant results from many menus without correction
