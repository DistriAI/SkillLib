ase-workflow

separate

Use when planning an ASE (IEEE/ACM Automated Software Engineering) research-track campaign backward from the deadline, through abstract registration, the double-anonymous submission, the early-rejection gate, rebuttal, the criteria-bound revision round, artifact evaluation, and the camera-ready in both IEEE Xplore and the ACM Digital Library.

When to use

  • planning ASE research-track campaign
  • managing abstract registration
  • preparing double-anonymous submission
  • handling early-rejection gate
  • managing rebuttal and revision rounds
  • preparing artifact evaluation
  • finalizing camera-ready for IEEE/ACM

When not to use

  • NIER track planning
  • Tools and Datasets track planning
  • Journal-First track planning
  • Industry Showcase planning
  • Doctoral Symposium planning
  • Workshops planning
