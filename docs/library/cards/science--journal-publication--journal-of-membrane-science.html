journal-of-membrane-science

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Membrane Science or deciding whether a membranes / membrane-process manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the transport-and-separation-mechanism bar, membrane characterization rigor, house style, the mechanism-vs-application routing, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • membrane-material, transport, separation, or fouling manuscript fit check
  • re-framing from black-box separation to structure-transport-mechanism story
  • deciding between Journal of Membrane Science and applied process or fundamentals venues
  • checking transport-mechanism and characterization rigor bar
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • membrane materials and formation
  • transport mechanisms and separation performance
  • fouling, wetting, aging, and long-term stability

When not to use

  • studies that use a commercial membrane as a black box to treat a feed
  • reporting a permeance number without structure-transport understanding
  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
