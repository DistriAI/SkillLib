jwe-transmission-channel

separate

Use when a 《世界经济》 (The Journal of World Economy) manuscript stops at "it promoted exports" without showing how an external shock propagates — making the trade / investment / financial / expectation channel explicit and testable, not a hand-wavy mechanism paragraph.

When to use

  • making trade channel explicit and testable
  • making investment channel explicit and testable
  • making financial channel explicit and testable
  • making expectation channel explicit and testable
  • identifying cross-border transmission mechanisms
  • providing testable proxies for open economy channels
  • excluding competing transmission channels
  • connecting channel evidence to general equilibrium or welfare

When not to use

  • domestic-only transmission mechanisms
  • hand-wavy mechanism paragraphs without testable proxies
  • using only Baron-Kenny mediation without identification
  • using correlations as mechanism evidence
