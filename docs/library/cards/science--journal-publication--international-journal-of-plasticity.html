international-journal-of-plasticity

separate

Use when targeting the International Journal of Plasticity (IJP) or deciding whether a plastic-deformation modeling manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the constitutive-model-plus-experimental-validation bar, the plasticity-centric scope, the IJP-vs-JMPS routing, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • plasticity, crystal-plasticity, or inelastic-behavior manuscript fit check
  • re-framing deformation observation into constitutive-model contribution
  • choosing between IJP and journal-of-the-mechanics-and-physics-of-solids
  • IJP constitutive-model rigor bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • phenomenological constitutive models of plasticity/viscoplasticity
  • crystal plasticity models and texture evolution
  • finite-deformation and large-strain plasticity
  • coupled plasticity with damage, fracture, or phase transformation

When not to use

  • deformation observation with no constitutive contribution
  • applying an off-the-shelf plasticity model with no modeling advance
  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
