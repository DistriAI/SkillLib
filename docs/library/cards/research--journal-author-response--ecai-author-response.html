ecai-author-response

separate

Use when writing the ECAI author response (rebuttal) — the single, short, double-blind reply during the response window (IJCAI-ECAI 2026: 7-10 April 2026) that must correct factual misreadings and supply asked-for evidence within a strict length limit, knowing there is no second round and the area chair decides.

When to use

  • Correcting factual misreadings
  • Supplying specific numbers or clarifications requested by reviewers
  • Pointing to exact lines, lemmas, or figures answering objections
  • Clarifying scope misjudged by reviewers
  • Writing a single, short, double-blind reply
  • Prioritizing reject-driving points

When not to use

  • Adding new experiments or theorems
  • Re-running the study
  • Promising a revised version
  • Arguing taste or significance against reviewer judgment
  • Revealing author identity
  • Debating the area chair's future decision
  • Introducing new claims reviewers cannot check
