career-inventory

authentic

Interview someone about their working life and turn the answers into a master CV inventory: every role, project and achievement with real numbers, before any CV is tailored. Use when asked to build my master CV, get my experience down properly, I don't know what to put on my CV, or as the first step before a tailored CV. Produces a structured inventory of roles, achievements in a fixed shape with evidence and numbers, skills with proof, and a list of gaps to fill. Never invents a number.

When to use

  • Build my master CV
  • Get my experience down properly
  • I don't know what to put on my CV
  • First step before a tailored CV
  • Interview me about my experience
  • Build my master CV from my old one and LinkedIn
  • What have I actually achieved?

When not to use

  • Tailoring a CV for a specific job
  • Inventing or rounding up numbers
  • Recording duties instead of achievements
  • Interviewing all roles at once
