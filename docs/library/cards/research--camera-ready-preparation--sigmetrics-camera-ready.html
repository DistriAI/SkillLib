sigmetrics-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing an accepted ACM SIGMETRICS paper for its POMACS camera-ready, covering de-anonymization, the acmsmall template and POMACS journal metadata (DOI, ORCID, CCS concepts), incorporating the shepherd's required changes without scope creep, permanentizing artifact links, and the ACM artifact-badge handoff.

When to use

  • preparing accepted ACM SIGMETRICS paper for POMACS camera-ready
  • de-anonymizing paper
  • applying acmsmall template
  • completing POMACS journal metadata
  • incorporating shepherd's required changes
  • permanentizing artifact links
  • ACM artifact-badge handoff

When not to use

  • strengthening claims beyond what was evaluated
  • expanding the bound the reviewers accepted
