ajps-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning an American Journal of Political Science (AJPS) manuscript against the literature. AJPS is double-blind, so positioning must engage the relevant debates while keeping the manuscript fully anonymized (third-person self-citation, no first-person "we showed"). Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction
  • Revising the contribution paragraph
  • Distinguishing contribution from closest prior papers
  • Checking self-citations for anonymization
  • Engaging live empirical or theoretical debates
  • Locating marginal contribution precisely
  • Pre-empting obvious rival explanations
  • Speaking to a broad political-science readership

When not to use

  • Writing the full literature review
  • Polishing the manuscript without checking venue fit
  • Claiming 'first to study' for incremental contributions
