jimf-identification

separate

Use when the identification argument is the bottleneck for a Journal of International Money and Finance (JIMF) manuscript — open-economy causal designs, high-frequency policy/FX surprises, capital-control natural experiments, or parameter identification in an open-economy model. Stress-tests the strategy to JIMF's international-finance bar.

When to use

  • open-economy causal designs
  • high-frequency policy/FX surprises
  • capital-control natural experiments
  • parameter identification in an open-economy model
  • cross-country panel causal design
  • staggered policy adoption
  • FX-intervention natural experiment
  • structural parameter identification

When not to use

  • tasks where identification is not the bottleneck
