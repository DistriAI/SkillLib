jle-theory-model

separate

Use when a The Journal of Law and Economics (JLE) manuscript needs a model of the legal rule — deterrence, liability, contracting, or regulation — to interpret estimates, generate testable predictions, or carry a theory contribution in the Chicago price-theory tradition. Calibrates how much theory belongs and where; it does not design the identification (jle-identification).

When to use

  • modeling legal rules for deterrence, liability, contracting, or regulation
  • interpreting reduced-form estimates with economic mechanisms
  • generating testable predictions or comparative statics
  • delivering welfare or optimal-penalty results
  • mapping coefficients to structural objects
  • carrying standalone theory contributions about legal institutions

When not to use

  • designing identification strategies
  • adding notation without testable predictions
  • producing comparative statics after seeing results
