ieee-transactions-on-information-theory

separate

Use when targeting IEEE Transactions on Information Theory or deciding whether an information-theory manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the theorem-driven achievability-and-converse bar, proof rigor, the paper-vs-correspondence framing, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Shannon-theory, coding-theory, or information-theoretic-limits manuscript fit check
  • Re-framing coding or communication results into fundamental-limit statements
  • Choosing between IEEE Transactions on Information Theory and applied-communications venues
  • Choosing between a full Paper and a shorter Correspondence
  • Checking achievability-plus-converse rigor bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • Evaluating information-theoretic security and privacy limits
  • Evaluating information-theoretic methods in statistics and learning
  • Evaluating quantum and combinatorial information theory

When not to use

  • Applied communication-system papers that design or simulate a scheme without establishing an information-theoretic result
  • Replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
