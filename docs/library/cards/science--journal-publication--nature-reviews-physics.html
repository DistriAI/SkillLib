nature-reviews-physics

separate

Use when targeting Nature Reviews Physics (Nat Rev Phys) or deciding whether a review proposal across any area of physics fits this commissioned, authoritative review venue that publishes no primary research. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • review, perspective, or roadmap for Nature Reviews Physics
  • authoritative synthesis of a field rather than report new findings
  • choosing between Nature Reviews Physics, Reviews of Modern Physics, and Reports on Progress in Physics
  • commissioning model, breadth-of-audience expectations, and presubmission-enquiry process
  • reviews of an active physics subfield aimed at a cross-disciplinary physics readership
  • perspectives that frame an emerging direction, debate, or paradigm shift
  • roadmaps and technical reviews assessing the state and trajectory of a measurement capability
  • conceptual syntheses that connect developments across traditionally separate physics communities

When not to use

  • primary research
  • reporting new findings
  • literature catalogue
  • unsolicited proposals if not accepted by the journal
