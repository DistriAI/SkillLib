academic-cv

authentic

Build or tailor an academic CV for faculty, postdoc, research or fellowship applications: publications, grants, teaching, supervision and service in the order the field expects, with consistent citations. Use when asked to write my academic CV, update my CV for a lectureship, tenure-track or postdoc application, format my publications list, or adapt my academic CV for an industry research role. Produces the full academic CV, a field-appropriate section order, a clean publications list in one citation style, and a two-page summary version when asked.

When to use

  • write my academic CV
  • update my CV for a lectureship
  • tenure-track or postdoc application
  • format my publications list
  • adapt my academic CV for an industry research role
  • order my CV for a postdoc
  • turn my academic CV into a two-page version

When not to use

  • using the full CV for industry
