aaai-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing an accepted AAAI paper for camera-ready source submission to AAAI Press, including proceedings page limits, two-column template compliance, copyright transfer, purchased extra technical pages, deanonymization, registration, oral or poster presentation, and final public artifact release.

When to use

  • preparing accepted AAAI paper for camera-ready source submission
  • applying AAAI proceedings template and page rules
  • purchasing extra technical pages
  • deanonymization sweep
  • copyright transfer and metadata
  • registration and presentation
  • final public artifact release

When not to use

  • single-column or NeurIPS-style submissions
  • style hacks to gain space
