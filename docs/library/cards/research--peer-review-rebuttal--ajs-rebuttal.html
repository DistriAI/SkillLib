ajs-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to an American Journal of Sociology (AJS) decision — an R&R response letter to multiple double-blind reviewers and the editor, or an author Reply to a published Comment in AJS's Comment-and-Reply tradition. Structures a persuasive, evidence-based response; it does not fabricate results or contact editors.

When to use

  • R&R response letter
  • author Reply to a published Comment
  • deciding whether and how to contest a reviewer point
  • reconciling conflicting reviewers
  • concede-or-contest decision aid
  • executable review pass
  • returning a ledger of claim, evidence, risk, and location
  • sibling guard comparison

When not to use

  • fabricating results
  • contacting editors
  • defensiveness or dismissiveness
  • ignoring a reviewer or commenter point
  • claiming to have made a change without actually making it
  • caving to a reviewer demand that would weaken the paper
  • straw-manning the Comment
  • treating a Comment as an attack to be won
