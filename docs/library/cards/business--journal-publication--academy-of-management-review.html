academy-of-management-review

separate

Use when targeting Academy of Management Review (AMR) or deciding whether a management / organizational research manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Academy of Management Review (AMR)
  • deciding whether a management / organizational research manuscript fits this venue
  • conceptual papers proposing new constructs, propositions, or a theoretical model without empirical testing
  • splitting out theory from empirical papers for AMR
  • identifying AMR desk-reject risks
  • providing alternative journal lists for AMR

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • empirical papers with hypotheses and results submitted as theory
  • literature reviews with no new theoretical contribution
  • broad empirical management papers
