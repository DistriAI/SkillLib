science-immunology

Use when targeting Science Immunology (Sci Immunol) or deciding whether an immunology manuscript fits this AAAS specialist venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Science Immunology
  • deciding if an immunology manuscript fits Science Immunology
  • placing immunology papers among Science Immunology, Nature Immunology, Immunity, and JEM
  • understanding Science Immunology positioning relative to Nature Immunology and Immunity
  • understanding Science Immunology desk-reject criteria
  • getting a credible alternative-venue list
  • assessing fit for mechanistic immunology with translational or human relevance
  • assessing fit for vaccines, infection, autoimmunity, cancer immunology, and developmental immunology

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
