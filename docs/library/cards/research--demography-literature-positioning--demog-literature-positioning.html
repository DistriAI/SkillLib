demog-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Demography (PAA / Duke University Press) manuscript against the population-science literature so it reads as a contribution of general interest to demographers. Demography readers span fertility, mortality, migration, family, and health, so the paper must engage the demographic literatures they expect. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Positioning a Demography manuscript
  • Drafting or revising the introduction
  • Revising the contribution paragraph
  • Connecting applied literature to population science
  • Distinguishing contribution from prior demographic work
  • Engaging cross-component demographic literatures
  • Pre-empting reviewer objections regarding demographic fit

When not to use

  • Writing the full literature review
  • Designing the research methodology
  • Handling submission anonymity rules
  • Building theoretical frameworks
