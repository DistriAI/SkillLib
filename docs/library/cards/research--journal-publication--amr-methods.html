amr-methods

separate

Use when choosing and applying a theory-construction method for an Academy of Management Review (AMR) manuscript — building constructs, defining their domain, specifying relationships, articulating the underlying logic/mechanisms, and setting boundary conditions. This is the THEORY-CONSTRUCTION craft, NOT empirical method; AMR publishes no datasets, no measures, and no statistical tests.

When to use

  • building constructs
  • defining construct domains
  • specifying relationships
  • articulating underlying logic and mechanisms
  • setting boundary conditions
  • choosing theory-building styles
  • construct re-specification
  • cross-level theorizing

When not to use

  • empirical method
  • datasets
  • measures
  • statistical tests
  • data collection
  • samples
  • estimation
  • results
