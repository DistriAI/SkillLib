ecopol-robustness

separate

Use when an Economic Policy (EP) manuscript's results may be specification-, sample-, or inference-fragile, especially ahead of the two-discussant panel. Organizes robustness by the threat a discussant will raise; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • Economic Policy manuscript robustness
  • Specification-fragile results
  • Sample-fragile results
  • Inference-fragile results
  • Pre-empting discussant objections
  • Defending policy-relevant magnitude
  • Few-cluster inference correction
  • Pre-trends sensitivity analysis

When not to use

  • Inventing evidence
  • Inventing citations
  • Mechanical appendix of alternative specifications
  • Defending only statistical significance
  • Hiding fragility
