interspeech-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what accompanies an INTERSPEECH paper beyond its 4 pages — audio sample pages and multimedia for listening-based judgment, what reviewers may ignore versus must see, the references-and-acknowledgments page's strict scope, and packaging extra analyses when no reviewed appendix exists.

When to use

  • Deciding what accompanies an INTERSPEECH paper beyond 4 pages
  • Designing audio sample pages and multimedia for listening-based judgment
  • Determining what reviewers may ignore versus must see
  • Managing the strict scope of the references-and-acknowledgments page
  • Packaging extra analyses when no reviewed appendix exists
  • Curating audio samples for synthesis, conversion, enhancement, and separation papers
  • Planning attachment vs link strategies for media files
  • Choosing overflow routes for extended analyses

When not to use

  • Writing the main 4-page paper content
  • Managing Long Paper track contracts (8+2 pages)
  • General journal extension strategies beyond Interspeech-specific paths
