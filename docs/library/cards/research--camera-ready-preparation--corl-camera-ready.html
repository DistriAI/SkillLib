corl-camera-ready

separate

Use when converting an accepted CoRL paper into its final form — the 9-page camera-ready main text with the extra page for review feedback, the final-mode corl LaTeX template, appendix merged into one PDF, de-anonymization, external hosting for videos because PMLR accepts none, and the October deadline into the PMLR volume.

When to use

  • converting accepted CoRL paper to camera-ready
  • expanding main text to 9 pages
  • switching to final-mode corl LaTeX template
  • de-anonymizing author identity and affiliations
  • merging appendix into single PDF
  • hosting videos externally for PMLR
  • verifying final PDF metadata and URLs

When not to use

  • initial paper submission
  • writing new claims not reviewed
  • hosting videos on PMLR
