journal-of-sports-research

separate

Use when targeting 《体育学研究》(Journal of Sports Research, Nanjing Sport Institute) — a CSSCI journal focused on sport social science / sport humanities, valuing disciplined problem framing and substantive theoretical dialogue (sport sociology, governance, policy, history, philosophy). Best for theory-engaged sport social science with rigorous method; route exercise science and applied competitive work to the natural-science / 中国体育科技 venues, and reflective/methodological essays to 《体育与科学》.

When to use

  • sport sociology
  • sport governance and policy
  • sport history and philosophy
  • body and society
  • sport and state/culture
  • theory-engaged sport social science
  • quantitative and qualitative methods with theoretical dialogue

When not to use

  • exercise science
  • applied competitive work
  • reflective/methodological essays
  • policy reporting without theoretical framework
  • pure descriptive facts without theoretical explanation
  • economics/management/sociology empirical studies using sport only as a case
