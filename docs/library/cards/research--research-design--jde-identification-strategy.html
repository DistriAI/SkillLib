jde-identification-strategy

separate

Use when the causal identification strategy is the bottleneck for a Journal of Development Economics (JDE) manuscript — RCT/field experiment, DID, IV, RDD in low- and middle-income settings. Stress-tests the design against development-economics empirical norms before tables are drafted.

When to use

  • RCT/field experiment design
  • DID with staggered timing
  • IV with weak first stage
  • RDD at eligibility threshold
  • Selection-on-observables complement
  • Pre-analysis plan and power analysis
  • Causal identification strategy bottleneck

When not to use

  • Theoretical papers judged on mechanism rigor
  • Drafting tables
