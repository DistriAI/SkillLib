vital-sign-extraction

separate

Estimate heart rate (HR) and breathing rate (BR) in bpm from a cleaned 1-D physiological time series such as radar phase, WiFi CSI amplitude, PPG, or a chest-motion signal. Use when Claude needs to choose HR/BR bandpass edges, pick a peak frequency from a noisy band, reject the HR second harmonic that often dominates the fundamental, handle respiration-harmonic leakage into the HR band on slow breathers, or flag low-confidence estimates. Not for arrhythmia / beat-to-beat analysis, multi-subject source separation, or rapidly non-stationary rates.
