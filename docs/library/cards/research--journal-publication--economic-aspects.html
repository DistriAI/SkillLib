economic-aspects

separate

Use when targeting 《经济纵横》(Economic Review Journal — 吉林省社会科学院/社科联主办的综合性经济理论月刊, 1985 年创刊, 不收版面费/审稿费) or deciding whether a Chinese econ/policy manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, fee-free policy, abstract/keyword house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《经济纵横》
  • deciding if a Chinese econ/policy manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese econ/policy manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks and alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to e-government, east-china-economic-management, economic-perspectives, or economic-problems if their readership is closer
  • submitting industry background or local case studies to narrower industry, fiscal, financial, or regional journals
