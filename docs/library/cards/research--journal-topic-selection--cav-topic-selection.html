cav-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a formal-methods project belongs at CAV (Computer Aided Verification) or should be routed to TACAS, FMCAD, VMCAI, LPAR/IJCAR, POPL/PLDI/OOPSLA, or a journal (FMSD/JAR/TOCL), and when choosing the right CAV category — Regular, Short Tool, Short Application, or Industrial Experience & Case Study.

When to use

  • deciding venue for formal-methods projects
  • routing to TACAS, FMCAD, VMCAI, or PL venues
  • choosing CAV category (Regular, Tool, Application, Industrial)
  • evaluating contribution shapes for CAV
  • applying guarantee and re-label tests
  • reconnaissance of CAV programs and benchmarks

When not to use

  • drafting the paper
  • writing verification code
  • performing model checking
  • submitting to journals
