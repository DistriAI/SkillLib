jedpsych-workflow

separate

Use when starting or navigating any Journal of Educational Psychology manuscript and you are unsure which sub-skill applies. Use when choosing among manuscript types (primary empirical study, multi-study package, exceptionally important meta-analysis) or returning with a decision letter. Routes by lifecycle stage and flags the journal's masked review, 12,000-word format, APA 7th style, and Transparency and Openness requirements. It dispatches; it does not draft content.
