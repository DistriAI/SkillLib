nbr-qualitative-case

separate

Use for qualitative theory-building submitted to 《南开管理评论》 (Nankai Business Review) — multi-case comparison (replication logic, case selection), grounded-theory coding (open / axial / selective), theoretical saturation, and trustworthiness (data triangulation, member checking, audit trail). Use when the contribution is built inductively from cases or interviews rather than from survey/experiment data.
