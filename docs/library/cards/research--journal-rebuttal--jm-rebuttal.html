jm-rebuttal

separate

Use when planning a revision and drafting the point-by-point response letter for a Journal of Marketing (JM) R&R — prioritizing the Coeditor's decisive concerns, strengthening substantive novelty and managerial relevance, and keeping the double-anonymized rules. Plans and drafts the response; it does not interpret the original decision (jm-review-process).

When to use

  • Planning a revision for a JM R&R
  • Drafting the point-by-point response letter
  • Addressing substantive novelty challenges
  • Addressing managerial relevance challenges
  • Addressing identification weaknesses
  • Preparing transparency deliverables
  • Showing movement in subsequent rounds

When not to use

  • Interpreting the original decision
  • Drafting the letter on an unrevised paper
