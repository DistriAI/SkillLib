wacv-supplementary

separate

Use when organizing WACV supplementary material, covering what belongs in the anonymized supplement versus the 8-page body, packaging qualitative videos and extra results without breaking double-blind, keeping the supplement consistent with the paper across the two-round revision, and honoring WACV's no-author-identifying-links rule.

When to use

  • organizing WACV supplementary material
  • deciding what belongs in the anonymized supplement versus the 8-page body
  • packaging qualitative videos and extra results without breaking double-blind
  • keeping the supplement consistent with the paper across the two-round revision
  • honoring WACV's no-author-identifying-links rule
  • anonymity sweep for videos, filenames, metadata, and links
  • indexing supplement items to claims
  • verifying consistency across revision rounds

When not to use

  • writing the 8-page body argument
  • conducting experiments
  • generating new qualitative results
  • managing submission deadlines
  • checking file size caps
