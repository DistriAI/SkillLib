jole-rebuttal

separate

Use when drafting a response letter to a Journal of Labor Economics (JOLE) revise-and-resubmit — addressing single-blind labor referees point by point under the journal's word economy. Strategy and structure; it does not run new estimations for you.

When to use

  • Drafting a response letter to a JOLE revise-and-resubmit
  • Addressing single-blind labor referees point by point
  • Converting new results into a referee-by-referee response
  • Reconciling demands from disagreeing referees
  • Deciding how much to concede versus defend on labor-identification critiques
  • Structuring a cover note to the editor
  • Formatting point-by-point replies with manuscript locations
  • Managing word economy for the revised manuscript

When not to use

  • Running new estimations
  • Re-anonymizing the manuscript
  • Adding exhibits that exceed the word cap
  • Conceding identification flaws with words instead of evidence
  • Ignoring one referee because another disagrees
  • Silently dropping a result a referee questioned
