jleo-theory-model

separate

Use when building or sharpening the formal model for a Journal of Law, Economics, and Organization (JLEO) manuscript — transaction-cost, incomplete-contracts / property-rights, principal-agent, or political-agency models of institutions and organizations. Right-sizes and disciplines the model; it does not run the empirics (jleo-identification, jleo-robustness).
