epsl-revision-and-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to an Earth and Planetary Science Letters (EPSL) revision decision. EPSL decisions are made by a subject editor on the advice of referees who often span two sub-fields, so the response must satisfy each specialist with evidence while keeping the Letter concise. It structures the revision and response letter; it does not fabricate new results.

When to use

  • responding to an EPSL revision decision
  • planning the response to a major/minor revision
  • handling referees from different sub-fields
  • managing requested additions that exceed the word cap
  • drafting the summary-of-changes note to the editor
  • structuring the revision and response letter

When not to use

  • fabricating new results
