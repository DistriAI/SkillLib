jqte-implications

separate

Use when writing the policy / practice implications of a 《数量经济技术经济研究》 (JQTE) manuscript — translating measurement, forecasting, or decomposition results into concrete guidance for planning, forecasting, industrial, or technology decisions. JQTE expects a substantive policy section (≥ 1000 characters, except pure-method papers) grounded in the paper's own quantitative findings.
