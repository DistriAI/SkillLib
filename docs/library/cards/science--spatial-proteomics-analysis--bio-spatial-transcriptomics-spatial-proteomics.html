When to use

  • Process multiplexed imaging data
  • Cell segmentation
  • Protein phenotyping
  • Spatial neighborhood analysis
  • Protein colocalization scoring
  • Analyzing protein spatial patterns
  • Integrating spatial proteomics with transcriptomics
