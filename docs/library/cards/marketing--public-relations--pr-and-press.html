pr-and-press

separate

Help users design and execute a press strategy that moves beyond vanity metrics to achieve specific business goals like funding, user acquisition, or B2B credibility.

When to use

  • designing a press strategy
  • achieving business goals like funding or user acquisition
  • building B2B credibility
  • refining product narratives
  • targeting specific journalists
  • developing outreach plans
  • using press for third-party validation

When not to use

  • using press as a vanity metric
  • mass pitching or generic PR blasts
  • using press as a direct lead generation channel
