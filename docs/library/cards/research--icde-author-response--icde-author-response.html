icde-author-response

separate

Use when responding to IEEE ICDE reviews, whether drafting a rebuttal to reviewer concerns or preparing a revise-and-resubmit change document for an invited ICDE revision under the 4-week window, coordinating with area chairs, addressing systems-reviewer pushback on baselines and cost, and deciding what is feasible before the revision deadline.

When to use

  • drafting a rebuttal to reviewer concerns
  • preparing a revise-and-resubmit change document
  • coordinating with area chairs
  • addressing systems-reviewer pushback on baselines and cost
  • deciding what is feasible before the revision deadline
