tar-review-process

separate

Use when understanding how The Accounting Review (TAR) review and decisions work, or when reading a TAR decision letter — the editor routing, double-blind two-reviewer model, decision types, and post-publication integrity rules. Explains and interprets the process; it does not draft the revision and response (tar-rebuttal).

When to use

  • Understanding TAR review and decisions
  • Reading a TAR decision letter
  • Understanding editor routing
  • Understanding the double-blind two-reviewer model
  • Understanding decision types
  • Understanding post-publication integrity rules
  • Setting realistic expectations before or after submitting to TAR
  • Interpreting the signal of a decision letter

When not to use

  • Drafting the revision and response
