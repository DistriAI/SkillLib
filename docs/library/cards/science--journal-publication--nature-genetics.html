nature-genetics

separate

Use when targeting Nature Genetics or deciding whether a genetics/genomics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Nature Genetics
  • deciding if a genetics/genomics manuscript fits the venue
  • assessing GWAS or multi-ancestry genomics scale and interpretation
  • assessing functional genomics significance
  • choosing between Nature Genetics, Nature Human Genetics, AJHG, or disease-specific journals
  • evaluating population genetics and complex trait genomics fit
  • evaluating rare-variant discovery or somatic genetics fit
  • evaluating single-cell genomics applied to genetic questions

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
