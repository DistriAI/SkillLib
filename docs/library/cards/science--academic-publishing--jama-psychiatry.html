jama-psychiatry

separate

Use when targeting JAMA Psychiatry or deciding whether a clinical-psychiatry or mental-health study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the psychiatric-trial and observational/neuropsychiatric evidence bar, reporting-guideline and trial-registration requirements, JAMA Network house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • JAMA Psychiatry fit check
  • Psychiatric trial framing
  • Mental-health study venue selection
  • Neuropsychiatric study evaluation
  • Reporting guideline requirements
  • Desk-reject heuristics
  • JAMA Network house style

When not to use

  • Clinical advice
  • Regulatory advice
  • Replacing current journal instructions
