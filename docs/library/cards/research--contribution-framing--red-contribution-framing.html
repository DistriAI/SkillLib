red-contribution-framing

separate

Use when framing the marginal contribution of a Review of Economic Dynamics (RED) manuscript — stating the quantitative or dynamic advance in terms the SED readership values (a new mechanism, a new method, a new quantitative result, or model-disciplining evidence) and aligning the abstract and introduction around it.

When to use

  • Distilling 'what is new' before drafting the abstract and introduction
  • Framing papers with several results but no clear single advance
  • Translating a technical contribution for the SED audience
  • Framing new mechanisms, methods, quantitative results, or model-disciplining evidence
  • Running a contribution stress test
  • Drafting a contribution statement block
  • Addressing referee pushback on contribution claims

When not to use

  • Literature positioning
  • Identification strategy
