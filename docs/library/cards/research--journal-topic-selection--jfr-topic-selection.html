jfr-topic-selection

separate

Use to sharpen the research question and contribution for a 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) manuscript — turning a finance data finding into a question with a clear financial-mechanism contribution and concrete relevance to China's financial system.

When to use

  • Sharpening research questions for Journal of Financial Research
  • Turning finance data findings into financial mechanism contributions
  • Evaluating topic suitability for Journal of Financial Research
  • Anchoring financial mechanisms and China institutional hooks
  • Defining contribution increments relative to existing literature
  • Refining contribution statements to avoid vagueness
  • Assessing topic strength using a three-step framework

When not to use

  • Macro topics like financial development promoting economic growth
  • Using new data or indicators as a substitute for new problems
  • Topics with focus on industry or accounting rather than finance
  • Correlation stories without identification strategies
  • Topics lacking a clear financial channel or institutional hook
