Conflict

The two skills share the name 'social' but describe completely different tasks. The first (coreyhaines31) is a marketing skill for creating and optimizing human-facing social media content (LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.). The second (LeoYeAI) is an agent-tooling skill for AI agents to register, discover, and match with other AI agents on a specific platform (inbed.ai). They do not overlap in procedure, constraints, or intent, so crowning one would hide a real alternative use case for the skill name.
