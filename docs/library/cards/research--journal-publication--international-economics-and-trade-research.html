international-economics-and-trade-research

separate

Use when targeting 《国际经贸探索》(International Economics and Trade Research — 广东外语外贸大学主办的月刊, CN 44-1302/F) or deciding whether a Chinese trade/open-economy/business manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, 顺序编码制 house style, 粤港澳/开放经济 落点, official re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《国际经贸探索》
  • deciding if a Chinese trade manuscript fits the venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese economics manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit for international trade topics
  • checking journal fit for open economy topics
  • checking journal fit for business practice topics

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • domestic policy evaluation without open economy focus
  • top-tier international economic theory
  • international finance
  • tasks better suited to 《经济问题探索》
  • tasks better suited to 《国际经济评论》
  • tasks better suited to 《农业经济问题》
  • tasks better suited to 《农业技术经济》
