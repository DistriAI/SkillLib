Conflict

The two skills share the name 'metadata' but address entirely different domains. The first is a marketing/SEO skill for auditing and implementing HTML page metadata (titles, descriptions, canonical, hreflang, Open Graph). The second is a research skill that acts as a router for 24 bibliometric and scholarly metadata tools (CrossRef, ORCID, Zotero, etc.). They do not overlap in procedure, inputs, or outputs, and neither is a reproduction of the other.
