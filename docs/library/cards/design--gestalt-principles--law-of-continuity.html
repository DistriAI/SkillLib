law-of-continuity

separate

Apply the Law of Continuity — the eye follows alignment and unbroken paths. Use when sequencing steps, aligning content, or designing carousels and timelines. For grouping rather than sequencing, use `law-of-proximity`.

When to use

  • sequencing steps
  • aligning content
  • designing carousels
  • designing timelines
  • establishing visual flow
  • using directional indicators
  • separating groups with interrupted continuity

When not to use

  • grouping rather than sequencing
