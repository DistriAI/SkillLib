co2-tank-monitor

separate

IoT monitoring simulation to predict CO2 tank depletion and prevent weekend gas outages in cell culture facilities. Monitors cylinder pressure, calculates consumption rates, provides early warnings, and supports automated scheduling via cron.

When to use

  • Predict CO2 cylinder depletion
  • Detect weekend gas outage risk
  • Monitor CO2 cylinder pressure
  • Calculate remaining cylinder life
  • Handle 10L and 40L cylinder sizes
  • Provide automated alerts and recommendations
  • Simulate monitoring scenarios for training
  • Automated scheduling via cron

When not to use

  • Monitor other gases
  • Control incubator temperature
  • Manage lab inventory
