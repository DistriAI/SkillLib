artificial-intelligence-and-statistics

separate

Use when targeting International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics (AISTATS) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for AI statistics.

When to use

  • targeting AISTATS
  • deciding if a manuscript fits AISTATS
  • conference-fit read for statistical machine learning
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style into CS conference narrative
  • diagnosing evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, or rebuttal issues
  • venue-selection strategy for AI statistics
  • comparing AISTATS with AAAI, IJCAI, UAI, or COLT

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • manuscripts where central contribution is not genuinely in AI statistics
  • papers where AISTATS is merely a convenient deadline
