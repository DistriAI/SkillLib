icdt-writing-style

separate

Use when shaping the prose of an ICDT (International Conference on Database Theory) paper — the theorem-proof structure, stating the data model and computation model precisely, leading with the result and its data-management consequence, proof-sketch-then-appendix discipline within the lipics-v2021 15-page budget, and the notation conventions a database-theory referee expects.

When to use

  • shaping ICDT paper prose
  • theorem-proof structure
  • stating data and computation models
  • leading with results and consequences
  • managing 15-page budget
  • notation conventions
  • proof-sketch-then-appendix discipline

When not to use

  • padding body with restated related work
  • shrinking font or margins
  • model-last writing
  • one-sided bounds sold as tight
  • proof-by-citation
  • survey-as-introduction
  • undefined asymptotics
