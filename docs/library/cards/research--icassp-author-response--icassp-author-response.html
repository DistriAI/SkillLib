icassp-author-response

separate

Use when drafting an ICASSP rebuttal or author response to reviews, covering the recently added and short author-response window, the single-blind setting where reviewers already know you, answering signal-processing reviewers from the four-page record without a revised PDF, and writing for the technical-committee decision rather than for tone.

When to use

  • drafting an ICASSP rebuttal
  • author response to reviews
  • answering signal-processing reviewers
  • triaging factual errors vs legitimate gaps
  • addressing baseline and metric pushback
  • writing for technical-committee decision

When not to use

  • uploading a revised PDF
  • adding new experiments
  • re-establishing anonymity
  • pasting new derivations
  • adding fresh result tables
