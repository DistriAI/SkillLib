fcr-revision-and-rebuttal

separate

Use when writing the response to a Field Crops Research (FCR) revision decision (major or minor) and revising the manuscript. FCR reviewers commonly press on scope/generality, statistics and G×E, reproducibility detail, and a discussion that interprets rather than repeats. The response must address every point while protecting the contribution. Structures the response letter; it does not fabricate new results.

When to use

  • writing response to FCR revision decision
  • revising manuscript for FCR
  • reconciling conflicting reviewer requests
  • writing cover note to editor
  • addressing scope and generality concerns
  • addressing statistics and GxE concerns
  • addressing reproducibility detail
  • interpreting discussion rather than repeating results

When not to use

  • fabricating new results
