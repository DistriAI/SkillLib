nbr-submission

separate

Use for the pre-submission preflight to 《南开管理评论》 (Nankai Business Review) — checking abstract/keyword limits, manuscript length, bilingual title/abstract, reference style (GB/T 7714 plus the journal's 来稿规范说明), the online submission system at nbr.nankai.edu.cn, anonymity, and document hygiene. Use right before submitting; verify every rule against the journal's current 来稿规范说明.
