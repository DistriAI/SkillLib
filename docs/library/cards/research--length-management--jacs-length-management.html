jacs-length-management

separate

Use when choosing between an Article and a Communication and meeting ACS format norms for a Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS) manuscript. Decides format and trims to fit — it does not add new results.

When to use

  • deciding Article vs Communication
  • manuscript over- or under-length for chosen format
  • determining what moves to SI to fit format norms
  • disciplining high figure/scheme count
  • right-sizing main text
  • trimming tactics without losing rigor
  • verifying current JACS format/limit norms
  • returning a claim-evidence-risk ledger

When not to use

  • adding new results
  • hard-coding outdated word/figure limits
  • padding an Article with optimization narration
  • figure sprawl with near-duplicate schemes
  • trimming so hard that a key claim loses supporting evidence
  • choosing Communication for a story needing Article-level support
