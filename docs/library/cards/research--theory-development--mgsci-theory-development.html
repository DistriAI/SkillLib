mgsci-theory-development

separate

Use when building the theoretical core of a Management Science (INFORMS) paper — either a formal analytical model (assumptions, equilibrium, propositions/theorems, comparative statics) or empirically testable hypotheses derived from a clear mechanism. Adapts to the paper's lane; it does not run the analysis (mgsci-data-analysis) or frame the contribution (mgsci-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • building the theoretical core of a Management Science paper
  • developing formal analytical models
  • deriving empirically testable hypotheses from mechanisms
  • stating and defending model assumptions
  • defining decision problems and equilibrium concepts
  • deriving propositions and theorems with proofs
  • performing comparative statics and robustness checks
  • naming mechanisms for empirical predictions

When not to use

  • running the analysis
  • framing the contribution
