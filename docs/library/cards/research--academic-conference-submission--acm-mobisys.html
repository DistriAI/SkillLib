acm-mobisys

separate

Use when targeting ACM MobiSys (MobiSys) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for mobile systems.

When to use

  • targeting ACM MobiSys
  • deciding manuscript fit for MobiSys
  • conference-fit read for mobile systems
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style to conference narrative
  • evidence-gap diagnosis
  • anonymity diagnosis
  • artifact diagnosis
  • rebuttal posture

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • wireless-protocol contributions better suited to MobiCom
  • mobile networking
  • ubiquitous interaction
  • networked systems
