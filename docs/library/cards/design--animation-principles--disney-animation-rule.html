disney-animation-rule

separate

Apply Disney's 12 principles as practical design and engineering rules for procedural animation. Use when creating, improving, reviewing, or debugging code-driven motion in web, SVG, canvas, React, Remotion, game, UI, character, camera, or 3D scenes; especially when motion feels stiff, weightless, mechanical, unclear, or physically correct but visually weak.

When to use

  • creating code-driven motion
  • improving code-driven motion
  • reviewing code-driven motion
  • debugging code-driven motion
  • web, SVG, canvas, React, Remotion, game, UI, character, camera, or 3D scenes
  • fixing stiff, weightless, mechanical, unclear, or physically correct but visually weak motion

When not to use

  • forcing all 12 principles into every shot
