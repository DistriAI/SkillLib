soctheory-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight for a Sociological Theory (ST) manuscript — Manuscript Central portal, ASA format and length cap, abstract, masking for double-anonymous review, ASA-style references, fee, ethics/originality, and required files. Theory-only journal; there is no data-availability or replication step.

When to use

  • final pre-submission preflight
  • Manuscript Central portal check
  • ASA format and length cap verification
  • abstract and masking for double-anonymous review
  • ASA-style references check
  • processing fee and ethics verification
  • required files for theory paper

When not to use

  • data-availability or replication step
  • empirical study submission
  • literature review submission
  • application that does not change theory
  • conceptual relabeling presented as new theory
  • freestanding speculation without engaged tradition
