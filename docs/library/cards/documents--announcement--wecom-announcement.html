wecom-announcement

authentic

Write an internal announcement for WeCom (企业微信) or a company group chat: policy changes, office notices, system outages, holiday arrangements and organisational updates, in the clear, polite register Chinese staff expect. Use when asked 写一则企业微信公告, 帮我写内部通知, 放假通知, 系统维护通知, 组织架构调整公告, or write a staff announcement in Chinese. Produces the announcement with a 标题, the one-line 要点, details by audience, actions and deadlines, a contact, plus a short version for group chats and a pinned-message version.

When to use

  • policy changes
  • office notices
  • system outages
  • holiday arrangements
  • organisational updates
  • staff announcements in Chinese
  • WeCom announcements
  • group chat notices

When not to use

  • notices to customers
