iclr-author-response

separate

Use when drafting ICLR OpenReview author discussion replies, revision notes, public comments, restricted comments, and responses to reviewer or AC concerns during the discussion period. Use when reviews land and you must decide what to answer publicly versus privately, how to log a discussion-period revision, or how to move a borderline score before the AC writes the meta-review for the permanent record.

When to use

  • drafting ICLR OpenReview author discussion replies
  • writing revision notes
  • creating public comments
  • creating restricted comments
  • responding to reviewer concerns
  • responding to AC concerns
  • logging a discussion-period revision
  • moving a borderline score

When not to use

  • arguing publicly about abusive reviews
  • revealing lab or institution identity
  • re-arguing minor points when score is unmovable
