uist-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a project belongs at UIST — testing it against the interface-systems contribution bar (novel technique, toolkit, or hardware that works), making the CHI-vs-UIST routing call explicitly, and re-routing to CSCW, IMWUT, TEI, ISMAR, IUI, or TOCHI when the artifact is not the contribution.

When to use

  • deciding whether a project belongs at UIST
  • testing against the interface-systems contribution bar
  • making the CHI-vs-UIST routing call
  • re-routing to CSCW, IMWUT, TEI, ISMAR, IUI, or TOCHI
  • applying the artifact-subtraction test
  • scoring the fit ledger
  • evaluating enabling novelty and technical depth
  • timing the routing decision

When not to use

  • projects where the contribution is a finding about people
  • projects where the contribution is a model or algorithm evaluated offline
  • deployment and appropriation studies of an interactive system
  • sensing without an interactive application
  • tangibles and fabrication where the argument is design-theoretic
  • AR/VR perception or display optics without an interface contribution
  • intelligent interfaces where the model is the contribution
  • mature systems with longitudinal evaluation that outgrew a 10-page conference paper
