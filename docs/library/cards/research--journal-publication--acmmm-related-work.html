acmmm-related-work

separate

Use when building or auditing the related-work section of an ACM MM (ACM Multimedia) paper — covering the multimedia literature spread across vision, audio/speech, language, HCI/QoE, and systems, handling arXiv-speed concurrency, keeping citations double-blind, and verifying that cited "ACM MM papers" are ACM MM and not ICMR, MMSys, CVPR, or TOMM.

When to use

  • building related-work section
  • auditing related-work section
  • covering multimedia literature
  • handling arXiv concurrency
  • keeping citations double-blind
  • verifying ACM MM citations

When not to use

  • writing single-modality papers
  • citing ICMR as ACM MM
  • citing CVPR as ACM MM
  • citing TOMM as ACM MM
