journal-of-business-and-economic-statistics

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Business and Economic Statistics (JBES) or deciding whether a manuscript at the statistics/econometrics interface fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Business and Economic Statistics
  • deciding if a statistics/econometrics manuscript fits JBES
  • re-framing time-series or microeconometric papers
  • assessing JBES desk-reject risks
  • checking JBES method-and-evidence bar
  • reviewing JBES house style and structure

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • pure econometric theory without application
  • applications with weak statistics
  • general-interest applied results without methodological advance
