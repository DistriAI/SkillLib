nejm-reporting

separate

Use to select and enforce the correct EQUATOR reporting guideline for a clinical study headed to NEJM — CONSORT for RCTs (with the participant flow diagram), STROBE for observational, PRISMA for systematic reviews — and to build the required checklist and diagram before submission.

When to use

  • Selecting reporting checklist for RCTs
  • Selecting reporting checklist for observational studies
  • Selecting reporting checklist for systematic reviews
  • Building CONSORT participant flow diagram
  • Building PRISMA study-selection flow diagram
  • Building STROBE checklist
  • Reconciling flow diagram numbers with Table 1
  • Formatting checklist with page and line references

When not to use

  • Selecting journal for submission
  • Writing clinical study design
  • Polishing manuscript text
  • Defining clinical question or endpoints
