journal-of-management-en

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Management (JOM, SAGE) or deciding whether a broad management / organizational behavior manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Management (JOM, SAGE)
  • deciding if broad management / organizational behavior manuscript fits
  • broad management or OB empirical paper
  • meta-analysis or review needing high-impact generalist home
  • paper near AMJ's bar but slightly less theory-forward
  • rigorous meta-analysis needing right outlet
  • identifying JOM desk-reject risks
  • generating alternative journal list

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • pure theory without data
  • descriptive or atheoretical empirical study
  • single-source cross-sectional survey with unaddressed common-method bias
  • meta-analysis without theoretical framework or bias diagnostics
  • niche specialty paper that does not travel beyond one subfield
