molcell-framing

separate

Use to lock the single-mechanism arc before drafting — converts a set of correct experiments into one Molecular Cell story that runs molecular question → the mechanism → physiological consequence, not a catalog of techniques.

When to use

  • Locking the single-mechanism arc before drafting
  • Converting correct experiments into one Molecular Cell story
  • Mapping figures to the proof of mechanism
  • Structuring the Molecular Cell introduction
  • Identifying 'why now' hooks
  • Refining title discipline
  • Rewriting narrative anti-frames

When not to use

  • Presenting parallel technique mini-stories
  • Burying the mechanism below background
  • Inflating with 'novel/unprecedented' labels
  • Framing a structure paper around the fold instead of the mechanism
