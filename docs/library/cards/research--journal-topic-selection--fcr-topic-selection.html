fcr-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether an agronomy or crop-science project fits Field Crops Research (FCR) and which article type to target. FCR's defining test is field-based agronomic significance with multi-season/-environment relevance — it rejects controlled-environment-only, single-site single-season, horticultural/woody-perennial, and corroborative/descriptive/local-only work. Helps frame the question; it does not collect data.

When to use

  • Choosing among possible projects or framings for an FCR submission
  • Deciding between an Original Research Paper and a Short Communication
  • Considering a Review or Opinion piece
  • Pressure-testing fit before investing in a project
  • Framing agronomic contribution angles
  • Evaluating borderline scope cases

When not to use

  • Collecting data
  • Controlled-environment-only studies
  • Single-site single-season trials
  • Horticultural or woody-perennial species
  • Corroborative, descriptive, or local-only work
  • Medicinal and non-cultivated species
  • Natural grasslands
