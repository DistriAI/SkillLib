osdi-experiments

separate

Use when designing or auditing the evaluation of an OSDI submission — choosing mature baselines and realistic workloads, structuring the section around research questions, measuring scalability and tail behavior, quantifying the design's costs, and fitting the evidence into the 12-page reviewed body.

When to use

  • designing OSDI evaluation
  • auditing OSDI evaluation
  • choosing mature baselines
  • selecting realistic workloads
  • structuring evaluation around research questions
  • measuring scalability and tail behavior
  • quantifying design costs
  • fitting evidence into 12-page body

When not to use

  • camera-ready formatting
  • full reproducibility ledger
  • review process simulation
