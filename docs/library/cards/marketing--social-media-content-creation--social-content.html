Conflict

The three copies overlap on the core social media strategy workflow (context gathering, platform selection, content pillars, and repurposing), but they disagree on the agent's capabilities and contain material steps the others lack. The LeoYeAI/openclaw-marketing-skills copy is the most complete standalone procedure, including a specific check for a product marketing context file and a detailed engagement routine. The LeoYeAI/openclaw-master-skills copy provides significantly more granular platform-specific constraints (best times, character limits, and 'what doesn't work' lists) and assumes direct access to a scheduling platform. The sickn33 copy is a wrapper that delegates to a separate guide and adds a 'critical' risk flag. Because the first copy lacks the scheduling assumption and the second lacks the context file check, crowning one would hide a real alternative procedure.
