jpart-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing a Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory (JPART) manuscript so it reads as theory-plus-rigor, follows OUP author-date style, and fits the ~12,000-word cap that includes the abstract, tables, and references. Tightens prose and format; it does not invent content.

When to use

  • Drafting the introduction
  • Framing the contribution
  • Final polish
  • Cutting content to fit word cap
  • Writing the abstract
  • Aligning citations to OUP author-date style
  • Aligning headings to OUP author-date style
  • Aligning format to OUP author-date style

When not to use

  • Inventing content
