public-administration-and-policy-review

separate

Use when targeting 《公共管理与政策评论》(Public Administration and Policy Review — 中国人民大学主办的公共管理与政策双月刊, 匿名评阅) or deciding whether a Chinese public-administration/policy manuscript fits this journal. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract house style, anonymous-review rules, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《公共管理与政策评论》
  • deciding if a manuscript fits the journal
  • rewriting a manuscript for the journal's narrative
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit and framing
  • verifying official submission requirements

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • manuscripts where government governance is not the main line
  • economic policy effects without public administration focus
  • using second-hand submission website formats
