jpube-topic-selection

separate

Use when assessing whether a research question fits the Journal of Public Economics (JPubE) — the economic role of government (taxation, public expenditure, social insurance, redistribution, externalities, public goods, fiscal policy) — and whether the policy stakes clear the bar for an international readership. Scopes the question; it does not run the analysis.

When to use

  • Assessing if a research question fits JPubE
  • Evaluating the government-role angle of a question
  • Choosing between general-interest and public-finance framing
  • Deciding between JPubE and generalist or specialty journals
  • Scoring manuscript venue fit and novelty
  • Comparing against sibling journals like JDE, JIE, or AEJ Economic Policy

When not to use

  • Running the analysis
  • Providing submission-ready advice before checking official source maps
  • Polishing format when a sibling venue owns the contribution
