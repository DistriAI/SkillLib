jpam-review-process

separate

Use when anticipating how a Journal of Policy Analysis and Management (JPAM) manuscript will be evaluated — desk screening, double-blind peer review, the article types and which are not peer-reviewed, decision categories, and what JPAM referees reward and punish. Sets expectations; it does not write the paper or the rebuttal.

When to use

  • stress-testing against likely referee objections
  • deciding which article type to target
  • interpreting a decision letter
  • understanding the desk-screen
  • planning next steps after a decision

When not to use

  • writing the paper
  • writing the rebuttal
