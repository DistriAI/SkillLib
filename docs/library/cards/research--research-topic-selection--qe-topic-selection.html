qe-topic-selection

separate

Use when first judging whether a project fits Quantitative Economics (QE) — a substantive economic question answered with serious quantitative methods (empirical, structural/computational, experimental, or simulation), sister to Econometrica and Theoretical Economics. Tests fit and sharpens the question; it does not design the estimation.

When to use

  • judging if a project fits QE
  • testing fit for substantive economic questions
  • sharpening the economic question
  • choosing between QE, Econometrica, and field journals
  • routing projects across the Econometric Society trio
  • evaluating structural, empirical, experimental, or simulation papers

When not to use

  • designing the estimation
  • pure theory without quantification
  • method-first contributions where the estimator is the point
  • descriptive notes without quantitative content
  • questions confined to one narrow subfield
