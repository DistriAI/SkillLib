mw-institutional-background

separate

Use when drafting or revising the institutional-background section of a Management-World manuscript, when the China policy timeline is unclear, or when reviewers ask for stronger institutional context.

When to use

  • drafting institutional-background section
  • revising institutional-background section
  • clarifying China policy timeline
  • addressing reviewer comments on institutional context
  • structuring policy timeline tables
  • linking policy to identification strategy

When not to use

  • writing empirical main body
  • writing introduction
  • writing footnotes for AER/AEJ style
