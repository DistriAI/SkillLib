diabetologia

separate

Use when targeting Diabetologia or deciding whether a diabetes clinical, epidemiological, or basic/translational study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's diabetes-focused fit across the evidence spectrum, the mechanistic and methodological bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, EASD house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • Diabetes clinical, epidemiological, or basic/translational study fit check
  • Re-framing diabetes studies around mechanism or prevention/treatment
  • Choosing between Diabetologia, JCEM, and The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology
  • Diabetes reporting-guideline and registration expectations
  • Diabetic complications, islet biology, and metabolic mechanism studies
  • Diabetes biomarkers, genetics, and -omics with disease relevance

When not to use

  • Clinical or regulatory advice
  • Broad endocrine topics not specific to diabetes
  • Small descriptive series or association studies without mechanistic or clinical payoff
