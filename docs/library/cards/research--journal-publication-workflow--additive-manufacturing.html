additive-manufacturing

separate

Use when targeting the journal Additive Manufacturing or deciding whether an AM-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the process–structure–property and qualification bar, AM-specific experimental rigor, the AM-vs-metallurgy and AM-vs-subtractive routing, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • AM-process, AM-materials, or AM-qualification manuscript fit check
  • Re-framing a paper into an AM process–structure–property or qualification contribution
  • Deciding between Additive Manufacturing, acta-materialia, and international-journal-of-machine-tools-and-manufacture
  • New AM processes and process physics
  • Process–structure–property relationships
  • AM materials: alloys, polymers, ceramics, composites, and multi-material structures
  • Design-for-AM: lattices, topology-optimized and architected structures
  • In-situ monitoring, sensing, and process control

When not to use

  • Papers that print a known part on a commercial machine with no AM-specific insight
  • Papers that report a property with no link to the process
  • Metallurgy-first venues where the microstructure mechanism is the point
  • Precision-machining venues (subtractive/precision)
