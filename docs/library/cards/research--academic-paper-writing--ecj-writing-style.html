ecj-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing the prose of a The Economic Journal (EJ) manuscript into its clear, generalist-legible, economics-first register. Tightens voice, structure, and citation/format house style; it does not fix the economic argument itself (see ecj-theory-model / ecj-identification).

When to use

  • Polishing EJ manuscript prose
  • Tightening voice and structure
  • Applying citation and format house style
  • Making writing generalist-legible
  • Late polish stage editing

When not to use

  • Fixing the economic argument
  • Fixing identification
  • Fixing the model
