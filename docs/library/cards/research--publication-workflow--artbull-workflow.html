artbull-workflow

separate

Use as the entry point for any The Art Bulletin manuscript in art history. Routes to the right Art Bulletin sub-skill based on where you are in the lifecycle, with early attention to the image-and-permissions workload that art-historical publishing requires. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new Art Bulletin article
  • Mid-project skill routing
  • Image rights and reproduction cost concerns
  • Returning with a decision letter
  • Routing from idea to submission

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Social-science venue tasks
  • Datasets, statistics, or replication
  • Cataloguing objects without argument
