nature-social-science

separate

社会科学及相邻学科学术写作与选题论证工作台，覆盖选题构思、文献梳理、论证组织、学术表达四大要素。Use for 找论文题目/研究问题、把兴趣或宽泛主题变成可讨论的研究方向、文献综述与领域版图、区分"研究少"与真正未决的知识问题、设计证据路线与主张边界、新颖性审计与"审稿人2"式攻击、博士/论文可行性评估、导师沟通话术、或检查论文中的过度断言与概念混用。也适用于开题报告、研究计划书、学位论文的选题阶段与论证自检。

When to use

  • finding paper topics or research questions
  • turning broad interests into research directions
  • literature review and domain mapping
  • distinguishing research gaps from knowledge problems
  • designing evidence routes and claim boundaries
  • novelty audits and reviewer 2 attacks
  • PhD or thesis feasibility assessment
  • advisor communication and proposal writing

When not to use

  • generating a correct topic
  • guaranteeing novelty, admission, funding, or publication
  • defaulting to specific methods as high-quality answers
