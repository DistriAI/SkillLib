debt-collector-scripts

authentic

Handle debt collectors without getting bullied or tricked — what to say, what never to say, and the rights that protect you from harassment and illegal tactics. Use when asked how do I deal with debt collectors, a collector keeps calling, can they do this, or how to respond to a collection notice. Produces ready scripts (request written validation, dispute, cease-contact, set boundaries), the phrases that accidentally restart the clock or admit the debt (and to avoid them), your rights under fair-debt-collection rules (harassment limits, validation, what's illegal), how to check the debt is real and yours, and safe next options. Not legal advice; points to legal aid.

When to use

  • dealing with debt collectors
  • responding to collection notices
  • requesting written debt validation
  • disputing a debt
  • setting contact boundaries
  • sending cease-contact requests
  • checking if a debt is valid or time-barred
  • identifying phrases that restart the statute of limitations

When not to use

  • legal advice
  • handling lawsuits directly
  • negotiating payment plans without validation
