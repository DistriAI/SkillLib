sociological-studies

separate

Use when targeting 《社会学研究》(Sociological Studies) — the CASS flagship sociology journal. Apply to social stratification, family, networks, urban/rural, work, and institutions, supporting both quantitative survey analysis (CGSS/CFPS/CLDS) and qualitative ethnography. Emphasizes dialogue with sociological theory over econometric policy evaluation; reframes economics-style papers toward a sociological problematic.

When to use

  • social stratification and mobility
  • family, gender, and life course
  • social networks and social capital
  • urban-rural, migration, and governance
  • quantitative survey analysis using CGSS/CFPS/CLDS
  • qualitative ethnography and in-depth interviews
  • reframing economics-style papers toward a sociological problematic

When not to use

  • economics-style policy evaluation
  • pure management and economics empirical applications
  • cross-disciplinary major theoretical propositions for 《中国社会科学》
  • organizational construct relationships with questionnaires for 《南开管理评论》
