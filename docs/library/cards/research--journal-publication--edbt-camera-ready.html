edbt-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing an accepted EDBT paper for its OpenProceedings camera-ready, covering the host/OpenProceedings LaTeX template, the CC-BY-NC-ND open-access license and copyright form, ISBN/volume metadata, integrating reviewer-required changes without scope creep, permanentizing artifact links, and the presentation obligation.

When to use

  • preparing an accepted EDBT paper for OpenProceedings camera-ready
  • using the host/OpenProceedings LaTeX template
  • completing the CC-BY-NC-ND open-access license and copyright form
  • entering ISBN/volume metadata
  • integrating reviewer-required changes without scope creep
  • permanentizing artifact links
  • confirming registration and presentation obligation
  • de-anonymizing double-blind submissions

When not to use

  • ACM/IEEE DL upload
  • filling in ACM e-rights or IEEE copyright transfer forms
  • switching to acmart or IEEEtran templates
