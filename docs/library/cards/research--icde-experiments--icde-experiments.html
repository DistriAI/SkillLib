icde-experiments

separate

Use when designing or auditing IEEE ICDE experiments for a data-engineering paper: workload realism, tuned baselines, scale curves over single points, throughput and tail-latency with declared variance, mechanism-isolating ablations, cost/loss disclosure, and hardware reporting that satisfies a builder-heavy committee.

When to use

  • designing IEEE ICDE experiments
  • auditing data-engineering paper experiments
  • checking workload realism
  • verifying tuned baselines
  • reviewing scale curves
  • checking throughput and tail-latency variance
  • designing mechanism-isolating ablations
  • auditing cost and hardware reporting

When not to use

  • topping a leaderboard
  • vague feasibility language
