campaign-planner

separate

Use when the user asks to "plan an influencer campaign", "build a campaign blueprint", "track or close a creator campaign", or "record a late campaign correction"; produces the plan and, when requested, a non-canonical evidence tracker with scoped identity, publication, reconciliation, close, and reopen receipts. Not for individual creator briefs — use brief-generator; not for overall product launches without creators — use launch-tier-planner; not for sending, publishing, amplifying, or paying — use the owning execution workflow. 达人营销策划/种草方案/活动追踪与关账

When to use

  • plan an influencer campaign
  • build a campaign blueprint
  • track or close a creator campaign
  • record a late campaign correction
  • launching a product with influencer support
  • building seasonal or always-on creator programs
  • maintaining an existing creator-campaign tracker
  • reconciling payment/measurement evidence

When not to use

  • individual creator briefs
  • overall product launches without creators
  • sending, publishing, amplifying, or paying
  • writing briefs
  • executing outreach
  • mutating external systems
