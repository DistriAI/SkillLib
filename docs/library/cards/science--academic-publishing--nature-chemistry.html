nature-chemistry

separate

Use when targeting Nature Chemistry (Nat Chem) or deciding whether a chemistry manuscript fits this Springer Nature venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Nature Chemistry
  • deciding if a chemistry manuscript fits Nature Chemistry
  • evaluating conceptual advance against JACS, Angewandte, and Nature Chemistry
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • providing alternative-venue guidance
  • checking official submission guidelines
  • pre-submission self-check
  • framing chemistry for broad audience

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • specialist technical journal tone
  • results significant only within a sub-field without conceptual reach
  • novelty-within-a-class framing without conceptual advance
