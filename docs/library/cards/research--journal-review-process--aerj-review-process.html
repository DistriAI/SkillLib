aerj-review-process

separate

Use to understand how an American Educational Research Journal (AERJ) manuscript will be judged — masked/double-anonymous peer review, desk screening, integrated-journal fit, and decision categories. Sets expectations and informs strategy; it does not contact editors or reviewers.

When to use

  • Setting expectations before or after submission
  • Anticipating likely reviewer objections
  • Understanding desk rejection or R&R signals
  • Framing cover letter for integrated AERJ fit
  • Understanding masked/double-anonymous review process
  • Assessing desk-screen risk
  • Identifying pre-submission strengthening needs

When not to use

  • Contacting editors or reviewers
  • Providing fixed timelines or reviewer counts
  • Submitting specialty-journal papers without field-wide fit
