interspeech-author-response

separate

Use when INTERSPEECH reviews arrive on CMT and a rebuttal is due — triaging three-or-more speech reviewers with mixed science/engineering instincts, answering WER/MOS evidence doubts with material already in the 4-page record, keeping replies anonymous, and writing for the meta-reviewer who decides in June.

When to use

  • triaging speech reviewers
  • answering WER/MOS evidence doubts
  • keeping replies anonymous
  • writing for the meta-reviewer
  • drafting rebuttal responses
  • triaging review points
  • verifying anonymity
  • checking word/character caps

When not to use

  • re-arguing the venue
  • promising a Long Paper resubmission
  • thanking at length
  • responding to score
  • adding new result tables unless permitted
