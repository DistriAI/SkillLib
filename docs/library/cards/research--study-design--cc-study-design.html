cc-study-design

separate

Use when designing or auditing the experimental plan for a Cancer Cell (Cell Press) study — orthogonal validation across in vitro, in vivo, and human tumor systems, with controls, replicates, power, randomization, and blinding. It plans design; it does not run statistics or write figures.

When to use

  • designing experimental plans
  • auditing experimental plans
  • planning orthogonal validation
  • defining controls and replicates
  • planning mouse, PDX, or organoid experiments
  • specifying randomization and blinding
  • designing human-sample cohorts

When not to use

  • running statistics
  • writing figures
