Skill page

Conflict

The two skills perform the same task but enforce different, non-interchangeable procedures. The phuryn copy is a general strategic framework that asks for 5-7 items per quadrant, includes a 'Strategic Applications' section (Build/Defend/Pivot/Exit), and does not require external sourcing. The deanpeters copy is an evidence-cited workflow that caps entries at 5 per quadrant, mandates Fact/Inference/Assumption labels with URLs, requires a specific 'Crossings' (S-O/W-T) section, and includes a search-plan gate. Because the phuryn copy lacks the evidence-citation and crossing requirements, and the deanpeters copy lacks the Build/Defend/Pivot/Exit framework, crowning one would hide a real alternative approach.
