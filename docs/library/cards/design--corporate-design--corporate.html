corporate

separate

Professional, brand-aligned design with structured grids, minimalist layouts, and consistent enterprise patterns.

When to use

  • Corporate UI/UX design
  • Design-system guideline authoring
  • Enterprise and premium visual styles
  • Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.2 AA)
  • Component anatomy and state definitions
  • Design token and foundation definition
  • QA checklist generation
  • Anti-pattern identification

When not to use

  • Low contrast text
  • Inconsistent spacing rhythm
  • Decorative motion without purpose
  • Ambiguous labels
  • Mixing multiple visual metaphors
  • Inaccessible hit areas
