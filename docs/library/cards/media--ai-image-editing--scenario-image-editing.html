scenario-image-editing

separate

Use when editing an existing image on Scenario through MCP with a tool model, not a new generation: upscale or enhance to 2x, 4K, 8K, super resolution, 3D LUT color grade, color correction, posterize, solarize, vignette, film grain, blur, sharpen, glow, chromatic aberration, oilify, cubism, crystallize, dodge and burn, tint, desaturate, expand or uncrop, reframe an aspect ratio, resize to exact pixels, slice tiles, contact sheet, split into layers, remove a background or watermark, vectorize.

When to use

  • upscale or enhance to 2x, 4K, 8K
  • super resolution
  • 3D LUT color grade
  • color correction
  • posterize, solarize, vignette
  • film grain, blur, sharpen, glow
  • chromatic aberration, oilify, cubism, crystallize
  • dodge and burn, tint, desaturate

When not to use

  • generating a new image
  • prompt-driven edits
  • masked inpainting
  • effects on footage
  • stacking layers
