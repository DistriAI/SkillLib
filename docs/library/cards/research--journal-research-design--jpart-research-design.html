jpart-research-design

separate

Use when defending the research design of a Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory (JPART) manuscript — survey/lab/field experiments on public employees and citizens, causal observational designs, multilevel structures, or mixed methods. JPART has moved strongly toward experimental and causal identification. Strengthens the design; it does not write code.

When to use

  • defending research design
  • survey/lab/field experiments
  • causal observational designs
  • multilevel structures
  • mixed methods
  • specifying identification
  • preparing pre-analysis plan
  • justifying design against rival accounts

When not to use

  • writing code
