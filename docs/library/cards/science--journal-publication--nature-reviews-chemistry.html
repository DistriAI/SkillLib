nature-reviews-chemistry

separate

Use when targeting Nature Reviews Chemistry (Nat Rev Chem) or deciding whether a chemistry review proposal fits this commissioned, authoritative-review venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Proposing an authoritative, field-defining review across any area of chemistry
  • Choosing between Nature Reviews Chemistry and other synthesis-style venues
  • Understanding the commissioned, conceptual, accessibility-first model
  • Reviewing expectations on conceptual framing and figure-as-pedagogy
  • Preparing a pre-submission proposal route before approaching editors
  • Assessing fit for conceptual syntheses organizing an active chemistry subfield
  • Evaluating emerging areas where an expert map clarifies direction
  • Reviewing cross-disciplinary interfaces like chemical biology or energy materials

When not to use

  • Primary research submissions
  • Exhaustive literature dumps without a conceptual organizing principle
  • Cold submissions without editorial collaboration
  • Articles presenting new primary research data
