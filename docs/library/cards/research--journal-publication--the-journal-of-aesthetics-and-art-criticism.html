the-journal-of-aesthetics-and-art-criticism

separate

Use when targeting The Journal of Aesthetics and Art Criticism or deciding whether an aesthetics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, its philosophical-argument bar, objection-handling and arts-criticism expectations, house style and review norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for philosophical-aesthetics manuscripts
  • sharpening arguments about art or aesthetic experience
  • choosing between aesthetics, music-theory, or art-historical venues
  • checking philosophical-argument bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • nature and definition of art
  • aesthetic experience, properties, taste, and value
  • philosophy of particular arts (visual, music, literature, film, dance, theater, architecture)
  • interpretation, criticism, expression, and representation

When not to use

  • technical music analysis
  • general analytic philosophy without an aesthetic center
  • art-historical and critical-theoretical work
  • appreciation, description, or art-historical narrative with no philosophical thesis
