  • targeting Available, Functional, and Reproduced badges
  • packaging attacks and measurements responsibly
  • writing the 2-page artifact appendix
  • handling dangerous or embargoed material
  • converting claims ledger to artifact package

  • re-execution of live targets or proprietary data
