saas-metrics-coach

separate

SaaS financial health advisor. Use when a user shares revenue or customer numbers, or mentions ARR, MRR, churn, LTV, CAC, NRR, or asks how their SaaS business is doing.

When to use

  • SaaS financial health assessment
  • Calculating ARR, MRR, churn, LTV, CAC, NRR
  • Benchmarking metrics by segment and stage
  • Prioritizing actionable business advice
  • Forward-looking unit economics projections

When not to use

  • DCF valuation
  • Budget variance analysis
  • Traditional financial modeling
  • Retention strategies
  • Customer health scoring
