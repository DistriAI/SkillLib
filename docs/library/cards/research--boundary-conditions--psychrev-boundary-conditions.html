psychrev-boundary-conditions

separate

Use when setting the scope of a Psychological Review theory — what it explains, what it does NOT, and (for formal models) whether its parameters are identifiable. Bounds the theory; it does NOT derive its predictions (psychrev-argument-development) or frame its advance over rivals (psychrev-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • setting the scope of a Psychological Review theory
  • defining phenomena in and out of scope
  • stating population and domain limits
  • naming conditions of breakdown
  • checking structural identifiability
  • demonstrating parameter recovery
  • addressing model mimicry
  • listing what the theory does not explain

When not to use

  • deriving theory predictions
  • framing advance over rivals
