journal-of-economic-geography

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Economic Geography (JoEG) or deciding whether a spatial-economics / economic-geography manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Economic Geography
  • deciding if spatial-economics manuscript fits venue
  • re-framing applied papers toward economic geography
  • identifying JoEG desk-reject risks
  • finding spatial-economics alternative journals

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • non-spatial economics papers with geography as label
  • pure econ or pure geography papers ignoring the other tradition
