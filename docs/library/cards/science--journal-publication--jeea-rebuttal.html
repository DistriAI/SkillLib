jeea-rebuttal

separate

Use when an R&R or decision letter arrives for a Journal of the European Economic Association (JEEA) manuscript and a response-letter strategy and revision plan are needed. Plans and drafts the response; it does not produce new estimates or rewrite the paper.

When to use

  • Triage referee and co-editor comments
  • Draft point-by-point response
  • Resolve referee conflicts
  • Plan revision structure
  • Verify replication package sync
  • Check EEA membership status

When not to use

  • Produce new estimates
  • Rewrite the paper
