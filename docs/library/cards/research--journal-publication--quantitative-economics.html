quantitative-economics

separate

Use when targeting Quantitative Economics (QE) or deciding whether a structural / quantitative manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Quantitative Economics (QE)
  • deciding whether a structural / quantitative manuscript fits this venue
  • choosing among quantitative venues for structural / computational papers
  • re-framing reduced-form or theory papers toward QE's strengths
  • assessing QE desk-reject risks
  • providing a credible quantitative / econometrics alternative list

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • pure econometric theory / estimators with proofs
  • applied-econometrics methods
  • general-interest structural work with broad ambition
  • macro-structural work
  • theory-only contributions
  • behavioral/experimental work
