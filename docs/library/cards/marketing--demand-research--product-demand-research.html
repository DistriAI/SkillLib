product-demand-research

separate

Use when the user wants to validate a product idea, find pain points, mine demand signals, discover objections, or gather voice-of-customer language from Reddit, social posts, video transcripts, and comments. Produces evidence-backed product research.

When to use

  • validate a startup or product idea
  • find pain points in a niche
  • mine Reddit/TikTok/YouTube comments for product ideas
  • find buyer language and objections
  • understand alternatives people use today
  • create messaging from demand evidence

When not to use

  • claim market validation from a handful of comments
  • ignore negative evidence or existing alternatives
  • paraphrase away the best customer language
