itcs-author-response

separate

Use to operate under the reality that ITCS has no rebuttal or author-response phase — pre-empting reviewer objections inside the submission, then handling the post-decision situation (camera-ready window on accept, or rerouting and reusing the reviews on reject) since there is no revise-and-resubmit.

When to use

  • pre-empting reviewer objections inside the submission
  • handling the post-decision situation on accept
  • handling the post-decision situation on reject
  • rerouting and reusing reviews on reject
  • folding reasonable fixes into camera-ready
  • mining reviews for the real objection
  • diagnosing novelty vs correctness issues

When not to use

  • writing a rebuttal document answering reviewers
  • writing a point-by-point response letter
  • submitting to a later ITCS round
  • appealing to overturn a reject
