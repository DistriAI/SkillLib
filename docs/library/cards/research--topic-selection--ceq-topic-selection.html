ceq-topic-selection

separate

Use when shaping or sharpening a 《经济学(季刊)》 (China Economic Quarterly, CEQ) research question — turning data/correlation into a clean causal, model-able, internationally-legible topic that a field economist can recognize as a contribution. Use before literature review and identification.

When to use

  • shaping CEQ research questions
  • sharpening research questions
  • turning data into causal topics
  • turning correlation into model-able topics
  • ensuring international legibility
  • diagnosing weak topics
  • reshaping policy evaluation topics
  • self-diagnosis of topic viability

When not to use

  • literature review
  • identification strategy design
  • data-driven topic selection
  • policy recommendation without causal question
  • re-running known conclusions with new data
