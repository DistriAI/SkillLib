phg-workflow

separate

Use when starting or navigating any Progress in Human Geography (PiHG) manuscript and you need the entry point. Routes to the right sub-skill based on lifecycle stage and whether the piece is a free-standing review article or a commissioned Progress Report. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new PiHG piece
  • Navigating mid-project skill selection
  • Deciding between review article and Progress Report
  • Checking if a topic is review-worthy
  • Routing after a decision letter

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Publishing empirical results
  • Publishing detailed cases
  • Submitting a Progress Report without a commission
