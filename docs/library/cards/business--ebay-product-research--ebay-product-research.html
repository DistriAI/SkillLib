Skill page

Conflict

The two copies describe the same high-level task (eBay product research) but provide materially different, non-overlapping procedures. The first is a natural-language prompt skill that instructs the agent to analyze completed listings, sell-through rates, competition density, seasonal demand, sourcing opportunities, fees, and trends, and to output structured recommendations. The second is a CLI-style tool with specific commands, parameters, marketplace selection, competitor monitoring, and a paid pricing model. Neither copy contains the other's concrete steps or constraints, so crowning one would hide a real alternative workflow.
