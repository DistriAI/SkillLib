arsoc-revision

separate

Use when responding to Editorial Committee and referee feedback on an Annual Review of Sociology (ARSoc) review — coverage gaps, balance/accuracy complaints, framework/structure asks, and accessibility. Drafts the response and revision plan; it does not run the delivery preflight (arsoc-submission) or redesign the spine from scratch (arsoc-organizing-framework).

When to use

  • responding to Editorial Committee and referee feedback
  • addressing coverage gaps
  • addressing balance and accuracy complaints
  • addressing framework and structure asks
  • addressing accessibility issues
  • drafting the response and revision plan
  • triaging review feedback into categories
  • writing the point-by-point response letter

When not to use

  • running the delivery preflight (arsoc-submission)
  • redesigning the spine from scratch (arsoc-organizing-framework)
  • responding to feedback on a primary paper
