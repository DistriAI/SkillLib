human-resource-management

separate

Use when targeting Human Resource Management (HRM, Wiley) or deciding whether a strategic HRM / employment-relationship manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • strategic HRM
  • employment relationship
  • HR systems and bundles
  • high-performance work practices
  • psychological contracts
  • talent management
  • multilevel HRM phenomena
  • venue-selection and re-framing

When not to use

  • atheoretical surveys
  • pure conceptual theory
  • pure OB study with no HR-systems focus
  • broad management/OB empirics
  • top-bar theory-driven empirics
  • pure theory
  • interdisciplinary/critical social science of work
  • strategy/firm performance
