mobicom-author-response

separate

Use when writing a MobiCom rebuttal after round-two reviews or executing a one-shot revision — triaging reviewer points by severity, fitting a factual, concession-honest rebuttal into its length limit, and turning a required-changes list into a change-highlighted revision with a change summary that the same reviewers can verify.

When to use

  • writing MobiCom rebuttals
  • executing one-shot revisions
  • triaging reviewer points by severity
  • fitting rebuttals into length limits
  • turning required-changes lists into change-highlighted revisions
  • creating change summaries for reviewer verification

When not to use

  • using rebuttal tactics on a revision
  • using revision ambition in a rebuttal
