  • **Starting from a research repo** to write a paper

  • **Resubmitting** to a different venue (format conversion)

Skill page

Conflict

The two copies share the same core philosophy, citation-verification rules, and repository-exploration workflow, but they disagree on the canonical citation authority and add different structural guidance. The Orchestra-Research copy is the upstream source (v1.2.0) and includes a pointer to a separate systems-paper-writing skill, while the brycewang-stanford copy (v1.0.0) adds a 'Default operating order' that designates references/citation-workflow.md as the canonical citation authority and explicitly demotes Google Scholar. Because the copies overlap but each contains material steps or constraints the other lacks, and they disagree on the verification authority, crowning one would hide a real alternative.
