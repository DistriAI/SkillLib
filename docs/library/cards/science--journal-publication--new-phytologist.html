new-phytologist

separate

Use when targeting New Phytologist or deciding whether a plant-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the integrative conceptual-advance and whole-plant/ecosystem-scale bar, evidence and data-deposition expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for integrative plant-ecology, physiology, or symbiosis paper
  • framing whole-plant or ecosystem-scale result as conceptual advance
  • deciding between New Phytologist and the-plant-journal
  • New Phytologist desk-reject heuristics
  • credible alternative route
  • plant physiology and whole-plant function
  • plant–microbe and plant–plant interactions
  • plant ecology and environmental responses

When not to use

  • single gene-to-function molecular mechanism with no physiological, ecological, or whole-organism reach
  • descriptive survey or correlation with no conceptual question or integrative framing
  • pot/glasshouse results over-generalized to field or ecosystem scale without justification
  • pseudoreplicated or under-replicated ecology presented as general
  • symbiosis claims from co-occurrence alone
