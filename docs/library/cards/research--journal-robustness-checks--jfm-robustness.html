jfm-robustness

separate

Use when results may be sensitive to liquidity-measure choice, sample filters, microstructure noise, or inference for a Journal of Financial Markets (JFM) manuscript. Builds the design-based robustness ledger; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • liquidity-measure choice sensitivity
  • sample filters and asset universe variations
  • microstructure noise checks
  • inference for autocorrelated panel data
  • threat-mapped robustness ledger construction
  • bid-ask bounce and price discreteness tests
  • two-way clustering and wild-cluster bootstrap
  • mechanism corroboration checks

When not to use

  • inventing evidence
  • inventing citations
  • creating robustness tables without named threats
