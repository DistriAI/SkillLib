education-data-source-edfacts

separate

EDFacts — K-12 outcomes: assessment proficiency, ACGR graduation rates, ESSA accountability at school/district level (2009-2020). Within-state trends and subgroup gaps. Complements CCD with outcome data. Cannot compare across states — use NAEP.

When to use

  • within-state achievement trends
  • subgroup proficiency gaps
  • adjusted cohort graduation rates
  • state assessment proficiency rates
  • ESSA accountability indicators
  • school and district level outcomes

When not to use

  • cross-state comparison of state assessment scores
  • national ranking by proficiency
  • comparing proficiency rates across different states
