arecon-submission

separate

Use when running the final delivery preflight for a commissioned Annual Review of Economics (ARE) review via the Annual Reviews production system — disclosure statements, reference/format compliance, figure permissions, and the transparency/reproducibility of any meta-analysis the review itself contains. Final checks; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • final delivery preflight
  • commissioned Annual Review of Economics review
  • disclosure statements
  • reference and format compliance
  • figure permissions
  • transparency and reproducibility of meta-analysis

When not to use

  • drafting content
  • cold-submission portal for unsolicited manuscripts
  • treating S2O as a pay-to-publish APC
