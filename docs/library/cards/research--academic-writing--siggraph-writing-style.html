siggraph-writing-style

separate

Use when structuring or revising a SIGGRAPH / TOG technical paper, covering the teaser figure, a results-first first page, the graphics contribution framing (technique + quality/performance), comparison-driven evaluation, honest limitations, and the acmart 7-page discipline for the conference track.

When to use

  • structuring a SIGGRAPH paper
  • revising a TOG technical paper
  • designing the teaser figure
  • framing graphics contributions
  • managing the 7-page acmart budget
  • writing honest limitations

When not to use

  • chronological related work surveys
  • font or margin tampering to recover space
  • boilerplate limitations
  • qualitative comparisons without metrics
