business-management-journal

separate

Use when targeting 《经济管理》(Business Management Journal — 中国社会科学院工业经济研究所主办的工商管理学术月刊, 不收费) or deciding whether a Chinese management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, fee-free policy, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《经济管理》
  • deciding if a Chinese management manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese management manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • listing alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting pure economic policy evaluation without management implications
  • confusing with 《经济与管理研究》
