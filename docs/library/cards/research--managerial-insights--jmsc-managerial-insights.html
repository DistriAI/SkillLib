jmsc-managerial-insights

separate

Use when deriving managerial insights for a 《管理科学学报》 (Journal of Management Sciences in China) manuscript — translating proven propositions and numerical findings into actionable decision rules tied to model parameters. Insights must follow from the model, NOT be policy slogans like "加强完善推进". Use near the end of the analytical pipeline.

When to use

  • Deriving managerial insights from model propositions
  • Translating numerical findings into decision rules
  • Writing the managerial implications section
  • Converting policy slogans into parameterized decision laws
  • Linking insights to specific model thresholds or intervals

When not to use

  • Writing generic policy slogans like 'strengthen' or 'improve'
  • Providing government policy recommendations
  • Extrapolating conclusions beyond model assumptions
  • Writing insights without traceable model support
  • Using vague statements like 'provides theoretical support'
