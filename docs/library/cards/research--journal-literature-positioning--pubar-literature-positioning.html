pubar-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Public Administration Review (PAR) manuscript against the literature so it reads as a field-wide contribution that also informs practice. PAR readers span public management, governance, and the practitioner community, so the paper must engage the core PA debates and not just one niche. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction
  • Revising the contribution paragraph
  • Connecting niche literature to broader public management
  • Distinguishing contribution from closest prior PAR/JPART papers
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on missing core PA work
  • Positioning contribution as a move in the debate

When not to use

  • Writing the full literature review
  • Writing a JPART theory-driven intro
  • Writing a JPAM policy analysis intro
  • Self-citation that breaks anonymity
