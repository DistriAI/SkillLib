scenario-seedance-storyboard

separate

Use when a Seedance video must hold continuous movement across shots: dance choreography, fights, sports, or any performance where limbs teleport, feet slide, or motion resets at every cut, or when turning a storyboard and character sheet into a multi-shot sequence that plays as one take. Keywords: storyboard to video, choreography, movement control, pose chain, timecoded shot script, first and last frame chaining, character sheet, multi-shot continuity, Seedance 2.5 and 2.0.
