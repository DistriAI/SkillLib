physical-review-b

separate

Use when targeting Physical Review B (PRB) or deciding whether a condensed matter or materials physics manuscript fits this APS archival venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting PRB for a condensed matter or materials physics manuscript
  • evaluating a result for PRB versus PRL, PRX, or Nature journals
  • distinguishing PRB's archival culture from selective Nature-family or PRL cultures
  • re-routing a manuscript rejected from PRL, PRX, or a Nature journal to PRB
  • checking scope and topic fit for electronic structure, superconductivity, or topological materials
  • assessing method and evidence bar for experimental or theoretical condensed matter papers
  • reviewing structure and house style for PRB Letters or Articles
  • performing an official-submission checklist for PRB

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
