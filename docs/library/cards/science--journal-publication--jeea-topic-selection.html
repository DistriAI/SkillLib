jeea-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a question fits the Journal of the European Economic Association (JEEA) rather than a field journal or a sibling general-interest outlet for a JEEA manuscript. Tests general-interest fit and sharpens the question; it does not design the empirics or model.

When to use

  • deciding if a question fits JEEA
  • testing general-interest fit
  • sharpening the research question
  • choosing between JEEA and sibling outlets
  • assessing if a lesson travels beyond subfield
  • triaging venue fit before deeper work

When not to use

  • designing the empirics
  • designing the model
  • method demonstration
  • purely methodological papers with toy applications
