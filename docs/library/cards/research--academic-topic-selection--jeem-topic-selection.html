jeem-topic-selection

separate

Use when scoping or scope-testing a Journal of Environmental Economics and Management (JEEM) manuscript — confirming the environmental/resource mechanism is load-bearing and the question clears the field-journal bar before any analysis is finalized. Decides fit and routes; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • scoping a JEEM manuscript
  • scope-testing a JEEM manuscript
  • confirming environmental/resource mechanism is load-bearing
  • confirming question clears field-journal bar
  • deciding fit and routing
  • distinguishing JEEM from sibling journals
  • evaluating environmental-policy causal papers
  • evaluating revealed-preference valuation papers

When not to use

  • inventing evidence
  • inventing citations
  • finalizing analysis
  • papers where environment is incidental to labor/IO/public-finance results
  • science-only climate-impacts papers without economics
