jegeo-literature-positioning

separate

Use when staking a Journal of Economic Geography (JEG) manuscript's contribution against both the geographical-economics and human-geography frontiers, and against sibling journals. Sharpens positioning; it does not invent citations.

When to use

  • staking JEG manuscript contribution
  • positioning against geographical-economics frontier
  • positioning against human-geography frontier
  • arguing boundary with sibling journals
  • building contribution claim
  • mapping referee pushback to positioning fixes

When not to use

  • inventing citations
