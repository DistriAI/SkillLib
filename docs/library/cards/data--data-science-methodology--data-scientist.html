Conflict

The first two copies are near-identical role prompts that list broad capabilities (statistical methods, ML, visualization, business analytics) but provide no concrete procedure. The third copy is a distinct, rigorous methodology skill that defines non-negotiable principles (data robustness, documentation, verification, code documentation, research focus) and specific workflow constraints (file-first execution, loading tool-specific skills). Because the third copy contains material steps and constraints the others lack, and the first two are generic role definitions, crowning one would hide the real alternative of a procedural methodology skill.
