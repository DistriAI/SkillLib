dac-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or tightening the prose of an ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference (DAC) Research Manuscript, covering the QoR-forward first-page arc, stating the design-automation contribution as a measured delta, fitting a complete argument into six double-column pages plus a references-only page, building column-width EDA figures, and third-person self-citation for double-blind review.

When to use

  • drafting DAC Research Manuscript prose
  • tightening DAC manuscript prose
  • structuring the QoR-forward first-page arc
  • stating design-automation contribution as a measured delta
  • fitting argument into six double-column pages
  • building column-width EDA figures
  • applying third-person self-citation for double-blind review

When not to use

  • explaining everything
  • carrying three loosely related ideas
  • long taxonomy or tutorial of standard EDA background
  • opening with general AI transformation statements
  • including acknowledgements, funding, or grant numbers in review version
