jama-surgery

separate

Use when targeting JAMA Surgery or deciding whether a surgical-outcomes or surgical-trial study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the surgical-trial and perioperative evidence bar, surgical-reporting challenges, reporting-guideline and trial-registration requirements, JAMA Network house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • surgical trial fit check
  • surgical outcomes study framing
  • perioperative research venue selection
  • surgical trial reporting requirements
  • JAMA Surgery desk-reject heuristics
  • comparative-effectiveness surgery studies
  • surgical registry analysis fit
  • venue selection between JAMA Surgery and subspecialty journals

When not to use

  • clinical advice
  • regulatory advice
  • single-surgeon case series
  • descriptive technique reports without comparative outcomes
  • underpowered studies
