design-sprint-plan

separate

Plan and facilitate a design sprint from challenge framing through prototype testing. Use when compressing discovery into days. For ongoing team cadence, use `team-workflow`.

When to use

  • Planning structured design sprints
  • Facilitating design sprints
  • Compressing discovery into days
  • Challenge framing
  • Prototype testing
  • Mini sprint planning
  • Remote sprint adaptation
  • Discovery sprint focus

When not to use

  • Ongoing team cadence
