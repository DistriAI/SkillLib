sn-ppt-creative

separate

Use when a prepared PPT task with a current outline.md should be generated as an image-first presentation with one 16:9 image per slide and optional PPTX packaging.

When to use

  • Generate 16:9 image per slide
  • Assemble PPTX from generated images
  • Visual translation of public Story
  • Resume generation from existing artifacts

When not to use

  • Research or fact-finding
  • Generating content outline
  • Merging, splitting, or reordering pages
  • Changing titles or core conclusions
  • Restoring deleted content
  • Silent switching to Standard, Dynamic, or Native PPTX
