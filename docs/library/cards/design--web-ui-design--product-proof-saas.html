product-proof-saas

separate

Create or redesign SaaS and AI product landing pages where a real workflow, interface, or deterministic demo is the central proof. Use for pale atmospheric shells, product UI in the hero, prompt-to-output stories, audience tabs, compact feature modules, honest pricing comparisons, and FAQ handoffs.

When to use

  • SaaS and AI product landing pages
  • pale atmospheric shells
  • product UI in the hero
  • prompt-to-output stories
  • audience tabs
  • compact feature modules
  • honest pricing comparisons
  • FAQ handoffs

When not to use

  • fake generation speed or output quality
  • decorative AI particles
  • constant floating
  • vague glowing orbs
  • fake dashboards
  • fake typing
  • unlabeled sample output
  • invented customers, outcomes, security claims, availability, or performance metrics
