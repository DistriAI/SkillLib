jams-rebuttal

separate

Use when planning a revision and drafting the point-by-point response letter for a Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science (JAMS) R&R — prioritizing the editor's decisive concerns, strengthening both the theoretical and managerial contribution, and preserving double-anonymized rules. Plans and drafts the response; it does not interpret the original decision (jams-review-process).

When to use

  • Planning a revision for a JAMS R&R
  • Drafting the point-by-point response letter
  • Addressing theoretical contribution challenges
  • Addressing managerial relevance challenges
  • Handling conflicting reviewers
  • Showing movement across multiple rounds
  • Triaging reviewer comments by priority

When not to use

  • Interpreting the original decision
