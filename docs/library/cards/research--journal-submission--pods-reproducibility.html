pods-reproducibility

separate

Use when strengthening the verifiability of an ACM PODS paper — a complete claim-to-proof map, self-contained proofs in the at-submission appendix (no external appendices), correctly stated assumptions, honest scope and open cases, the full-version-on-arXiv norm, and consistency between what the paper claims and what the proofs actually establish.

When to use

  • strengthening verifiability of ACM PODS papers
  • creating claim-to-proof maps
  • ensuring self-contained proofs in at-submission appendix
  • stating assumptions and scope honestly
  • maintaining consistency between paper claims and proofs
  • preparing full-version-on-arXiv norms
  • conducting verifiability audits
  • pinning assumptions and scope

When not to use

  • using external or online appendices for proofs
  • deferring key proof steps to 'full version' without appendix inclusion
  • overclaiming in abstracts beyond what theorems prove
  • treating 'proof omitted' as acceptable without appendix proof
