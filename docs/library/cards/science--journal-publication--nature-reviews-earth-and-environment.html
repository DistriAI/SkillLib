nature-reviews-earth-and-environment

separate

Use when targeting Nature Reviews Earth & Environment (Nat. Rev. Earth Environ.) or deciding whether a review-type Earth or environmental science manuscript fits this commissioned-review venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • publishing a comprehensive, authoritative review or perspective across an Earth or environmental science topic
  • preparing a pre-submission enquiry or responding to a commissioning invitation
  • synthesizing a field's state of knowledge, reconciling competing models, and setting research priorities
  • understanding the commissioned, no-primary-research model and the enquiry route
  • synthesis reviews across any Earth-system domain
  • climate science and global change mechanisms, feedbacks, and evidence integration
  • environmental science and human-Earth interactions framed as a scientific synthesis
  • methodological and conceptual reviews or perspectives and agenda-setting pieces

When not to use

  • reporting new data or experiments
  • unsolicited full manuscripts
  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • replacing the editorial commissioning process
