amanthro-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight for American Anthropologist (AA) via the Research Exchange platform — section selection, anonymization, word/abstract caps, Chicago author-date (free-format accepted), DOIs, ORCID, ethics statement, and figure permissions/alt text. Final checks; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • final pre-submission preflight
  • section selection verification
  • anonymization checks
  • word and abstract cap verification
  • Chicago author-date style check
  • DOI and ORCID verification
  • ethics statement finalization
  • figure permissions and alt text check

When not to use

  • drafting content
