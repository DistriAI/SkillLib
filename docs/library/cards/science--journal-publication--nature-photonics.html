nature-photonics

separate

Use when targeting Nature Photonics (Nat Photonics) or deciding whether an optics or photonics manuscript fits this high-impact, broad-significance Nature-family venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Nature Photonics for fundamental or applied optics/photonics results
  • evaluating photonics or laser physics manuscripts against Nature Photonics
  • understanding Nature Photonics triage criteria and framing expectations
  • re-routing papers returned without review within optics/photonics landscape
  • assessing fit for fundamental light-matter interaction discoveries
  • assessing fit for laser science breakthroughs
  • assessing fit for photonic materials and structures
  • assessing fit for quantum photonics and transformative photonic technologies

When not to use

  • solar-energy photovoltaics and solar-fuel photocatalysis unless photonics advance is primary
  • quantum-information-first framing (consider prx-quantum)
  • simulation-only demonstrations of known phenomena
  • optimization of existing designs without genuine breakthrough
