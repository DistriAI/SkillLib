msom-rebuttal

separate

Use after a Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM) revise-and-resubmit — planning the revision around the Department Editor and Associate Editor priorities, then drafting a point-by-point response to referees while preserving operations centrality, the structured abstract, and the 32-page typeset cap. Drives the revision; revise the manuscript before drafting the letter.

When to use

  • Planning M&SOM revision around DE/AE priorities
  • Drafting point-by-point response to referees
  • Addressing requests for numerical studies or robustness
  • Strengthening identification or analytical proofs
  • Managing the 32-page typeset cap and supplement
  • Updating structured abstract and managerial implications

When not to use

  • Writing the response letter before doing modeling or analysis work
  • Treating referee comments as equal to DE/AE priorities
  • Adding results that exceed the 32-page cap without using the supplement
  • Silently ignoring or dropping inconvenient referee comments
  • Diluting the operations decision at the core of the manuscript
