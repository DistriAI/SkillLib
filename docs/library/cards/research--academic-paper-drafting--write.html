Skill page

Conflict

The two skills share the name 'write' but perform different tasks. The first is a meta-skill for authoring agent skills (defining structure, triggers, and token efficiency), while the second is a domain-specific skill for drafting academic paper sections in LaTeX with specific quality checks and humanization passes. They do not overlap in procedure or intent, so crowning one would hide the other's distinct utility.
