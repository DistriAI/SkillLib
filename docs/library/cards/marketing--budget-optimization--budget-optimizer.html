budget-optimizer

separate

Use when the user asks to "allocate my influencer budget", "optimize spend across tiers", or "compare budget scenarios"; produces a tier/platform/content allocation table, ROI and CPM/CPE projections, scenario comparisons, and mid-campaign reallocation moves. Not for building the full campaign plan — use campaign-planner. 达人预算分配/投放预算优化

When to use

  • allocate influencer budget
  • optimize spend across tiers
  • compare budget scenarios
  • splitting spend across nano/micro/macro tiers
  • estimating influencer costs
  • projecting ROI
  • modeling conservative vs aggressive scenarios
  • reallocating budget mid-campaign

When not to use

  • building the full campaign plan
  • reading in-flight pacing
  • issuing scale-up/down moves
  • bid-strategy choice
  • outreach or send authorization
