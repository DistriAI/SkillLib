jar-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing the prose of a Journal of Accounting Research (JAR) manuscript — a design-forward, economically literate style that states the question, setting, and identification early and reads like recent empirical-archival accounting papers. Polishes prose and house style; it does not build the contribution (jar-contribution-framing) or format exhibits (jar-tables-figures).

When to use

  • polishing JAR manuscript prose
  • front-loading research design in introductions
  • improving design clarity and concrete identification
  • adjusting results prose to interpret economic magnitude
  • matching JAR author-date house style
  • ensuring consistency of variable names and labels

When not to use

  • building the contribution
  • formatting exhibits
  • substantive issues requiring contribution framing
  • substantive issues requiring methods review
