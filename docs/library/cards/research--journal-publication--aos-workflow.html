aos-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which aos-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal for an Accounting, Organizations and Society (AOS) manuscript. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which aos-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal
  • routing AOS manuscript tasks
  • identifying the next step for a half-built AOS manuscript
  • planning revision and response after an R&R decision

When not to use

  • performing stage work itself
  • replacing specialized skills
  • capital-markets archival-financial-economics papers without social or organizational theory
