write-outreach

separate

Cold outreach a person sends to business buyers: email and LinkedIn sequences, a first line per prospect, reply and objection answers, and a read of replies. Use when the marketer says "write a cold email sequence", "what do I send to this list", "write LinkedIn messages for these leads", "how do I reply to this prospect" or "handle this objection", "reply to this demo request", "win back or reconnect with accounts that churned or went quiet", or "why is nobody replying to my outreach". Drafts only; the marketer sends from their own tool. Not for finding the people — use research-audience; not for emails to subscribers or users, or consumer win-back — use email-lifecycle.
