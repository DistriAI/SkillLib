jmsc-rebuttal

separate

Use when writing the response letter for an R&R at 《管理科学学报》 (Journal of Management Sciences in China) — addressing reviewer concerns about assumptions, proof gaps, algorithm analysis, numerical design, and model contribution, with point-by-point responses and exact pointers to revised propositions / appendix. Use only after the manuscript itself is revised.

When to use

  • writing response letter for R&R at Journal of Management Sciences in China
  • addressing reviewer concerns about assumptions
  • addressing proof gaps
  • addressing algorithm analysis
  • addressing numerical design
  • addressing model contribution
  • point-by-point responses with pointers to revised propositions
  • point-by-point responses with pointers to appendix

When not to use

  • generating response before manuscript is revised
