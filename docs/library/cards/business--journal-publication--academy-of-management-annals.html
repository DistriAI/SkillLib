academy-of-management-annals

separate

Use when targeting Academy of Management Annals (AMA) or deciding whether a management / organizational review or integrative article fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, contribution bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Academy of Management Annals
  • deciding if a management review fits AMA
  • upgrading narrative review to synthesizing framework
  • identifying AMA desk-reject risks
  • proposing alternative journals for management reviews

When not to use

  • primary theory papers
  • empirical tests
  • replacing current official submission guidelines
