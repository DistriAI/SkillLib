ier-robustness

separate

Use when an International Economic Review (IER) result may be sensitive to specification, sample, functional form, calibration, or inference choices. Organizes robustness by threat to the load-bearing assumption; it does not run the regressions.

When to use

  • Organizing robustness by threat to load-bearing assumption
  • Assessing sensitivity to functional form or calibrated parameters
  • Evaluating inference fragility such as few clusters or serial correlation
  • Checking structural counterfactuals against re-anchored parameters
  • Distinguishing main text robustness checks from appendix checks
  • Reporting robustness as ranges or functions of uncertain parameters
  • Assessing numerical fragility in structural models
  • Identifying which robustness checks IER referees demand

When not to use

  • Running the regressions
  • Performing mechanical appendix re-runs without threat mapping
