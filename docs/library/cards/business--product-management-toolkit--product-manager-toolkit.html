Conflict

The three copies share the same core toolkit (RICE prioritization, interview analysis, PRD templates, discovery frameworks), but the LeoYeAI copy adds material steps and constraints the other two lack: explicit validation checklists (sensitivity analysis, dependency checks, engineering estimate validation), a recruitment step (5-8 interviews per segment, mix of power/churned users), a post-launch tracking step, and a table-of-contents structure. The anbeime and sickn33 copies are near-identical to each other and omit those steps. Crowning one copy would hide the more complete workflow in the LeoYeAI version, so the copies should be combined.
