hri-workflow

separate

Use to plan an ACM/IEEE HRI campaign backward from the full-paper deadline through the abstract-then-paper two-step, the rebuttal, the program-committee meeting, camera-ready, and the March conference, coordinating the parallel alt.HRI, Late-Breaking Reports, video, and Student Design deadlines.

When to use

  • Planning HRI campaign backward from full-paper deadline
  • Coordinating abstract-then-paper two-step
  • Managing rebuttal and program-committee meeting
  • Planning camera-ready and March conference
  • Coordinating parallel alt.HRI, Late-Breaking Reports, video, and Student Design deadlines
  • Backward planning for IRB, study design, recruiting, analysis, and writing
  • Decision branches after notification (accept, shepherding, reject)
  • Reverifying dates and policies each cycle

When not to use

  • Writing the actual rebuttal (see hri-author-response)
  • Finalizing camera-ready metadata and video (see hri-camera-ready)
  • Anonymization sweep on final PDF (see hri-submission)
  • Topic selection for resubmission (see hri-topic-selection)
