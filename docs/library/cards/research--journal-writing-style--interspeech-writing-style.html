interspeech-writing-style

separate

Use when writing or compressing an INTERSPEECH paper into its 4 content pages — the abstract-and-index-terms opening, task-first framing for a mixed science/engineering readership, table and figure economy, protocol one-liners that preserve rigor under compression, and the cuts that shrink pages without shrinking claims.

When to use

  • writing INTERSPEECH papers
  • compressing INTERSPEECH papers
  • structuring abstracts and index terms
  • framing tasks for mixed readerships
  • optimizing table and figure economy
  • writing protocol one-liners
  • applying density moves for space
  • calibrating titles for searchability

When not to use

  • writing long papers
  • writing journal articles
  • formatting PDFs for submission
  • writing related work sections in isolation
