asr-transcribe-to-text

separate

Transcribes audio or video to speaker-labeled, timestamped text, locally with MLX on Apple Silicon or remotely. Use for 转录 / 录音转文字 / 说话人分离 / 字幕, and also for preparing audio for ASR without transcribing: 转格式, 降采样到 16kHz, merging recorder segments, or compressing and speeding up audio before 飞书妙记 — even when it looks like a one-line ffmpeg job.

When to use

  • Transcribing audio or video to text
  • Speaker separation and labeling
  • Generating subtitles
  • Converting audio formats
  • Downsampling audio to 16kHz
  • Merging recorder segments
  • Compressing and speeding up audio
  • Preparing audio for Feishu Minutes

When not to use

  • Regenerating text when a verified human-reviewed transcript exists
  • Running local ASR merely to make a two-route process look complete
