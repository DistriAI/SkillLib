cscw-review-process

separate

Use when interpreting where a CSCW paper stands in review — the PACMHCI journal model's decision vocabulary (Revise for External Review, Revise and Resubmit, Conditional Accept), same-reviewer re-review, the rolling editorial board, and what each outcome statistically implied in the last cycle.

When to use

  • interpreting CSCW review status
  • understanding PACMHCI decision vocabulary
  • same-reviewer re-review process
  • rolling editorial board mechanics
  • statistical implications of review outcomes
  • waiting-time interpretation
  • standing-report format

When not to use

  • writing author responses
  • camera-ready preparation
  • post-mortem analysis of rejections
