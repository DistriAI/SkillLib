Skill page

Conflict

The openai/skills and composio-community copies are identical and describe a single-document workflow driven by a template-selection guide. The Prat011 copy overlaps on the core Notion search/fetch/create steps but diverges materially: it mandates producing two separate documents (an internal pre-read and an external agenda), provides detailed content structures for each, and includes a document-linking step. Crowning the openai/composio version would hide the two-document alternative, so the copies should be combined.
