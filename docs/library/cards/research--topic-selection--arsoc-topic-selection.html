arsoc-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a sociology literature is an Annual Review of Sociology (ARSoc)-scale review topic — mature, broadly important, and genuinely in need of an authoritative cross-subfield synthesis — and framing the review's animating question. Decides fit; it does not gather the literature (arsoc-literature-synthesis) or pitch the Committee (arsoc-proposal-and-commissioning).

When to use

  • Judging if a literature is ARSoc-shaped
  • Deciding between ARSoc and sibling outlets
  • Framing the review's animating question
  • Assessing topic maturity and broad importance
  • Checking need for synthesis
  • Evaluating tractability for the ARSoc envelope

When not to use

  • Gathering the literature
  • Pitching the Committee
  • Reporting original data or findings
  • Developing pure theoretical arguments
  • Writing handbook chapters
  • Submitting unsolicited manuscripts
