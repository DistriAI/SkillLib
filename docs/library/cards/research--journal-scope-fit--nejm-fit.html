nejm-fit

separate

Use this first, before any writing, to stress-test whether a clinical study clears NEJM's bar — practice-changing clinical impact, methodological rigor, and generalizability. Decides NEJM vs Lancet/JAMA vs a specialty journal.

When to use

  • Deciding if NEJM is the right venue before drafting
  • Evaluating clinical importance, rigor, and generalizability
  • Choosing between NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, BMJ, and specialty journals
  • Assessing if a study clears NEJM's bar for practice-changing impact
  • Identifying fatal desk-reject triggers
  • Placing work on the significance ladder

When not to use

  • Writing or drafting the manuscript
  • Detailed study design review (referenced as next step)
  • Rationalizing narrow results as practice-changing
  • Confusing statistical significance with clinical importance
