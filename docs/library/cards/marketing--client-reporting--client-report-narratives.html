client-report-narratives

separate

Takes your raw campaign performance data and writes the executive summary paragraph that goes at the top of the report. The plain English explanation of what happened, why it happened, and what's being done about it. The part every client actually reads. Platform: Google and Meta.

When to use

  • Weekly and monthly client reporting
  • Internal stakeholder updates where you need to explain performance fast
  • QBR prep when you need narratives for multiple accounts
  • Any time you're staring at a spreadsheet and need to turn it into words
