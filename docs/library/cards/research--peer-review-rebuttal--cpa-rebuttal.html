cpa-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to reviewer reports for a 《中国行政管理》 revision. Produces the point-by-point response letter and aligned manuscript edits, with special attention to theory-frame and method-fit comments common at PA journals. 本技能服务于《中国行政管理》(Chinese Public Administration, CPA)。

When to use

  • Responding to reviewer reports for 《中国行政管理》
  • Producing point-by-point response letter
  • Producing aligned manuscript edits
  • Handling theory-frame comments
  • Handling method-fit comments
  • Revising manuscript before writing response letter

When not to use

  • Writing response letter before revising manuscript
  • Ignoring editorial office comments
  • Providing one-line generic responses
  • Writing defensive rebuttals for rejected comments
