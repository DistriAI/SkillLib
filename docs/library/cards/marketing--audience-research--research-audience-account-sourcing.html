research-audience-account-sourcing

separate

Step of the research-audience playbook: finds candidate accounts matching the fit definition, confirms gates, applies exclusions and dedupes. Run research-audience instead; not for direct use.

When to use

  • finding candidate accounts
  • confirming hard gates
  • applying exclusions
  • deduping accounts
  • searching ad libraries
  • using human-supplied lists
  • identifying lookalikes
  • noting rough universe size

When not to use

  • direct use
  • spending on people before account gates are passed
  • treating search snippets as confirmed facts
  • seeding lists with well-known brands
