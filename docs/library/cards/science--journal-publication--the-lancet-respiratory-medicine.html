the-lancet-respiratory-medicine

separate

Use when targeting The Lancet Respiratory Medicine or deciding whether a respiratory or critical-care study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the practice-changing trial and major-cohort evidence bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, Lancet specialty house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for respiratory or critical-care studies
  • re-framing trials or cohorts around international practice-changing questions
  • choosing between The Lancet Respiratory Medicine, ATS Blue Journal, and general medicine
  • checking reporting-guideline, registration, and desk-reject expectations
  • assessing randomized trials in airways, pulmonary vascular, or interstitial lung disease
  • evaluating critical-care and intensive-care trials and cohorts
  • reviewing large prospective cohorts and high-quality observational studies
  • assessing lung-cancer screening, diagnosis, and prevention studies

When not to use

  • clinical or regulatory advice
  • small single-center series
  • mechanistic or basic-science work without a clinical endpoint
  • incremental subgroup re-analyses
  • translational or broader-scope respiratory journal submissions
