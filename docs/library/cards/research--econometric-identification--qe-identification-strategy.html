qe-identification-strategy

separate

Use when the identification argument is the bottleneck for a Quantitative Economics (QE) manuscript — whether causal identification in an empirical design, parameter identification in a structural/computational model, or treatment-effect identification in an experiment. Stress-tests the strategy to the QE general-interest quantitative bar before exhibits are finalized.

When to use

  • causal identification in an empirical design
  • parameter identification in a structural/computational model
  • treatment-effect identification in an experiment
  • stress-testing identification strategy to QE bar
  • structural model parameter identification
  • empirical causal claim identification
  • experiment estimand and assumptions
  • simulation and measurement identification

When not to use

  • finalizing exhibits
  • describing design without estimation or audit
