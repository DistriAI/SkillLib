cpa-heterogeneity

separate

Use when designing or writing heterogeneity / conditionality analysis for a 《中国行政管理》(Chinese Public Administration, CPA) manuscript — variation across regions, administrative levels, and governance contexts. 当主结果只笼统适用、只切了一个维度、或审稿人要求差异化分析时使用。Enforces theory-guided cuts and significance-of-difference discipline for this Chinese-Public-Administration-Society journal.

When to use

  • designing heterogeneity analysis
  • writing conditionality analysis
  • analyzing variation across regions
  • analyzing variation across administrative levels
  • analyzing variation across governance contexts
  • addressing reviewer requests for differentiated analysis
  • systematic cross-case comparison in qualitative research

When not to use

  • using control variables with interaction terms as heterogeneity
  • interpreting results from extremely small subsamples
  • cutting dimensions without theoretical guidance
  • listing dimensions without significance or theory
  • failing to systematically compare conditions in qualitative research
  • ignoring conflicts between heterogeneity conclusions and mechanisms
