scientific-visualization

combined

Creates and audits truthful, accessible, publication-ready scientific figures with Matplotlib, Seaborn, or Plotly. Use it for figure design, multi-panel layouts, uncertainty and missing-data displays, color/contrast review, image metadata validation, and journal export planning. Meta-skill for publication-ready figures. Use when creating journal submission figures requiring multi-panel layouts, significance annotations, error bars, colorblind-safe palettes, and specific journal formatting (Nature, Science, Cell). Orchestrates matplotlib/seaborn/plotly with publication styles. For quick exploration use seaborn or plotly directly.

When to use

  • Creating plots or visualizations for scientific manuscripts
  • Preparing figures for journal submission (Nature, Science, Cell, PLOS, etc.)
  • Ensuring figures are colorblind-friendly and accessible
  • Making multi-panel figures with consistent styling
  • Exporting figures at correct resolution and format
  • Following specific publication guidelines
  • Improving existing figures to meet publication standards
  • Creating figures that need to work in both color and grayscale

When not to use

  • altering, hiding, inventing, or selectively enhancing data
  • inferring journal requirements without verification
  • claiming automated reports make figures accessible or compliant
  • silently connecting missing observations or suppressing points
  • upsampling images as if detail increased
  • tuning axes to exaggerate a conclusion
