science-communication

separate

Translating technical findings for non-technical audiences. Narrative frameworks (Pyramid Principle, SCQA), plain-language translation, executive summaries, policy briefs, causal language. Use when presenting to stakeholders or reviewing deliverables

When to use

  • presenting findings to stakeholders
  • writing executive summaries
  • writing policy briefs
  • communicating statistical results to non-statisticians
  • reviewing a draft deliverable for clarity
  • audience analysis
  • narrative structure selection
  • plain-language translation

When not to use

  • chart construction
  • encoding
  • color palettes
  • export standards
  • how to build charts
