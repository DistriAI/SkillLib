journal-of-business-economics

separate

Use when targeting 《商业经济与管理》(Journal of Business Economics — 浙江工商大学主办的"商"字特色学术月刊, 1981 创刊, 双向匿名审稿, 不收费) or deciding whether a Chinese business/management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, double-blind house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《商业经济与管理》
  • deciding if a Chinese business/management manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese business/management manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency desk-reject risks and alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to non-official or fake fee-charging websites
  • using for journals with closer reader profiles like 《农业经济问题》 or 《财经研究》
