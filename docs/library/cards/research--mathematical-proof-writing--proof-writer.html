proof-writer

authentic

Writes rigorous mathematical proofs for ML/AI theory. Use when asked to prove a theorem, lemma, proposition, or corollary, fill in missing proof steps, formalize a proof sketch, 补全证明, 写证明, 证明某个命题, or determine whether a claimed proof can actually be completed under the stated assumptions.

When to use

  • prove a theorem
  • prove a lemma
  • prove a proposition
  • prove a corollary
  • fill in missing proof steps
  • formalize a proof sketch
  • determine whether a claimed proof can be completed

When not to use

  • writing directly into paper sections
  • writing into appendix .tex files
