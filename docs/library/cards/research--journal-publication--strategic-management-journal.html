strategic-management-journal

separate

Use when targeting Strategic Management Journal (SMJ) or deciding whether a strategy / firm-performance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Strategic Management Journal (SMJ)
  • deciding whether a strategy / firm-performance manuscript fits this venue
  • competitive advantage, firm performance, corporate or business strategy
  • diversification, alliances, M&A, governance, or capabilities
  • re-framing a management paper around a strategy-theory contribution
  • identifying SMJ desk-reject risks
  • generating alternative journal lists

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • OB or general-management studies without a strategy core
  • pure strategy theory without data
  • organization theory, innovation, or computational studies
  • sociology of organizations
  • broad management empirics or meta-analysis
  • European theory-method tradition
  • international or global strategy
