scenario-product-shots

separate

Use when producing product photography with Scenario from a real product photo: e-commerce packshots on white, transparent, or brand backgrounds, lifestyle scenes placing the product in an environment, hero shots for a landing page or marketplace listing, angle and colorway sets, relighting or shadow fixes, or any shot where the label, logo, and shape must stay exact. Keywords: product photo, packshot, e-commerce, lifestyle shot, compositing, relight, label fidelity, catalog.

When to use

  • e-commerce packshots on white, transparent, or brand backgrounds
  • lifestyle scenes placing the product in an environment
  • hero shots for a landing page or marketplace listing
  • angle and colorway sets
  • relighting or shadow fixes
  • shots where label, logo, and shape must stay exact

When not to use

  • generating the product from text prompts
  • animating an approved still into an ad
