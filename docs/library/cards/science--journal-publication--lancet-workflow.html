lancet-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which lancet-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing a clinical or global-health manuscript from significance test through reviewer rebuttal for The Lancet. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which lancet-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing a clinical or global-health manuscript
  • diagnosing the current bottleneck in a draft
  • routing between trial conduct, writing, and revision
  • handling reviews and R&R decisions
  • managing statistical reviewer feedback

When not to use

  • replacing specialized lancet-* skills
  • targeting Lancet family titles like Lancet Global Health
  • targeting specialty journals like Lancet Oncology
  • targeting EClinicalMedicine
  • targeting NEJM, JAMA, or BMJ
