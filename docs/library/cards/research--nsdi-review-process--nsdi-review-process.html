nsdi-review-process

separate

Use when interpreting where an NSDI submission stands — the per-deadline PC pipeline, the three-way outcome space of accept, one-shot revision, and reject, the cross-deadline resubmission ban, reviewer continuity across revisions, and what each decision letter actually commits the authors to.

When to use

  • interpreting NSDI submission status
  • understanding per-deadline PC pipeline
  • analyzing accept, one-shot revision, and reject outcomes
  • calculating cross-deadline resubmission bans
  • assessing reviewer continuity across revisions
  • decoding decision letter commitments
  • mining signals from NSDI reviews

When not to use

  • writing NSDI papers
  • designing networked systems
  • conducting practical evaluations
  • managing HotCRP accounts
  • contacting PC members about pending papers
