nature-reviews-methods-primers

separate

Use when targeting Nature Reviews Methods Primers (Nat Rev Methods Primers) or deciding whether a cross-disciplinary methods "Primer" proposal fits this commissioned Springer Nature venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, methods-and-reproducibility bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • writing an authoritative Primer on a method used across multiple fields
  • deciding between a methods Primer and a primary methods paper
  • understanding the commissioning model
  • checking journal scope and expected Primer structure
  • identifying common reasons proposals are declined
  • assessing fit for experimental, computational, or analytical methods
  • evaluating cross-cutting methodological topics like reproducibility and benchmarking

When not to use

  • primary research
  • narrow single-lab protocols better suited to a protocols journal
  • methods of interest only within one tight sub-community
