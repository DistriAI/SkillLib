red-review-process

separate

Use when understanding or planning around the Review of Economic Dynamics (RED) editorial process — the editor desk/suitability screen, single-anonymized refereeing with a minimum of two reviewers, and RED's fast-review orientation without promising a fixed decision date. Sets expectations and timelines; it is not the submission preflight (see red-submission).
