jpart-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a public-administration project fits the Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory (JPART) rather than a sibling venue (PAR, JPAM, Governance). JPART rewards a contribution to public-management theory tested with rigor, not a stand-alone policy finding. Helps frame the question; it does not collect data.

When to use

  • Choosing among possible projects or framings for a JPART submission
  • Deciding between JPART and a sibling journal (PAR, JPAM, Governance)
  • Sharpening the question so it speaks to a public-management mechanism
  • Pressure-testing fit before investing in a project

When not to use

  • Collecting data
  • Broad, integrative, or practitioner-facing essays
  • Program evaluation or policy-analysis estimates
  • Comparative institutions or government with lighter management theory
