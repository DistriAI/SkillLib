er-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to reviewer and editorial-board reports for an 《经济研究》 (Economic Research Journal) R&R — sequencing manuscript revision before letter writing, drafting point-by-point replies in the accept / partial-accept / decline modes, prioritizing 编委 theory-positioning comments, and deciding whether new results go in the body or an appendix. 使用时机：收到《经济研究》外审或编委意见、准备修订正文并撰写逐条回复信、或多轮修改中回复与正文出现不一致时。

When to use

  • Responding to 《经济研究》 R&R
  • Sequencing manuscript revision before letter writing
  • Drafting point-by-point replies
  • Prioritizing 编委 theory-positioning comments
  • Deciding whether new results go in body or appendix
  • Handling accept, partial-accept, or decline modes
  • Creating opinion-revision comparison tables

When not to use

  • Initial manuscript submission
  • Writing the main body of the paper from scratch
  • Responding to journals other than 《经济研究》
