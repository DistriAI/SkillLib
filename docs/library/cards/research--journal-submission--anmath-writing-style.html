anmath-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing the prose and rigor of a pure-mathematics manuscript for Annals of Mathematics — eliminating gaps, removing "clearly"/"it is easy to see", precise quantifiers, and consistent mathematical English. Late-stage polish; run only after the proof and architecture are fixed.

When to use

  • polishing prose and rigor
  • eliminating logical gaps
  • removing 'clearly' and 'it is easy to see'
  • ensuring precise quantifiers
  • maintaining consistent mathematical English
  • tightening prose after proof completion

When not to use

  • fixing proof architecture
  • resolving open logical gaps
  • early-stage drafting
