jie-literature-positioning

separate

Use to position a Journal of International Economics (JIE) manuscript against the international-trade or open-economy-macro frontier — staking the contribution against gravity/structural-trade or international-finance literatures without writing a standalone survey. Sharpens positioning; it does not draft the paper.

When to use

  • Positioning JIE manuscripts against international-trade frontier
  • Positioning JIE manuscripts against open-economy-macro frontier
  • Staking contribution against gravity or structural-trade literatures
  • Staking contribution against international-finance literatures
  • Sharpening literature positioning claims
  • Building a three-column map of incumbent conversation and delta
  • Returning a ledger of claim, evidence, risk, and location
  • Comparing against sibling venues like JPubE, JDE, and JME

When not to use

  • Drafting the paper
  • Writing a standalone survey
  • Giving submission-ready advice before checking official source map
  • Positioning a trade paper only against macro work
  • Comparing to a broad genre instead of closest papers
