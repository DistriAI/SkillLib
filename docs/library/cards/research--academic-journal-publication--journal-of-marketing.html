journal-of-marketing

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Marketing (JM) or deciding whether a marketing manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Marketing
  • deciding if a marketing manuscript fits JM
  • positioning marketing-strategy papers
  • re-framing method-strong papers
  • identifying JM desk-reject risks
  • checking JM house style
  • pre-submission self-check for JM

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • method/measurement or behavioral-experiment rigor as headline
  • analytical or structural/empirical IO modeling
  • consumer-behavior theory with multi-study process evidence
  • consumer-psychology process mechanisms
  • narrower marketing-strategy / B2B / relationship work
