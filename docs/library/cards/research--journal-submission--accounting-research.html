accounting-research

separate

Use when targeting 《会计研究》(Accounting Research) — the top accounting journal of the Accounting Society of China. Apply to financial accounting, auditing, management accounting, disclosure/governance, and tax papers, emphasizing archival capital-market identification, accurate institutional/standard-setting detail, and contribution to accounting theory and practice. Distinguishes accounting framings from generic corporate finance.

When to use

  • financial accounting
  • auditing
  • management accounting
  • disclosure and governance
  • tax papers
  • archival capital-market identification
  • institutional and standard-setting detail
  • accounting theory and practice

When not to use

  • generic corporate finance
  • pure financial mechanisms
  • industrial or macro effects of tax
  • management theory of corporate governance
