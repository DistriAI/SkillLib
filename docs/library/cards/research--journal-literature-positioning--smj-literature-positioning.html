smj-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Strategic Management Journal (SMJ) manuscript within the live strategy conversation. Structures the framing and gap; it does not build the mechanism (see smj-theory-development) or frame the contribution claim (see smj-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • Positioning an SMJ manuscript
  • Structuring the framing and gap
  • Identifying the live scholarly debate
  • Organizing prior work by position or finding
  • Differentiating from closest papers
  • Checking for missing recent SMJ citations

When not to use

  • Building the mechanism
  • Framing the contribution claim
