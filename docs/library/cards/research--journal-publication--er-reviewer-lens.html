er-reviewer-lens

separate

Use when self-auditing a 《经济研究》 manuscript before submission against the high-frequency, structural reviewer criticisms recurrent at this journal.

When to use

  • Self-auditing a 《经济研究》 manuscript before submission
  • Checking for high-frequency structural reviewer criticisms
  • Systematic review after rejection to identify high-frequency pitfalls
  • Peer review among collaborators using a unified standard
  • Pre-submission self-check of theoretical positioning
  • Pre-submission self-check of identification strategy
  • Pre-submission self-check of mechanism and heterogeneity
  • Pre-submission self-check of data, robustness, and writing norms

When not to use

  • Speculating about specific reviewer preferences
  • Simulating a specific reviewer
  • Post-rejection analysis only (intended for pre-submission)
  • Surface-level fixes without addressing underlying issues
  • Ignoring theoretical positioning in favor of technical details
  • Avoiding fragile conclusions by hoping reviewers miss them
