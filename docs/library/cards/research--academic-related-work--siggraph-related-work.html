siggraph-related-work

separate

Use when writing or auditing the related-work positioning of a SIGGRAPH / TOG paper, covering the computer-graphics literature lanes (rendering, geometry, animation, simulation, imaging, fabrication, learning-for-graphics), capability-delta positioning against the strongest prior method, and correct attribution across SIGGRAPH, SIGGRAPH Asia, TOG, EG/CGF, and sibling venues.

When to use

  • writing related-work positioning
  • auditing related-work positioning
  • covering computer-graphics literature lanes
  • capability-delta positioning
  • correct attribution across venues

When not to use

  • chronological literature tour with no capability delta
  • omitting the single strongest baseline
  • attributing methods to wrong venues without checking dblp
  • treating CVPR neural-rendering line as non-existent
  • positioning against a strawman
