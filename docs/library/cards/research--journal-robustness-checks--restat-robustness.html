restat-robustness

separate

Use when the headline estimate of a The Review of Economics and Statistics (REStat) manuscript needs to survive specification, sample, measurement, and inference choices before submission. Builds the robustness suite that REStat referees expect; it does not establish the primary identification.

When to use

  • testing stability of headline estimates
  • building robustness suites for REStat
  • checking specification robustness
  • checking sample robustness
  • checking measurement robustness
  • checking identification robustness
  • checking inference robustness
  • reporting fragility of estimates

When not to use

  • establishing primary identification
