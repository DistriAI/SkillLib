respol-methods

separate

Use when research design, identification, or measurement is the bottleneck for a Research Policy (RP) manuscript — choosing and defending a method (patent/bibliometric, causal policy evaluation, survey, case study, or mixed) appropriate to an innovation-studies claim. Sets the design; it does not execute the estimation/coding (respol-data-analysis).

When to use

  • Choosing and defending a method for a Research Policy manuscript
  • Patent and bibliometric quantitative design
  • Causal policy evaluation design
  • Survey design for firm innovation behavior
  • Case study and qualitative design
  • Mixed methods design with integration logic
  • Defending constructs and measurement validity

When not to use

  • Executing estimation or coding
  • respol-data-analysis tasks
