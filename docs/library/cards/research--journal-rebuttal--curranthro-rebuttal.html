curranthro-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to a Current Anthropology (CA) decision OR writing the author's Reply to the published CA✩ Comments. CA has two distinct response tasks — a normal revise-and-resubmit letter to referees/editor before acceptance, and the public, signed Reply to invited commentators printed alongside an accepted Major Article. Structures both; it does not fabricate new evidence.

When to use

  • responding to a Current Anthropology decision
  • writing the author's Reply to published CA✩ Comments
  • planning a revise-and-resubmit letter
  • reconciling cross-subfield reviewers
  • advancing the debate in public
  • defending the core argument
  • synthesizing comments by theme
  • protecting interlocutors in public

When not to use

  • fabricating new evidence
  • treating the CA✩ Reply like a private R&R letter
  • de-anonymizing or endangering interlocutors
  • capitulating to requests that break the argument
  • ignoring commentators from non-metropolitan traditions
