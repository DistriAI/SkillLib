ecopol-identification

separate

Use when the identification argument is the bottleneck for an Economic Policy (EP) manuscript — causal identification of a policy effect, or parameter identification in a quantitative policy model. Stress-tests it to the EP bar (credible to an academic discussant, legible to a policy discussant) before exhibits are finalized.

When to use

  • causal identification of a policy effect
  • parameter identification in a quantitative policy model
  • stress-testing identification to the EP bar
  • designing empirical causal strategies
  • defending structural model parameters
  • translating design for policy readers

When not to use

  • finalizing exhibits
  • tasks where identification is not the bottleneck
