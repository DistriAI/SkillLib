dithered

separate

Dot-pattern rendering technique that simulates shades with a limited palette for nostalgic, retro, high-contrast visuals.

When to use

  • dot-pattern rendering
  • simulating shades with limited palette
  • nostalgic retro visuals
  • high-contrast artistic styles
  • design-system guideline authoring
  • component-level rules
  • accessibility requirements
  • QA checklist generation

When not to use

  • low contrast text
  • inconsistent spacing rhythm
  • decorative motion without purpose
  • ambiguous labels
  • inaccessible hit areas
