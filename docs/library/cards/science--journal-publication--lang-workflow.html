lang-workflow

separate

Use when starting or navigating any manuscript aimed at Language (Linguistic Society of America) and unsure which skill applies — it reads your stage and piece type (General Research Article, Research Report, Review Article, Discussion Note, or an online-only section: Perspectives, Phonological Analysis, Language and Public Policy, Teaching Linguistics) and routes you to the matching sub-skill. It dispatches; it does not draft analysis.

When to use

  • Starting a new Language manuscript
  • Navigating mid-project skill selection
  • Deciding between General Research Article and Research Report
  • Considering online-only sections
  • Routing based on decision letters
  • Mapping stages to specific skills

When not to use

  • Drafting analysis
