jmis-literature-positioning

separate

Use when staking a Journal of Management Information Systems (JMIS) manuscript's contribution against the IS literature and the basket siblings — showing it advances an IS-management/economics conversation rather than only a reference discipline. Positions the work; it does not build the mechanism (jmis-theory-development) or write the contribution statement (jmis-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • staking a JMIS manuscript's contribution against the IS literature
  • positioning work to show it advances an IS-management/economics conversation
  • naming the specific IS conversation and its current frontier
  • locating the gap as a tension rather than an absence
  • stating the delta in one sentence
  • engaging the IS basket and citing right precedents
  • using sibling boundaries to sharpen positioning
  • reframing absence into a tension

When not to use

  • building the mechanism
  • writing the contribution statement
