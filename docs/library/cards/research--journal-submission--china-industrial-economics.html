china-industrial-economics

separate

Use when targeting 《中国工业经济》(China Industrial Economics) — a top journal for industrial organization, firm behavior, regional/innovation/digital economy with empirical rigor and strong policy relevance. Apply when a paper needs exhaustive robustness, clean China-policy quasi-experiments, mechanism + heterogeneity, and actionable industrial-policy implications. Famous for methodological rigor norms.

When to use

  • industrial organization
  • firm behavior
  • innovation and R&D
  • digital economy
  • regional economy
  • environmental regulation
  • China policy quasi-experiments
  • exhaustive robustness checks

When not to use

  • theoretical contributions higher than industrial scenarios
  • management theory, case studies, or surveys
  • financial mechanisms as the main focus
  • papers where other journals' readership is a closer fit
