smr-derivation-and-properties

separate

Use when stating assumptions, identification, and analytical properties (bias, consistency, efficiency, asymptotics, validity conditions) of a method in a Sociological Methods & Research (SMR) paper. Audits the theory and proof economy; does not design the Monte Carlo or the real-data illustration.

When to use

  • stating assumptions
  • identification
  • analytical properties
  • bias
  • consistency
  • efficiency
  • asymptotics
  • validity conditions

When not to use

  • design the Monte Carlo
  • design the real-data illustration
