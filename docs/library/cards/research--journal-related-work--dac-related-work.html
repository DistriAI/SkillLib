dac-related-work

separate

Use when writing or auditing the related-work and positioning of an ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference (DAC) Research Manuscript, covering the EDA literature lanes (DAC/ICCAD/DATE/ASP-DAC/TCAD/TODAES), delta-first positioning against the strongest prior tool, honest benchmark-suite lineage, and third-person self-citation under double-blind review within the six-page budget.

When to use

  • Writing related work for DAC Research Manuscripts
  • Auditing related work positioning
  • Covering EDA literature lanes (DAC/ICCAD/DATE/ASP-DAC/TCAD/TODAES)
  • Delta-first positioning against strongest prior tool
  • Honest benchmark-suite lineage
  • Third-person self-citation under double-blind review
  • Fitting related work into six-page budget

When not to use

  • Writing a standalone survey
  • Padding with distant literature lanes
