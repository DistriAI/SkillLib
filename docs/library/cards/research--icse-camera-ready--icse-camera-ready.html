icse-camera-ready

separate

Use when converting an accepted ICSE research-track paper into its final published form, covering the extra camera-ready page, de-anonymization order, finalizing the Data Availability section with archival DOIs, IEEE-template production checks, and conference-presentation obligations to verify.

When to use

  • converting accepted ICSE research-track paper to final published form
  • managing the extra camera-ready page
  • de-anonymization order and systematic sweep
  • finalizing Data Availability section with archival DOIs
  • IEEE-template production checks
  • verifying conference-presentation obligations
  • maintaining consistency with the accepted version
  • publication-week hygiene and preprint posting

When not to use

  • adding new contributions that never faced review
  • quiet second revisions without a change log
  • silent number changes without flagging to track chairs
