short-form-pipeline

separate

Extracts viral short-form video clips from long-form YouTube videos by handling download, transcription, AI segmentation, cutting, vertical cropping, and caption burn-in.

When to use

  • Extracting short-form clips from YouTube videos
  • Converting 16:9 video to 9:16 vertical format
  • Burning in word-highlighted captions
  • AI segmentation of video moments
  • Face-detected smart cropping
  • Heuristic clip scoring without Claude

When not to use

  • Long-form video editing
  • Audio-only processing
  • Image generation
  • Text-only content creation
