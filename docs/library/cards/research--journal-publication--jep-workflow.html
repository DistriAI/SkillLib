jep-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jep-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing work on an accessible synthesis article from topic idea through editor revision for a Journal of Economic Perspectives (JEP) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which jep-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing work on an accessible synthesis article
  • Routing from topic idea through editor revision
  • Handling JEP proposal or symposium pitches
  • Addressing accessibility, balance, or narrative issues
  • Managing editor strategy or submission preflight

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized jep-* skills
  • Primary research or identification strategy work
  • Replication package creation
  • JEL survey writing
  • Writing full unsolicited manuscripts before pitching
