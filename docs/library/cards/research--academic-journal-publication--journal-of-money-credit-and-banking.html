journal-of-money-credit-and-banking

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Money, Credit and Banking (JMCB) or deciding whether a money / banking / monetary-policy / macro-finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Money, Credit and Banking
  • deciding venue fit for money, banking, monetary-policy, or macro-finance manuscripts
  • re-framing empirical-macro or banking papers for money/credit mechanism and policy relevance
  • identifying JMCB desk-reject risks
  • providing credible monetary, banking, or macro-finance alternative venue lists
  • checking JMCB method and evidence bar
  • reviewing JMCB structure and house style
  • performing official-submission re-checks for JMCB

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • general macro or corporate-finance papers with no money/credit/banking core
  • field-leading quantitative macro and monetary mechanism papers
  • general-interest macro papers
  • macro-finance asset pricing and intermediation at the finance top-3
  • long-run growth and development papers
  • international monetary and capital flows papers
  • quantitative dynamics papers
