YouTube channel business email and contact extractor: accepts a channel id (UCxxx), handle (@name), or URL; navigates the channel About view; extracts the business email from the description text plus full channel metadata (name, id, country, subscriber count, view count, video count, joined date, external links classified as social/aggregator/personal). When the description has no email, follows non-social outbound links (personal site, business site, link aggregator) and scans those pages for an email so creators who place their inquiry email on their own site are still covered.
