cie-heterogeneity

separate

Use when the heterogeneity analysis of a 《中国工业经济》 (China Industrial Economics) manuscript is thin — only one crude cut (e.g. east/central/west). Pushes multi-dimensional cuts (ownership, size, industry, factor intensity, market structure, region) each tied to an economic interpretation, not just a significant subsample.

When to use

  • heterogeneity analysis of China Industrial Economics manuscript
  • multi-dimensional cuts (ownership, size, industry, factor intensity, market structure, region)
  • economic interpretation of subsample effects
  • testing significance of between-group coefficient differences
  • linking heterogeneity to mechanism and policy implications

When not to use

  • only one crude cut (e.g. east/central/west)
  • cutting dimensions without explaining why one group is larger/smaller
  • listing subsample regressions without clear meaning
  • cutting dimensions unrelated to mechanism purely for robustness
