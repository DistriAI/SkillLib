aaag-data-analysis

separate

Use when running and reporting the analysis for an Annals of the American Association of Geographers manuscript — spatial statistics and modeling, remote-sensing accuracy, or qualitative coding and interpretation. Sets analysis and reporting norms across the four areas; it does not choose the design.

When to use

  • Estimating models
  • Running spatial statistics
  • Classifying imagery
  • Coding qualitative material
  • Preparing the results section
  • Reporting uncertainty and robustness
  • Addressing reviewer questions on spatial autocorrelation or accuracy

When not to use

  • Choosing the research design
