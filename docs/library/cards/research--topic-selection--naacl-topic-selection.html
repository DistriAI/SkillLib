naacl-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a project should target NAACL rather than ACL, EMNLP, EACL, AACL, LREC-COLING, TACL, or an ML venue — weighing the chapter's Nations-of-the-Americas identity, theme tracks, timing across skipped years, and whether the paper's audience actually convenes at this conference.

When to use

  • deciding between NAACL and ACL, EMNLP, EACL, AACL, LREC-COLING, TACL, or ML venues
  • weighing the Nations-of-the-Americas identity
  • evaluating theme tracks
  • checking timing across skipped years
  • determining if the paper's audience convenes at NAACL
  • routing among near-identical siblings
  • applying the three-question fit test
  • shaping the project once NAACL is chosen

When not to use

  • assessing if a paper is good enough for NAACL based on rigor alone
  • choosing NAACL for prestige-ranking reasons
  • sitting on finished work for a year for NAACL
  • framing translated-benchmark studies as Americas-languages papers
