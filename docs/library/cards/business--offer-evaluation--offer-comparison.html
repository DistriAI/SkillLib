offer-comparison

authentic

Compare two or more job offers as total-comp curves over four years — vesting cliffs, bonuses, 401(k) match, and the crossover year computed, not vibed. Use when asked to compare job offers, which offer pays more over time, model my equity vesting, or is the startup offer actually worth it. Produces a year-by-year and cumulative comp table per offer, the crossover analysis, and negotiation levers ranked by dollar impact.

When to use

  • compare job offers
  • which offer pays more over time
  • model my equity vesting
  • is the startup offer actually worth it

When not to use

  • financial advice
  • comparing risky equity dollar to a salary dollar 1:1
  • hiding the vesting cliff inside annual averages
  • modeling unwritten refreshers as income
  • declaring a winner without naming what assumption the win depends on
  • presenting the model's output without its assumptions attached
