kai-strategic-decision

separate

CEO/executive-mode strategic decision making. Challenge premises, diagnose problems, design definitive strategies. Four modes: AGGRESSIVE (dream big), SELECTIVE (hold baseline + cherry-pick expansions), DIAGNOSTIC (maximum rigor), VALIDATION (strip to essentials). Use when founders, executives, or product leaders need strategic decisions on product, growth, market, technology, or resource allocation.

When to use

  • strategic decisions on product
  • strategic decisions on growth
  • strategic decisions on market
  • strategic decisions on technology
  • strategic decisions on resource allocation
  • challenging premises
  • diagnosing problems
  • designing definitive strategies

When not to use

  • rubber-stamping decisions
  • providing a menu of options without a clear path
  • silently adding or removing scope
  • drifting toward a different mode after selection
