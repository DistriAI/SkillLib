ieee-transactions-on-robotics

separate

Use when targeting IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO) or deciding whether a robotics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the complete-and-experimentally-validated-contribution bar, real-robot evidence rigor, the T-RO-vs-IJRR routing, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • T-RO fit and framing check
  • Re-framing simulation results to real-robot validation
  • Choosing between T-RO and IJRR
  • T-RO experimental-validation bar
  • Desk-reject heuristics
  • T-RO house style and structure
  • Official-submission re-check
  • Pre-submission self-check

When not to use

  • Replacing the journal's current official author information
