lancet-research-in-context

separate

Use to build The Lancet's signature Research in context panel — the three mandatory headed parts (Evidence before this study, Added value of this study, Implications of all the available evidence), grounded in a systematic literature search with stated databases, terms, and dates. Mandatory for original research Articles.

When to use

  • Writing original research Articles for The Lancet
  • Creating the Research in context panel
  • Documenting systematic literature searches
  • Updating panels with new evidence during review

When not to use

  • Writing discussion paragraphs
  • Cherry-picked introductions
  • Non-original research articles
