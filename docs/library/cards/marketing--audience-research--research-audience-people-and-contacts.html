research-audience-people-and-contacts

separate

For a list of companies, picks 1-3 current people per company in different buying roles, confirms each title with a dated source, finds a public contact route, labels email status honestly and flags regions with contact rules. Never contacts anyone. Use alone when you already have a vetted company list and the buyer titles; otherwise run research-audience.

When to use

  • picking 1-3 current people per company
  • confirming titles with dated sources
  • finding public contact routes
  • labeling email status honestly
  • flagging regions with contact rules
  • suppression check for people

When not to use

  • contacting anyone
  • using personal webmail or role addresses for companies with named buyers
  • using anything behind a login
  • labeling an address verified without a checker
