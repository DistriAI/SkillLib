jue-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jue-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal for a Journal of Urban Economics (JUE) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which jue-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal
  • diagnosing current spatial-economics bottleneck
  • switching into revision mode after a decision letter
  • deciding between JUE and sibling journals

When not to use

  • replacing specialized skills
  • treating JUE as interchangeable with RSUE, Journal of Regional Science, JEG, or JPubE
  • polishing exhibits or prose while spatial identification is moving
  • deferring replication folder when data is geocoded or restricted
  • presenting national-level results with no urban mechanism
