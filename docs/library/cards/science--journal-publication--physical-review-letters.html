physical-review-letters

separate

Use when targeting Physical Review Letters (PRL) or deciding whether a physics manuscript fits this Letter-format, broad-importance venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting PRL for a concise result with claimed broad physics significance
  • deciding between PRL and a Physical Review subfield journal after rejection from Nature/Science
  • assessing if a result clears PRL's cross-subfield-significance bar
  • applying PRL's length-gate and significance heuristics before beginning a manuscript
  • evaluating fundamental results across all subfields of physics with broad implications
  • reviewing experimental discoveries or precise measurements with broad physics relevance
  • assessing theoretical results introducing new physical concepts or exact solutions
  • evaluating cross-disciplinary physics where physics insight is primary

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • work-in-progress or preliminary reports to be followed by a longer paper
  • condensed-matter papers relevant only to condensed-matter readers
  • narrower QIS focus where prx-quantum may be more appropriate
