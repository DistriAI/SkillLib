jme-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Monetary Economics (JME) manuscript against the monetary-economics and macroeconomics frontier — staking the contribution against monetary-policy, business-cycle, DSGE, intermediation, and expectations literatures without writing a standalone survey.

When to use

  • Positioning JME manuscripts against monetary-policy, business-cycle, DSGE, intermediation, and expectations literatures
  • Clarifying fuzzy or oversold contributions relative to prior work
  • Pre-empting 'already known' objections from reviewers
  • Distinguishing empirical vs. theoretical contributions
  • Building a three-column map of incumbent conversation, unresolved tension, and manuscript delta
  • Comparing against sibling venues like JIE, RED, and AEJ Macro
  • Ensuring literature discussion is lean for the 40-page limit

When not to use

  • Writing a standalone survey
  • Polishing format before checking official source maps
  • Giving submission-ready advice before checking volatile rules
