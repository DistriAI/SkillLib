io-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning an International Organization (IO) manuscript against the international-relations literature so it reads as a contribution to an IR debate. IO readers span rationalist, constructivist, institutionalist, IPE, and security traditions, so the paper must engage the IR conversation it speaks to and the closest prior IR work — while staying double-blind. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction and the 'contribution' paragraph
  • Addressing reviewer feedback about missing key IR work or not engaging the debate
  • Connecting a solid case to a general IR argument
  • Distinguishing the contribution from the closest prior IR papers
  • Positioning the paper inside a recognizable IR debate
  • Engaging across paradigms such as rationalist, constructivist, and institutionalist
  • Pre-empting the strongest IR objection

When not to use

  • Writing the lit review
  • Creating a citation pile
  • Treating a domestic-politics literature as the home debate
  • Self-citation that breaks anonymity
