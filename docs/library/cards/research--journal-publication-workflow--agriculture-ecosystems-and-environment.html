agriculture-ecosystems-and-environment

separate

Use when targeting Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment or deciding whether an agroecosystem-environment manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the field-to-landscape environmental-outcome bar, data-reporting expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for an agriculture–environment paper
  • framing management or land-use study around environmental outcome
  • choosing between AGEE, field-crops-research, and soil-biology-and-biochemistry
  • AGEE desk-reject heuristics and alternative route
  • greenhouse-gas and nutrient fluxes from agroecosystems
  • biodiversity and ecosystem services in agricultural landscapes
  • soil and water quality outcomes of agricultural management
  • land-use and management effects on agroecosystem function

When not to use

  • pure crop-yield/agronomy study with no environmental outcome
  • soil-process mechanism study with no agroecosystem/management framing
  • replacing the journal's current author guidelines
