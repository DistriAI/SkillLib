energy-scheduling

authentic

Schedule work by energy, not just time — the week of self-observation that maps your real peaks and troughs, the work-to-energy matching (hard creative work on peaks, admin on slopes, meetings on shoulders), and the calendar rebuild that honors the map. Use when asked when should I do my hardest work, I waste my best hours on email, map my energy levels, or why is 3pm always useless. Produces the observation protocol, the personal energy map, the work-type matching, and the rebuilt week.

When to use

  • mapping real energy peaks and troughs
  • matching hard creative work to energy peaks
  • scheduling admin tasks during energy troughs
  • rebuilding the weekly calendar based on energy maps
  • identifying when to do the hardest work
  • optimizing work-to-energy matching

When not to use

  • scheduling by assumed energy curves without observation
  • spending peak hours on email or arrival-order tasks
  • scheduling hard work during energy troughs
  • faking a chronotype to fit a specific schedule
