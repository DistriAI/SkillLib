ugc-ad-remix

separate

Turn a proven UGC video ad into a shot-by-shot script for your own product. Use when the user shares a TikTok, Reels or Shorts ad (link or transcript) and wants a version for their product, asks to "remix", "rewrite" or "copy the structure of" an ad, or needs a UGC script that is ready to film. Uses the free UGCfy AI MCP when it is connected; works from pasted ad text without it.
