modern-economic-science

separate

Use when targeting 《当代经济科学》(Modern Economic Science — 教育部主管、西安交通大学主办的综合性经济学双月刊, 1979 年创刊, 双向匿名审稿、不收版面费) or deciding whether a Chinese econ/finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, anonymous-review/fee-free policy, abstract/keyword house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《当代经济科学》
  • deciding if a Chinese econ/finance manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a generic Chinese econ/finance manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency desk-reject risks and alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to management-world
  • submitting to modern-economic-research
  • submitting to modern-finance-and-economics
  • submitting to modern-financial-research
  • submitting to studies-of-financial-economics
