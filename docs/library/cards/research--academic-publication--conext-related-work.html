conext-related-work

separate

Use when writing the related-work and positioning of an ACM CoNEXT paper — covering the networking literature lanes (SIGCOMM, NSDI, IMC, SIGMETRICS, MobiCom, CCR), delta-first positioning against the venue's own recent programs, and keeping self-citations double-anonymous.

When to use

  • writing related-work for ACM CoNEXT
  • positioning against networking literature lanes
  • delta-first positioning against recent programs
  • maintaining double-anonymous self-citations

When not to use

  • writing literature dumps without delta arguments
  • using first-person self-citations
  • ignoring the specific venue lane for the paper
