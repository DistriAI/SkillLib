isca-author-response

separate

Use when ISCA reviews arrive and the rebuttal-plus-revision window opens — triaging objections by what a three-week window can actually fix, deciding between textual rebuttal and a revised PDF with new experiments, structuring responses that arm a champion at the PC meeting, and avoiding window-burning mistakes.

When to use

  • triaging objections by what a three-week window can actually fix
  • deciding between textual rebuttal and a revised PDF with new experiments
  • structuring responses that arm a champion at the PC meeting
  • avoiding window-burning mistakes
  • classifying review points into F, E, E∞, D, P, and T classes
  • freezing the experiment plan for high-leverage objections
  • writing the response document for committee reading
  • using the revision half of the window for surgical edits

When not to use

  • new contributions beyond the paper's claimed scope
  • rebutting every sentence in a review
  • premise litigation at length
  • simulator relitigating
  • restructuring the paper
  • spending the window composing indignation
