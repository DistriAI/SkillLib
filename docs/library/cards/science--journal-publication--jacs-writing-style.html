jacs-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing prose to ACS house style and claim discipline for a Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS) manuscript. Edits language, structure, and nomenclature — it does not add data or design figures.

When to use

  • polishing prose to ACS house style
  • editing language, structure, and nomenclature
  • fixing wordy or over-claimed prose
  • correcting inconsistent nomenclature, units, or abbreviations
  • converting citations to ACS-numbered style
  • replacing boosters with facts or numbers
  • calibrating hedges to evidence
  • ensuring consistent compound numbering

When not to use

  • adding data
  • designing figures
