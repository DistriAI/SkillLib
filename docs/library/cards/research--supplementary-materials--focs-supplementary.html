focs-supplementary

separate

Use when architecting everything after page ten of a FOCS (IEEE Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science) submission — a venue with no separate supplement channel where the paper itself continues past the guaranteed-read window — covering body organization, proof placement, and navigation for the discretionary reader.

When to use

  • FOCS submission body organization
  • Proof placement after page 10
  • Navigation for discretionary readers
  • Managing the window boundary
  • Pointer discipline for proofs
  • Length judgment for full versions
  • Mechanics for long document navigation

When not to use

  • Writing the first ten pages
  • Abstract and reference formatting
  • Journal version expansion
  • Supplementary material upload
