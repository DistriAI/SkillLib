jeg-identification-strategy

separate

Use when the inferential backbone of a Journal of Economic Growth (JEG) manuscript needs stress-testing — empirical papers via causal/econometric identification for growth, theory papers via assumptions, results, proof exposition, and generality. Forks by paper type, as a specialist growth and dynamic-macroeconomics outlet requires.

When to use

  • empirical growth claims with endogenous regressors
  • theoretical results with unjustified assumptions
  • cross-country or dynamic-panel growth regressions
  • clean causal shock designs like IV, DID, or RDD
  • theory paper assumption justification and proof exposition
  • persistence-design defenses for historical determinants

When not to use

  • tasks not involving Journal of Economic Growth manuscripts
  • papers that do not require stress-testing of inferential backbones
