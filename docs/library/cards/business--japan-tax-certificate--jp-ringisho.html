jp-ringisho

authentic

Use when asked 稟議書の書き方, write a ringi approval request in Japanese, 稟議書を作って, how to get a purchase or project approved in a Japanese company, or 帮我写一份日本公司的稟議書. Produces a finished 稟議書 in standard Japanese corporate structure and polite register: purpose, background, proposal, cost and its basis, alternatives compared, expected effect, risks, approval route, and attachments, plus the questions approvers are likely to ask.

When to use

  • writing a ringi approval request in Japanese
  • creating a 稟議書 for purchase, contract, hire, or project
  • explaining how to get a purchase or project approved in a Japanese company
  • drafting a Japanese corporate approval document with cost and alternatives

When not to use

  • writing in English unless requested alongside Japanese
  • inventing figures or quotes
  • using overly humble filler or padding
