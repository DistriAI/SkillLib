jegeo-tables-figures

separate

Use when exhibits — especially maps — for a Journal of Economic Geography (JEG) manuscript are dense, decorative, or do not answer the spatial question. Makes maps and tables argue; it does not invent results.

When to use

  • Maps that are pretty but make no argument
  • Key spatial patterns in coefficient tables
  • Dense regression tables with significance asterisks
  • Exhibits where geography and magnitudes are unclear
  • Choropleth classification and projection choices
  • Event-study and gradient plots for spatial DID
  • Spatial-lag/SDM direct and indirect effects
  • JEG house style for tables and figures

When not to use

  • Inventing results
