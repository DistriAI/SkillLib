jim-topic-selection

separate

Use when choosing or auditing a research question for a Journal of International Marketing (JIM) manuscript — testing whether the international dimension is the theoretical core and whether JIM beats sibling outlets. It vets the question; jim-theory-development builds the mechanism.

When to use

  • Choosing a research question for JIM
  • Auditing a research question for JIM
  • Testing if the international dimension is the theoretical core
  • Deciding between JIM and sibling outlets
  • Vetting if a domestic finding extends to new markets
  • Evaluating cross-national variation as a moderator
  • Assessing cross-border phenomena as the object
  • Evaluating global-local tension questions

When not to use

  • Building the mechanism
  • International business questions without marketing focus
  • New-context replication without theoretical difference
  • Choosing countries by data convenience
  • Framing international business questions with thin marketing veneer
