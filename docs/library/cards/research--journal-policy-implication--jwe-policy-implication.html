jwe-policy-implication

separate

Use when writing the policy-implication section of a 《世界经济》 (The Journal of World Economy) manuscript — pitching at the opening-up policy level (trade, exchange rate, capital account, industrial opening) rather than as a domestic operational to-do list. Use after results, channels, and heterogeneity are settled.

When to use

  • Writing policy-implication sections for 《世界经济》
  • Aligning policy recommendations with opening-up policy tools
  • Deriving policy implications from identified mechanisms and boundaries
  • Distinguishing conclusion layer from policy layer
  • Checking for domestic operational list anti-patterns

When not to use

  • Writing domestic operational to-do lists
  • Providing unconditional policy calls without boundary conditions
  • Writing local government operation manuals
  • Extrapolating partial equilibrium results to global welfare conclusions without GE context
