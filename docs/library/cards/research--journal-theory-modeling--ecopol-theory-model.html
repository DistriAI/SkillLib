ecopol-theory-model

separate

Use when building or tightening the conceptual frame or quantitative model that disciplines an Economic Policy (EP) manuscript's policy logic. Keeps the model minimal-but-honest and legible to a policy audience; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • building conceptual frame
  • tightening quantitative model
  • disciplining policy logic
  • mapping estimates into welfare or policy-cost statement
  • clarifying opaque mechanisms
  • reducing model elaboration
  • showing counterfactual policy sensitivity
  • providing welfare interpretation

When not to use

  • inventing evidence
  • inventing citations
