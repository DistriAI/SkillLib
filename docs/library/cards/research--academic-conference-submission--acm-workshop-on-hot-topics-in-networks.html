acm-workshop-on-hot-topics-in-networks

separate

Use when targeting ACM Workshop on Hot Topics in Networks (HotNets) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for networking workshop.

When to use

  • targeting ACM Workshop on Hot Topics in Networks
  • deciding whether a manuscript fits HotNets
  • conference fit read for early-stage networking ideas
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style into conference narrative
  • diagnosing evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, or rebuttal issues
  • routing decisions between HotNets, SIGMETRICS, HPDC, and WiSec

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • manuscripts where central contribution is not genuinely in networking workshop
  • measurement or modeling methodology contributions (route to SIGMETRICS)
  • distributed/HPC runtime or scheduling at scale contributions (route to HPDC)
