global-biogeochemical-cycles

separate

Use when targeting Global Biogeochemical Cycles (GBC) or deciding whether a biogeochemical-cycling manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's system-scale fluxes/budgets/feedbacks fit, the quantitative biogeochemistry evidence bar, AGU FAIR data-deposition expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a biogeochemical-cycling, flux/budget, or feedback paper
  • re-framing a local flux measurement or experiment into a system-scale budget, feedback, or generalizable biogeochemical mechanism
  • choosing between GBC, soil-biology-and-biochemistry, and water-resources-research
  • GBC's quantitative system-scale bar and AGU FAIR data-deposition expectations
  • Carbon cycle: land and ocean carbon uptake, storage, and exchange; atmospheric CO2/CH4 budgets and their drivers
  • Nutrient and element cycles: nitrogen, phosphorus, iron, silicon, sulfur, and trace elements, and their coupling to carbon
  • Land–atmosphere and ocean–atmosphere biogeochemical exchange, including greenhouse-gas and reactive-gas fluxes
  • Marine biogeochemistry: productivity, export, remineralization, and ocean biogeochemical–physical coupling

When not to use

  • site-level process measurement of interest mainly to one community
  • descriptive local incubation
  • single-station time series
  • ecological/soil study with no implication for an element budget or earth-system feedback
  • site-level flux measurement or incubation with no system-scale budget or feedback implication
