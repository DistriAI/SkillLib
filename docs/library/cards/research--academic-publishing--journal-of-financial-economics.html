journal-of-financial-economics

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Financial Economics (JFE) or deciding whether a finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Financial Economics
  • deciding if a finance manuscript fits JFE
  • choosing among JF, JFE, or RFS
  • re-framing applied financial data papers
  • assessing JFE desk-reject risks
  • checking JFE method and evidence bar
  • reviewing JFE house style and structure
  • pre-submission self-check for JFE

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • accounting papers framed as finance
  • economics papers framed as finance
  • pure quantitative or derivatives theory
  • econometrics-of-finance methods
  • macro-finance topics
