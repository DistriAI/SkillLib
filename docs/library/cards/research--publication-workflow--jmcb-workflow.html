jmcb-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jmcb-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal for a Journal of Money, Credit and Banking (JMCB) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which jmcb-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work for JMCB submission
  • diagnosing current manuscript bottlenecks
  • switching into revision mode after a decision letter
  • weighing JMCB against sibling journals

When not to use

  • replacing specialized jmcb-* skills
  • routing pure corporate-finance or asset-pricing papers
  • treating JMCB as interchangeable with JME or AEJ:Macro
