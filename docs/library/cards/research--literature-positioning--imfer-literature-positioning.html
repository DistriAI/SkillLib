imfer-literature-positioning

separate

Use when the contribution of an IMF Economic Review (IMFER) manuscript relative to the international-macro frontier and the sibling journals is unclear or undersold. Stakes the claim against JIE/JIMF/JMCB and the IMF research stream; it does not select the topic (imfer-topic-selection) or design identification (imfer-identification).

When to use

  • Clarifying contribution relative to international-macro frontier
  • Positioning against JIE, JIMF, and JMCB
  • Placing paper in IMF research stream
  • Writing a one-sentence delta over closest prior work
  • Distinguishing empirical, methodological, or policy-design contributions
  • Pre-empting 'this is JIE/JIMF' reviewer reflexes

When not to use

  • Selecting the topic
  • Designing identification
