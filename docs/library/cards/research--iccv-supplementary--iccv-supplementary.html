iccv-supplementary

separate

Use when planning or auditing ICCV supplementary material under the same-day paper-plus-supplement deadline, covering what moves out of the 8-page body, parallel production of videos and code from February onward, double-blind hygiene inside the archive, and making depth material navigable for reviewers who choose to open it.

When to use

  • Planning ICCV supplementary material
  • Auditing ICCV supplementary material
  • Managing same-day paper and supplement deadlines
  • Dividing content between 8-page body and supplement
  • Producing videos and code in parallel
  • Ensuring double-blind hygiene in archives
  • Making depth material navigable for reviewers
  • Structuring supplement archives for quick review

When not to use

  • Writing the main 8-page paper body
  • Managing non-ICCV conference submissions
  • Handling post-deadline packaging weeks
