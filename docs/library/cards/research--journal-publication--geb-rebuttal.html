separate

Use after a Games and Economic Behavior revise-and-resubmit to organize responses on theory novelty, equilibrium/proof details, experiments, simulations, Editor-in-Charge priorities, and Elsevier revision files.

When to use

  • GEB revision decision response
  • Theory novelty and positioning
  • Equilibrium and proof details
  • Experiment and simulation design
  • Editor-in-Charge priorities
  • Elsevier revision files
  • Response letter organization
  • Manuscript revision mechanics

When not to use

  • Initial manuscript submission
  • Non-GEB journal submissions
  • General academic writing advice
  • Data collection design
  • Literature review generation
