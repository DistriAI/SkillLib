research-refine-pipeline

authentic

Run an end-to-end workflow that chains `research-refine` and `experiment-plan`. Use when the user wants a one-shot pipeline from vague research direction to focused final proposal plus detailed experiment roadmap, or asks to "串起来", build a pipeline, do it end-to-end, or generate both the method and experiment plan together.

When to use

  • one-shot pipeline from vague research direction to focused final proposal
  • generate both the method and experiment plan together
  • build a pipeline
  • do it end-to-end
  • 串起来

When not to use

  • planning a large experiment suite on top of an unstable method
  • stopping at a refined method without experiment planning
