nsdi-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a project belongs at NSDI, picking among its research, operational-systems, and frontiers tracks, or re-routing to SIGCOMM, OSDI, SOSP, FAST, or SIGMETRICS — testing the networking-stack contribution, the CFP's out-of-scope lines, and which of the two yearly deadlines to aim at.

When to use

  • judging whether a project belongs at NSDI
  • picking among research, operational-systems, and frontiers tracks
  • re-routing to SIGCOMM, OSDI, SOSP, FAST, or SIGMETRICS
  • testing the networking-stack contribution
  • checking the CFP's out-of-scope lines
  • determining which of the two yearly deadlines to aim at

When not to use

  • work with no contribution to the design of networked systems or the networking stack
  • hardware architecture and physical-layer contributions
  • sensing and localization work
