tar-methods

separate

Use when the research design and identification strategy are the bottleneck for a The Accounting Review (TAR) manuscript — choosing the setting, shock, and design that credibly identify an accounting effect, or structuring an analytical model or experiment. Designs the study; it does not run the estimation and robustness (tar-data-analysis) or frame the contribution (tar-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • Choosing the setting, shock, and design for credible identification
  • Structuring an analytical model
  • Designing an experiment to isolate a channel
  • Defending a DiD design for staggered adoption
  • Fixing primitives and solution concept for analytical models
  • Selecting identification strategies for archival accounting

When not to use

  • Running the estimation and robustness
  • Framing the contribution
