jm-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Marketing (JM) manuscript in the literature — joining a substantive marketing conversation and defending against the "new-context replication" rejection. Positions the paper; it does not build the mechanism (jm-theory-development) or write the contribution statement (jm-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • Positioning a JM manuscript in the literature
  • Joining a substantive marketing conversation
  • Defending against 'new-context replication' rejection
  • Rewriting gap-spotting front ends
  • Naming the substantive conversation
  • Stating prevailing understanding and its limits
  • Citing the substantive canon
  • Bridging to managerial or societal relevance

When not to use

  • Building the mechanism
  • Writing the contribution statement
