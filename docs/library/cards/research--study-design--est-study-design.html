est-study-design

separate

Use when designing experiments, sampling campaigns, or modeling studies for Environmental Science & Technology (ES&T) so the design survives expert review — environmental relevance, controls, replication, mass/energy balances, and a QA/QC plan built in from the start. It guides design decisions; it does not run the study.

When to use

  • Planning a lab/mesocosm/field study
  • Planning a sampling campaign
  • Planning a modeling experiment
  • Choosing concentrations, matrices, controls, replicates, and endpoints
  • Setting up the QA/QC and mass/energy-balance plan
  • Addressing reviewer questions on environmental relevance, controls, or replication

When not to use

  • Running the study
  • Execution of the study
  • Reporting of results
