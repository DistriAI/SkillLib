journal-of-urban-economics

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Urban Economics (JUE) or deciding whether an urban-, regional-, or spatial-economics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Urban Economics
  • deciding if urban, regional, or spatial-economics manuscript fits
  • re-framing regional or local-policy paper as spatial economics
  • assessing JUE desk-reject risks
  • checking JUE method and evidence bar
  • reviewing JUE house style and structure
  • verifying official submission checklist items

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • general labor or public economics with no genuine spatial contribution
  • top-5 general-interest results
  • broad applied micro without spatial focus
