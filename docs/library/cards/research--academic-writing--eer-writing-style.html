eer-writing-style

separate

Use when revising prose for a European Economic Review (EER) manuscript — the abstract, introduction arc, research highlights, and Elsevier house conventions. Makes the general-interest idea land; it does not establish identification, theory, or exhibits.

When to use

  • Revising EER manuscript prose
  • Structuring the introduction arc
  • Writing result-forward abstracts
  • Creating research highlights
  • Formatting JEL codes and keywords
  • Reporting numbers with standard errors
  • Ensuring generalist legibility

When not to use

  • Establishing identification
  • Developing theory
  • Creating exhibits
