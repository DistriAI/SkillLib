cn-5s-audit

authentic

Use when asked 5S 检查表, 6S 管理, 现场管理检查, 5S 评分标准, 车间 6S 稽核, 做一份 5S 推行计划, or design a 5S or 6S audit for a workshop, warehouse or office in a Chinese factory. Produces an audit checklist for the area with scored criteria for 整理, 整顿, 清扫, 清洁, 素养 (and 安全 for 6S), a scoring guide with photo evidence rules, a results summary and red-tag list, a corrective-action tracker with owners and dates, and an audit rota, in Chinese.

When to use

  • 5S 检查表
  • 6S 管理
  • 现场管理检查
  • 5S 评分标准
  • 车间 6S 稽核
  • 做一份 5S 推行计划
  • design a 5S or 6S audit for a workshop, warehouse or office in a Chinese factory

When not to use

  • legal safety obligations and risk assessments under the 安全生产法
  • handling serious hazards found in an audit
