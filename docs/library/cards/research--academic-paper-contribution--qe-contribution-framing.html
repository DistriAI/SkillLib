qe-contribution-framing

separate

Use to sharpen the one-sentence contribution of a Quantitative Economics (QE) manuscript so a general-interest Econometric Society reader sees the quantitative payoff immediately. Frames the claim and its scope; it does not redo estimation or write full prose.

When to use

  • Sharpening one-sentence contribution
  • Framing quantitative answers
  • Defining claim scope
  • Matching framing to paper type
  • Attaching uncertainty to claims
  • Distinguishing QE from Econometrica fit

When not to use

  • Redoing estimation
  • Writing full prose
  • Proposing new estimators as primary contribution
