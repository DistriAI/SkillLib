split-pdf

separate

Download, split, and deeply read academic PDFs. Use when asked to read, review, or summarize an academic paper. Splits PDFs into 4-page chunks, reads them in small batches, and produces structured reading notes — avoiding context window crashes and shallow comprehension.

When to use

  • read academic paper
  • review academic paper
  • summarize academic paper
  • download PDF from search query
  • split PDF into 4-page chunks
  • produce structured reading notes

When not to use

  • papers shorter than ~15 pages
  • reading full PDFs
  • guessing paper identity without specific query
