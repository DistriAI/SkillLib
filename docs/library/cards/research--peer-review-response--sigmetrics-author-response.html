sigmetrics-author-response

separate

Use when responding to ACM SIGMETRICS reviews, covering any initial rebuttal and — distinctively — the one-shot revision response letter that must map every item on the reviewers' required-changes list to a concrete change in the revised POMACS paper, resubmitted to a subsequent rolling deadline and re-reviewed once by the original reviewers under double-anonymity.

When to use

  • responding to ACM SIGMETRICS reviews
  • initial rebuttal
  • one-shot revision response letter
  • mapping required changes to paper revisions
  • triaging review comments
  • choosing resubmission deadline
  • addressing reviewer pushback patterns
  • maintaining double-anonymity in response letters

When not to use

  • simultaneous submission to other venues before withdrawing
  • vague promises without concrete evidence
  • identity-revealing references in pre-decision letters
