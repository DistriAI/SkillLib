humrel-data-analysis

separate

Use when executing and reporting the analysis for a Human Relations (HR) manuscript — qualitative coding and data-to-theory construction, critical interpretation, or quantitative estimation and robustness. Makes the evidence-to-theory link transparent; it does not design the study (see humrel-methods).

When to use

  • Qualitative coding and data-to-theory construction
  • Critical interpretation of texts or practices
  • Quantitative estimation and robustness checks
  • Making the inference from data to theory auditable
  • Documenting first-order codes and aggregate dimensions
  • Reporting construct validity and reliability
  • Interpreting magnitudes in substantive relational terms
  • Probing mechanisms beyond main effects

When not to use

  • Designing the study
