Use when preparing a SOSP camera-ready after acceptance, working with an assigned shepherd toward the final deadline, expanding from the 12-page submission to the 13-page final limit (14 with shepherd approval), de-anonymizing safely, adding artifact badges, and meeting ACM proceedings requirements.

When to use

  • preparing SOSP camera-ready
  • working with assigned shepherd
  • expanding page limit to 13 or 14 pages
  • de-anonymizing paper
  • adding artifact badges
  • meeting ACM proceedings requirements
  • managing revision plan
  • coordinating parallel threads

When not to use

  • adding new unreviewed claims
  • silently skipping agreed items
  • guessing attendance expectations
