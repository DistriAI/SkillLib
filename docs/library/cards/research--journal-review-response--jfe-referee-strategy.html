jfe-referee-strategy

separate

Use when anticipating and pre-empting the objections a Journal of Financial Economics (JFE) referee will raise — endogeneity, fragility, multiple testing, alternative explanations. Builds a defense plan before or during review; it does not draft the post-R&R response letter (jfe-rebuttal).

When to use

  • Anticipating referee objections before submission
  • Planning which battles to fight after a first round
  • Addressing suspected weaknesses like contested instruments
  • Refereeing your own paper to answer hard questions
  • Triaging referee comments into substantive, clarification, or disagreement
  • Identifying the editor's emphasis in reports
  • Deciding where new tests go in main text or Internet Appendix

When not to use

  • Drafting the post-R&R response letter
