lancet-writing

separate

Use to structure and hold the length of a Lancet Article main text — Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion (~3000–3500 words), the Research in context panel, a limited reference list (~30), and a cautious, globally minded Discussion that does not overstate.

When to use

  • Drafting a Lancet original-research Article
  • Trimming a Lancet Article main text
  • Structuring IMRaD sections
  • Calibrating Discussion claims
  • Managing reference budget
  • Auditing main-text word count

When not to use

  • Writing a long literature-review Introduction
  • Making causal claims from observational designs
  • Claiming superiority from a non-inferiority trial
  • Re-listing Results numbers in the Discussion
