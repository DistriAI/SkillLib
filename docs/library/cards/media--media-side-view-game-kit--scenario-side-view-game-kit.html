scenario-side-view-game-kit

separate

Use when making the art for a side-scroller or 2D platformer: side-view pixel-art sprite sheets for a hero and ground enemies (run, walk, idle, attack, hurt cycles, air spin, jump and fall poses) and per-level layers (parallax background and midground, seamless wall texture, platform strip, props). Also use when a magenta key eats part of a sprite, a walk loop will not close, a slash trail keys into a solid block, generated textures do not tile, or poses on one sheet will not split.

When to use

  • side-view pixel-art sprite sheets
  • hero and ground enemy animations
  • run, walk, idle, attack, hurt cycles
  • air spin, jump and fall poses
  • parallax background and midground layers
  • seamless wall texture and platform strip
  • fixing magenta keying issues
  • fixing walk loop closure

When not to use

  • music and sound effects
