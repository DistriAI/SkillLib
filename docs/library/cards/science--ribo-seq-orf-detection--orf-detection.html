orf-detection

separate

Detect and quantify translated ORFs from Ribo-seq using 3-nucleotide periodicity, including uORFs, internal ORFs, dORFs, and novel ORFs. Use when finding actively translated regions beyond annotated CDS, classifying ORFs by the 2022 community standard, quantifying ORF-level translation, or choosing between periodicity-based callers.

When to use

  • Detecting translated ORFs from Ribo-seq
  • Identifying uORFs, internal ORFs, dORFs, and novel ORFs
  • Classifying ORFs by the 2022 community standard
  • Quantifying ORF-level translation
  • Choosing between periodicity-based callers
  • Finding actively translated regions beyond annotated CDS

When not to use

  • Tasks not stated in the skill text
