scenario-kling

separate

Use when generating or editing video with Kling models on Scenario via MCP: text-to-video, image-to-video with first and last frames, multi-shot sequences with per-shot prompts, consistent characters via elements and reference images, prompt-based video editing, motion transfer from a driving video (motion control, mocap), lipsync to audio or text, talking avatars, native audio and dialogue, or picking 720p, 1080p, or 4K tiers. Keywords: Kling V3, O1, 2.6, Omni, Kuaishou, T2V, I2V, V2V.

When to use

  • text-to-video
  • image-to-video with first and last frames
  • multi-shot sequences with per-shot prompts
  • consistent characters via elements and reference images
  • prompt-based video editing
  • motion transfer from a driving video
  • lipsync to audio or text
  • talking avatars

When not to use

  • Kling Video to Audio
