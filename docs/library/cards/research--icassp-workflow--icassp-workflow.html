icassp-workflow

separate

Use when planning an ICASSP project timeline — the annual clock from venue fit through the September paper deadline, single-blind review, the short winter rebuttal, spring notification, IEEE Xplore camera-ready, and the May conference — with backward-planning offsets and the choice among standard, OJSP-ICASSP, and grand-challenge tracks.

When to use

  • planning an ICASSP project timeline
  • backward-planning offsets from the September paper deadline
  • choosing among standard, OJSP-ICASSP, and grand-challenge tracks
  • managing ICASSP submission milestones
  • coordinating CMS submission and reproducibility package ownership

When not to use

  • generic-ML papers
  • speech papers better suited to Interspeech
