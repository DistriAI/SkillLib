jse-tju-topic-selection

separate

Use when selecting or sharpening a research topic for 《系统工程学报》 (Journal of Systems Engineering, Tianjin University), especially when an idea mentions a system but has not yet fixed its boundary, interacting actors or components, states, feedback, constraints, method increment, testable question, and system-level outcome.

When to use

  • Selecting research topics for Journal of Systems Engineering
  • Sharpening research ideas with system boundaries
  • Defining interacting actors and components
  • Identifying states, feedback, and constraints
  • Formulating testable system-level questions
  • Aligning topics with journal main lines

When not to use

  • Modeling after topic selection
  • Expanding topics to vague complex systems without variable changes
  • Choosing algorithms before finding system context
  • Using single case improvements as transferable claims
