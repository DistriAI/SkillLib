programmatic-seo-playbook

separate

Replicate the 7 programmatic SEO plays that took Zapier, Clay, Composio, Gamma, Mintlify, HeyGen and HubSpot to #1 in Google and ChatGPT. Use when a company wants to generate pages at scale from its own data (integrations, companies, languages, user content, customer backlinks, free tools) and needs the page type, URL pattern, data source, template and rollout plan chosen for its business. Platform: Google + AI search.

When to use

  • generating pages at scale from own data
  • choosing page type and URL pattern
  • designing data model and template
  • planning rollout and index monitoring
  • setting AI-citation checks
  • scoring programmatic SEO plays

When not to use

  • tasks not involving generating pages at scale
  • tasks not involving company data sources
