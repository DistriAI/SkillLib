tar-topic-selection

separate

Use when choosing or sharpening a research question for a The Accounting Review (TAR) manuscript — testing whether the question is contribution-driven, identifiable with accounting data, and a fit for TAR versus JAR/JAE or a specialist AAA section journal. Selects and scopes the question; it does not build the model (tar-theory-development) or design identification (tar-methods).

When to use

  • Choosing a research question for TAR
  • Sharpening a research question
  • Testing if a question is contribution-driven
  • Testing if a question is identifiable with accounting data
  • Determining fit for TAR versus JAR/JAE
  • Determining fit for TAR versus specialist AAA section journals
  • Repositioning a question from finance to accounting
  • Scoping a question with a regulatory or institutional shock

When not to use

  • Building the model
  • Designing identification
  • Data-first fishing without an accounting question
  • Finance in disguise
  • Replication without contribution
  • Prestige targeting
