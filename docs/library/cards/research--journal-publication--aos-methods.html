aos-methods

separate

Use when designing the study for an Accounting, Organizations and Society (AOS) manuscript — qualitative field studies (access, case logic, interviews, observation), behavioral experiments, surveys, historical/archival inquiry, or theory-laden archival designs, each with the ethics and site-anonymity groundwork done early. Designs the inquiry; it does not run the coding or estimation (aos-data-analysis).

When to use

  • Choosing between field study, experiment, survey, or historical design
  • Negotiating field access, case selection, or interview/observation protocols
  • Designing manipulations and participant pools for a behavioral experiment
  • Ethics approval, informed consent, or site-anonymity commitments
  • Qualitative field study design
  • Behavioral experiment design
  • Survey and historical designs
  • Theory-laden archival designs

When not to use

  • Running the coding or estimation
