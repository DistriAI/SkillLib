geo-content-research

separate

Researches what prompts people ask AI engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) about a product category and produces a prompts.csv artifact — a prioritized, strictly-schema'd list of the queries where the brand should be cited. Feeds the monitor workflow.

When to use

  • Researching AI engine prompts
  • Producing prompts.csv artifact
  • Product intelligence gathering
  • AI prompt research
  • Scoring and prioritizing queries
  • Reddit mining for user questions

When not to use

  • Content Blueprint
  • Content Generation
  • Authority Infiltration
  • SEO keyword ranking
