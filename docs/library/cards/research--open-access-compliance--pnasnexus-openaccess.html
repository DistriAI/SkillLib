pnasnexus-openaccess

separate

Use to settle PNAS Nexus's open-access decisions — confirm the gold-OA model and the article processing charge (APC), check eligibility for waivers/discounts (low-/middle-income countries, Read & Publish, membership) and funder compliance, and choose the license (CC BY vs CC BY-NC). This is PNAS Nexus's signature pre-submission decision; it replaces flagship PNAS's Direct/Contributed track choice (PNAS Nexus has no such track).

When to use

  • Confirm PNAS Nexus gold-OA model
  • Check APC and waiver eligibility
  • Choose CC BY or CC BY-NC license
  • Verify funder compliance
  • Determine corresponding author eligibility for discounts

When not to use

  • Flagship PNAS Direct/Contributed track choice
  • Member-communicated paper submission
  • Prearranged-editor route
