icde-review-process

separate

Use when explaining or planning around IEEE ICDE peer review: the two-round-per-edition structure, area-chair-coordinated assignment with at least three reviewers, two-phase reviewing with a possible Revise & Resubmit, single-blind dynamics, how a builder-heavy committee reads a systems paper, and how outcomes feed IEEE Xplore.

When to use

  • explaining ICDE peer review structure
  • planning review-stage strategy
  • understanding area-chair-coordinated assignment
  • analyzing single-blind review dynamics
  • assessing builder-heavy committee expectations
  • evaluating Revise & Resubmit phases
  • determining IEEE Xplore publication requirements

When not to use

  • making process claims without checking current edition details
