offensive-advanced-redteam

separate

Comprehensive red team operations methodology covering full engagement lifecycle from planning through reporting. Addresses engagement scoping and rules of engagement negotiation, multi-tier C2 infrastructure design with redirectors and domain fronting, malleable traffic profiles and beacon tradecraft, OPSEC discipline including attribution avoidance and indicator management, EDR and AMSI evasion techniques using direct syscalls and unhooking, data collection with chain-of-custody controls, and structured reporting with purple team debrief workflows. Covers assumed-breach, external-to-internal, insider threat, and hybrid physical-cyber engagement scenarios with MITRE ATT&CK mapping throughout. Targets operators planning or executing adversary simulation engagements against mature defenders.

When to use

  • engagement scoping and rules of engagement negotiation
  • multi-tier C2 infrastructure design
  • malleable traffic profiles and beacon tradecraft
  • OPSEC discipline and attribution avoidance
  • EDR and AMSI evasion techniques
  • data collection with chain-of-custody controls
  • structured reporting with purple team debrief workflows
  • assumed-breach and external-to-internal engagement scenarios

When not to use

  • penetration tests maximizing vulnerability discovery
  • destructive actions
  • social engineering of specific individuals
  • production systems that cannot tolerate downtime
  • third-party SaaS platforms without authorization
