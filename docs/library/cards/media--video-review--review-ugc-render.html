review-ugc-render

separate

Mandatory pre-publish review gate for a UGC video render. Transcribes the finished render's AUDIO with Whisper and word-diffs it against the approved spoken script, then gates keeping the render as the final version (video_review) — blocking a render whose generated audio mis-voices a word (e.g. the approved "human-vetted" spoken as "human witted"), says a different number or brand name, flips a negation, drops an approved phrase, or comes back silent. Correct speech written differently ("5mg" said "five milligrams", "30%" said "thirty percent", a spoken URL, "don't" said "do not", "larkfieldstraps" said "larkfield straps", a confirmed pronunciation like "TG1" said "T G one") passes. Runnable, gating counterpart to GooseWorks' fuller transcript-integrity review. Every ugc-video-formats recipe runs this after render and BEFORE keeping the final version.

When to use

  • pre-publish review gate for UGC video render
  • transcribe finished render audio with Whisper
  • word-diff audio against approved spoken script
  • gate keeping render as final version
  • detect mis-voiced words, numbers, or brand names
  • detect flipped negations or dropped phrases
  • detect silent renders
  • validate confirmed pronunciations

When not to use

  • eyeball /watch review
  • fuzzy matching
  • publishing without passing the gate
  • running without ffmpeg on PATH
