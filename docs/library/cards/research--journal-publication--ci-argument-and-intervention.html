ci-argument-and-intervention

separate

Use to forge the central argument of a Critical Inquiry (CI) essay into a genuine intervention. CI rewards a bold claim that reorients a conversation in criticism and theory, with stakes that cross disciplines — not a competent reading, a survey, or a method demonstration. Sharpens the thesis and its stakes; it does not fabricate evidence or theory.
