chinese-journal-of-management

separate

Use when targeting 《管理学报》(Chinese Journal of Management — 华中科技大学主办、教育部主管的全国性管理学月刊, 2004 年创刊, 双向匿名审稿) or deciding whether a Chinese management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, double-blind review, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《管理学报》
  • deciding if a Chinese management manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a generic Chinese management manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • listing alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • targeting 《管理学刊》
  • targeting 《中国管理科学》
  • targeting 《管理科学学报》
  • targeting 《中国农村观察》
  • targeting 《中国软科学》
  • targeting 《中国行政管理》
  • targeting 《南开管理评论》
