ai-meeting-notes

separate

Messy notes → Clear action items. Instantly. Paste any meeting notes, transcript, or text. Get summaries, action items with owners and deadlines. Auto-saved, searchable, with integrated to-do tracking. No bot. No subscription. No setup.

When to use

  • Extracting action items from meeting notes
  • Summarizing meeting transcripts
  • Identifying owners and deadlines
  • Capturing key decisions
  • Saving notes to searchable files
  • Tracking to-do items
  • Referencing previous meetings
  • Parsing unstructured text

When not to use

  • Bot-based meeting recording
  • Subscription-based services
  • Complex setup requirements
