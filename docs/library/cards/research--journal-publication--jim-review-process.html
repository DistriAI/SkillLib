jim-review-process

separate

Use when a Journal of International Marketing (JIM) manuscript is under review or about to be — understanding the editorial pipeline, decision categories, desk-reject triggers, and how to wait productively. It explains the process; jim-rebuttal handles the response once a letter arrives.

When to use

  • understanding the editorial pipeline
  • identifying decision categories
  • identifying desk-reject triggers
  • waiting productively during review
  • estimating revision timelines
  • pre-submission engineering against desk-reject triggers
  • nudging the editor after silence
  • triaging decision letters

When not to use

  • handling the response once a letter arrives
  • writing the rebuttal
