make-static-ads-graphic-request

separate

Makes one simple non-ad brand graphic, such as an email header, social feed or story image, or a blog or link-preview image, in the brand's style with at most a short line of text, after claim and rights checks and a yes to the quote; returns the link and alt text. Not for carousels, decks or print. Use alone when you already have the purpose, placement and exact wording; otherwise run make-static-ads.

When to use

  • email header
  • social feed image
  • social story image
  • blog image
  • link-preview image
  • single image with brand styling
  • short line of text graphic

When not to use

  • carousels
  • decks
  • print
  • packaging
  • multi-page layouts
  • data-heavy infographics
