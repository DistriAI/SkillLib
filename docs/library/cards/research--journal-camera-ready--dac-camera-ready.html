dac-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing the final ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference (DAC) Research-Manuscript camera-ready after acceptance, covering de-anonymization, restoring author/affiliation/acknowledgement/funding blocks, the ACM copyright form and rights stamp, the April proceedings deadline, ACM Digital Library metadata and archival-flow checks, and the still-fixed 6+1 page budget.

When to use

  • preparing final DAC camera-ready
  • de-anonymizing accepted paper
  • restoring author and affiliation blocks
  • restoring acknowledgements and funding
  • completing ACM copyright form
  • inserting rights stamp and DOI
  • checking ACM DL metadata
  • verifying 6+1 page budget

When not to use

  • rewriting scope or claims
  • altering QoR numbers
  • shrinking fonts
  • altering the template
