auditing-research

separate

Use when targeting 《审计研究》(Auditing Research — 审计署主管、中国审计学会主办、匿名审稿、不收版面费的审计权威核心双月刊) or deciding whether a Chinese auditing manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/keyword/citation house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《审计研究》
  • deciding if a Chinese auditing manuscript fits the venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese business manuscript for 《审计研究》
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit for national auditing and public fund performance
  • checking journal fit for CPA audit quality and independence
  • checking journal fit for internal audit and internal control

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • manuscripts where accounting confirmation, audit supervision, disclosure, or internal control are not core contributions
  • manuscripts relying only on corporate financial data or control variables
  • manuscripts better suited to 《亚太经济》, 《审计与经济研究》, 《中国科学院院刊》, or 《经济管理》
