eurosys-workflow

separate

Use when planning a EuroSys campaign end to end — choosing between the spring and fall gates of an edition, backward-scheduling system building and evaluation from the abstract deadline, handling the notification-to-revision-to-resubmission pipeline, and landing camera-ready, artifacts, and April travel for the conference.

When to use

  • planning a EuroSys campaign end to end
  • choosing between spring and fall gates
  • backward-scheduling system building and evaluation
  • handling the notification-to-revision-to-resubmission pipeline
  • landing camera-ready, artifacts, and April travel
  • managing the quiet zones between submission and notification
  • coordinating multi-author teams
  • two-gate portfolio thinking

When not to use

  • writing the actual paper content
  • conducting the experiments
  • submitting the paper to other venues
