ssc-literature-dialogue

separate

Use when building or fixing the literature review for 《中国社会科学》 (Social Sciences in China) — entering the theoretical lineage (classic authors and schools) rather than piling up recent empirical citations, and positioning the paper as a move within an ongoing scholarly conversation. Use when references are a variable-list instead of a theory dialogue.

When to use

  • Building literature review for 《中国社会科学》
  • Fixing literature review lacking theoretical lineage
  • Positioning paper within scholarly conversation
  • Replacing variable-list references with theory dialogue
  • Organizing review by theoretical source and contemporary progress
  • Integrating local theoretical resources into dialogue

When not to use

  • Piling up recent empirical citations
  • Writing review as a list of recent findings
  • Listing variables without theoretical context
  • Treating local literature as decoration only
