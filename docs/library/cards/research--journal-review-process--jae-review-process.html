jae-review-process

separate

Use when anticipating or interpreting the Journal of Accounting and Economics (JAE) review process — the editor suitability screen and Article Transfer Service desk-reject culture, double-anonymized review by a minimum of two reviewers, compact editor-final decision model, and annual JAE Conference special-issue pipeline.

When to use

  • anticipating JAE review process
  • interpreting JAE decision letters
  • weighing regular pipeline against JAE Conference route
  • understanding editor final decision model
  • handling desk-reject outcomes
  • calibrating expectations for JAE submission

When not to use

  • inventing fixed review timelines or acceptance rates
  • resubmitting lightly edited versions after a final decision
  • expecting INFORMS-style departmental area-editor routing
