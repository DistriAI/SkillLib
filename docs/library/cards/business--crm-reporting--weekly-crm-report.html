weekly-crm-report

separate

Cleans a weekly CRM export and produces a regional sales summary. Activates when the user asks to clean a CRM export, deduplicate sales rows, calculate regional totals, or generate a weekly sales report from a CSV.

When to use

  • clean a CRM export
  • deduplicate sales rows
  • calculate regional totals
  • generate a weekly sales report from a CSV

When not to use

  • general spreadsheet questions
  • analytics questions that don't involve a CRM export
  • general BI tool tasks
  • fetching from a live CRM API
