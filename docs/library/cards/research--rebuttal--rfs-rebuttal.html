rfs-rebuttal

separate

Use after a decision letter (R&R or reject-and-resubmit) on a The Review of Financial Studies (RFS) manuscript, to structure the response letter and revision plan. Writes the rebuttal; assumes the manuscript itself is being revised in parallel.

When to use

  • Structuring RFS response letters
  • Planning revision responses to referee comments
  • Handling Registered Report Stage 2 adherence
  • Managing RFS code-release conditions
  • Resolving conflicting referee demands

When not to use

  • Revising the manuscript itself
  • Drafting responses for changes not actually made
