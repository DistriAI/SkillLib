When to use

  • 퇴직금 calculation
  • average wage calculation
  • DB vs DC retirement pension differences
  • IRP payment rules
  • severance tax withholding
  • unpaid severance claims
  • eligibility check for continuous service

When not to use

  • legal or tax advice
  • dispute resolution
  • unusual contract interpretation
