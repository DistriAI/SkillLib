pods-writing-style

separate

Use when revising an ACM PODS paper for a precise model and result on the first page, exact theorem statements with matching bounds, complete and self-contained proofs (body plus at-submission appendix), honest assumptions and open cases, double-anonymous wording, and disciplined use of the acmsmall 15-page budget.

When to use

  • revising an ACM PODS paper
  • stating precise models and results on the first page
  • ensuring exact theorem statements with matching bounds
  • verifying complete and self-contained proofs
  • maintaining honest assumptions and open cases
  • applying double-anonymous wording
  • managing the acmsmall 15-page budget

When not to use

  • writing systems papers
  • using online or external appendices
