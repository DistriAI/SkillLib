osdi-workflow

separate

Use when planning or managing a full OSDI cycle — the December title-registration and submission deadlines on HotCRP, the silent no-rebuttal review window, March notification, the May artifact deadline, June shepherded final papers, and the July conference — including rejection retargeting to SOSP or NSDI.

When to use

  • planning OSDI cycle
  • managing OSDI deadlines
  • OSDI title registration
  • OSDI full submission
  • OSDI notification branches
  • OSDI artifact submission
  • OSDI final papers
  • rejection retargeting to SOSP or NSDI

When not to use

  • planning non-OSDI conferences
  • managing non-OSDI deadlines
