environmental-science-and-technology

separate

Use when targeting Environmental Science & Technology (ES&T) or deciding whether an environmental science or engineering manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Environmental Science & Technology (ES&T)
  • deciding whether an environmental science or engineering manuscript fits this venue
  • venue selection or framing guidance for environmental chemistry, exposure, fate-and-transport, remediation, or green-engineering papers
  • deciding between the main ES&T journal and ES&T Letters
  • comparing ES&T with other environmental science venues
  • checking desk-reject risks before submitting

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
