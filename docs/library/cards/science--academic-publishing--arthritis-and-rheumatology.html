arthritis-and-rheumatology

separate

Use when targeting Arthritis & Rheumatology or deciding whether a rheumatology/autoimmune-disease study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the clinical-trial/observational and basic-translational evidence bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, ACR/Wiley house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • rheumatology clinical, observational, or basic/translational study fit check
  • re-framing studies around autoimmune/musculoskeletal-disease mechanisms
  • choosing between A&R and other rheumatology or immunology venues
  • checking reporting-guideline, registration, and desk-reject expectations
  • randomized and pragmatic trials in inflammatory arthritis and vasculitis
  • observational, cohort, registry, and comparative-effectiveness studies
  • disease-classification and outcome-measure development/validation
  • basic and translational immunology and joint/connective-tissue biology

When not to use

  • clinical or regulatory advice
  • underpowered single-center series
  • bench studies with no autoimmune/musculoskeletal-disease anchor
