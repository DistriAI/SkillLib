music-theory-spectrum

separate

Use when targeting Music Theory Spectrum or deciding whether a music-theory manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, its argument and analytical-method bar, notational and close-analysis expectations, house style and review norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Music Theory Spectrum fit check
  • Analytical method manuscripts
  • History and philosophy of music theory
  • Close analysis of specific works
  • Venue selection between music theory and historical musicology
  • Analytical-method bar assessment
  • Desk-reject heuristics
  • Re-framing close analysis into theoretical argument

When not to use

  • Loose appreciation
  • Theoretical claims untethered from music
  • Analysis serving historical or cultural arguments
  • Philosophical aesthetics
  • Replacing current submission guidelines
