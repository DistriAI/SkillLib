jmr-rebuttal

separate

Use when a Journal of Marketing Research (JMR) Revise-and-Resubmit arrives — planning the revision and drafting a point-by-point response that satisfies two independent reviewers and the Coeditor, addresses rigor-bar concerns (identification, exact statistics, replication) and substance-bar concerns (contribution), and uses the 'W'-prefixed Web Appendix to absorb new analyses without breaking the 50-page limit.

When to use

  • Planning JMR R&R revisions
  • Drafting point-by-point response letters
  • Addressing rigor-bar concerns
  • Addressing substance-bar concerns
  • Managing Web Appendix additions
  • Reconciling conflicting reviewer requests
  • Tracking revision evidence in a ledger

When not to use

  • Writing response letters before manuscript revision
  • Providing cosmetic replies without concrete changes
  • Exceeding the 50-page print limit
  • Reporting results with thresholds instead of exact statistics
  • Treating rigor and substance as separate silos
