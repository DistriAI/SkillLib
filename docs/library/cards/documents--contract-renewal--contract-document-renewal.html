contract-document-renewal

authentic

Contract register: counterparty, owner, start and end dates, auto-renewal flag, renewal notice deadline, value and tax basis. Use for renewal tracking and notice deadlines.

When to use

  • contract tracker
  • renewal calendar
  • contract expiry alerts
  • vendor agreement register

When not to use

  • Empty template only. It does not compute payroll, tax, leave balances or KPIs.
  • Notion relations need both databases imported before the link column resolves.
  • Select options are a starting set. Rename them to match how the business talks.
  • No automation, reminders or sync. Those need the integration layer.
  • Does not review contract terms or provide legal advice.
  • Legal, tax and HR review is still required before this drives real decisions.
