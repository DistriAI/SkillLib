jae-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Accounting and Economics (JAE) manuscript within the positive-accounting and economics literatures — joining the right conversation (capital-markets, contracting, disclosure, standards), engaging seminal economics-based work, and avoiding gap-spotting.

When to use

  • Positioning JAE manuscripts
  • Joining economics-based conversations
  • Engaging seminal economics-based work
  • Problematizing literature assumptions
  • Checking JAE venue boundaries
  • Mapping incumbent conversations
  • Testing accounting-economics reviewer expectations

When not to use

  • Gap-spotting without economic stake
  • Pure asset-pricing conversations
  • Corporate-finance conversations with little accounting content
  • Measurement or behavioral conversations for TAR
  • Citation dumps without synthesized conversation
