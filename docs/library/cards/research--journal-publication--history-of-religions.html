history-of-religions

separate

Use when targeting History of Religions or deciding whether a comparative-historical religious-studies manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, its comparative-method and primary-source bar, textual/philological and morphological expectations, house style and review norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • comparative study of religious phenomena
  • historical study of religious forms
  • morphological and typological analysis
  • close source-based study of specific traditions
  • method and theory in comparative history of religions
  • fit and framing check for History of Religions
  • reframing single-tradition studies for comparative argument
  • choosing between History of Religions and other venues

When not to use

  • theological advocacy
  • comparison floating free of sources and languages
  • confessional or constructive concerns
  • parallel-hunting catalogue without principled argument
  • comparison untethered from primary sources
  • claims out of proportion to evidence
