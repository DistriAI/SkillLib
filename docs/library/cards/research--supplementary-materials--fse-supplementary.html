fse-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what belongs in an ESEC/FSE paper body versus its anonymized artifact and appendices, covering the ACM-template page budget, the rule that decision-critical evidence stays inside the reviewed pages, double-anonymous supplementary material, and how to split an empirical-SE paper between body and package.

When to use

  • deciding what belongs in an ESEC/FSE paper body versus its anonymized artifact and appendices
  • covering the ACM-template page budget
  • ensuring decision-critical evidence stays inside the reviewed pages
  • managing double-anonymous supplementary material
  • splitting an empirical-SE paper between body and package
  • assembling FSE supplementary material
  • checking partition of content between body and artifact
  • verifying anonymity of supplementary archives

When not to use

  • writing the main body of the paper
  • conducting the empirical study
  • performing statistical analysis
  • creating the code or scripts
  • designing the interview protocol
  • managing the repository history
  • submitting the paper to the conference
  • reviewing the paper
