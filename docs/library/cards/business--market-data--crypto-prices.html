crypto-prices

authentic

Fetch live cryptocurrency prices with zero API keys — CoinGecko's public endpoints primary, Coinbase spot fallback, via plain curl. Use when asked what's bitcoin at, ETH price in euros, how's the crypto market today, or price of some altcoin. Produces the current price with 24h context, the source and timestamp, the rerunnable command, and the volatility caveat that crypto answers must carry.

When to use

  • Fetching live cryptocurrency prices
  • Getting bitcoin or ETH price in specific currencies
  • Checking how the crypto market is doing today
  • Getting the price of an altcoin
  • Resolving coin names to CoinGecko ids
  • Batching multi-coin price requests

When not to use

  • Predicting future prices
  • Providing buy, sell, or hold advice
  • Performing portfolio math presented as guidance
  • Answering from memory without fetching
