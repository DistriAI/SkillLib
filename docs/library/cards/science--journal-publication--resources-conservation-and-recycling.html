resources-conservation-and-recycling

separate

Use when targeting Resources, Conservation & Recycling or deciding whether a resource-efficiency/circular-economy manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the materials-and-waste circularity contribution bar, material-flow-analysis expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a resource-efficiency or recycling paper
  • re-framing material-flow, recycling-technology, or resource-recovery studies
  • deciding between RCR and the Journal of Cleaner Production
  • deciding between RCR and a process-engineering venue
  • checking RCR's materials/waste-circularity bar
  • checking desk-reject heuristics
  • Material-flow analysis (MFA) and substance-flow analysis (SFA)
  • Recycling and resource-recovery technologies and systems

When not to use

  • general sustainability-management essay
  • recycling case with no quantified material outcome
  • replacing the journal's current author guidelines
