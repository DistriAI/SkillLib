journal-of-financial-intermediation

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Financial Intermediation (JFI) or deciding whether a banking / intermediation manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Financial Intermediation
  • deciding if banking manuscript fits JFI
  • bank lending and liquidity creation studies
  • financial regulation and supervision effects
  • shadow banking and FinTech intermediaries
  • re-framing general finance papers around intermediation
  • JFI desk-reject risk assessment
  • banking and top-finance alternative venue lists

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • papers using bank data without intermediation contribution
  • credit regressions without credible identification
  • generic contracting models without intermediary mechanism
  • macro-finance or monetary transmission papers
  • general corporate finance papers framed as intermediation
