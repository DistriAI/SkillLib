c-level-advisor

separate

Provides strategic business advice by channelling the perspectives of 10 executive roles — CEO, CTO, COO, CPO, CMO, CFO, CRO, CISO, CHRO, and Executive Mentor — across decisions, trade-offs, and org challenges. Runs multi-role board meetings, routes questions to the right executive voice, and delivers structured recommendations (Bottom Line → What → Why → How to Act → Your Decision). Use when a founder or executive needs business strategy advice, leadership perspective, executive decision support, board-level input, fundraising guidance, product-market fit review, hiring or culture frameworks, risk assessment, or competitive analysis.
