benchmark-due-diligence

separate

Runs adversarial due-diligence on a benchmark the user envies — a founder, KOL, company, or product whose success looks inflated — splitting marketing bubble from real signal, then mapping the validated playbook onto the user's own resources. Use for 尽调/对标/拆解 a competitor, 抄/偷师 their playbook, or suspecting 水分/泡沫 in claims. Prefer over deep-research when debunking inflated claims, not a neutral briefing.

When to use

  • adversarial due-diligence
  • splitting marketing bubble from real signal
  • mapping validated playbook onto user's resources
  • debunking inflated claims
  • teardown of founder, KOL, company, or product
  • 尽调/对标/拆解 a competitor
  • 抄/偷师 their playbook
  • suspecting 水分/泡沫 in claims

When not to use

  • neutral briefing
  • deep-research for trustworthy picture of the world
