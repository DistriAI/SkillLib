icde-workflow

separate

Use when planning an IEEE ICDE project timeline around the two-round-per-edition calendar, choosing between the June and November research deadlines, budgeting for a possible revise-and-resubmit window, and backward-planning a data-engineering systems paper from CMT submission through review, camera-ready, and IEEE Xplore publication.

When to use

  • planning an IEEE ICDE project timeline
  • choosing between June and November research deadlines
  • budgeting for a possible revise-and-resubmit window
  • backward-planning a data-engineering systems paper
  • managing CMT submission through review
  • managing camera-ready and IEEE Xplore publication

When not to use

  • planning for SIGMOD PACMMOD rounds
  • planning for PVLDB monthly deadlines
  • managing journal-of-the-conference submissions
