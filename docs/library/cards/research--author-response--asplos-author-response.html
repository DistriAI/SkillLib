asplos-author-response

separate

Use when drafting an ASPLOS author response inside the short fixed window — triaging reviews into factual errors versus questions versus disagreements, budgeting for the ~800 words reviewers are expected to read, arguing only from evidence already in the submission, and positioning the paper for the Accept / Major Revision / Reject decision.

When to use

  • drafting an ASPLOS author response
  • triaging reviews into factual errors, questions, and disagreements
  • budgeting for the 800-word limit
  • arguing from evidence already in the submission
  • positioning the paper for Accept, Major Revision, or Reject
  • managing a 3-4 day response window
  • resolving contradictions between reviews

When not to use

  • producing new numbers during the response window
  • making unbounded promises for revisions
  • restating weaknesses in stronger words than the reviewer used
