restat-theory-model

separate

Use when deciding how much theory or structure a The Review of Economics and Statistics (REStat) manuscript should carry — right-sizing a model so it interprets or disciplines the empirical estimate without becoming the contribution. Calibrates the theory's role; it does not develop new theory for its own sake.

When to use

  • deciding how much theory a REStat manuscript should carry
  • right-sizing a model to interpret an empirical estimate
  • determining if a reduced-form result needs economic interpretation
  • evaluating if a model section overshadows the empirical contribution
  • deciding whether to estimate a structural model or stay reduced-form
  • addressing referee questions about mechanisms or models behind regressions

When not to use

  • developing new theory for its own sake
  • publishing the model itself as the primary contribution
