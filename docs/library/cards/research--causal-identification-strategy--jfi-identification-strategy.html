jfi-identification-strategy

separate

Use when auditing the core analytical engine of a Journal of Financial Intermediation (JFI) paper — for empirics, the causal design that separates credit supply from demand in banking data; for theory, the assumptions, equilibrium discipline, and proof exposition. It pressure-tests the design; it does not run the analysis.

When to use

  • auditing empirical design of banking/intermediation papers
  • defending causal design separating credit supply from demand
  • auditing assumptions and propositions of theory papers
  • pressure-testing identification strategies
  • evaluating staggered DID, IV, and RDD designs
  • assessing within-firm benchmark limitations
  • auditing proof exposition and generality arguments

When not to use

  • running the analysis
  • empirical estimation execution
