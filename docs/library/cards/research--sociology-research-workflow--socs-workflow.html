socs-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which socs-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from problematic framing through rebuttal for 《社会学研究》 (Sociological Studies). Routes — does not replace — the specialized skills, and re-routes off-discipline drafts to other journal packs.

When to use

  • Deciding which socs-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from problematic framing through rebuttal
  • Routing off-discipline drafts to other journal packs
  • Determining next steps for manuscripts targeting 《社会学研究》
  • Handling CGSS/CFPS/CLDS regression results or fieldwork materials
  • Addressing missing sociological problem awareness
  • Responding to external review comments

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized socs-* skills
  • Handling interdisciplinary grand propositions for 《中国社会科学》
  • Handling economic causality and policy evaluation for 《经济研究》 or 《中国工业经济》
