cn-open-class

authentic

Use when asked 公开课怎么准备, 帮我写说课稿, 优质课比赛, 评优课, 教研课, 汇报课, 青年教师赛课, 试讲, or prepare an observed lesson in a Chinese school. Produces a 公开课 preparation pack in Chinese: a 说课稿 (说教材, 说学情, 说教学目标, 说重难点, 说教法学法, 说教学过程, 说板书) timed to the required length, the lesson's highlights (亮点) and how observers will see them, a rehearsal and 磨课 plan, contingency plans, and answers to likely 评课 questions.

When to use

  • 公开课怎么准备
  • 帮我写说课稿
  • 优质课比赛
  • 评优课
  • 教研课
  • 汇报课
  • 青年教师赛课
  • 试讲

When not to use

  • full lesson plan
