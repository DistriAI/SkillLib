soctheory-boundary-conditions

separate

Use when specifying the scope, domain, and limits of a Sociological Theory (ST) manuscript's theory — stating where it holds, where it does not, and what it does NOT claim. Bounds the theory as a contribution; it does NOT build the concepts (soctheory-theory-construction) or audit the reasoning (soctheory-argument-development).

When to use

  • specifying the scope, domain, and limits of a Sociological Theory (ST) manuscript's theory
  • stating where the theory holds, where it does not, and what it does NOT claim
  • Bounding the theory as a contribution
  • Converting counter-cases from soctheory-argument-development into boundary conditions
  • Stating substantive, level, and contextual/historical scope
  • Theorizing boundaries using the mechanism
  • Stating explicit non-claims
  • Tying scope conditions to propositions and conceptual figures

When not to use

  • building the concepts (soctheory-theory-construction)
  • auditing the reasoning (soctheory-argument-development)
