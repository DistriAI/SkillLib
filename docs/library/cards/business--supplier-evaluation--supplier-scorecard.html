supplier-scorecard

authentic

Build a quarterly supplier performance scorecard with a weighted grade and a clear escalate/develop/exit call. Use when asked to review supplier performance, prepare a quarterly business review for a vendor, score a supplier on OTIF and quality, or decide whether to escalate or exit a supplier. Produces a weighted scorecard with trend arrows, per-dimension evidence, corrective-action status, and a recommendation.

When to use

  • review supplier performance
  • prepare a quarterly business review for a vendor
  • score a supplier on OTIF and quality
  • decide whether to escalate or exit a supplier

When not to use

  • do not let a good price excuse failing OTIF
  • do not score quality on PPM alone if there was a customer escape or stop-ship
  • do not average the year
  • do not recommend exit for a sole-source supplier without a qualified alternative and transition plan
  • do not carry the same corrective action across two reviews without escalating
  • do not soften the band to avoid an awkward QBR
