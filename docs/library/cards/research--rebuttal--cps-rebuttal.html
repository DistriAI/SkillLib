cps-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to a Comparative Political Studies (CPS) revise-and-resubmit decision — structuring the response letter to multiple anonymous reviewers and the editor. Plans the revision and response; it does not fabricate results or relitigate the decision.

When to use

  • Responding to CPS revise-and-resubmit decisions
  • Structuring response letters to anonymous reviewers and editors
  • Planning revisions and point-by-point responses
  • Reconciling conflicting reviewer demands
  • Addressing requests for analyses changing identification or scope
  • Classifying reviewer concerns by role
  • Updating replication packages and data availability statements

When not to use

  • Fabricating results
  • Relitigating the decision
  • Cosmetic edits dressed as substantive responses
  • Ignoring or merging away reviewer points
  • Defensive or dismissive tone
  • Adding caveats instead of design work
  • Re-introducing identifying information
