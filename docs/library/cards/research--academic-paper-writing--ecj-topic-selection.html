ecj-topic-selection

separate

Use when scoping or framing a research question for a The Economic Journal (EJ) manuscript and you need to test whether it is "EJ-shaped" — a substantial contribution of broad interest to economists at large, full-length or short-paper. Diagnoses fit and frames the contribution; it does not design the empirics.

When to use

  • scoping a research question for The Economic Journal
  • framing a research question for The Economic Journal
  • testing if a manuscript is 'EJ-shaped'
  • diagnosing fit for The Economic Journal
  • framing the contribution for The Economic Journal
  • deciding between full-length article and short-paper route
  • deciding between EJ, field journal, or top-5
  • framing contribution as economic claims

When not to use

  • designing the empirics
