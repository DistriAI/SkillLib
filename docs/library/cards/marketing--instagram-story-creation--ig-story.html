ig-story

separate

Write the day's Instagram Stories - the frame-by-frame sequence, which stickers to use where, and the one that moves people into the DMs. Use when the user says "stories", "what do I post on my story", "story sequence", "poll idea", "I have nothing to story about", or wants to sell something without making a post about it.

When to use

  • story sequence
  • poll idea
  • what do I post on my story
  • I have nothing to story about
  • sell something without making a post

When not to use

  • feed posts
  • bulk DMing people who did not interact
