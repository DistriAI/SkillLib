migrate-birp-to-urp

separate

Plans, executes, and troubleshoots migration from the Built-in Render Pipeline to URP. Use when asked to convert a project, scene, material, or shader to URP, or to fix visuals broken by the change.

When to use

  • convert a project to URP
  • convert a scene to URP
  • convert a material to URP
  • convert a shader to URP
  • fix visuals broken by the change
  • migrate Built-in to URP
  • troubleshoot URP migration
  • plan URP migration

When not to use

  • modify files when user says do not modify
  • automatic conversion of custom shaders
  • automatic conversion of GrabPass
  • automatic conversion of Surface Shaders
  • automatic conversion of OnRenderImage
  • automatic conversion of replacement shaders
  • automatic conversion of package-owned shaders
