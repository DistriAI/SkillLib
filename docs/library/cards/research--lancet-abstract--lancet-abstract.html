lancet-abstract

separate

Use to write The Lancet's structured abstract — ≤300 words under the exact headings Background, Methods, Findings, Interpretation, Funding — leading with the clinical question, reporting the primary outcome with effect size and 95% CI, citing the registration number, and naming the funder.

When to use

  • Writing The Lancet structured abstract
  • Formatting abstract with Background, Methods, Findings, Interpretation, Funding
  • Reporting primary outcome with effect size and 95% CI
  • Including trial registration number
  • Naming funder in abstract

When not to use

  • Writing unstructured prose abstracts
  • Using headings Results and Conclusions
  • Abstracts over 300 words
