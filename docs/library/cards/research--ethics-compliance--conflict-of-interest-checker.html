Check for co-authorship and institutional conflicts between authors and suggested reviewers to support peer review integrity. Coauthorship and institutional conflict detection supported.

When to use

  • Journal submission preparation
  • Editorial conflict screening
  • Peer review integrity verification
  • Compliance verification for grant review panels

When not to use

  • Writing a review
  • Evaluating manuscript quality
  • General author searches
