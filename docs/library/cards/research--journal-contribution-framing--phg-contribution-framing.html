phg-contribution-framing

separate

Use when framing the forward-looking contribution of a Progress in Human Geography (PiHG) review essay — the portable "so what / what next" agenda the field can carry forward. Frames and stakes the contribution; it does not make the critical move (see phg-critical-intervention) or polish prose (see phg-writing-style).

When to use

  • framing the forward-looking contribution
  • creating a portable agenda
  • deciding how ambitiously to pitch the contribution
  • writing the abstract and closing agenda section
  • naming the contribution type
  • making the agenda portable and generative
  • staking the contribution early and late

When not to use

  • making the critical move
  • polishing prose
