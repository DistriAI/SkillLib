motif-deviation

separate

Analyze TF motif accessibility variability across samples or single cells using chromVAR. Use when identifying TF motifs whose accessibility correlates with conditions, computing per-sample motif z-scores after matched background correction, comparing to ArchR / Signac equivalents, or distinguishing motif-accessibility signal from per-site footprinting.

When to use

  • identifying TF motifs whose accessibility correlates with conditions
  • computing per-sample motif z-scores
  • comparing to ArchR / Signac equivalents
  • distinguishing motif-accessibility signal from per-site footprinting
  • per-cell TF activity in scATAC
  • Which TF families distinguish my cell clusters?

When not to use

  • per-site footprinting
  • Is THIS specific motif site bound by a TF?
  • Per-cell TF binding at specific sites
  • Differential bound vs unbound between conditions
  • TF activity inference requiring paired RNA-seq
