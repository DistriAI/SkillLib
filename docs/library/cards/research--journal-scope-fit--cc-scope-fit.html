cc-scope-fit

separate

Use when deciding whether a molecular / translational oncology study belongs in Cancer Cell (Cell Press) before investing in full submission. Diagnoses mechanism + translational-relevance fit; it does not design experiments or edit prose.

When to use

  • deciding if Cancer Cell is the right venue
  • diagnosing mechanism and translational-relevance fit
  • considering a Cell Press presubmission inquiry
  • deciding between Cancer Cell and other journals
  • evaluating fit for mechanistic cancer biology
  • assessing translational oncology studies

When not to use

  • designing experiments
  • editing prose
  • methods or tool papers without cancer-biology discovery
  • descriptive omics or atlas with no mechanism
  • strong clinical correlation but no mechanism
