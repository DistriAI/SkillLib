operations-research

separate

Use when targeting Operations Research (OR) or deciding whether an optimization / stochastic-models / queueing / applied-mathematical-modeling manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Operations Research
  • deciding venue fit for optimization manuscripts
  • deciding venue fit for stochastic-models manuscripts
  • deciding venue fit for queueing manuscripts
  • deciding venue fit for applied-mathematical-modeling manuscripts
  • re-framing applied modeling papers around methodological advance
  • identifying OR desk-reject risks
  • providing INFORMS alternative list

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • pure benchmark engineering without a methodological contribution
  • application or case study with no methodological advance
  • incremental extensions with trivial or unproven results
  • managerial-insight paper better suited to an OM venue
  • behavioral/empirical paper with no OR methodology
