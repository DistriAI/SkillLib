pmla-scholarly-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a PMLA (Publications of the Modern Language Association) essay within the critical conversation so it reads as a significant contribution to literary and language studies. PMLA readers span periods, languages, and schools, so the essay must engage the criticism they expect and stake a clear intervention. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the literature survey.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction
  • Revising the intervention paragraph
  • Engaging the live critical disagreement
  • Distinguishing the reading from closest prior critics
  • Addressing feedback about ignoring obvious criticism
  • Reaching the wider PMLA membership
  • Cross-field engagement for single-author or period-specific essays

When not to use

  • Writing the literature survey
  • Developing the argument structure
  • Handling submission logistics
