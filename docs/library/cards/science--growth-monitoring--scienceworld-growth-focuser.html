scienceworld-growth-focuser

separate

Use when you have planted a seed or need to track a plant's growth stage (sprouting, flowering, reproduction). Applies the 'focus on' action to a specific plant or biological entity to signal intent and monitor its development. Trigger after planting or when you need to observe life cycle progression in the ScienceWorld environment.

When to use

  • Apply the `focus on` action to a specific biological entity (e.g., a planted seed or growing plant) to signal your intent to monitor its development. This action is often a prerequisite for the environment to process growth stages or trigg…
  • **After Planting:** You have just placed a seed or seedling into a suitable growth medium (e.g., soil with water).
  • **Stage Monitoring:** You need to check or trigger a progression in the plant's life cycle (e.g., from seed to sprout, sprout to mature plant, flowering, or reproduction).
  • **Intent Signaling:** The task requires you to explicitly indicate which entity you are observing or nurturing.
