management-review

separate

Use when targeting 《管理评论》(Management Review — 中国科学院大学主办的管理学术月刊, 全程匿名审稿) or deciding whether a Chinese management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/keyword/citation house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《管理评论》
  • deciding if a Chinese management manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese management manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency desk-reject risks
  • checking abstract, keyword, and citation house style
  • verifying official submission requirements

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to other journals like 《管理科学学报》 or 《管理世界》
  • macro policy effect evaluation without management theory
  • quantitative models or algorithms as core contribution
