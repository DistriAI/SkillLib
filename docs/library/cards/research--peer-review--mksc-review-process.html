mksc-review-process

separate

Use when understanding how Marketing Science evaluates a manuscript — the double-anonymous Senior-Editor / Associate-Editor routing, what the first-pass and review look for, and how to read a decision letter. Explains the process; it does not draft the revision response (mksc-rebuttal).

When to use

  • Understanding MKSC manuscript evaluation
  • Interpreting decision letters
  • Explaining double-anonymous review process
  • Identifying first-pass screening criteria
  • Mapping reviewer points to model/identification/fit
  • Assessing venue fit against neighboring journals
  • Creating a decision ledger for reviewer objections

When not to use

  • Drafting the revision response
  • Single-blind economics submission review
