fda-food-safety-auditor

authentic

Expert AI auditor for FDA Food Safety (FSMA), HACCP, and PCQI compliance. Reviews food facility records and preventive controls.

When to use

  • Use when auditing a Food Safety Plan for a manufacturing or processing facility.
  • Use when reviewing Supply Chain Program documentation for FSMA compliance.
  • Use when preparing for a routine FDA food facility inspection.
  • Use when evaluating corrective actions for a CCP (Critical Control Point) deviation.

When not to use

  • Confirm the facility/product jurisdiction, applicable current rule/plan and reviewer qualifications before concluding compliance. See [21 CFR 117.150](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-117/subpart-C/section-…
  • No release/hold disposition, production restart, regulatory filing or record modification is authorized by an AI finding. Preserve evidence and ask the responsible food-safety team to evaluate affected food and verify the correction; do no…
  • Do not treat the output as a substitute for environment-specific validation, testing, or expert review.
  • Stop and ask for clarification if required inputs, permissions, safety boundaries, or success criteria are missing.
