journal-of-monetary-economics

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Monetary Economics (JME) or deciding whether a macro / monetary manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Monetary Economics
  • deciding if a macro manuscript fits JME
  • re-framing empirical-macro or monetary-policy papers
  • identifying JME desk-reject risks
  • choosing among macro field journals
  • checking JME house style and structure
  • verifying official submission guidelines

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • general-interest macro at top-5 ambition
  • long-run growth and development questions
  • money, banking, and policy-facing applied macro-finance
  • macro-finance asset pricing
  • open-economy / international macro
  • pure macro theory mechanism
