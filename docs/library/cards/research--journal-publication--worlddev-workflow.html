worlddev-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which worlddev-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal for a World Development (WD) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which worlddev-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal
  • Routing WD-targeted drafts
  • Handling WD decision letters and revision mode
  • Guiding quantitatively-trained authors with qualitative or mixed-methods papers

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized worlddev-* skills
  • Treating WD as an econometrics journal
  • Treating WD as interchangeable with Journal of Development Economics
  • Treating WD as World Bank Economic Review
  • Treating WD as Journal of Development Studies
