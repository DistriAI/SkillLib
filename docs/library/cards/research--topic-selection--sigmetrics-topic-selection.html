sigmetrics-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a computer-systems performance project belongs at ACM SIGMETRICS or should be routed to IMC, SIGCOMM/NSDI/OSDI, INFOCOM, a learning venue (NeurIPS/ICML), or a performance journal (Performance Evaluation/TON/QUESTA), and when picking the right SIGMETRICS track (Theory / Measurement & Applied Modeling / Learning / Operational Systems).

When to use

  • deciding venue for computer-systems performance projects
  • routing to IMC, SIGCOMM, NSDI, OSDI, INFOCOM, or learning venues
  • routing to performance journals like Performance Evaluation, TON, or QUESTA
  • picking the right SIGMETRICS track
  • selecting between Theory, Measurement & Applied Modeling, Learning, or Operational Systems tracks

When not to use

  • drafting the paper
  • choosing the deadline
  • defining the paper shape
