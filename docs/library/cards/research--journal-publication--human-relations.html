human-relations

separate

Use when targeting Human Relations or deciding whether an interdisciplinary social-science-of-work / organizations manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Human Relations
  • deciding venue fit for interdisciplinary social-science-of-work manuscripts
  • assessing fit for critical or qualitative studies of work
  • evaluating interdisciplinary theorizing across OB, sociology, and industrial relations
  • identifying desk-reject risks
  • providing alternative journal lists
  • checking official submission guidelines
  • assessing method and evidence bar

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • narrow technical results without conceptual insight
  • descriptive or atheoretical empirical papers
  • polemical critical work
  • applied HR or managerial-prescription papers without theoretical payoff
