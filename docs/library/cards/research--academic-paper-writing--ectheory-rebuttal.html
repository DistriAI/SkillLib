ectheory-rebuttal

separate

Use when an Econometric Theory (ET) decision letter arrives and a response strategy and letter are needed — handling proof-checking referee objections, fixing assumptions and gaps, and using the online Supplement, then writing a point-by-point response. It structures the revision, not the proofs themselves.

When to use

  • ET decision letter response strategy
  • Handling proof-checking referee objections
  • Fixing assumptions and gaps
  • Using the online Supplement
  • Writing a point-by-point response letter
  • Structuring the revision
  • Prioritizing conflicting referee demands
  • Classifying referee comments

When not to use

  • Writing the proofs themselves
  • Writing the response letter before manuscript proofs reflect fixes
  • Promising to address gaps in future work
  • Hand-waving disputed steps
  • Defensive tone treating proof-check as an attack
  • Letting a fix push the Article past 50 pages
