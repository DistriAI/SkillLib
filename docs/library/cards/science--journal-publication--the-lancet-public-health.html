the-lancet-public-health

separate

Use when targeting The Lancet Public Health or deciding whether a population-health study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the population-level intervention, epidemiology, and policy evidence bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, Lancet specialty house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • population-level interventions
  • health policy and systems research
  • epidemiology and disease burden studies
  • global-health and health-equity research
  • modelling with public-health decision value
  • systematic reviews on population-health questions
  • venue selection and re-framing checks

When not to use

  • individual-level clinical studies
  • drug or device effect studies in patients
  • clinical or regulatory advice
