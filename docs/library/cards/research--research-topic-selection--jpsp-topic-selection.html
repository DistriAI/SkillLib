jpsp-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a personality/social-psychology project fits the Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP) and — critically — which of the three independently edited sections (ASC, IRGP, PPID) it belongs to. Tests theoretical-contribution fit and section choice; it does not generate research ideas.

When to use

  • Deciding if a project is a JPSP paper
  • Choosing among ASC, IRGP, and PPID sections
  • Reframing single-study findings into JPSP-scale packages
  • Testing theoretical-contribution fit
  • Resolving borderline section routing
  • Scoring manuscript venue fit and novelty
  • Comparing against sibling venues

When not to use

  • Generating research ideas
  • Polishing format without checking official rules
  • Providing submission-ready advice without checking official-source-map.md
