composites-part-b-engineering

separate

Use when targeting Composites Part B: Engineering or deciding whether a composite-materials or composite-structures manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the composites-science bar spanning materials and structures, characterization and validation rigor, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for composite-materials or composite-structures manuscripts
  • re-framing papers into composites-science mechanism, manufacturing, or structural contribution
  • choosing between Composites Part B and metallurgy, plasticity, or AM-focused venues
  • assessing composites-rigor bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • mechanics of composites including damage, delamination, fatigue, impact, and failure
  • composite manufacturing and processing with process–property links
  • characterization and multiscale modeling of composite response
  • multifunctional, bio-based, recyclable, and sustainable composites

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
  • papers reporting marginally improved laminates with no mechanism
  • generic mechanics studies where the composite is incidental
