critical-inquiry

separate

Use when targeting Critical Inquiry or deciding whether a theoretical or interdisciplinary critical-humanities manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's high-theory fit, the agenda-setting argument bar, conceptual-stakes and cross-disciplinary expectations, house style and double-blind norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for Critical Inquiry
  • elevating field-bound arguments into conceptual interventions
  • choosing between Critical Inquiry and other venues
  • assessing agenda-setting bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • theory and critique across arts and humanities
  • interdisciplinary readings with larger arguments
  • reflections on method and interpretation stakes
  • philosophy and arts seam arguments

When not to use

  • solid disciplinary case studies
  • careful application of existing theory
  • readings with no conceptual reach
  • single-field essays better served by specialist journals
  • broad-readership literary essays less theory-forward
  • replacing current submission guidelines
