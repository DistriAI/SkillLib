jmcb-writing-style

separate

Use when the prose — abstract, introduction, and the policy framing — is the bottleneck for a Journal of Money, Credit and Banking (JMCB) manuscript. Makes the monetary/banking idea and its policy payoff land for a specialist-but-policy audience; it is a late-stage polish, not a substitute for fixing the analysis.

When to use

  • Polishing JMCB abstracts
  • Structuring JMCB introductions
  • Improving policy framing
  • Interpreting results for policy readers
  • Recovering space under the 40-page cap
  • Refining the JMCB voice

When not to use

  • Fixing the analysis
  • Substituting for analytical corrections
