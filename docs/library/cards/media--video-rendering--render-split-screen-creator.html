render-split-screen-creator

separate

Assemble a split-screen creator ad from a config: an AI-creator lip-sync take fills the bottom half while real 16:9 product or demo clips play uncropped in the top half over a blurred fill of the same clip (never black bars), with a brand-colour divider between them. Scenes follow the voiceover timing and are joined hard, an end card holds about 3 seconds, then the finished cut is transcribed and word-level captions are burned in the chosen style. Free, deterministic assembly and captions; the voiceover comes from create-vo-elevenlabs, the creator anchor from create-image-gpt-image-fal, and the lip-sync take is supplied as an input. Use for the split-screen-creator format.

When to use

  • split-screen-creator format
  • two-zone vertical composite
  • hard-concat scenes
  • end card assembly
  • word-level caption burn
  • blurred-cover fill
  • deterministic assembly

When not to use

  • creative choices
  • voiceover generation
  • AI-creator anchor generation
  • lip-sync take generation
