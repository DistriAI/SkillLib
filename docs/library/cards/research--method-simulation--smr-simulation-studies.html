smr-simulation-studies

separate

Use when designing the Monte Carlo simulation study for a Sociological Methods & Research (SMR) paper — data-generating processes, competing methods, performance metrics, and the regimes where the method wins or breaks. Designs the simulation; does not derive properties or run the real-data illustration.

When to use

  • designing Monte Carlo simulation studies
  • specifying data-generating processes
  • selecting competing methods
  • defining performance metrics
  • identifying regimes where methods win or break

When not to use

  • deriving analytical properties
  • running real-data illustrations
