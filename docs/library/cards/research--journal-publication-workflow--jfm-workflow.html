jfm-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jfm-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing work on a Journal of Financial Markets (JFM) manuscript from topic selection through rebuttal. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized microstructure-focused skills.

When to use

  • deciding which jfm-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing work on a JFM manuscript
  • routing from topic selection through rebuttal
  • managing JFM manuscript workflow
  • routing based on paper archetype
  • handling JFM decision letters
  • planning JFM rebuttals
  • preflighting JFM submissions

When not to use

  • replacing specialized microstructure-focused skills
  • broad-finance generalist tasks
  • corporate finance mechanisms
  • broad asset pricing mechanisms
  • banking mechanisms with thin liquidity wrappers
