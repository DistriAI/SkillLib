energy-storage-materials

separate

Use when targeting Energy Storage Materials or deciding whether an electrochemical-energy-storage materials manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the materials-structure-property-mechanism bar, characterization rigor, house style, the materials-vs-device routing, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • electrode and electrolyte materials manuscripts
  • structure-property-performance relationships
  • storage mechanism analysis
  • batteries and supercapacitors
  • materials characterization rigor
  • venue selection between materials and device journals
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • re-framing synthesis papers into mechanism stories

When not to use

  • device-engineering papers with no new materials understanding
  • synthesis papers with property numbers but no mechanism
  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
