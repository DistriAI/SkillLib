poq-survey-design-and-measurement

separate

Use when defending the survey design and measurement of a Public Opinion Quarterly (POQ) manuscript through the Total Survey Error framework — coverage, sampling, nonresponse, measurement (question wording/order/scales), mode effects, and weighting. POQ is the leading survey-methodology journal and expects AAPOR-standard rigor. Strengthens the design; it does not write code.

When to use

  • Specifying sampling frame, questionnaire, mode, or weighting scheme
  • Defending coverage, nonresponse, question wording, mode, or representativeness
  • Building Appendix A: Disclosure Elements
  • Designing survey experiments (wording/order split-ballot, conjoint, list experiment)
  • Auditing Total Survey Error chain components
  • Estimating and auditing survey design using StatsPAI or Stata MCP

When not to use

  • Writing code
