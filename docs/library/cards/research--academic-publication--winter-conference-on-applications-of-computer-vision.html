winter-conference-on-applications-of-computer-vision

separate

Use when targeting IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for computer vision applications.

When to use

  • targeting IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for computer vision applications
  • applied computer vision, datasets, systems, and methods with practical evaluation
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • manuscripts where the central contribution is not genuinely in computer vision applications
  • graphics/visualization results where vision is not the core
  • flagship vision contributions better suited to CVPR/ICCV/ECCV
  • Asian community/cycle fit better suited to ACCV
