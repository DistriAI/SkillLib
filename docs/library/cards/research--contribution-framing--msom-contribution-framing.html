msom-contribution-framing

separate

Use when turning analytical or empirical results into an explicit operations contribution for a Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM) manuscript — stating the managerial implication, the new operational insight, and why an operations decision is central, including the structured-abstract framing M&SOM requires.

When to use

  • turning analytical or empirical results into an explicit operations contribution
  • stating the managerial implication
  • stating the new operational insight
  • explaining why an operations decision is central
  • structured-abstract framing for M&SOM
  • writing the introduction's contribution paragraph
  • framing contribution for target Department
  • referee-pushback patterns and venue fix

When not to use

  • methodological novelty alone
  • generic implications for managers with no operational lever named
  • framing the work outside any department's scope
  • structured abstract missing the Managerial-implications section
  • structured abstract stuffed with notation
