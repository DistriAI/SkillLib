academic-writing

separate

Activate when the user is drafting any academic economics content from scratch (e.g., outlines, abstracts, introductions, data/methods/identification sections, results narratives, conclusions, referee responses, table/figure captions) and needs economics-specific structure, phrasing options, and quality checks to produce publication-ready prose.

When to use

  • drafting academic economics content from scratch
  • creating outlines and paragraph plans
  • writing abstracts and introductions
  • drafting data and identification sections
  • writing results narratives and conclusions
  • drafting referee responses
  • creating table and figure captions
  • polishing user-provided drafts

When not to use

  • copying template sentences word-for-word
  • inventing estimates, effect sizes, or citations
  • using causal language for correlational designs
