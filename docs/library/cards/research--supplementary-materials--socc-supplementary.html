socc-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what belongs in an ACM SoCC paper body versus its released artifact and appendices, covering the acmart 12-page (full) or 6-page (short) budget with unlimited references, the rule that decision-critical evidence stays inside the reviewed pages, dual-anonymous supplementary material, and how to split a cloud-systems measurement paper between body and package.

When to use

  • deciding what belongs in an ACM SoCC paper body versus its released artifact and appendices
  • covering the acmart 12-page (full) or 6-page (short) budget with unlimited references
  • ensuring decision-critical evidence stays inside the reviewed pages
  • managing dual-anonymous supplementary material
  • splitting a cloud-systems measurement paper between body and package
  • assembling SoCC supplementary material
  • checking page-budget discipline
  • verifying dual-anonymous supplementary rules

When not to use

  • writing the main body of the paper
  • conducting the actual experiments or measurements
  • designing the cloud system mechanism
  • creating the raw logs or workload generators
  • managing the repository or code hosting
