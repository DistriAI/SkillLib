soda-workflow

separate

Use when planning a SODA (ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms) campaign end to end — backward-planning to the July AoE deadline, SODA's seat in the theory calendar between STOC and FOCS deadlines, the September rebuttal and October decision, satellite deadlines (ALENEX, SOSA), and the January conference.

When to use

  • planning a SODA campaign end to end
  • backward-planning to the July AoE deadline
  • managing SODA's seat in the theory calendar
  • handling September rebuttal and October decision
  • managing satellite deadlines (ALENEX, SOSA)
  • preparing for the January conference
  • triaging paper status in deadline week
  • assigning owner discipline for group submissions

When not to use

  • planning for STOC or FOCS deadlines
  • planning for ITCS deadlines
  • conducting new mathematics during rebuttal
  • broadening claims to justify a rush
