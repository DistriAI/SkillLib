socs-abstract-keywords

separate

Use to write the abstract and keywords for 《社会学研究》 (Sociological Studies) per the journal's norms — Chinese abstract ≤ 200 characters, 3–5 Chinese keywords, English abstract ≤ 150 words, leading with the sociological problematic and the mechanism/concept rather than background. Facts from resources/journal-profile.md.

When to use

  • writing abstracts for Sociological Studies
  • writing keywords for Sociological Studies
  • revising abstracts to be proposition-first
  • checking abstract word counts
  • selecting theoretical and methodological keywords

When not to use

  • writing the main body text
  • defining the research problem
  • developing mechanisms or concepts
  • providing policy recommendations
