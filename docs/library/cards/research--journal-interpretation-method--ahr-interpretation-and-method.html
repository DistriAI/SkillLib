ahr-interpretation-and-method

separate

Use when choosing and defending an interpretive approach for an American Historical Review (AHR) manuscript — social, cultural, intellectual, political, economic, transnational, global, microhistory, or the history of knowledge. The AHR welcomes many methods judged on their own terms. Sharpens interpretation and scale; it does not impose a single template.

When to use

  • Choosing an interpretive approach for an AHR manuscript
  • Defending an interpretive approach
  • Deciding the analytical lens and scale
  • Bridging an interpretive claim to documents
  • Addressing method-fit reviewer complaints
  • Selecting between social, cultural, intellectual, political, economic, transnational, global, or microhistory approaches

When not to use

  • Imposing a single template
  • Statistical significance analysis
  • Robustness table generation
