journal-of-economic-literature

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Economic Literature (JEL) or deciding whether a survey/review article fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, contribution bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • writing or proposing a survey/review for JEL
  • deciding if a field-defining synthesis fits JEL
  • framing a synthesis/agenda-setting review
  • checking JEL proposal route and desk-reject risks
  • evaluating fit for comprehensive authoritative surveys
  • assessing review essays on influential books or bodies of work
  • evaluating methodological or conceptual syntheses

When not to use

  • original empirical estimation
  • new model development
  • narrow papers
  • accessible non-technical symposium pieces
  • original empirical or theoretical contributions
  • field-specific surveys better placed in field outlets
  • methods-focused reviews for methods venues
