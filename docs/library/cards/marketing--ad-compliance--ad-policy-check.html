ad-policy-check

separate

Checks paid ads against the ad platform's rules (Meta, TikTok, Google, LinkedIn and others) and the brand's category rules: the copy, on-image or on-screen text, pictures and people. Rewrites what can be fixed honestly, adds the disclosures that are needed, and flags anything that needs a human or legal review, with one pass, fixed or flag line per ad and platform. Use alone when you already have the ad text or images and know where they will run; otherwise run the playbook for the job (e.g. make-static-ads, make-content, email-lifecycle).

When to use

  • Checking paid ads against platform rules
  • Checking ads against brand category rules
  • Rewriting ad copy that can be fixed honestly
  • Adding required disclosures to ads
  • Flagging ads needing human or legal review
  • Reviewing on-image or on-screen text
  • Reviewing pictures and people in ads

When not to use

  • Organic content
  • Email content
  • Creator content
  • Legal advice
  • Claims checking
