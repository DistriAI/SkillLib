newms-transparency-and-data

separate

Use when handling research ethics, transparency, and data sharing for a New Media & Society (NM&S) manuscript — consent and anonymization, the ethics of scraping and platform ToS, qualitative analytic transparency, and any quantitative data/code sharing. Prepares ethics and documentation; it does not over-state requirements.

When to use

  • Planning or reporting consent, anonymization, IRB/ethics approval
  • Scraping or using platform data with ToS and consent considerations
  • Documenting qualitative or computational work for credibility
  • Addressing reviewer questions on verification or ethics concerns
  • Handling research ethics for online and platform data
  • Preparing ethics and documentation without over-stating requirements

When not to use

  • Over-stating NM&S policy as a mandatory verified replication deposit
  • Promising open data that consent, ToS, or confidentiality forbid
