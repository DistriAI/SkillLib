jmsc-fit-positioning

separate

Use when judging whether a manuscript fits 《管理科学学报》 (Journal of Management Sciences in China) — a 数理/定量 (mathematical / quantitative) management-science journal whose deliverable is a model plus provable properties plus an algorithm. Use when the draft is regression coefficients + policy talk, or survey-SEM, to detect OFF-FIT and re-route to 管理世界 / 南开管理评论 / 金融研究. Use before drafting and whenever a reviewer asks whether the contribution is in method or in conclusion.
