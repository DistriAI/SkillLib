curranthro-data-analysis

separate

Use when executing and reporting the analysis/interpretation for a Current Anthropology (CA) manuscript so it survives expert review and the published CA✩ Comments — disciplined ethnographic/qualitative inference, honest interpretation, and (for biological/archaeological work) sound quantitative analysis. Guides analysis and interpretation norms; it does not fabricate evidence or quotations.

When to use

  • Building results/interpretation sections from fieldnotes, transcripts, material, or measurements
  • Responding to reviewer requests for evidence, alternative readings, or robustness
  • Reconciling interlocutors' statements with actions or pre-planned vs. emergent themes
  • Making analysis transparent without exposing protected sources
  • Preparing a comment-facing analysis ledger for CA Major Articles
  • Documenting mixed-methods or computational procedures on field/archival data

When not to use

  • Fabricating evidence or quotations
  • Design decisions (covered by curranthro-research-design)
  • Transparency and data management specifics (covered by curranthro-transparency-and-data)
