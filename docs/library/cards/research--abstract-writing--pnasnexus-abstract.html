pnasnexus-abstract

separate

Use to write the PNAS Nexus abstract — a single self-contained paragraph of up to 250 words, with no headings, quantified and accessible to a broad scientific audience. Distinguishes the abstract (what/how/found, for scientists) from the 50–120-word Significance Statement (why-it-matters, for everyone). Late-stage polish.

When to use

  • Writing PNAS Nexus abstracts
  • Late-stage abstract polish
  • Ensuring abstracts are single paragraphs without headings
  • Distinguishing abstracts from Significance Statements
  • Quantifying key results in abstracts

When not to use

  • Writing Significance Statements
  • Tasks where fit, structure, figures, stats, or data are not settled
  • Abstracts that are methods recaps with no result
  • Abstracts over 250 words or with subheadings
