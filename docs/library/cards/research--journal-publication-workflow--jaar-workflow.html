jaar-workflow

separate

Use as the entry point for any Journal of the American Academy of Religion (JAAR) manuscript. Routes to the right JAAR sub-skill by lifecycle stage, and enforces the journal's gatekeeping demand up front — the article must speak to a question of broad and fundamental interest to the study of religion, not just a subfield. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a JAAR article
  • Determining which skill applies next
  • Reframing subfield-only drafts
  • Routing decision letters
  • Resolving mid-project complaints
  • Enforcing broad-interest gate

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Empirical social science analysis
  • Using footnotes for citations
  • Planning unsolicited book reviews
