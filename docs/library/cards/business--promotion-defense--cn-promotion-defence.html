cn-promotion-defence

authentic

Prepare a promotion defence (晋升答辩) for a level-based promotion process common at Chinese technology companies: the materials, the presentation structure, the evidence for the next level, and a rehearsal of the committee's questions. Use when asked 帮我准备晋升答辩, 晋升述职, 晋升 PPT, how do I prepare for my promotion committee, or 答辩问题. Produces a level-gap analysis against the target level's criteria, the defence presentation outline in Chinese, a timed talk track, and a question bank with answer points, then a mock committee round.
