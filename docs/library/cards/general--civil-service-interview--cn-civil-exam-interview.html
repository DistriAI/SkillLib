cn-civil-exam-interview

authentic

Practise the structured interview (结构化面试) for Chinese civil service and public institution posts (公务员面试 / 事业单位面试): the common question types, a clear answer structure for each, timed mock rounds and feedback on content, structure and delivery. Use when asked 公务员面试怎么准备, 结构化面试题, 帮我模拟面试, 综合分析题怎么答, 应急应变题, or practise the civil service interview. Produces a question-type guide, answer frameworks with worked examples, a timed mock interview asked one question at a time, scored feedback, and a practice plan up to the interview date.
