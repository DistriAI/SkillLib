webconf-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or revising a Web Conference (WWW) paper's prose — building the web-native framing that answers "why is this a WWW paper" on page one, writing for a mixed computing/social-science/economics readership, compressing into 8 self-contained pages, and handling scale numbers and platform names with precision.

When to use

  • drafting WWW paper prose
  • revising WWW paper prose
  • building web-native framing
  • writing for mixed computing/social-science/economics readership
  • compressing into 8 self-contained pages
  • handling scale numbers and platform names with precision
  • defining constructs at first use
  • structuring introduction and abstracts

When not to use

  • topic selection
  • supplementary material management
