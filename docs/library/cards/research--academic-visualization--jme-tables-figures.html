jme-tables-figures

separate

Use when designing the main-text exhibits of a Journal of Monetary Economics (JME) manuscript under the strict 40-page / no-more-than-10-tables-and-figures-combined cap — impulse-response plots, FEVDs, moment tables — and deciding what to push to the online supplementary appendix.

When to use

  • Designing impulse-response plots
  • Creating FEVD exhibits
  • Formatting moment tables
  • Deciding which exhibits go to online supplement
  • Checking exhibit count against 10-item cap
  • Verifying page count against 40-page limit
  • Formatting tables and figures with Arabic numerals

When not to use

  • DSGE calibration
  • Endnotes usage
  • Exceeding 10 tables and figures in main text
