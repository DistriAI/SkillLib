jebo-writing-style

separate

Use when a Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization (JEBO) manuscript's prose buries the behavioral mechanism, or the abstract and introduction do not land for a pluralist behavioral audience. Shapes the mechanism-forward narrative and Elsevier-format front matter; it does not change the analysis.

When to use

  • JEBO manuscript prose
  • Abstract and introduction structure
  • Behavioral mechanism narrative
  • Elsevier-format front matter
  • Jargon calibration for plural audience
  • Keywords and JEL classification
  • Reference formatting (Elsevier Harvard)

When not to use

  • Changing the analysis
