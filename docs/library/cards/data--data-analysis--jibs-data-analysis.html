jibs-data-analysis

separate

Use when running and reporting the empirical analysis for a Journal of International Business Studies (JIBS) manuscript — cross-national measurement equivalence, common-method-variance checks, the right multilevel/dynamic-panel estimator, endogeneity and dynamic-endogeneity identification, and robustness aligned to the JIBS methods-editorial canon. Executes and reports; it does not design the study (jibs-methods) or frame the contribution (jibs-contribution-framing).
