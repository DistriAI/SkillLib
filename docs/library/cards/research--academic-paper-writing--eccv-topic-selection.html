eccv-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a computer-vision project should target ECCV — weighing the two-year even-year cadence against CVPR, ICCV, WACV, BMVC, ACCV, NeurIPS, and journal routes, testing whether the work matches ECCV's breadth from geometry to vision-language, and reading the Koenderink Prize lineage as a durability signal.

When to use

  • deciding if a project should target ECCV
  • weighing ECCV against CVPR, ICCV, WACV, BMVC, ACCV, NeurIPS, and journals
  • testing if work matches ECCV's breadth from geometry to vision-language
  • reading the Koenderink Prize lineage as a durability signal
  • evaluating the even-year calendar bet
  • assessing substrate independence and evidence budget

When not to use

  • projects where the pitch depends on beating a SOTA number being chased by multiple groups
  • core contributions that are engineering integration without an isolable idea
  • papers whose audience is one company's product context rather than the research community
  • contributions needing more than 14 LNCS pages of development
  • learning contributions where vision is just one testbed
