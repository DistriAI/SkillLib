Skill page

Conflict

The two copies are not reproductions of the same procedure. The first is a concrete, self-contained skill for building a knowledge graph from a list of concepts and exporting it to Mermaid. The second is a meta-skill (an index) that routes to nine different knowledge-graph-related skills, including ontology design, citation networks, and specific engines like Graphiti and OpenSPG. Neither contains the material steps of the other, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
