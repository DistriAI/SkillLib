design-negotiation

separate

Advocate for design quality, scope, and timeline with partners and leadership using evidence and shared goals. Use in the conversation itself. For the commercial vocabulary behind it, use `business-design` (ux-strategy).

When to use

  • Advocating for design quality
  • Navigating scope compression
  • Managing timeline pressure
  • Addressing organizational under-investment
  • Translating design needs for PMs and engineers
  • Handling stakeholder overrides
  • Making resource requests
  • Building long-term influence

When not to use

  • Commercial vocabulary behind design
