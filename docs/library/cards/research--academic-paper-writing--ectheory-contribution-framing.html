ectheory-contribution-framing

separate

Use to frame the central result of an Econometric Theory (ET) paper so its generality and importance are legible — stating the theorem and its scope up front, choosing Article vs Miscellanea framing, and matching the new editorial program's themed directions.

When to use

  • Framing central results for Econometric Theory
  • Stating theorem scope and assumptions
  • Choosing between Article and Miscellanea formats
  • Aligning contributions with ET themed directions
  • Defining generality delta vs known results
  • Structuring introduction for theory papers

When not to use

  • Data analysis
  • Identification strategy
  • Empirical application
  • Software release
  • Submission to Phillips Award
  • Submission to ET Interview
