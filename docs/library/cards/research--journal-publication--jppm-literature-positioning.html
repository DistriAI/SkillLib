jppm-literature-positioning

separate

Use when situating a Journal of Public Policy & Marketing (JPP&M) manuscript against both the marketing-and-policy literature and the regulatory record, and defending the JPP&M lane against siblings. Positions the contribution; it does not build the framework (jppm-theory-development) or design studies (jppm-methods).

When to use

  • situating a JPP&M manuscript against marketing-and-policy literature
  • situating a JPP&M manuscript against the regulatory record
  • defending the JPP&M lane against sibling journals
  • identifying decision-relevant unknowns for named stakeholders
  • mapping the JPP&M research stream and claiming the bend
  • citing the agency as an interlocutor
  • bridging disciplines explicitly
  • date-stamping the debate

When not to use

  • building the framework
  • designing studies
