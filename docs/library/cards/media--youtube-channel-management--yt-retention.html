yt-retention

separate

Read a YouTube Studio audience-retention export and find where viewers actually leave, then say what to change. Use for "why do people stop watching", "my retention is bad", a pasted retention chart or CSV, or "fix my pacing".

When to use

  • diagnosing why people stop watching
  • analyzing bad retention
  • processing pasted retention charts or CSVs
  • fixing video pacing
  • identifying hook leaks
  • detecting retention cliffs
  • analyzing steady bleed or slide

When not to use

  • publishing content
  • packaging videos when retention is healthy
