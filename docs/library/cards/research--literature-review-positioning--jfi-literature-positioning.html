jfi-literature-positioning

separate

Use when working on a Journal of Financial Intermediation manuscript to stake the contribution against the banking and financial-intermediation frontier — name the closest intermediation papers and the precise mechanism gap you fill. It positions; it does not write a standalone survey.

When to use

  • Writing or revising the introduction and related-work section for a JFI paper
  • Clarifying contribution relative to existing intermediation work
  • Positioning against the banking and financial-intermediation frontier
  • Naming the two to four closest papers
  • Stating the specific mechanism or margin added, changed, or overturned
  • Distinguishing work from neighboring fields like pure asset pricing or corporate finance
  • Positioning empirics or theory against canonical anchors and the working-paper frontier

When not to use

  • Writing a standalone survey
  • Creating a literature dump with no mechanism contrast
  • Citing only general-finance landmarks while ignoring the intermediation sub-literature
  • Claiming novelty without naming the closest paper improved upon
  • Positioning against a field journal's question rather than an intermediation mechanism
