Conflict

The copies define 'content strategy' in three distinct ways. coreyhaines31 and LeoYeAI are nearly identical marketing-focused skills (searchable vs. shareable, content pillars, distribution). kostja94 is a specialized SEO skill focused on topic clusters, pillar pages, and internal linking structures. Owl-Listener is a product/UX skill focused on content governance, content models, and voice/tone. Because they cover different domains (marketing vs. SEO vs. Product/UX) and have conflicting definitions of the task, no single copy is the 'authentic' procedure for the others.
