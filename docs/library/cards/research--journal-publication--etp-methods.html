etp-methods

separate

Use when designing or defending the research design for an Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice (ETP) manuscript — matching a method to an entrepreneurial question and handling the selection, survivorship, and process-observation problems unique to new ventures.

When to use

  • designing research for ETP manuscripts
  • matching methods to entrepreneurial questions
  • handling selection into entrepreneurship
  • addressing survivorship bias
  • ensuring process observability
  • validating entrepreneurial constructs
  • designing qualitative rigor for ETP
  • choosing between quantitative, qualitative, and mixed methods

When not to use

  • estimating models
  • framing the contribution
