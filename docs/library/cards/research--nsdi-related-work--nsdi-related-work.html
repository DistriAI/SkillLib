nsdi-related-work

separate

Use when positioning an NSDI submission against the networked-systems literature — covering the NSDI/SIGCOMM/OSDI/SOSP lanes plus both accepted-paper cohorts of the current edition, verifying venues against dblp and open-access USENIX proceedings, and handling self-citations and concurrent submissions under double-blind rules.

When to use

  • Positioning NSDI submissions against networked-systems literature
  • Sweeping NSDI, SIGCOMM, OSDI, and SOSP lanes
  • Verifying venues against dblp and USENIX proceedings
  • Handling self-citations under double-blind rules
  • Citing concurrent submissions
  • Sequencing related work sweeps across deadlines

When not to use

  • Writing the full related work section text
  • Designing experiments
  • Author response drafting
