nature-reviews-drug-discovery

separate

Use when targeting Nature Reviews Drug Discovery (Nat. Rev. Drug Discov.) or deciding whether a drug-discovery review or analysis fits this commissioned, authoritative review venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • commissioned or proposed review on a drug target
  • modality or platform reviews
  • therapeutic-area landscapes
  • drug discovery and development paradigms
  • regulatory, R&D-strategy, and pipeline analyses
  • mechanism-of-action and pharmacology reviews
  • venue selection between Nature Medicine, Nature Reviews Methods Primers, or Cell Reports Medicine
  • redirecting primary-research drug-discovery studies

When not to use

  • primary research
  • original data
  • narrow methodological updates
  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
