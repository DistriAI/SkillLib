european-urology

separate

Use when targeting European Urology or deciding whether a urology clinical/surgical study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the high-impact clinical-and-surgical-outcomes evidence bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, EAU/Elsevier house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • Uro-oncology studies
  • Surgical and robotic technique with outcomes
  • Functional and reconstructive urology
  • Endourology and stone disease
  • Diagnostic and biomarker studies
  • Comparative-effectiveness and registry work
  • Venue-fit and re-framing checks
  • Reporting-guideline and registration requirements

When not to use

  • Clinical or regulatory advice
  • Small single-center case series
  • Retrospective slices without a comparator
  • Technique notes without outcome data
  • General-medicine or oncology journal selection
