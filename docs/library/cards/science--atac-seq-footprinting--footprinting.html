footprinting

separate

Detect transcription factor binding footprints in ATAC-seq using TOBIAS, HINT-ATAC, Wellington, or scprinter. Use when identifying bound TF sites within accessible regions, correcting Tn5 insertion bias before footprinting, choosing between cleavage-based and aggregate-based footprinters, or comparing differential TF activity between conditions.

When to use

  • identifying bound TF sites within accessible regions
  • correcting Tn5 insertion bias before footprinting
  • choosing between cleavage-based and aggregate-based footprinters
  • comparing differential TF activity between conditions
  • detecting short DNA stretches of reduced Tn5 cleavage
  • per-base footprint scoring
  • motif-anchored detection

When not to use

  • tasks not stated in the skill text
