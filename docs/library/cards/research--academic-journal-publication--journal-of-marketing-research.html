journal-of-marketing-research

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Marketing Research (JMR) or deciding whether a marketing manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Marketing Research
  • deciding if a marketing manuscript fits JMR
  • positioning quantitative-modeling marketing papers
  • positioning measurement marketing papers
  • positioning behavioral-experiment marketing papers
  • framing papers with strong identification or experimental control
  • identifying JMR desk-reject risks
  • suggesting alternative marketing venues

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
