acm-conference-on-fairness-accountability-and-transparency

separate

Use when targeting ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for responsible AI.

When to use

  • targeting ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for responsible AI
  • manuscript in fairness needs a conference-fit read before being formatted or submitted
  • re-framed from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis for this venue
  • fairness, accountability, transparency, audits, policy, measurement, and socio-technical AI systems
  • responsible-AI paper whose claims are grounded in both technical measurement and social context

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • generic CS paper until the introduction names the exact subcommunity, contribution type, and proof or empirical standard
  • broader AI ethics and policy work to AIES
  • collaboration/social-platform studies to CSCW
  • pure ML methods to ML venues unless accountability or fairness is central
  • method-only fairness can fit NeurIPS/ICML
