jfr-policy-implication

separate

Use to write the policy-implication section of a 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) manuscript — addressing the monetary authority, financial regulators, or financial institutions with implications that follow from the evidence and are more operational than 经济研究, without sliding into a policy-report tone.

When to use

  • Writing policy-implication sections for 《金融研究》
  • Addressing monetary authorities, regulators, or financial institutions
  • Converting vague policy suggestions into operational recommendations
  • Ensuring policy advice is supported by manuscript evidence
  • Defining policy tools and parameter directions
  • Specifying applicable boundaries and conditions

When not to use

  • Writing policy reports or work summaries
  • Providing specific numerical policy instructions
  • Making suggestions unsupported by the manuscript's evidence
  • Using vague phrases like 'strengthen', 'improve', 'promote', or 'deepen'
