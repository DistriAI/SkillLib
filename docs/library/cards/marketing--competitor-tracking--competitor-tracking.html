Skill page

Conflict

The two skills share the name and the high-level goal of ongoing competitor monitoring, but they target different product categories and prescribe different procedures. The first is an ASO/mobile-app skill that relies on the Appeeky API to track app-store metadata, keyword rankings, ratings, chart positions, and pricing, and it outputs a weekly app-store competitive report. The second is a developer-tools marketing skill that tracks changelogs, pricing pages, positioning, content strategy, GitHub/community signals, and developer sentiment, and it outputs competitive battlecards. Neither copy is a shortened or wrapped version of the other; each contains material steps and constraints the other lacks, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
