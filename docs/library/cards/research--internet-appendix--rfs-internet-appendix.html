rfs-internet-appendix

separate

Use when deciding what belongs in the journal-hosted Internet Appendix vs. the main paper for a The Review of Financial Studies (RFS) manuscript, and how to structure it. Organizes supplementary material; does NOT generate the robustness content itself.

When to use

  • deciding what belongs in the Internet Appendix vs. the main paper
  • structuring the Internet Appendix
  • organizing supplementary material
  • planning the replication package
  • documenting adherence for Registered Reports

When not to use

  • generating the robustness content itself
