jfr-tables-figures

separate

Use to finalize the main tables and figures of a 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) manuscript to journal house style — trimming over-wide regression tables, standardizing significance/standard-error notes, and ensuring continuous numbering of tables, figures, equations, and footnotes.

When to use

  • trimming over-wide regression tables
  • standardizing significance and standard-error notes
  • ensuring continuous numbering of tables, figures, equations, and footnotes
  • converting analysis-stage outputs to submission-quality tables and figures
  • designing main tables with core specifications
  • creating event study and impulse response figures

When not to use

  • writing policy implications
  • generating raw empirical results from scratch
