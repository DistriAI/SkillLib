implement-fog-of-war

separate

Implement, tune, debug, or validate soft wall-aware fog of war and gameplay perception in Three.js action games. Use for orthographic or isometric visibility masks, obstacle-aware line of sight, player and enemy vision ranges, hidden-enemy targeting rules, fog shader artifacts such as spokes or seams, mobile ray budgets, lifecycle and menu-state integration, and deterministic fog-of-war tests.

When to use

  • soft wall-aware fog of war
  • gameplay perception
  • orthographic or isometric visibility masks
  • obstacle-aware line of sight
  • player and enemy vision ranges
  • hidden-enemy targeting rules
  • fog shader artifacts such as spokes or seams
  • deterministic fog-of-war tests

When not to use

  • inferring combat truth from fog pixels
  • introducing a second renderer, render target, composer, or per-ray meshes
  • hiding bad obstacle data with extra blur
  • making the entire scene darker to compensate for weak visibility boundaries
