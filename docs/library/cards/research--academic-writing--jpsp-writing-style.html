jpsp-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing the prose of a Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP) manuscript — long-format APA 7th writing that fits the section's capped introduction and discussion, plus a ≤250-word abstract. Guides style and structure; it does not ghost-write the paper.

When to use

  • Drafting JPSP manuscripts
  • Polishing prose for APA 7th
  • Writing abstracts under 250 words
  • Fitting introduction and discussion to word caps
  • Structuring multi-study narratives
  • Writing limitations statements

When not to use

  • Ghost-writing the paper
  • Writing short reports under 2,000 words
