nbr-discussion-contribution

separate

Use to write the discussion section for 《南开管理评论》 (Nankai Business Review) so it loops back to the introduction's gap and advances a management theory — extension, boundary condition, or integration — rather than restating results. Use when the discussion only summarizes findings, or when the theoretical-contribution paragraph repeats the hypotheses without saying what changed in the theory.

When to use

  • Writing discussion section for Nankai Business Review
  • Looping back to introduction's gap
  • Advancing management theory via extension, boundary condition, or integration
  • Handling unsupported hypotheses with theoretical explanation
  • Structuring theoretical and situational contributions
  • Calibrating discussion to journal standards

When not to use

  • Restating results or hypotheses
  • Summarizing findings without theoretical advancement
  • Writing policy-oriented implications
  • Providing vague or empty contributions
