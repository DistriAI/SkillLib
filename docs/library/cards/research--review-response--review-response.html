Skill page

Conflict

The two skills share the name 'review-response' but target completely different domains and tasks. The first skill (mohitagw15856) is for writing public customer service replies to e-commerce reviews (Google, Amazon, etc.), focusing on brand voice, de-escalation, and marketing to future shoppers. The second skill (brycewang-stanford) is for academic paper rebuttals, focusing on classifying reviewer comments, providing evidence-based justifications, and maintaining professional academic tone. They do not overlap in procedure, inputs, or output format, so crowning one would hide a real alternative use case for the same skill name.
