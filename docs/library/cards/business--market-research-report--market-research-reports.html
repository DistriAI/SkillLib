market-research-reports

combined

Builds evidence-traceable market research reports and assumption-driven market sizing or forecast scenarios. Use for market definition, industry and customer evidence, competitive landscapes, TAM/SAM/SOM reconciliation, forecast sensitivity, and auditable report scaffolds. Generate comprehensive market research reports (50+ pages) in the style of top consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Gartner). Features professional LaTeX formatting, extensive visual generation with scientific-schematics and generate-image, deep integration with research-lookup for data gathering, and multi-framework strategic analysis including Porter's Five Forces, PESTLE, SWOT, TAM/SAM/SOM, and BCG Matrix.

When to use

  • imitate or imply affiliation with a consulting, analyst, or research brand;
  • invent citations, quotes, market shares, or paid-market figures;
  • present TAM/SAM/SOM or a forecast as one certain truth;
  • treat a framework, chart, or fluent narrative as evidence;
  • provide investment, legal, antitrust, tax, accounting, or regulatory advice.
  • Creating comprehensive market analysis for investment decisions
  • Developing industry reports for strategic planning
  • Analyzing competitive landscapes and market dynamics

When not to use

  • investment advice
  • legal advice
  • antitrust advice
  • tax advice
  • accounting advice
  • regulatory advice
  • imitating consulting brands
  • inventing citations or market shares

Skill page

Conflict

The three copies share the same name and high-level goal (market research reports with TAM/SAM/SOM and strategic frameworks), but they are materially different procedures. The K-Dense copy is an evidence-audit workflow (source ledgers, claim IDs, validation scripts, uncertainty preservation) that explicitly forbids inventing citations and does not mandate a page count or specific visual tools. The Vibe-Skills copy is a consulting-style deliverable workflow (50+ pages, 6 planned visuals, LaTeX/Markdown output, routing boundaries) that does not include the evidence-ledger scripts. The LeoYeAI copy is a visual-generation-heavy workflow that hard-codes specific external skills (scientific-schematics, generate-image) and a 50+ page LaTeX output. Because each copy contains steps and constraints the others lack, and they disagree on output format, length, and tooling, crowning one would hide real alternatives.
