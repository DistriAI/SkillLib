aejpol-referee-strategy

separate

Use when anticipating how AEJ: Economic Policy referees and editors will read a manuscript, before submission or before an R&R, to pre-empt the objections that sink policy-evaluation papers. Maps likely pushback to fixes and calibrates expectations; it does not write the response letter (see aejpol-rebuttal) or fix the identification.

When to use

  • Anticipating referee objections before submission
  • Calibrating expectations for AEA single-blind process
  • Triaging weaknesses to fix vs. disclose
  • Mapping likely pushback to fixes
  • Assessing readiness for submission

When not to use

  • Writing the response letter
  • Fixing the identification
