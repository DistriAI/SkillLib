infocom-related-work

separate

Use when positioning an IEEE INFOCOM submission against the networking literature across INFOCOM, SIGCOMM, NSDI, MobiCom, ICNP, and the journals (IEEE/ACM ToN, IEEE JSAC/TMC), writing delta-first contrast rather than a citation catalog, keeping self-citations double-blind on EDAS, and handling concurrent, preprint, and prior-version overlap.

When to use

  • positioning an IEEE INFOCOM submission against the networking literature
  • writing delta-first contrast
  • keeping self-citations double-blind on EDAS
  • handling concurrent, preprint, and prior-version overlap
  • auditing novelty and eligibility
  • separating networking novelty from engineering effort
  • covering networking lanes
  • declaring overlap with prior versions

When not to use

  • citation catalog
  • re-submitting archival work as new
  • linking reviewers to identity-revealing preprints
  • citing exclusively non-networking venues
