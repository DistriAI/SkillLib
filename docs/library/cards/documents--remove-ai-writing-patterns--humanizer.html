humanizer

combined

Remove signs of AI-generated writing from text. Use when editing or reviewing text to make it sound more natural and human-written. Based on Wikipedia's comprehensive "Signs of AI writing" guide. Detects and fixes patterns including: inflated symbolism, promotional language, superficial -ing analyses, vague attributions, em dash overuse, rule of three, AI vocabulary words, negative parallelisms, and excessive conjunctive phrases. Strip AI writing patterns from text. Checks 24 patterns across 4 categories (structural, lexical, rhetorical, formatting) with academic economics adaptation. This skill should be used on any text that reads too "AI-generated", or as a final pass on drafted sections. Triggers on "humanize", "de-AI", "make it sound natural", or "strip AI patterns".

When to use

  • editing text to sound natural
  • reviewing text for AI patterns
  • removing AI-generated writing signs
  • fixing inflated symbolism
  • fixing promotional language
  • fixing superficial -ing analyses
  • fixing vague attributions
  • fixing em dash overuse

Skill page

Conflict

The first two copies are the same general-purpose humanizer skill (version 2.1.1) that removes AI writing patterns from arbitrary text and adds personality. The third copy is a specialized academic economics humanizer that operates on .tex/.md/.txt/.qmd files, checks 24 specific patterns across 4 categories, preserves econometric terminology and LaTeX, and produces a structured report. These overlap in purpose but each has material steps and constraints the others lack, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
