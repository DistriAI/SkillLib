progress-in-energy-and-combustion-science

separate

Use when targeting Progress in Energy and Combustion Science or deciding whether an energy/combustion review manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's review-only fit, the critical-synthesis-and-authority bar, comprehensiveness rigor, house style, the review-vs-primary-research routing, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Assessing if an energy or combustion review meets the venue's authority and depth bar
  • Re-framing a manuscript from primary research toward a critical synthesis
  • Deciding between this review venue and a primary-research energy/combustion journal
  • Deciding between this venue and a shorter perspective or mini-review
  • Evaluating comprehensiveness and critical-synthesis requirements
  • Applying desk-reject heuristics for the journal
  • Checking official submission requirements and pre-submission proposals

When not to use

  • Primary research manuscripts with new experiments or one model
  • Shallow literature surveys that catalog papers without critical judgment
  • Replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
