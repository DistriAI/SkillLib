wp-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing a World Politics manuscript so it reads across cases, follows the World Politics house style, and fits the limits (≤ 12,500 words including notes and references; abstract ≤ 150 words; online supplement ≤ 15 pages). Manuscripts are double spaced with the word count indicated and anonymized for triple-blind review. Tightens prose and format; it does not invent content.

When to use

  • Drafting the introduction
  • Framing the contribution
  • Final polish
  • Cutting text to meet word limits
  • Writing the abstract
  • Aligning citations and headings to house style
  • Anonymizing for triple-blind review

When not to use

  • Generating claims
  • Inventing content
