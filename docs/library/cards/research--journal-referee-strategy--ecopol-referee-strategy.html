ecopol-referee-strategy

separate

Use when preparing an Economic Policy (EP) manuscript for its panel review — the two named discussants and the conference debate that replace standard anonymous refereeing. War-games the discussants; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • Preparing an EP manuscript for panel review
  • Anticipating two named discussants
  • War-gaming the academic discussant
  • War-gaming the policy discussant
  • Building an objection ledger
  • Preparing live answers for the conference debate
  • Stress-testing the policy angle
  • Deciding what to fix before the conference

When not to use

  • Inventing evidence
  • Inventing citations
  • Preparing for anonymous written refereeing
  • Writing a defensive response letter
