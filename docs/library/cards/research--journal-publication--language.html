Use when targeting Language (the Linguistic Society of America journal) or deciding whether a linguistics manuscript fits this discipline-wide flagship. Encodes the journal's fit across all subfields, the claim-plus-data bar, theoretical and cross-linguistic expectations, house style, glossing and double-blind norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for Language
  • re-framing subfield-specific results
  • choosing between Language and specialist venues
  • Language evidence/argument bar
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • house style and glossing norms
  • official-submission re-check

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
  • providing exact reporting/statistical specifics
  • resolving conflicts with live official instructions
