fse-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing an accepted ESEC/FSE paper for its PACMSE camera-ready, covering de-anonymization, the ACM Primary Article Template and PACMSE journal metadata (DOI, ORCID, CCS concepts), integrating reviewer-required changes without scope creep, permanentizing the Data Availability links, and the ACM artifact-badge handoff.

When to use

  • preparing accepted ESEC/FSE paper for PACMSE camera-ready
  • de-anonymization of author block and tool names
  • applying ACM Primary Article Template
  • completing PACMSE journal metadata
  • integrating reviewer-required changes
  • permanentizing Data Availability links
  • ACM artifact-badge handoff

When not to use

  • formatting pass only
  • strengthening claims beyond what was evaluated
  • waiting for artifact result to finalize the paper
