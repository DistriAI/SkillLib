amj-literature-positioning

separate

Use when the front end of an Academy of Management Journal (AMJ) manuscript needs to engage the right literatures and stake a clear position in an ongoing theoretical conversation. Positions the paper among theories; it does not derive the hypotheses (amj-theory-development) or write the contribution paragraph (amj-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • engaging the right literatures
  • staking a clear position in an ongoing theoretical conversation
  • positioning the paper among theories
  • identifying the focal literature and supporting literatures
  • naming the conversation and the tension
  • placing the paper relative to the closest prior studies

When not to use

  • derive the hypotheses
  • write the contribution paragraph
