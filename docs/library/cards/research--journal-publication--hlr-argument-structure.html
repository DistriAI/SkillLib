hlr-argument-structure

separate

Use when organizing the body of a Harvard Law Review (HLR) piece along the doctrine to theory to normative-prescription arc that legal scholarship rewards. Structures the argument and its internal logic; it does not forge the thesis (hlr-thesis-and-contribution) or format the citations (hlr-sources-and-bluebook).

When to use

  • organizing the body of a Harvard Law Review piece
  • structuring the argument and its internal logic
  • deciding Part order and responsibilities
  • ensuring the prescription follows from the diagnosis
  • adjudicating the strongest counterargument
  • engaging literature at the point of the move

When not to use

  • forging the thesis
  • formatting the citations
