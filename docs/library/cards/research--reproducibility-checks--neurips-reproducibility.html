neurips-reproducibility

separate

Use when strengthening NeurIPS reproducibility evidence, aligning Paper Checklist answers with the paper, writing code/data instructions, setting random-seed and compute disclosure, or deciding whether the MLRC/TMLR reproducibility route fits better than the main track or Datasets & Benchmarks track.

When to use

  • strengthening NeurIPS reproducibility evidence
  • aligning Paper Checklist answers with the paper
  • writing code/data instructions
  • setting random-seed and compute disclosure
  • deciding whether the MLRC/TMLR reproducibility route fits better
  • main-track reproducibility bar
  • MLRC route check
  • Checklist-to-evidence cross-check

When not to use

  • ordinary main-track submissions not focused on reproduction
  • pretending full release is possible for restricted data
