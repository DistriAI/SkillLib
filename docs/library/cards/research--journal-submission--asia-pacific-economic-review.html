asia-pacific-economic-review

separate

Use when targeting 《亚太经济》(Asia-Pacific Economic Review — 福建社会科学院主办的双月刊, 1984 创刊, CN 35-1014/F, 不收版面费/审稿费) or deciding whether an Asia-Pacific regional-economics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fee-free policy, fit, house style, official re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《亚太经济》
  • deciding if Asia-Pacific regional-economics manuscript fits
  • rewriting general Chinese economics manuscript for 《亚太经济》
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit for RCEP and supply chain topics
  • checking journal fit for country-specific economics
  • checking journal fit for trade investment and cross-border finance
  • checking journal fit for China-Asia Pacific economic interaction

When not to use

  • replacing latest official submission guidelines
  • domestic policy evaluation without international focus
  • news commentary style articles
  • articles lacking comparative framework
  • articles with unverifiable data sources
  • accounting or auditing research better suited to other journals
