Use when targeting Journal of Accounting Research (JAR) or deciding whether an accounting manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Accounting Research
  • deciding if an accounting manuscript fits JAR
  • positioning archival capital-markets or analytical accounting papers
  • framing papers with strong identification strategies
  • assessing JAR desk-reject risks
  • considering the JAR annual conference issue
  • re-routing to alternative accounting venues

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
