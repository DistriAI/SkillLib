scenario-godot-audio

separate

Use when adding or fixing sound in Godot 4.7: audio buses and effects, volume sliders, 'too many sounds', combat audio with hundreds of enemies, sounds clipping or distorting, 3D sound too quiet or not panning, reverb zones not working, occlusion, adaptive or interactive music (AudioStreamInteractive, layers, stingers), music loops with gaps, SFX that repeat, AudioStreamRandomizer, AudioStreamGenerator, import settings, or importing AI-generated music, SFX and voice from Scenario or ElevenLabs.

When to use

  • audio buses and effects
  • volume sliders
  • combat audio with hundreds of enemies
  • sounds clipping or distorting
  • 3D sound too quiet or not panning
  • reverb zones not working
  • adaptive or interactive music
  • importing AI-generated music, SFX and voice
