hrm-review-process

separate

Use when calibrating expectations for the Human Resource Management (Wiley "HRM") editorial process — the EIC fit/relevance screen, double-blind peer review, the developmental R&R culture, and reading a decision letter. Explains the process and how to read decisions; it does not draft the response (hrm-rebuttal).

When to use

  • Calibrating timeline and decision-type expectations before submitting
  • Interpreting a decision letter (R&R vs. reject; major vs. minor)
  • Understanding the weight of the action editor vs. reviewers
  • Planning a revision strategy before drafting the response
  • Extracting editor's priorities from a decision letter
  • Categorizing reviewer comments into theory, method, analysis, framing, and writing
  • Separating fatal from fixable concerns
  • Identifying which half of the dual contribution is doubted

When not to use

  • Drafting the response letter
  • Drafting the rebuttal
