journal-of-econometrics

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Econometrics or deciding whether a theoretical or applied econometrics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Econometrics
  • deciding if theoretical econometrics manuscript fits
  • deciding if applied econometrics manuscript fits
  • assessing method-and-evidence bar
  • checking house style
  • official-submission re-check
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • re-framing manuscript for venue

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • standard application of existing estimator
  • empirical paper dressed as methods
  • pure theory of identification with no estimator
  • applied econometrics with modest method twist and substantive finding
