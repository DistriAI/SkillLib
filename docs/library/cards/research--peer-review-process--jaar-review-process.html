jaar-review-process

separate

Use to understand how the Journal of the American Academy of Religion (JAAR) evaluates a manuscript — editor pre-screening for broad significance, double-blind review, a roughly 90% rejection rate, a months-long timeline, and a commissioned-only book-review section. Sets expectations and shapes the article to survive; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • Stress-test manuscript against JAAR gates before submitting
  • Interpret decision letters and timelines
  • Understand JAAR editorial pre-screening
  • Understand JAAR double-blind review process
  • Assess book review submission eligibility
  • Shape article to pass JAAR review
  • Predict pre-screening bounce risks

When not to use

  • Contacting editors
  • Submitting unsolicited book reviews
  • Publishing reviews from graduate students
