the-american-historical-review

separate

Use when targeting The American Historical Review (AHR) or deciding whether a history manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the broad-significance argument bar, primary-source and historiographical expectations, Chicago house style and double-blind norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for AHR
  • re-framing specialist studies for discipline-wide significance
  • choosing between AHR and specialist history journals
  • AHR argument-significance bar
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • historiographical intervention assessment
  • Chicago house style and double-blind norms
  • official-submission re-check

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
  • narrowly specialist studies with no broader stake
  • adding a case to a settled debate without reframing
  • thin or uncritical use of primary sources
  • pieces pitched to one area community better served by specialist journals
