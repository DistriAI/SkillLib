Conflict

The two copies share the same severity table, runbook structure, and most of the Service Outage template, but they disagree on safety-critical steps. The sickn33 copy adds a 'Runbook prerequisites' section requiring binding of service/namespace/cluster context, approved incident authority, and evidence sources before adapting templates, and it explicitly states that thresholds and financial amounts are illustrative. It also replaces the direct 'pg_terminate_backend' command with a guarded procedure requiring an exact authorized PID and the approved database runbook. The rmyndharis copy lacks these constraints and includes the direct termination command. Crowning one copy would hide the other's safety constraints and the conflicting mitigation step.
