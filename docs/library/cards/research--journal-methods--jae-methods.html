jae-methods

separate

Use when designing the empirical (or analytical) approach for a Journal of Accounting and Economics (JAE) manuscript — choosing an identification strategy (natural experiments, DiD, IV, RD) for large-sample archival capital-markets/contracting/disclosure data, or structuring an economic model, so the design can credibly support the economic prediction.

When to use

  • Choosing identification strategies for large-sample archival data
  • Designing natural experiments, DiD, IV, or RD
  • Structuring analytical economic models
  • Defining sample and measurement design
  • Conducting referee pre-mortem on causal claims
  • Estimating and auditing empirical designs

When not to use

  • Normative prescriptions
  • Behavioral lab experiments
  • Design-science artifacts
