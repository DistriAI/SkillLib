render-vo-anchored-motion-listicle

separate

Assemble a narrated motion-graphic listicle video ad from a config: a voiceover (cloned or cast, in the chosen tone) reads a numbered list while N animated web-graphic beats in one branded design system (alternating tiles, big numerals, callouts) are rendered frame by frame and timed to the voiceover's words. Optional B-roll windows (a brand clip, an AI clip or a motion graphic) give the eye a break, and captions appear only inside those windows, with the voiceover mixed over a low music bed. Free, deterministic assembly; the voiceover, music and any AI clips come from create-vo-elevenlabs, create-music-elevenlabs and create-video-fal. Use for the vo-anchored-motion-listicle format.

When to use

  • narrated motion-graphic listicle video ad
  • vo-anchored-motion-listicle format
  • deterministic assembly of video beats
  • frame-by-frame rendering of web-animated hyperframes
  • window-masked caption burn
  • VO and music bed mixing
  • final video composite

When not to use

  • lipsync
  • on-camera human
  • i2v
  • free stock-footage source
  • AI-rendering the brand lockup
