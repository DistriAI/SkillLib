msom-theory-development

separate

Use when building the analytical model or operational mechanism at the core of a Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM) manuscript — formulating the decision, objective, and uncertainty; deriving structural results; or specifying the operational mechanism behind an empirical hypothesis. Adapts theory work to M&SOM's dominant analytical/stochastic-modeling tradition.

When to use

  • building analytical models
  • specifying operational mechanisms
  • formulating decision variables and objectives
  • deriving structural results
  • justifying model class
  • defining uncertainty primitives
  • mapping empirical tests to operations theory

When not to use

  • pure economics or marketing models without operational levers
  • models with no decision variable
  • correlation hunts without operational mechanism
  • topic selection
