jhr-topic-selection

separate

Use when checking whether a research question fits the Journal of Human Resources (JHR) — empirical microeconomics with a policy-relevant, causal emphasis — and to filter out off-scope "HR management" framings before paying the nonrefundable fee. A fit gate, not an idea generator.

When to use

  • checking whether a research question fits JHR
  • filtering out off-scope HR management framings
  • confirming a policy-relevant micro question is in scope
  • deciding whether a descriptive paper is JHR-worthy
  • choosing a target journal where JHR is a candidate

When not to use

  • idea generation
  • HR management research
  • personnel research
  • organizational behavior
  • theory with no empirical content
