jme-topic-selection

separate

Use when assessing whether a research question fits the Journal of Monetary Economics (JME) — monetary theory and policy, central banking, business cycles, growth, financial intermediation, fiscal interactions, and expectations — before investing in a model or estimation. Checks scope fit and the policy/conceptual payoff bar.

When to use

  • assessing if a research question is monetary enough for JME
  • checking scope fit for monetary theory and policy
  • evaluating central banking and business cycle topics
  • reviewing fiscal-monetary interactions and expectations
  • targeting Carnegie-Rochester-NYU policy issues
  • targeting Gerzensee macro-policy issues
  • judging if a question has a clear macro or policy lesson
  • comparing against sibling venues like JIE, RED, and AEJ Macro

When not to use

  • pure labor, IO, or development micro papers with no macro aggregation
  • new estimators with no monetary or macro application
  • finance-asset-pricing papers with no monetary or macro mechanism
  • descriptive data papers with no conceptual or policy payoff
  • narrow micro-empirical estimates dressed in macro language
