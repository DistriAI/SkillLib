monetization-strategy

combined

When the user wants to design or optimize their app's monetization — pricing, paywalls, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Also use when the user mentions "pricing", "paywall", "subscription", "IAP", "how to monetize", "revenue optimization", "free trial", or "conversion to paid". For retention impact, see retention-optimization. For competitive pricing, see competitor-analysis. Brainstorm 3-5 monetization strategies with audience fit, risks, and validation experiments. Use when exploring revenue models, evaluating pricing strategies, or deciding how to monetize a product.

Skill page

Conflict

The two skills share the same name and both cover monetization models, but they target different contexts and contain material steps the other lacks. phuryn/pm-skills is a general product/business-model brainstorming skill that requires 3-5 strategies with audience fit, unit economics, competitive position, and validation experiments. appeeky/aso-skills is a mobile-app-specific optimization skill that adds paywall timing, pricing psychology, category benchmarks, free-trial strategy, IAP design, and App Store conversion metrics. Neither is a reproduction of the other, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
