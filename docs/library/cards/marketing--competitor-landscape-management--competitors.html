Skill page

Conflict

The two skills share the name 'competitors' but solve different problems. The first is a content-marketing skill for writing public SEO pages (alternatives, vs pages) with strict evidence and honesty rules. The second is a founder-facing operational skill for maintaining a local competitive landscape (files, discovery, watch passes, archiving). They overlap only in the general concept of tracking competitors; their steps, file structures, and outputs are distinct, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
