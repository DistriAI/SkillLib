cie-policy-implication

separate

Use when the policy-implication / 启示 section of a 《中国工业经济》 (China Industrial Economics) manuscript is vague. Forces ACTIONABLE recommendations — who, at which node, does what — derived from the findings and heterogeneity. This concreteness is the key difference from 《经济研究》, where implications stay at the significance level.
