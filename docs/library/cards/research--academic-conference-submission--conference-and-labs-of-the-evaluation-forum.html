conference-and-labs-of-the-evaluation-forum

separate

Use when targeting Conference and Labs of the Evaluation Forum (CLEF) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for evaluation forum.

When to use

  • IR/NLP evaluation campaigns
  • shared tasks
  • multilingual retrieval
  • benchmark methodology
  • conference-fit read
  • re-framing from journal or arXiv style
  • evidence-gap diagnosis
  • rebuttal posture

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • generic CS paper without specific subcommunity focus
