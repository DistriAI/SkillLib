aeja-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which aeja-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal for an American Economic Journal: Applied Economics (AEJ: Applied) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which aeja-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work for AEJ: Applied submission
  • diagnosing current manuscript bottlenecks
  • switching into revision mode after a decision letter

When not to use

  • replacing specialized aeja-* skills
  • framing papers as policy questions for AEJ: Policy
  • framing papers as broad general-interest for AER
