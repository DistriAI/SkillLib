acm-mobicom

separate

Use when targeting ACM MobiCom (MobiCom) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for mobile networking.

When to use

  • targeting ACM MobiCom
  • deciding manuscript fit for MobiCom
  • conference-fit read for mobile computing
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style to conference narrative
  • evidence-gap diagnosis
  • anonymity diagnosis
  • artifact diagnosis
  • rebuttal diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • end-to-end mobile systems or sensing platforms
  • broader networking or systems claims
  • security or privacy central contributions
  • sensing and ubiquitous computing
