large-document-reader

separate

Split and read long documents chapter-by-chapter for structured analysis

When to use

  • Splitting long documents into structured chapters
  • Reading books, reports, theses, legal filings, and technical manuals
  • Systematic chapter-by-chapter reading and analysis
  • Maintaining cross-references between document parts
  • Literature reviews and systematic reviews
  • Comprehensive document analysis tasks

When not to use

  • Naive fixed-size chunking that breaks mid-sentence or mid-argument
