kr-cover-letter

authentic

Use when asked 자기소개서 써 줘, 자소서 첨삭해 줘, 지원동기 어떻게 써, 입사 후 포부 항목, 대기업 공채 자소서, 공기업 NCS 자기소개서, or write a Korean self-introduction essay for a job application. Produces a 항목별 자기소개서 for a Korean employer: one answer per question within the character limit (공백 포함 or 제외 as specified), each with a 소제목, a 두괄식 opening and a concrete experience told as situation, action and result, tied to the job and company, plus a character count and the interview questions each answer invites.

When to use

  • Writing Korean self-introduction essays
  • Reviewing existing self-introduction drafts
  • Writing support motivation (지원동기)
  • Writing post-hire ambitions (입사 후 포부)
  • Writing NCS self-introductions for public enterprises
  • Writing self-introductions for large corporate public recruitment

When not to use

  • Inventing projects, numbers, or awards
  • Writing free-form cover letters
  • Including school names or family background in blind hiring applications
