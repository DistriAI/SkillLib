geology

separate

Use when targeting Geology (GSA) or deciding whether a short, high-impact geoscience research letter fits this venue. Encodes the journal's broad-significance-in-tight-length fit, the novelty and rapid-communication bar, GSA house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a short, high-significance geoscience result
  • compressing a finding with wide implications into a tight letter
  • choosing between Geology and earth-and-planetary-science-letters
  • assessing broad-significance-in-tight-length bar
  • checking GSA house style and structure
  • pre-submission self-check for Geology
  • identifying common desk-reject triggers

When not to use

  • thorough but incremental studies
  • regional reports without wide implications
  • papers that genuinely need full article length
  • replacing the journal's current author guidance
