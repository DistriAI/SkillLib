rje-topic-selection

separate

Use to test whether a research question fits The RAND Journal of Economics (RJE) — the flagship industrial-organization journal with a deliberately narrow applied-microeconomics scope (regulated industries, antitrust/competition, market structure, firm strategy, the economics of organizations). Screens fit before you invest in a full draft.

When to use

  • Deciding if RJE is the right home for an applied-micro project
  • Checking scope for projects drifting toward general theory or macro
  • Sharpening vague firm or market questions into IO-flagship-worthy ones
  • Evaluating competition, antitrust, and market power topics
  • Assessing regulated industries and regulation design
  • Reviewing market structure and firm strategy questions
  • Screening economic analysis of organizations and contracts
  • Evaluating auctions and mechanism design with market applications

When not to use

  • General economic theory or macro without IO mechanisms
  • Labor, public, or development questions without an IO core
  • Pure methodology with no substantive market question
  • General-micro or macro papers lacking market mechanisms
  • Reduced-form correlations without competition or regulation stakes
  • Projects too broad for the 40-page main-text cap
