company-contact-finder

separate

Find decision-makers at a specific company using Fiber, Crustdata and PDL people search inside the GooseWorks coworker. Given a company name and target titles, returns a list of contacts with name, title, LinkedIn URL, and location.

When to use

  • Find decision-makers at a specific company
  • Search for people by company name and target titles
  • Use Fiber, Crustdata, and PDL people search
  • Return contacts with name, title, LinkedIn URL, and location

When not to use

  • Running outside the GooseWorks coworker
  • Using tools other than fiber_person_search, crustdata_person_search, and pdl_person_search
