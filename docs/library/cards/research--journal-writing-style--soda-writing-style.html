soda-writing-style

separate

Use when writing or revising a SODA (ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms) paper — headline bounds stated exactly, the title-page abstract that wins bidding, a first-ten-pages overview for triage readers, prior-bound comparison tables, and prose discipline for an uncapped full-version document.

When to use

  • writing SODA papers
  • revising SODA papers
  • stating headline bounds
  • crafting title-page abstracts
  • creating first-ten-pages overviews
  • building prior-bound comparison tables
  • applying prose discipline for long documents
  • calibrating register and titles

When not to use

  • writing papers for conferences with page limits
  • non-algorithms papers
  • experimental papers without theoretical bounds
