jmgmt-review-process

separate

Use when you need to understand or set expectations for the Journal of Management (JOM) editorial and masked-review process — desk screening, the developmental R&R culture, the Review Issue track, and reading a decision letter. Explains the process; it does not draft the response letter (jmgmt-rebuttal).

When to use

  • calibrate expectations about timeline and decision types
  • interpret a decision letter (R&R vs. reject; major vs. minor)
  • understand how reviewers vs. the action editor weigh in under masked review
  • understand how the Review Issue track works
  • plan a revision strategy before drafting the response

When not to use

  • draft the response letter
