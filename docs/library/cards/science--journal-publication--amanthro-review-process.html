amanthro-review-process

separate

Use when you need to understand how American Anthropologist (AA) evaluates a manuscript — editorial screening, anonymous peer review under an ethics of care, the four-field editorial board, decision categories, and how section choice shapes the path. Sets expectations and shapes the paper to survive review; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • stress-test against editorial screening grounds
  • choosing the right section
  • interpreting a decision letter
  • understanding what reviewers are asked to weigh
  • shaping the paper to pass review

When not to use

  • contacting editors
