anmath-scope-fit

separate

Use when judging whether a pure-mathematics result clears the Annals of Mathematics importance and originality bar and falls within scope, before investing in framing or submission. Assesses fit and significance; does not evaluate whether the proof is correct (that is the proof's job).

When to use

  • judging whether a pure-mathematics result clears the Annals of Mathematics importance and originality bar
  • assessing fit and significance
  • choosing a journal for a finished or near-finished result
  • screening if a result is good but not Annals-level
  • evaluating if the area or style of result fits the journal

When not to use

  • evaluating whether the proof is correct
  • framing or submission
  • survey or exposition without major new theorem
  • applied or numerical work with no pure-math theorem of lasting interest
