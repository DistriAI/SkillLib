reviews-of-geophysics

separate

Use when targeting Reviews of Geophysics (Rev Geophys) or deciding whether an Earth or space science review fits this authoritative, very-high-impact invited-review venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Reviews of Geophysics
  • deciding if a review fits the venue
  • comprehensive syntheses across AGU domains
  • cross-disciplinary integration reviews
  • emerging field state-of-the-art syntheses
  • methodological or observational-paradigm reviews
  • quantitative meta-syntheses
  • forward-looking open question framing

When not to use

  • primary research
  • annotated bibliographies
  • short-format articles
  • replacing official submission guidelines
