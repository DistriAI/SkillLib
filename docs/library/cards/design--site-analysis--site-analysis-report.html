site-analysis-report

authentic

Analyse a site before designing on it — what is physically there, what the law and the neighbours allow, and which constraints will actually shape the building. Use when asked to carry out a site analysis, site appraisal, feasibility study for a plot, or to assess whether a site can take a proposed development. Produces the physical survey summary, the constraints and opportunities map, the statutory and legal position, the developable-capacity estimate, and the risks that need resolving before design starts.

When to use

  • site analysis
  • site appraisal
  • feasibility study for a plot
  • assess whether a site can take a proposed development
  • physical survey summary
  • constraints and opportunities map
  • statutory and legal position
  • developable-capacity estimate

When not to use

  • substitute for professional surveys
  • substitute for legal advice
  • confirming title, easements, covenants, or rights of way
  • ground conditions, trees, drainage, or contamination survey
  • checking planning designations against authority records
