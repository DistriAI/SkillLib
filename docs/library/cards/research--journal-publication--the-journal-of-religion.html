the-journal-of-religion

separate

Use when targeting The Journal of Religion or deciding whether a religious-studies manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, its argument/evidence bar, textual/comparative and constructive-analytic expectations, house style and review norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for theology or philosophy-of-religion manuscripts
  • framing constructive or analytic arguments for a broad scholarly readership
  • choosing between The Journal of Religion and comparative-historical or textual venues
  • checking the journal's argument bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • assessing scope for religious ethics, history of religions, or religion and culture
  • reviewing method and evidence requirements for original arguments
  • checking structure and house style including Chicago notes-and-bibliography
  • verifying official submission checklist items and anonymization

When not to use

  • devotional, pastoral, or in-house confessional writing with no scholarly argument
  • replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
  • tasks centered on the comparative and historical-morphological method
  • tasks with a textual, biblical, and early-Christian emphasis
