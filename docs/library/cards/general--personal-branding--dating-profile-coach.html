dating-profile-coach

separate

Review and improve a dating profile on Tinder, Hinge or Bumble — rank the user''s photos and pick the lead photo, check them against the app''s official photo and AI-photo rules, then tighten the bio and suggest openers. Use when the user asks "which photo should be my first", "rate my dating photos", "can I use AI photos on Hinge/Bumble/Tinder", "fix my dating profile", or shares profile photos or a bio for feedback. Uses the free DatePhotos.AI MCP for official rules and photo scores when connected; falls back to a built-in checklist when it is not.
