ieee-international-requirements-engineering-conference

separate

Use when targeting IEEE International Requirements Engineering Conference (RE) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for requirements engineering.

When to use

  • targeting IEEE International Requirements Engineering Conference
  • deciding if a manuscript fits the venue
  • conference-fit read before formatting
  • re-framing from journal or arXiv style
  • evidence-gap diagnosis
  • anonymity or artifact diagnosis
  • rebuttal posture
  • re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • generic CS paper without requirements focus
  • automated program analysis or testing
  • general SE process or empirical studies where requirements are only background
