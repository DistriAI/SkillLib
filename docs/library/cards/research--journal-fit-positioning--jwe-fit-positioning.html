jwe-fit-positioning

separate

Use to judge whether a manuscript's international / open-economy dimension genuinely holds up for 《世界经济》 (The Journal of World Economy), or whether it is really a domestic paper that should be re-routed to 金融研究 / 中国工业经济 / 经济研究. Run this first, before drafting.

When to use

  • judge manuscript fit for The Journal of World Economy
  • assess international or open-economy dimension
  • determine if paper is domestic or international
  • decide between Journal of World Economy, Financial Research, and China Industrial Economics
  • evaluate if open dimension is genuine or superficial
  • provide re-routing suggestions for domestic papers
  • check if treatment, mechanism, and data have international attributes

When not to use

  • drafting the manuscript
  • writing the introduction
  • conducting empirical analysis
  • collecting data
  • formatting the paper
