dbs-ai-check

separate

dontbesilent AI 写作特征识别。扫描文案中的 AI 生成痕迹，输出检测报告。默认只诊断不改。 触发方式：/dbs-ai-check、/AI检测、「帮我看看有没有 AI 味」「检测一下 AI 特征」 AI writing fingerprint detection. Scans copy for AI-generated patterns and outputs a diagnostic report. Diagnosis only by default. Trigger: /dbs-ai-check, "check for AI writing", "does this sound like AI"

When to use

  • AI writing fingerprint detection
  • Scanning copy for AI-generated patterns
  • Outputting a diagnostic report
  • Identifying AI traces in text
  • Diagnosing AI writing characteristics

When not to use

  • Rewriting text to remove AI flavor
  • Directly rewriting user content
  • General content quality assessment
  • Topic selection advice
