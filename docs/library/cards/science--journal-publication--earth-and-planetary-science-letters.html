earth-and-planetary-science-letters

separate

Use when targeting Earth and Planetary Science Letters (EPSL) or deciding whether a quantitative solid-earth or planetary-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's broad-interest process-science fit, the quantitative geochemistry/geophysics evidence bar, Elsevier house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for quantitative solid-earth or planetary-science paper
  • re-framing regional geochemical or geophysical study into transferable process
  • choosing between EPSL and geology
  • assessing broad-interest significance bar
  • assessing quantitative evidence expectations
  • geochemistry and isotope geochemistry
  • geophysics and geodynamics
  • petrology, mineral physics, and high-pressure/high-temperature experiments

When not to use

  • purely descriptive regional report
  • method note with no process payoff
  • result of interest only to a narrow sub-field
  • replacing the journal's current author guidance
