education-data-source-crdc

separate

CRDC — biennial OCR survey of all U.S. public schools (2011-2021). Discipline, course access, harassment, restraint/seclusion by race/sex/disability/EL. Use for civil rights and equity analysis. 2020-21 COVID-impacted; 2011-14 sampled, not universe.

When to use

  • school discipline disparities
  • course access equity
  • harassment and bullying analysis
  • restraint and seclusion data
  • chronic absenteeism
  • civil rights compliance monitoring
  • disaggregation by race, sex, disability, and EL status

When not to use

  • even-numbered school years
  • direct comparison with 2020-21 data
  • universe data for 2011-14
