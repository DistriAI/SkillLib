cjms-topic-selection

separate

Use when choosing or vetting a topic for a 《中国管理科学》 (Chinese Journal of Management Science) manuscript — testing fit with the journal's applied-modeling mission, mapping the paper to a column, and screening out sister-journal mismatches. Judges fit only; it does not build the model or write the review.
