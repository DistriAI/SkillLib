msom-topic-selection

separate

Use when shaping or stress-testing a research question for Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM) — confirming an operations decision is central, choosing the analytical-vs-empirical lane, and matching the question to the right editorial Department before any modeling or data work begins.

When to use

  • shaping a research question for M&SOM
  • stress-testing a research question for M&SOM
  • confirming an operations decision is central
  • choosing the analytical-vs-empirical lane
  • matching the question to the right editorial Department
  • scoring fit, novelty, evidence readiness, and audience ownership
  • comparing against sibling venues
  • identifying the target Department

When not to use

  • modeling or data work
  • polishing the manuscript
  • targeting M&SOM generically without picking a department
  • pricing/finance/ML papers with no real operational lever
  • applying methods to operations datasets without improving an operations decision
