mobisys-submission

separate

Use when auditing a MobiSys submission for HotCRP readiness — the paper-registration prerequisite, the single December UTC deadline, the 12-page double-column body including figures and tables, double-blind anonymity including device and trace giveaways, desk-reject triggers, and final-week submission sequencing.

When to use

  • MobiSys paper submission audit
  • HotCRP readiness check
  • Paper registration verification
  • Deadline compliance
  • Page limit validation
  • Double-blind anonymity check
  • Desk-reject trigger identification
  • Final-week submission sequencing

When not to use

  • Workshop submissions
  • Poster track submissions
  • Demo track submissions
