er-theory-hypotheses

separate

Use when writing the theoretical analysis and hypothesis-development section of an Economic-Research manuscript — deriving testable, directional, falsifiable hypotheses that map one-to-one onto the empirical design.

When to use

  • Writing theoretical analysis section
  • Developing directional hypotheses
  • Deriving falsifiable propositions
  • Mapping hypotheses to empirical design
  • Constructing mathematical models
  • Logical deduction of mechanisms
  • Addressing competing hypotheses

When not to use

  • Writing literature review
  • Presenting regression results in theory section
  • Post-hoc explanation of results
  • Running Y on M regressions for mechanism proof
