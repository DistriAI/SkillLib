combined

律师助理与法律助手、项目管理专家在审核合同、排查条款风险时必备。自动执行基础、商业、法律三层审查，仅添加批注不改原文，一键生成带批注合同、审查摘要、综合意见及业务流程图，让合同风控与业务梳理更高效专业。 Review and summarise any contract or legal agreement. Use when asked to review a contract, check an agreement, flag legal risks, or summarise key clauses. Produces a structured review with key terms, flagged clauses, risk rating, and plain English summary. Not a substitute for qualified legal advice.

  • producing consolidated review opinion

Skill page

Conflict

The two copies describe different procedures for the same task. The anbeime/skill copy is a technical, file-manipulation workflow that unpacks a .docx, adds Word comments, and generates multiple .docx outputs plus a Mermaid flowchart. The mohitagw15856 copy is a text-analysis workflow that produces a structured Markdown review with a risk rating, flagged clauses, missing clauses, and a plain-English summary. They overlap in the goal of reviewing a contract and flagging risks, but each contains material steps and constraints the other lacks (e.g., docx comment injection vs. plain-English summary and missing-clause check). Crowning one would hide a real alternative.
