ijcai-writing-style

separate

Use when revising an IJCAI or IJCAI-ECAI paper for broad AI significance, 7-page-body compression, self-contained exposition, double-blind wording, optional ethics discussion, reproducibility clarity, and policy-safe use of LLM editing.

When to use

  • revising an IJCAI or IJCAI-ECAI paper
  • broad AI significance
  • 7-page-body compression
  • self-contained exposition
  • double-blind wording
  • optional ethics discussion
  • reproducibility clarity
  • policy-safe use of LLM editing

When not to use

  • LLM-written manuscript text
  • fake citations
  • unverified claims
