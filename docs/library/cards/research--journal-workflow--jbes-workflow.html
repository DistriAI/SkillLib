jbes-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jbes-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing methods-paper work from topic selection through rebuttal for a Journal of Business & Economic Statistics (JBES) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which jbes-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing methods-paper work for JBES submission
  • Diagnosing the current bottleneck in a draft
  • Switching into revision mode after a decision letter
  • Routing between theory, simulations, application, and writing

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized jbes-* skills
  • Pure theory without empirical motivation
  • Pure applications without methodological novelty
  • General-interest economics findings without methodological novelty
  • Pure theorem-proving without empirical motivation
