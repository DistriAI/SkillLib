advmat-cover-letter

separate

Use when drafting the cover letter for an Advanced Materials submission, whose core job is to convince the editor that the work is a genuine materials advance of broad impact worthy of the journal. Argues the impact case; does not fix the science or format the manuscript.

When to use

  • Drafting cover letter for Advanced Materials submission
  • Arguing the impact case for editorial review
  • Distinguishing work from recent related papers
  • Resubmitting after transfer or rejection
  • Pre-empting venue questions for broad impact

When not to use

  • Fixing the science
  • Formatting the manuscript
