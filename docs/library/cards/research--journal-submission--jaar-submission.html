jaar-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight for the Journal of the American Academy of Religion (JAAR) via ScholarOne / Manuscript Central — anonymized main document plus separate title page, the broad-significance framing, length and abstract limits, in-text citation style, and house conventions. Final checks; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • final pre-submission preflight
  • anonymized main document check
  • separate title page preparation
  • broad-significance framing verification
  • length and abstract limits check
  • in-text citation style verification
  • house conventions check
  • ScholarOne / Manuscript Central upload preparation

When not to use

  • drafting content
  • unsolicited book review submissions
  • graduate student book reviews
