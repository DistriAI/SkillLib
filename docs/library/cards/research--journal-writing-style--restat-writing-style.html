restat-writing-style

separate

Use when revising the prose of a The Review of Economics and Statistics (REStat) manuscript — especially the abstract and introduction — so the applied question and the estimate land immediately in REStat house style. Polishes framing and prose; it does not change results or identification.

When to use

  • Revising REStat manuscript prose
  • Polishing abstract and introduction
  • Ensuring headline estimate lands on page one
  • Checking abstract word count and content
  • Formatting contribution as a delta against named work

When not to use

  • Changing results
  • Changing identification
  • Polishing intro before estimate and robustness are final
