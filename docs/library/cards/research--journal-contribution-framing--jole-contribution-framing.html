jole-contribution-framing

separate

Use when articulating what is new in a Journal of Labor Economics (JOLE) manuscript for a general labor-economics audience — the marginal contribution, the labor lesson, and why a labor economist outside the subfield should care. Frames the pitch; it does not run analysis.

When to use

  • articulating what is new in a JOLE manuscript
  • defining the marginal contribution
  • stating the labor lesson
  • explaining why a labor economist outside the subfield should care
  • framing the pitch for intro and cover letter
  • calibrating claims to design and sample

When not to use

  • running analysis
