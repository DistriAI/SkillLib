jfr-institutional-background

separate

Use to write or stress-test the institutional-background section of a 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) manuscript — stating China's financial-system detail accurately (regulatory rules, market segmentation, ownership), since wrong regulatory caliber or market structure is a common desk-reject trigger.

When to use

  • Writing institutional-background sections
  • Stress-testing regulatory caliber accuracy
  • Clarifying market segmentation for identification
  • Distinguishing ownership and entity types
  • Linking policy shocks to identification
  • Correcting regulatory subject errors

When not to use

  • Writing general financial common sense popularization
  • Creating long policy reviews unrelated to identification
  • Confusing draft, pilot, and effective policy stages
