competitor-creative-analysis

separate

Pulls competitor ads from Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center, categorizes their messaging angles, formats, CTAs, and creative types, then identifies gaps in their approach you can exploit and patterns worth testing in your own campaigns. Platform: Meta.

When to use

  • Quarterly competitive reviews to stay ahead of messaging shifts
  • Before creative brainstorms to identify white space in the market
  • When entering a new market or vertical and you need to understand the landscape fast
  • When your CPA rises and you suspect competitors are getting more aggressive
