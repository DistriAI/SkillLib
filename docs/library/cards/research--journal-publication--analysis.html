analysis

separate

Use when targeting Analysis or deciding whether a short, focused philosophy manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's identity as a home for crisp, single-point articles, counterexamples, and replies; the argument-rigor bar; the brevity-and-focus house style and double-blind norms; official-submission re-check; and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for short philosophy notes
  • compressing a paper to Analysis's brevity-and-focus form
  • deciding between a short Analysis note or a full-length article
  • checking Analysis's brevity/single-point bar
  • applying desk-reject heuristics
  • reviewing crisp original arguments in analytic philosophy
  • evaluating decisive counterexamples
  • assessing tight replies and rejoinders

When not to use

  • long, multi-part treatises developing several threads
  • contributions needing full-article length
  • sustained arguments in the UK/analytic tradition
  • moral, political, or normative-ethics focus needing room
  • replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
