influencer-prospecting

combined

Use when the user wants to find creators, influencers, affiliates, or social accounts in a niche for outreach, partnerships, sponsorships, UGC, seeding, or competitive research. Produces scored prospect lists from public social data. Find and evaluate creators, influencers, affiliates, and UGC partners using audience fit, content fit, authentic product adjacency, engagement quality, public contactability, commerce signals, and brand-safety evidence—not follower count alone.

When to use

  • find influencers or creators in a niche
  • build a UGC, affiliate, sponsorship, or seeding list
  • find micro-influencers with strong engagement
  • identify creators who already talk about a product category
  • export prospects as CSV

When not to use

  • scrape or expose private contact data
  • infer protected attributes
  • equating large follower count with good fit

Skill page

Conflict

Both copies describe the same task—building a scored, contactable creator shortlist from public social data—but they disagree on the discovery mechanism and the scoring model. The gooseworks copy is a composite that explicitly depends on other skills (scrapecreators-api, audience-research, creator-profile-teardown) and uses a multi-dimensional score (audience fit, content fit, product adjacency, engagement quality, consistency, creative quality, sponsorship density, contactability, commerce relevance, brand safety) with a test/nurture/pass segmentation. The ScrapeCreators copy is a standalone skill that names specific public sources (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, link-in-bio services, Amazon/TikTok Shop), uses a simpler 1-5 score on six dimensions (niche fit, audience fit, reach, engagement quality, brand safety, contactability), and mandates a CSV export. Crowning one would hide the other's real alternative: the composite's deeper teardown and segmentation versus the standalone's explicit source list and CSV output. They also disagree on whether reach/follower count is a scored dimension (ScrapeCreators scores it; gooseworks explicitly says not follower count alone and does not list reach as a score).
