jim-contribution-framing

separate

Use when articulating what a Journal of International Marketing (JIM) manuscript contributes — converting results into theory advances for international marketing and decisions for border-crossing managers, and defending against the "context extension" verdict. It frames the payoff; jim-writing-style polishes the prose.

When to use

  • articulating JIM manuscript contributions
  • converting results into theory advances
  • defining managerial decision rules for border-crossing managers
  • defending against the 'context extension' verdict
  • framing Insight-format submissions
  • creating a contribution ledger
  • phrasing country conditions as dimension profiles

When not to use

  • polishing prose
  • developing theory when the international dimension did not change it
  • compressing a rejected full paper into Insight format
