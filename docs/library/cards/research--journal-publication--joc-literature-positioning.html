joc-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Communication (JoC) manuscript against the literature so it reads as a field-wide contribution. JoC readers span communication subfields and traditions, so the paper must engage the literatures they expect rather than only a single platform or topic niche. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction
  • Revising the contribution paragraph
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on missed work
  • Connecting platform literature to communication broadly
  • Distinguishing contribution from closest prior papers
  • Building a three-column map of conversation, tension, and delta
  • Comparing against sibling venues
  • Producing a ledger of claims, evidence, and risks

When not to use

  • Writing the lit review
  • Polishing format
  • Giving submission-ready advice before checking official source map
