reform

separate

Use when targeting 《改革》(Reform — 重庆社会科学院主管主办的改革政策类 CSSCI 月刊, 1988 年创刊, 不收版面费) or deciding whether a Chinese social-science/econ/management manuscript fits this journal. Applies the journal's fit, framing, GB/T 7714 citation house-style, official-submission-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《改革》 or Reform
  • deciding if a Chinese social-science/econ/management manuscript fits
  • applying journal fit and framing heuristics
  • applying GB/T 7714 citation house-style
  • official-submission-check
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • rewriting a general Chinese econ/management manuscript for 《改革》
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks and alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting work summaries
  • submitting policy propaganda
  • submitting general reviews
  • using second-hand submission website formats
