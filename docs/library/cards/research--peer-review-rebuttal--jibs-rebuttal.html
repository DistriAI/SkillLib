jibs-rebuttal

separate

Use when planning revisions and drafting the point-by-point response letter after a Journal of International Business Studies (JIBS) revise-and-resubmit — prioritizing by the area editor's letter, executing the cross-national measurement, CMV, and (dynamic-)endogeneity work reviewers demand, and citing the relevant methods editorials. Drafts the revision and response; it does not interpret the decision (jibs-review-process).

When to use

  • planning revisions after JIBS revise-and-resubmit
  • drafting point-by-point response letter
  • executing cross-national measurement work
  • executing common-method variance (CMV) analysis
  • executing endogeneity and dynamic endogeneity work
  • citing relevant JIBS methods editorials
  • reconciling conflicting reviewer requests
  • updating Data Accessibility Statement

When not to use

  • interpreting the decision
