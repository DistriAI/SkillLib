Use when targeting Review of Financial Studies (RFS) or deciding whether a finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Review of Financial Studies
  • deciding if a finance manuscript fits RFS
  • choosing among JF, JFE, and RFS
  • considering RFS registered-reports track
  • assessing RFS desk-reject risks
  • re-framing for RFS submission

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • accounting papers
  • economics papers framed as finance
