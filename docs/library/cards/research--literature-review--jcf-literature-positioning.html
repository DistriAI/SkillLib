jcf-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a manuscript within the corporate-finance literature for the Journal of Corporate Finance (JCF) — locating the paper in the right strand (capital structure, governance, payout, contracting, M&A, international) and stating the marginal contribution. It frames citations and gap statements; it does not draft full sections.

When to use

  • Writing related-literature paragraphs
  • Writing the 'we contribute by' sentence
  • Mapping which corporate-finance strand the paper extends
  • Ensuring cited lineage matches JCF remit
  • Stating marginal contribution against closest papers
  • Differentiating from 2-3 closest papers
  • Identifying real, current gaps
  • Drawing cross-area links to sharpen corporate-finance point

When not to use

  • Drafting full sections
  • Positioning against old papers while ignoring recent work
  • Borrowing asset-pricing framing that obscures corporate-finance question
  • Creating a literature dump that never says what is new
  • Claiming contributions to three literatures
