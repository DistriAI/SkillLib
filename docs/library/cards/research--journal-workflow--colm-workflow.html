colm-workflow

separate

Use when planning a COLM submission campaign across the calendar — working backward from the late-March abstract and paper deadlines through the May-June rebuttal, July decisions, August camera-ready, and October conference, coordinating co-authors, compute, and reciprocal-reviewing duties, and slotting COLM into a multi-venue LM-research pipeline.

When to use

  • planning a COLM submission campaign
  • coordinating co-authors
  • coordinating compute
  • coordinating reciprocal-reviewing duties
  • slotting COLM into a multi-venue LM-research pipeline
  • backward induction from March deadlines
  • managing multi-author LM paper coordination artifacts
  • adapting plans for small-lab variants

When not to use

  • writing the full paper
  • conducting experiments
  • performing rebuttals
  • preparing camera-ready submissions
  • selecting topics
  • managing reproducibility
  • handling related work
  • creating supplementary materials
