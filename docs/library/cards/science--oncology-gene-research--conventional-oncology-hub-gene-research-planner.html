conventional-oncology-hub-gene-research-planner

separate

Generates complete conventional oncology bulk-transcriptome biomarker and hub-gene research designs from a user-provided cancer type and study direction. Always use this skill whenever a user wants to design, plan, or build a tumor bioinformatics study centered on differential expression, prognostic filtering or risk modeling, PPI-based hub-gene prioritization, diagnostic/prognostic evaluation, clinical association, immune infiltration context, methylation context, and optional tissue or cell validation. Covers five study patterns (signature-first prognostic workflow, hub-gene-first biomarker workflow, hybrid signature-to-hub workflow, immune-context biomarker workflow, translational validation workflow) and always outputs four workload configs (Lite / Standard / Advanced / Publication+) with recommended primary plan, step-by-step workflow, figure plan, validation strategy, minimal executable version, publication upgrade path...

When to use

  • tumor bioinformatics study design
  • differential expression analysis
  • prognostic filtering or risk modeling
  • PPI-based hub-gene prioritization
  • diagnostic/prognostic evaluation
  • clinical association analysis
  • immune infiltration context
  • methylation context

When not to use

  • clinical trial protocols
  • dosing or prescribing
  • patient-specific treatment recommendations
  • pure scRNA-only studies
  • MR-only studies
  • GWAS-only studies
  • wet-lab-only studies
  • non-biomedical requests
