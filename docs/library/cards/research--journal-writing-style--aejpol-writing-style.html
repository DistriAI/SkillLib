aejpol-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or revising the prose of an AEJ: Economic Policy manuscript — especially the abstract and introduction — to translate causal estimates into a clear policy takeaway without overclaiming. Shapes the policy-first narrative and house style; it does not design identification or build the welfare model.

When to use

  • drafting AEJ: Economic Policy abstracts
  • revising AEJ: Economic Policy introductions
  • translating causal estimates into policy takeaways
  • shaping policy-first narrative
  • applying AEA house style
  • calibrating claims to avoid overclaiming
  • polishing prose for submission

When not to use

  • designing identification
  • building the welfare model
  • rewriting intro before identification settles
  • rewriting intro before welfare bridge settles
  • rewriting intro before robustness settles
