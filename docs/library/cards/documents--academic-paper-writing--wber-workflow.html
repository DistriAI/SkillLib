wber-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which wber-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal for a The World Bank Economic Review (WBER) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which wber-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work for WBER submission
  • routing based on current manuscript symptoms
  • switching into revision mode after a decision letter
  • identifying binding constraints in draft work

When not to use

  • replacing specialized wber-* skills
  • treating WBER as interchangeable with Journal of Development Economics
  • treating WBER as World Development
  • routing survey or literature overview papers to WBER
  • polishing prose before design and evidence are stable
