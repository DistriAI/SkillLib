academic-baseline

separate

Use when working on any empirical academic research context — paper writing, data analysis, literature review, or any task involving citations, results, or publication artifacts. Establishes non-negotiable principles that govern all other superpapers skills.

When to use

  • Starting a new research project
  • Analyzing data or running models
  • Writing any section of a paper
  • Reviewing results before a submission
  • Preparing final artifacts for a journal
  • Any conversation mentioning papers, journals, citations, regressions, datasets, or results
