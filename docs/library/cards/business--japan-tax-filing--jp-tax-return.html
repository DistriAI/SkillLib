jp-tax-return

authentic

Use when asked 確定申告のやり方, do I need to file a tax return in Japan, how do I claim medical expense deduction, ふるさと納税の確定申告, 副業の確定申告, how to file with e-Tax, or 日本报税怎么弄. Produces whether the person must or should file, the income and deductions to declare, a step-by-step filing plan with e-Tax or on paper, the documents to gather, and the deadlines and payment options to confirm with the National Tax Agency.

When to use

  • Determining if a tax return is required
  • Claiming medical expense deductions
  • Filing for side income or freelance work
  • Handling ふるさと納税 declarations
  • Filing with e-Tax or on paper
  • Managing housing loan deductions
  • Confirming filing deadlines and payment options

When not to use

  • Business accounts
  • Blue returns
  • Inheritance tax matters
