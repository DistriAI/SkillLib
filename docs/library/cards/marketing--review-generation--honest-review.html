honest-review

separate

Shared step of the marketing playbooks (make-static-ads, run-organic-social): how to review generated images and video honestly. Covers what was seen by eye versus read automatically, findings framed as things for the human to confirm, remakes only after a yes to the price, and at most two tries per item. Loaded by those playbooks; not for direct use.

When to use

  • reviewing generated images
  • reviewing generated video
  • comparing output against product photos
  • comparing output against approved text
  • reporting findings for human confirmation
  • managing remakes for confirmed defects
  • recording human feedback to the engine

When not to use

  • direct use
  • claiming to watch motion or hear audio
  • presenting engine status as review
  • calling media ready on automatic reads alone
  • unquoted paid edits
  • using earlier generated images as reference
