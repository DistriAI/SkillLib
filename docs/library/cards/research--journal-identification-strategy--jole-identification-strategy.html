jole-identification-strategy

separate

Use when the causal identification strategy is the bottleneck for a Journal of Labor Economics (JOLE) manuscript — DID/event study, IV including shift-share, RDD, RCT, and AKM firm–worker designs common in labor economics. Stress-tests the design to JOLE's bar before tables are drafted.

When to use

  • DID/event study
  • IV including shift-share
  • RDD
  • RCT
  • AKM firm–worker designs
  • Stress-testing causal claims
  • Auditing identification strategies

When not to use

  • Drafting tables
  • Descriptive analysis without causal claims
