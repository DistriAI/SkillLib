quarterly-journal-of-economics

separate

Use when targeting Quarterly Journal of Economics (QJE) or deciding whether an economics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Quarterly Journal of Economics
  • deciding if an economics manuscript fits QJE
  • choosing among QJE, AER, or JPE
  • re-framing field papers for general interest
  • assessing QJE desk-reject risks
  • checking QJE method and evidence bar
  • reviewing QJE house style and structure
  • verifying official submission guidelines

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • submitting to non-top-5 journals without re-routing
  • handling niche-field framing without re-framing
