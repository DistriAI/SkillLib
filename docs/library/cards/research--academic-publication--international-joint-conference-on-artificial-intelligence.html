When to use

  • targeting IJCAI
  • deciding if a manuscript fits IJCAI
  • conference-fit read for international AI research
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style into CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap diagnosis for IJCAI
  • anonymity, artifact, or rebuttal diagnosis for IJCAI
  • re-routing diagnosis for IJCAI

When not to use

  • substitute for author kit

  • US-oriented broad AI cycle fit
  • European community fit
  • deep ML representation or optimization claims
