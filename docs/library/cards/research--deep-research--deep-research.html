Skill page

Conflict

The four copies define 'deep-research' as four distinct, non-interchangeable procedures. Imbad0202 is a 13-agent academic pipeline for systematic reviews and meta-analysis. davidondrej is a specific API wrapper for DeepAPI/Perplexity. jezweb is a pre-build product discovery workflow focused on local code exploration and competitor analysis. daymade is a multi-agent coordination system with strict asset contracts and provider lane management. No single copy is the 'original' that the others reproduce; they are different tools sharing a name. Combining them is necessary to capture the full range of 'deep research' capabilities (academic, API-based, product discovery, and coordinated multi-agent).
