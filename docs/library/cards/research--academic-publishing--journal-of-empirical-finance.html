journal-of-empirical-finance

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Empirical Finance (JEF) or deciding whether an empirical-finance / financial-econometrics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Empirical Finance
  • deciding if empirical-finance manuscript fits venue
  • framing empirical method or measurement contributions
  • checking JEF desk-reject risks
  • re-framing general finance papers with empirical-finance core
  • evaluating method-and-evidence bar for JEF

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • general asset pricing papers
  • pure econometrics framed as empirical finance
  • pricing/risk theory with proofs
  • general econometric methods
