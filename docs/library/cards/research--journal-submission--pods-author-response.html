pods-author-response

separate

Use when drafting ACM PODS author responses, covering the short ~48-hour double-anonymous rebuttal that corrects misreadings of proofs and definitions, and — distinctively — the shepherded revision cover letter that maps every required item to a concrete change in the resubmitted paper and survives the shepherd's second read.

When to use

  • drafting ACM PODS author responses
  • 48-hour double-anonymous rebuttal
  • correcting misreadings of proofs and definitions
  • shepherded revision cover letter
  • mapping required items to concrete changes
  • triaging reviewer concerns
  • addressing reviewer pushback patterns
  • maintaining anonymity in responses

When not to use

  • presenting new mathematics in the rebuttal
  • revealing authors, affiliations, or named systems
  • ignoring required items in the revision
