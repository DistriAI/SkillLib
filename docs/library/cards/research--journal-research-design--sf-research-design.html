sf-research-design

separate

Use when defending the research design of a Social Forces (SF) manuscript — causal identification for quantitative work, formal demographic design, case selection and process tracing for comparative-historical and ethnographic work, network and computational designs. SF's reputation rests on methodological rigor. Strengthens the design; it does not write code.

When to use

  • defending research design
  • causal identification for quantitative work
  • formal demographic design
  • case selection and process tracing
  • network and computational designs
  • specifying identification or pipelines
  • justifying design against rival accounts

When not to use

  • writing code
