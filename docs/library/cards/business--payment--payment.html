Shopping Claw | Give your agent spending power. Financial management for Agents and OpenClaw bots. Binance Pay Assistant - Send and Receive crypto payments. Send: QR code payment from Funding Wallet (C2C + PIX). Use when user wants to buy/purchase/pay/transfer/send, confirm/cancel payment, or query order status. Requires QR code data. PIX QR codes (pix, br.gov.bcb.pix) are auto-detected. Receive: Generate QR codes and payment links to collect crypto. Use when user wants to receive/collect payment (generate receive link, receive QR). Do NOT use for earning, buying/selling crypto, or digital goods.

When to use

  • Accept card details securely
  • Make purchases within strict guardrails
  • Enable x402 payments
  • Manage stablecoin wallet
  • Product management for bots
  • Transaction history
  • Profile updates
  • Discover vendors and merchants

Skill page

Conflict

The two skills are unrelated implementations of the generic 'payment' label. The first is a Binance Pay Assistant for sending and receiving crypto via QR codes, while the second is a CreditClaw financial enablement platform for agents to make card and x402 payments. They share no steps, APIs, or constraints, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
