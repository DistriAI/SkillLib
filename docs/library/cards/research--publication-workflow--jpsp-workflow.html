jpsp-workflow

separate

Use as the entry point for any Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP) manuscript. Routes to the right JPSP sub-skill based on where you are in the lifecycle and — first of all — which of the three independently edited sections (ASC, IRGP, PPID) fits the paper. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new JPSP paper
  • Determining which of the three JPSP sections fits the paper
  • Routing mid-project to the next applicable skill
  • Routing after receiving a decision letter
  • Reviewing manuscript fit against JPSP expectations

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Short-report journal submissions
  • Single-study papers without multi-study logic
