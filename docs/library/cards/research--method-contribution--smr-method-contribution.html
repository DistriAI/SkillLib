smr-method-contribution

separate

Use when sharpening the core methodological claim of a Sociological Methods & Research (SMR) paper — the new estimator/design/diagnostic and exactly what it fixes versus existing methods. Frames and bounds the contribution; does not derive its properties or run simulations.

When to use

  • sharpening the core methodological claim
  • framing and bounding the contribution
  • identifying the new estimator, design, or diagnostic
  • stating what the method fixes versus existing methods
  • positioning against the incumbent method
  • defining the contribution sentence
  • applying proportionality guardrails
  • checking contribution-type templates

When not to use

  • deriving properties
  • running simulations
