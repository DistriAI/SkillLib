oopsla-author-response

separate

Use when writing an OOPSLA author response inside the short per-round window — reading reviews through the four-outcome lens (Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, Reject), steering borderline papers toward a revision outcome instead of rejection, and committing only to changes deliverable within the round's revision mechanics.

When to use

  • writing an OOPSLA author response
  • reading reviews through the four-outcome lens
  • steering borderline papers toward revision
  • committing to changes deliverable within the round
  • reframing objections as bucket questions
  • shaping expectation lists for Major Revision

When not to use

  • classic rebuttals
  • introducing results the PDF cannot support
  • promising additions exceeding the 25-page revision limit
