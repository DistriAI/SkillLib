jep-revision

separate

Use when responding to Journal of Economic Perspectives (JEP) editor feedback — accessibility, balance, length, and framing — on an invited or accepted article. Plans the revision and the reply to the editorial team; it does not draft the original article or run the submission preflight.

When to use

  • Responding to JEP editor feedback
  • Planning JEP article revisions
  • Drafting reply to JEP editorial team
  • Addressing accessibility and balance concerns
  • Managing JEP article length and framing

When not to use

  • Drafting the original article
  • Running the submission preflight
  • Responding to anonymous referee reports
