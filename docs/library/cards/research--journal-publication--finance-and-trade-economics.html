finance-and-trade-economics

separate

Use when targeting 《财贸经济》(Finance and Trade Economics — 中国社会科学院主管、财经战略研究院主办的月刊, 2016 年起仅网络在线投稿) or deciding whether a public-finance/tax/trade-circulation manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's online-only submission, 匿名正文/顺序编码制 house style, official re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Targeting 《财贸经济》 or Finance and Trade Economics
  • Deciding if public-finance/tax/trade-circulation manuscript fits venue
  • Rewriting general Chinese economics manuscript for 《财贸经济》 narrative
  • Listing high-frequency rejection risks and alternative submission directions
  • Checking manuscript fit for fiscal, tax, budget, or trade circulation topics

When not to use

  • Replacing the journal's latest official submission guidelines
  • Manuscripts where financial institutions or capital markets are the main line
  • Pure international trade manuscripts (route to journal-of-international-trade)
  • Pure financial market manuscripts (route to journal-of-financial-research)
  • Using second-hand submission website formats
