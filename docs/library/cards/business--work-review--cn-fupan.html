cn-fupan

authentic

Run a 复盘 (fupan), the structured project review widely used in Chinese companies: review the goal, assess the result, analyse why, and draw rules for next time, with a blame-free tone and owned actions. Use when asked 帮我做复盘, 项目复盘, 活动复盘, 复盘会怎么开, or a fupan or retrospective in Chinese. Produces the 复盘 document in Chinese with goal versus result, root causes split into controllable and uncontrollable, lessons stated as reusable rules, and an action list with owners and dates, plus a meeting agenda.

When to use

  • 项目复盘
  • 活动复盘
  • 复盘会怎么开
  • fupan or retrospective in Chinese
  • structured project review
  • goal versus result assessment
  • root cause analysis
  • meeting agenda generation

When not to use

  • incident postmortem
  • sprint retrospective
