cre-policy-implication

separate

Use when drafting or strengthening the policy-implications section of a 《中国农村经济》 manuscript. Frames implications at the significance / institutional level but anchored in concrete 三农 scenarios — not the empty "加强完善推进" formula.

When to use

  • drafting policy-implications section
  • strengthening policy-implications section
  • framing implications at institutional level
  • anchoring implications in 三农 scenarios
  • reviewing policy implications for 《中国农村经济》
  • checking policy advice against empirical evidence

When not to use

  • writing execution memoranda for specific ministries
  • using empty 'strengthen/improve/promote' formulas
  • providing policy advice unrelated to the paper's evidence
  • repeating government work reports
  • claiming 'first study' as policy implication
