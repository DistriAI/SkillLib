journal-of-public-management

separate

Use when targeting 《公共管理学报》(Journal of Public Management — 哈尔滨工业大学管理学院主办的公共管理顶级中文季刊, 三审制, 不收版面费/审稿费) or deciding whether a Chinese public-administration/governance manuscript fits this journal. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract house style, fee-free policy, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《公共管理学报》
  • deciding if a Chinese public-administration manuscript fits
  • rewriting a general Chinese public administration manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • listing alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • manuscripts where management science or business administration is the core contribution
  • macro policy effects without public management theory
