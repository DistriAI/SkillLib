cvpr-submission

separate

Use when auditing a CVPR submission before the OpenReview deadline, covering abstract registration and profile requirements, the 8-page limit including figures and tables, the Compute Reporting Form, anonymity and external-link bans, dual-submission attestations, reviewer-duty enrollment, and desk-reject triage.

When to use

  • auditing CVPR submission
  • checking abstract registration and OpenReview profiles
  • verifying 8-page limit including figures and tables
  • reviewing Compute Reporting Form
  • checking anonymity and external-link bans
  • verifying dual-submission attestations
  • checking reviewer-duty enrollment
  • desk-reject triage

When not to use

  • writing the paper content
  • designing figures or tables
  • conducting experiments
  • managing post-deadline author changes
