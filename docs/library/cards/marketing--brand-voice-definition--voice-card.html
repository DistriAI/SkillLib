voice-card

separate

Builds or updates a voice card from a company blog ("extract our blog voice") or how an account or person actually writes, measured from about 8 or more of their real posts (length, casing, emoji, openings, pet phrases, opinions with their source). When there are too few posts, it runs a short interview instead. It ends with a test that checks a draft against the card. The brand's rules always beat the card. Use alone when you already have the account's posts or the person on hand; otherwise run the playbook for the job (e.g. run-organic-social, write-outreach, email-lifecycle).

When to use

  • extract our blog voice
  • measure account writing style
  • measure person writing style
  • run voice interview
  • test draft against voice card

When not to use

  • tasks without account posts or person on hand
