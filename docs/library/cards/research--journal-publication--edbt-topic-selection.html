edbt-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a data-management project belongs at EDBT or should be routed to SIGMOD, VLDB/PVLDB, ICDE, the co-located ICDT theory venue, or a database journal, and when choosing the EDBT paper shape (Regular / Experiments-&-Analysis / Vision) and the nearest submission cycle.

When to use

  • deciding if a project belongs at EDBT
  • routing to SIGMOD, VLDB/PVLDB, ICDE, or ICDT
  • choosing EDBT paper shape (Regular, Experiments-&-Analysis, Vision)
  • selecting the nearest EDBT submission cycle
  • distinguishing theory vs systems contributions
  • comparing EDBT with US flagship venues

When not to use

  • drafting the paper content
  • writing the introduction
  • performing the experiments
  • formatting the submission
