Conflict

The copies describe two different procedures that share a name. mattpocock/skills and sickn33/agentic-awesome-skills are the same thin wrapper that delegates to a separate 'grilling' skill, while plugin87/ux-ui-agent-skills is a self-contained, detailed UX brief interrogation with specific question banks, a BRIEF.md output format, and a kit-dependency check. Crowning the thin wrapper would hide the substantive UX procedure, and crowning the UX procedure would hide the generic plan/design interview. They overlap in intent (relentless questioning) but disagree on scope, target, and output.
