founder-sales

separate

Help users master the transition from product development to active selling by leveraging their unique status as founders to bridge the trust gap and define a scalable sales motion.

When to use

  • Assessing readiness for direct selling
  • Refining founder-led pitch and value proposition
  • Designing high-volume discovery and closing processes
  • Overcoming psychological barriers to sales
  • Building human trust with early customers
  • Determining when to hire a full-time salesperson

When not to use

  • Hiring sales too early
  • Focusing on product features instead of founder vision
  • Avoiding the commercial ask
