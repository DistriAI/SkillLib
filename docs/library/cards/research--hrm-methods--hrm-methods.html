hrm-methods

separate

Use when the research design is the bottleneck for a Human Resource Management (Wiley "HRM") manuscript — matching multilevel structure, multi-source/multi-wave timing, construct validity, and common-method-bias defenses to the theoretical claim. Designs the study; it does not run the estimation (hrm-data-analysis).

When to use

  • matching multilevel structure to theoretical claims
  • designing multi-source and multi-wave timing
  • defending construct validity
  • planning common-method-bias defenses
  • justifying aggregation of unit-level constructs
  • specifying endogeneity strategies for HR adoption
  • aligning levels of analysis across theory and measurement

When not to use

  • running the estimation
  • hrm-data-analysis
