lancet-submission

separate

Use as the final preflight before submitting to The Lancet — a complete checklist across importance, registration, reporting guideline + flow diagram, statistics, structured abstract, the Research in context panel, ethics/declarations/data-sharing, and required files. Bundles a checklist and a cover-letter template.

When to use

  • final preflight before submitting to The Lancet
  • confirming other lancet-* skill outputs landed
  • checking importance and global relevance
  • verifying trial registration and design
  • checking reporting guideline and flow diagram
  • reviewing statistics and structured abstract
  • validating Research in context panel
  • checking ethics, declarations, and data sharing

When not to use

  • initial manuscript drafting
  • statistical analysis execution
  • literature search execution
  • figure creation
  • protocol development
