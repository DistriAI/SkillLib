authentic

Faithfully append a human modeler's choice and rationale to one canonical per-subquestion JSONL decision ledger. Use after a choice card is answered or when migrating legacy decision artifacts; never originate or improve the decision.

When to use

  • Make human judgment traceable without multiplying decision files.
