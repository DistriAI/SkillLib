advmat-revision

separate

Use when responding to Advanced Materials reviewer reports and an editor's decision — major or minor revision, or a rejection with a resubmission or transfer path. Structures the response and the manuscript changes; does not perform the underlying new experiments.

When to use

  • Responding to Advanced Materials reviewer reports
  • Handling major or minor revision decisions
  • Managing rejections with resubmission or transfer paths
  • Structuring point-by-point responses
  • Addressing requests for additional characterization or benchmarking
  • Drafting rebuttal language for coauthors
  • Deciding between appeal and transfer for rejections

When not to use

  • Performing underlying new experiments
  • Adding unrelated new results that reopen length budget
