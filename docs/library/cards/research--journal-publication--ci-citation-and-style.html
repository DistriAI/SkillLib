ci-citation-and-style

separate

Use to format a Critical Inquiry (CI) essay's references and figures to journal standard — Chicago Manual of Style (17th ed.) footnotes numbered consecutively, no works-cited list, with all bibliographical information carried in the notes (and counted toward the word limit), plus images supplied as separate 300 ppi JPEG/TIFF with author-secured permissions. Formats the apparatus; it does not write the argument.

When to use

  • Setting up or cleaning citations before submission
  • Converting an author-date draft to Chicago notes style
  • Preparing the figure list, captions, and credit lines
  • Checking that notes are not silently blowing the word budget
  • Auditing notes, references, permissions, and house style
  • Returning a ledger of claim, evidence, risk, and manuscript location

When not to use

  • Writing the argument
  • Using author-date or a works-cited list
  • Embedding low-resolution images in the Word file
  • Assuming permissions are the publisher's job
