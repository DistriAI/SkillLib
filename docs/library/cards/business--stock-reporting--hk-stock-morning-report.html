hk-stock-morning-report

separate

Generate HK stock market morning report (股市晨報) for bank trading desks. Triggers: "生成晨报","股市晨报","今日股市","港股晨報" 推送：微信個人 + 飛書群 | 數據：騰訊財經+stcn.com+格隆匯+實時搜索

When to use

  • Generate HK stock market morning report
  • Create macro special report for non-trading days
  • Fetch Hang Seng and Tech indices
  • Search southbound capital flows
  • Compile hot news and popular HK stocks
  • Send report via WeChat and Feishu

When not to use

  • Guessing holidays without checking gov.hk
  • Citing same-day closing numbers in morning reports
  • Writing 'to be updated' for missing data
  • Skipping report generation on weekends or holidays
