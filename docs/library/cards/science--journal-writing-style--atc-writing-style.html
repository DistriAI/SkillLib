atc-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or revising an ATC (ACM SIGOPS Annual Technical Conference, formerly USENIX ATC) systems paper — writing a self-standing two-page extended abstract for the round-one gate, structuring the implementation-and-measurement narrative, reporting costs beside gains, and holding the two-column 12-page budget.

When to use

  • drafting ATC systems papers
  • revising ATC systems papers
  • writing self-standing two-page extended abstracts
  • structuring implementation-and-measurement narratives
  • reporting costs beside gains
  • managing two-column 12-page budget

When not to use

  • single-round venue writing
  • shrinking font or margins to fit page budget
  • quarantining costs in late limitations paragraphs
  • using average-only evaluation without tails or variance
