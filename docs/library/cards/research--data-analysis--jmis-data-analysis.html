jmis-data-analysis

separate

Use when the estimation, identification, construct validity, or modeling is the bottleneck for a Journal of Management Information Systems (JMIS) manuscript — econometrics on firm/platform data, SEM/PLS for behavioral constructs, analytical-model derivations, or ML/analytics evaluation. Executes and stress-tests the analysis the design (jmis-methods) chose; it does not redesign the study.
