joe-writing-style

separate

Use to make a Journal of Econometrics (JoE) manuscript read in house style — a precise abstract under 250 words, a contribution-first introduction, legible theorem-and-proof exposition, and Elsevier elsarticle/[dataset] reference conventions. Late-stage polish; it does not invent results.

When to use

  • Making a JoE manuscript read in house style
  • Refining abstracts to be under 250 words
  • Structuring contribution-first introductions
  • Improving legible theorem-and-proof exposition
  • Applying Elsevier elsarticle reference conventions
  • Late-stage polish of econometric manuscripts

When not to use

  • Inventing results
  • Locking estimands or theorems
  • Setting assumptions
  • Generating asymptotic or simulation evidence
  • Determining applied relevance
