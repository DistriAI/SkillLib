ijcai-reproducibility

separate

Use when strengthening IJCAI or IJCAI-ECAI reproducibility evidence for theory, algorithms, datasets, and computational experiments under the official convincing/credible/irreproducible reviewer rubric and optional reproducibility-section guidance.

When to use

  • strengthening IJCAI reproducibility evidence
  • strengthening IJCAI-ECAI reproducibility evidence
  • theory reproducibility
  • algorithm reproducibility
  • dataset reproducibility
  • computational experiment reproducibility
  • mapping results to reviewer rubric
  • drafting reproducibility sections

When not to use

  • tasks not related to IJCAI or IJCAI-ECAI reproducibility
