analytics-and-advisory-intelligence

separate

Cross-client analytics for Greek accounting firms. Surfaces trends, anomalies, and risks across financial data. Read-only, outputs to /data/reports/.

When to use

  • Portfolio-level analysis
  • Client-level analysis
  • Anomaly detection
  • Compliance risk scoring
  • Cashflow forecasting
  • Tax planning
  • Benchmarking clients against peers
  • Generating morning advisory and monthly reports

When not to use

  • Modifying client records
  • Submitting anything
  • Taking automated action
  • Writing to /data/clients/
  • Writing to /data/compliance/
