alphagbm-news-impact

separate

Separate reported claims from impact inferences, with related assets and next checkpoints. Use when the user asks to news impact with AlphaGBM. Use the bundled Python runner; never silently replace real results with demos.

When to use

  • Analyze published news impact
  • Identify related assets
  • Distinguish reported claims from inferences
  • List next checkpoints
  • Verify news workflow and revision

When not to use

  • Independent verification of arbitrary web claims
  • Silently replacing real results with demos
  • Silently switching to paid analysis
  • Sending reports to news impact
  • Claiming the workflow is live in production
