china-sport-science

separate

Use when targeting 《体育科学》(China Sport Science) — the China Sport Science Society flagship and top CSSCI sport-science journal (体育学). Apply to sport & exercise science spanning 运动人体科学 (生理/生化/生物力学/运动医学/体质健康), 体育人文社会学 (体育社会学/管理/经济/政策/史哲), 运动训练与竞技体育, 学校体育, 民族传统体育. Supports both natural-science experiments/interventions and social-science survey/qualitative/policy work; demands a real sport-science problem and discipline-appropriate rigor, not generic econometrics or pedagogy borrowed from other fields.

When to use

  • 运动人体科学 (生理/生化/生物力学/运动医学/体质健康)
  • 体育人文社会学 (体育社会学/管理/经济/政策/史哲)
  • 运动训练与竞技体育
  • 学校体育
  • 民族传统体育
  • natural-science experiments/interventions
  • social-science survey/qualitative/policy work

When not to use

  • generic econometrics
  • pedagogy borrowed from other fields
  • pure economics/management empirical studies using sport as a case
  • pure medical clinical research
  • pure education experience summaries lacking sport-science mechanisms
