the-lancet-psychiatry

separate

Use when targeting The Lancet Psychiatry or deciding whether a psychiatry or mental-health study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the clinical-trial, population, and policy evidence bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, Lancet specialty house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • targeting The Lancet Psychiatry
  • deciding if a psychiatry study fits the venue
  • framing a trial or cohort for global relevance
  • choosing between The Lancet Psychiatry, JAMA Psychiatry, and specialist journals
  • checking reporting-guideline and registration expectations
  • assessing desk-reject heuristics
  • re-framing mental-health questions for practice or policy change

When not to use

  • clinical advice
  • regulatory advice
  • small single-site studies
  • mechanistic neuroscience without clinical or population endpoint
  • underpowered intervention pilots
