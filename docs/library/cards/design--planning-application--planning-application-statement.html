planning-application-statement

authentic

Write the design and access statement that supports a planning application — the site analysis, the design rationale, the policy compliance, and the answers to the objections the case officer will receive. Use when asked to write a design and access statement, planning statement, or supporting statement for a planning application, or to respond to a planning objection or refusal. Produces the site and context analysis, the design evolution narrative, the policy-by-policy compliance table, the access and amenity assessment, and the pre-empted objections. Not planning or legal advice; local policy and procedure govern.
