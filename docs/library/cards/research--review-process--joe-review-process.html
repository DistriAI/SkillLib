joe-review-process

separate

Use to understand and navigate the Journal of Econometrics (JoE) editorial process — single-anonymized review, editor screening before a minimum of two referees, the three submission tracks (Regular/Annals/Themed), Guest Associate Editors, and what referees of a methods paper look for.

When to use

  • understand JoE evaluation of methodological submissions
  • choose among Regular, Annals, or Themed Issue tracks
  • anticipate referee objections
  • identify decision-makers like Co-Editors and Guest Associate Editors
  • assemble an editor-screen packet
  • understand what methods referees look for

When not to use

  • double-anonymized journal processes
  • purely applied papers without methodological advance
  • Themed Issues where scope does not match
  • formatting before fixing the formal core
