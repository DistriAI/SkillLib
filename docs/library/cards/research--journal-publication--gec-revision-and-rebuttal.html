gec-revision-and-rebuttal

separate

Use when writing the response to a Global Environmental Change (GEC) revise decision. GEC reviewers are interdisciplinary and often disagree across disciplines, so the response must convert each reviewer on their own terms without alienating the handling editor. Structures the response letter; it does not fabricate new results.

When to use

  • planning the revision and response letter
  • reconciling reviewers who disagree across disciplines
  • responding to requests that change paper claims
  • writing the cover note to the editor

When not to use

  • fabricating new results
