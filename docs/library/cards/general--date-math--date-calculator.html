date-calculator

separate

Calculate medical date windows including gestational age, estimated delivery dates, and follow-up visit scheduling. Produces structured JSON output for clinical research and trial coordination workflows.

When to use

  • Computing gestational age and estimated delivery date from last menstrual period
  • Scheduling follow-up visit windows for clinical trials
  • Generating date ranges for protocol-defined visit windows

When not to use

  • Scheduling general appointments
  • Performing time-zone conversions unrelated to clinical dates
  • Calculating non-medical intervals
