advanced-materials

separate

Use when targeting Advanced Materials (Wiley) or deciding whether a materials science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Advanced Materials (Wiley)
  • deciding whether a materials science manuscript fits this venue
  • choosing between Advanced Materials, Nature Materials, and specialty Wiley Advanced journals
  • identifying desk-reject triggers and credible alternatives
  • assessing fit for electronic and energy materials
  • assessing fit for nanomaterials and nanostructures
  • assessing fit for soft matter, hydrogels, and bio-inspired materials
  • assessing fit for flexible/wearable electronics and biomedical materials

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • primarily a biology study without a clear materials-science contribution
