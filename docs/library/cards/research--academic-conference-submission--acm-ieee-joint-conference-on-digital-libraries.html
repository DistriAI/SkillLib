acm-ieee-joint-conference-on-digital-libraries

separate

Use when targeting ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for digital libraries.

When to use

  • targeting ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for digital libraries
  • digital libraries, archives, scholarly communication, metadata, preservation, and information access
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis
  • submission strategy for digital libraries

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • generic CS paper without naming exact subcommunity, contribution type, and proof or empirical standard
  • retrieval algorithms (route to SIGIR/ECIR)
  • broader information/knowledge management (route to CIKM)
  • social or user-study contribution dominates (route to CSCW/CHI)
  • semantic web/knowledge graphs (route to ISWC / K-CAP)
