cell

Use when targeting Cell (Cell Press) or deciding whether a cell/molecular-biology manuscript fits this flagship venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Cell (Cell Press)
  • deciding if a cell/molecular-biology manuscript fits Cell
  • choosing among Cell, molecular-cell, nature-cell-biology, or nature
  • Cell-specific framing guidance for new mechanistic frameworks
  • Cell's method-and-evidence bar
  • STAR Methods requirements
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • fit / venue-selection / re-framing

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • general biology journal submissions
  • descriptive '-omics' surveys without mechanistic validation
  • structure for structure's sake
  • population-scale association studies alone
  • primary clinical contributions
