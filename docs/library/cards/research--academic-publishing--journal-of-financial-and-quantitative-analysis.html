journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis (JFQA) or deciding whether a finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis
  • deciding whether a finance manuscript fits JFQA
  • re-framing applied financial data papers
  • assessing JFQA desk-reject risks
  • evaluating method and evidence bar for JFQA
  • checking JFQA house style and structure
  • re-routing to alternative finance venues

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • papers that are really accounting or economics framed as finance
  • thin contributions better suited to broad-volume outlets
