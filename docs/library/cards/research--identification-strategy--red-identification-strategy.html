red-identification-strategy

separate

Use when making the inferential backbone of a Review of Economic Dynamics (RED) manuscript credible, adapting to the paper type. For theoretical/computational papers it covers model assumptions, regularity conditions, and what disciplines the parameters; for empirical dynamic papers it covers causal design. RED's scope spans all three, so this skill branches accordingly.

When to use

  • Establishing why the paper's central claim is credible
  • Theoretical/computational dynamic models
  • Methodological/computational-method papers
  • Empirical dynamic papers
  • Parameter discipline documentation
  • Model-solution audit
  • Causal/identification design

When not to use

  • Importing reduced-form identification language into calibrated models
  • Hiding free parameters or equilibrium-existence gaps
  • Asserting generality without showing what relaxing assumptions does
