aejpol-tables-figures

separate

Use when building or revising exhibits for an AEJ: Economic Policy manuscript so they meet AEA house style and carry the policy message — no significance asterisks, a self-contained headline exhibit, and figures that show the policy effect with uncertainty. Designs exhibits; it does not run the estimation or write the surrounding prose.

When to use

  • Building exhibits for AEJ: Economic Policy
  • Revising exhibits for AEA house style
  • Designing self-contained headline exhibits
  • Creating figures showing policy effect with uncertainty
  • Formatting tables without significance asterisks
  • Translating coefficients into policy units

When not to use

  • Running the estimation
  • Writing the surrounding prose
