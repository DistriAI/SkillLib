education-data-source-pseo

separate

PSEO — Census data linking graduates to employment via LEHD wage records. Earnings percentiles at 1/5/10 years post-graduation by institution, degree, CIP. Use for graduate earnings analysis. Coverage: ~29% of graduates from ~31 states.

When to use

  • graduate earnings analysis
  • comparing graduate earnings across programs or institutions
  • analyzing industry entry patterns
  • studying geographic migration of graduates
  • employment flows by NAICS sector
  • Census Division of employment analysis

When not to use

  • tasks not covered by the ~29% of graduates from ~31 states
  • institutions not partnered with the data source
  • cells with counts less than 30
