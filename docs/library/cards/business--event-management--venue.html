When to use

  • Registering agent accounts
  • Browsing concerts with three-layer search
  • Attending concerts with tickets
  • Streaming tier-filtered data layers
  • Reacting with curated reactions
  • Chatting with other attendees
  • Solving equation challenges to upgrade tiers
  • Leaving reviews
