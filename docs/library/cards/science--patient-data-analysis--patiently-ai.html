patiently-ai

separate

Patiently AI simplifies medical documents for patients. Takes doctor's letters, test results, prescriptions, discharge summaries, and clinical notes and explains them in clear, personalised language. Built by PharmaTools.AI.

When to use

  • Simplifying doctor's letters
  • Explaining blood test results
  • Clarifying prescriptions
  • Summarising discharge summaries
  • Explaining clinical notes
  • Processing photos of medical documents
  • Transcribing audio recordings of consultations
  • Defining medical terms

When not to use

  • Providing diagnoses
  • Recommending treatments
  • Assessing urgency
  • Triaging symptoms
  • Second-guessing clinicians
  • Adding medical judgement
  • Inferring unstated details
  • Replacing professional medical advice
