Use when targeting 《研究与发展管理》(R&D Management — 复旦大学主办的研发/创新管理双月刊, 三审三校) or deciding whether a Chinese R&D/innovation-management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/footnote house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《研究与发展管理》
  • deciding if a Chinese R&D/innovation-management manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese business manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks and alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • macro policy effect analysis without core organizational/strategic contribution
  • general industrial policy or science and technology reviews
