reports-on-progress-in-physics

separate

Use when targeting Reports on Progress in Physics (RoPP) or deciding whether a physics review manuscript fits this IOP authoritative progress-report venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Reports on Progress in Physics
  • deciding if a physics review fits RoPP
  • comparing RoPP versus Reviews of Modern Physics
  • re-routing mis-targeted primary research manuscripts
  • understanding RoPP scope and editorial expectations
  • assessing fit for comprehensive physics progress reports

When not to use

  • primary experimental or theoretical research results
  • reviews of a single group's research program
  • reviews of a single technique without field-wide synthesis
  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
