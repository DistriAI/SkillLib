the-year-of-firsts

authentic

Get through the first year after losing someone — the birthdays, holidays, and ordinary triggers that ambush you — with a gentle plan for the hard days instead of being blindsided. Use when asked how do I get through the holidays after a death, the first birthday without them, grief is hitting me in waves, or coping with the first year of loss. Produces a map of the anticipated hard days (anniversaries, holidays, their birthday, firsts), permission and options for how to handle each (keep, change, or skip a tradition), grounding practices for the ambush waves, ways to include their memory, and gentle markers for when grief may need more support — so the first year is met with tenderness and a plan, not dread. Not therapy; points to grief counseling and support groups.

When to use

  • getting through holidays after a death
  • navigating the first birthday without them
  • coping with grief hitting in waves
  • planning for the first year of loss
  • deciding whether to keep, change, or skip traditions
  • finding grounding practices for ambush waves
  • including the memory of the lost person
  • identifying when grief may need more support

When not to use

  • therapy
  • providing medical treatment
  • forcing traditions unchanged
  • pathologizing normal grief
