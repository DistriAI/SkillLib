geb-data-analysis

separate

Use when a Games and Economic Behavior (GEB) manuscript involves experimental data or numerical illustration — analyzing strategic-game experiments and building verified worked examples. Adapts analysis to GEB's game-theory nature, where data supports the theory rather than carrying a causal claim.

When to use

  • analyzing strategic-game experiments
  • building verified worked examples
  • fitting behavioral models to choices
  • numerical examples or simulations to illustrate a theorem
  • auditing research design
  • checking reproducibility

When not to use

  • estimating a treatment effect for its own sake
  • treating individual subjects as independent observations
  • presenting simulations as evidence a theorem is probably true
  • running treatment comparisons with no power justification
  • over-interpreting an experiment as a general causal claim
