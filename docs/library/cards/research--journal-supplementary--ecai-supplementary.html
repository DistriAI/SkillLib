ecai-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what goes in the ECAI 7-page body versus the anonymized supplement — splitting content by decision-criticality so nothing a reviewer needs to judge the paper lives outside the tight body, given there is no unlimited in-paper appendix and no separate artifact track.

When to use

  • splitting content between ECAI body and supplement
  • determining decision-critical content for ECAI
  • anonymizing ECAI supplementary material
  • compressing ECAI body text to fit 7 pages
  • verifying ECAI supplement submission mechanics

When not to use

  • writing the full ECAI paper
  • managing unlimited in-paper appendices
  • using reference pages for body text
