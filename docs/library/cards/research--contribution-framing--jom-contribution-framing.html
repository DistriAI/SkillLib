jom-contribution-framing

separate

Use when articulating the theoretical and practical contribution of a Journal of Operations Management (JOM) manuscript — stating what new OM theory the study delivers, why operations is central to the contribution, and what it means for operations managers, in the intro and discussion.

When to use

  • articulating theoretical contribution
  • articulating practical contribution
  • stating new OM theory
  • explaining why operations is central
  • defining implications for operations managers
  • writing contribution sentences in intro and discussion

When not to use

  • tasks where operations is only the setting
  • novelty claims with no theory movement
  • generic practical implications
  • phenomenon-only papers without OM-theory advance
