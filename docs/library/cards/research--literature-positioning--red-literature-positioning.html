red-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Review of Economic Dynamics (RED) manuscript within the dynamic-economics and SED literature — mapping the quantitative-macro lineage a paper builds on, citing in author-year style, and making the marginal advance legible to the Society for Economic Dynamics readership rather than a generalist audience.

When to use

  • Writing the introduction and related-work sections for a RED submission
  • Identifying which strand of the dynamic-economics literature the paper extends
  • Pitching the contribution to the SED community
  • Mapping the quantitative-macro lineage a paper builds on
  • Creating a lineage map for dynamic-model positioning

When not to use

  • Generalist top-5 framing
  • Reduced-form empirical work without a dynamic-modeling anchor
  • Generalist 'big literature' sweeps that do not locate the paper in a dynamic-model tradition
