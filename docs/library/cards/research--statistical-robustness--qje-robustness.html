qje-robustness

separate

Use when building the robustness suite and online appendix for a Quarterly Journal of Economics (QJE) manuscript — the extensive checks QJE referees expect, exploiting the journal's no-page-limit norm. Plans and prioritizes robustness; it does not change the core identification design.

When to use

  • building the robustness suite
  • building the online appendix
  • planning and prioritizing robustness checks
  • pre-empting referee reports
  • organizing appendix by threat category
  • defending identifying assumptions
  • handling few-cluster inference
  • benchmarking effect sizes

When not to use

  • changing the core identification design
