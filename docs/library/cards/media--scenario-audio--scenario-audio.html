scenario-audio

separate

Use when generating or handling audio on Scenario via MCP. Triggers include music tracks, full-length songs with vocals written from lyrics, background scores, soundtracks, game sound effects, SFX, foley, ambience, looping audio, voiceover, narration, speech, TTS, text-to-speech, dialogue, voice cloning, re-voicing a recording, scoring or adding sound to a video, transcription, or requests to create, wait on, play, or download audio files (MP3, WAV) with Scenario tools.

When to use

  • music tracks
  • full-length songs with vocals
  • background scores
  • soundtracks
  • game sound effects
  • SFX
  • foley
  • ambience
