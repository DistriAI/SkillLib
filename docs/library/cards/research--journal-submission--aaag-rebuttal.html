aaag-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to an Annals of the American Association of Geographers decision letter (major/minor revision) — building a point-by-point response to the subject editor and 1-3 referees across the journal's four areas. Structures the response and revisions; it does not fabricate results.

When to use

  • Responding to Annals of the American Association of Geographers decision letters
  • Building point-by-point responses to subject editors and referees
  • Reconciling conflicting referee comments
  • Strengthening geographic arguments in revisions
  • Addressing spatial-method and cartography fixes
  • Managing the 11,000-word inclusive cap during revisions

When not to use

  • Fabricating results
  • Abandoning qualitative contributions to satisfy quantitative referees
  • Dismissing or arguing combatively with referees
  • Patching mechanics while leaving theoretical critiques unanswered
