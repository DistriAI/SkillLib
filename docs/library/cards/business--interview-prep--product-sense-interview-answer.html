product-sense-interview-answer

separate

Structure a spoken PM product-sense answer with assumptions, segmentation, pain-point prioritization, and MVP tradeoffs. Use when practicing design, improve, or build-next interview questions.

When to use

  • Help PM candidates and interview coaches structure product-sense answers that sound strong out loud, not just on paper. Use this when practicing prompts like "How would you improve X?", "Design a product for Y", or "What would you build ne…

When not to use

  • Behavioral interviews that need STAR stories
  • Execution and analytics cases revolving around metrics diagnosis
  • Go-to-market or pricing interviews
  • Feature brainstorming without target user selection
  • TAM presentations requiring market numbers
