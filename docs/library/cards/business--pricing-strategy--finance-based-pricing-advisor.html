finance-based-pricing-advisor

separate

Evaluate pricing changes using ARPU, conversion, churn risk, NRR, and payback. Use when deciding whether a pricing move should ship.

When to use

  • Evaluate the **financial impact** of pricing changes (price increases, new tiers, add-ons, discounts) using ARPU/ARPA analysis, conversion impact, churn risk, NRR effects, and CAC payback implications. Use this to make data-driven go/no-go…

When not to use

  • Comprehensive pricing strategy design
  • Value-based pricing frameworks
  • Willingness-to-pay research
  • Competitive positioning
  • Psychological pricing
  • Packaging architecture
  • Monetization model selection
  • Changes too small to matter (<5% price change, <10% of customers affected)
