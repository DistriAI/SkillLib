naacl-submission

separate

Use when a paper aimed at NAACL is about to be uploaded to an ACL Rolling Review cycle — verifying that the chosen cycle can still reach the next NAACL edition, that the PDF passes ARR's format and anonymization gates, and that every declaration on the OpenReview form survives a desk-reject audit.

When to use

  • Verifying ARR cycle reaches NAACL
  • Checking PDF format and length limits
  • Auditing anonymization and metadata
  • Reviewing OpenReview form declarations
  • Checking resubmission links and change summaries
  • Validating supplement archives

When not to use

  • Submitting to non-ARR venues
  • Writing the paper content
  • Managing camera-ready versions
