aerj-tables-figures

separate

Use when building tables and figures for an American Educational Research Journal (AERJ) manuscript. Exhibits must be self-contained, accessible, and formatted to APA 7th edition, and they must be anonymized for masked review. Improves exhibits; it does not generate data.

When to use

  • Designing tables/figures
  • Revising exhibits after analysis
  • Fixing unreadable or mislabeled exhibits
  • Preparing exhibits for masked submission
  • Deciding body vs appendix placement
  • Formatting to APA 7th edition
  • Ensuring accessibility and colorblind safety
  • Anonymizing qualitative exhibits

When not to use

  • Generating data
