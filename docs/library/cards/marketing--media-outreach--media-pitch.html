media-pitch

authentic

Write a media pitch or press outreach email for any story or announcement. Use when asked to write a media pitch, journalist outreach email, press pitch, or story angle for PR. Produces a concise pitch with a compelling news angle, journalist-specific hook, and clear call to action.

When to use

  • Write a media pitch
  • Journalist outreach email
  • Press pitch
  • Story angle for PR
  • Draft a journalist outreach email
  • Help me pitch a story
  • Find a good angle for a media pitch

When not to use

  • Mass BCC pitches
  • Press releases in an email
  • Pitches leading with company history
  • Vague data points
  • Follow-ups without new information
