itcs-submission

separate

Use when auditing an ITCS submission for HotCRP readiness — the early-September abstract-then-paper deadlines, single-column >= 11pt format with no hard page limit, the first-10-pages merits window, complete proofs of all central claims, the lightweight double-blind sweep, and the archival-prior-publication check.

When to use

  • ITCS submission audit
  • HotCRP readiness check
  • Abstract and paper deadline verification
  • Single-column 11pt format check
  • First-10-pages merits window review
  • Complete proofs verification
  • Lightweight double-blind anonymity sweep
  • Archival prior-publication check

When not to use

  • Camera-ready formatting
  • Writing style improvement
  • Supplementary material management
  • Related work citation management
