economic-theory-and-business-management

separate

Use when targeting 《经济理论与经济管理》(Economic Theory and Business Management — 教育部主管、中国人民大学主办的经济学月刊, 1981 年创刊, CSSCI) or deciding whether a Chinese econ/management manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/keyword house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《经济理论与经济管理》
  • deciding if a Chinese econ/management manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a generic Chinese econ/management manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency desk-reject risks and alternative submission directions

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to other journals like 《经济评论》 or 《经济科学》 without comparison
  • submitting pure management theory to nankai-business-review
  • submitting serious mathematical models to journal-of-management-sciences-china
