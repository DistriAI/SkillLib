aerj-research-design

separate

Use when defending the research design of an American Educational Research Journal (AERJ) manuscript — quantitative (multilevel, IRT, quasi-experimental, RCT), qualitative (case study, ethnography, interview), or mixed methods. AERJ judges each tradition on its own terms against the AERA reporting standards. Strengthens the design; it does not write code.

When to use

  • Defending quantitative research designs
  • Defending qualitative research designs
  • Defending mixed methods designs
  • Specifying sampling and measurement
  • Justifying causal claims
  • Preparing pre-analysis plans
  • Addressing rival explanations

When not to use

  • Writing code
