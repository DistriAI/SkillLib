work-with-creators

separate

Real creators and influencers, researched, priced and briefed. Use when the marketer says "find influencers", "which creators are worth paying, at what price?", "find real UGC creators to film for our ads", "who should we seed product to", "is this creator's engagement real", "what should I pay this creator" or "write a creator brief". Free research first; one credit yes only to vet deeper. Drafts only: contacts nobody, agrees nothing, moves no money. Not for AI-made UGC-style video — use goose-video; if unsure, ask: a real person filming, or AI-made?

When not to use

  • Sending messages, negotiation, contracts, gifting, codes, payouts — the marketer (backlog: the ops half) · measuring results across channels — **analyze-performance** · approving finished creator content — the marketer · AI-made UGC-style…
