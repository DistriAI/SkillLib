acm-international-conference-on-the-foundations-of-software-engineering

separate

Use when targeting ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering (FSE) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for software engineering flagship.

When to use

  • targeting ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering (FSE)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks
  • software engineering foundations, tools, empirical studies, program analysis, testing, and AI for SE
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • manuscripts where the central contribution is not genuinely in software engineering flagship
  • routing to ICSE, ASE, or ISSTA without comparing contribution type, evidence shape, and reviewer community
