jep-balance-and-objectivity

separate

Use when a Journal of Economic Perspectives (JEP) article must treat competing views and policy debates fairly — synthesis, not advocacy — and acknowledge uncertainty honestly. Audits balance and tone; defer evidence presentation to jep-evidence-without-equations and structure to jep-narrative-arc.

When to use

  • Auditing balance and tone
  • Treating competing views fairly
  • Acknowledging uncertainty honestly
  • Separating positive from normative claims
  • Checking for advocacy red flags
  • Steelmanning opposing views

When not to use

  • Evidence presentation
  • Narrative structure
