Both copies describe the same task (writing a client/service proposal) and share the core structure: problem understanding, solution, scope/deliverables, timeline, pricing, and next steps. However, each contains material steps and constraints the other lacks. jezweb/claude-skills provides detailed tone guidance, concrete examples of 'too corporate' vs 'right tone', specific scope detail levels, and multiple pricing presentation formats (single price, good/better/best, line items). mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills adds a 'Why Us' section, explicit 'Valid until' date requirement, a 'Next Steps' section with named actions and dates, a 'Quality Checks' checklist, and 'Anti-Patterns' that include specific prohibitions (e.g., no vague pricing, no omitting 'not included'). Crowning one would hide the other's unique structural and quality-control elements.
