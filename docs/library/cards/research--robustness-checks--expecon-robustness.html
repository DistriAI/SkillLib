expecon-robustness

separate

Use when an Experimental Economics (ExpEcon) result may be a power artifact, multiple-comparisons artifact, or sensitive to the inference unit, exclusions, or design choices. Hardens the statistical case; it does not design the experiment or draft prose.

When to use

  • Power and sample-size justification
  • Unit-of-observation and cluster-robust inference
  • Multiple comparisons correction
  • Non-parametric vs. parametric testing
  • Robustness to design and analysis choices
  • Handling few-cluster inference

When not to use

  • Designing the experiment
  • Drafting prose
