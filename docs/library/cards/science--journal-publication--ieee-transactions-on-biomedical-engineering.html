ieee-transactions-on-biomedical-engineering

separate

Use when targeting IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering (TBME) or deciding whether a biomedical-engineering methods manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the engineering-method-validated-on-biological-or-clinical-data bar, validation rigor, the TBME-vs-NBME-vs-TMI routing, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • biosignal processing and analysis
  • biomedical instrumentation, sensors, and devices
  • neural engineering
  • biomechanics, rehabilitation engineering, and wearable/assistive systems
  • physiological-system and computational modeling
  • therapeutic and diagnostic technology
  • TBME-vs-NBME-vs-TMI routing
  • desk-reject heuristics

When not to use

  • pure methods with no biomedical validation
  • clinical observations with no engineering contribution
