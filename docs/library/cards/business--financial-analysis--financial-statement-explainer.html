financial-statement-explainer

authentic

Explain a financial statement (P&L, balance sheet, or cash flow) in plain English. Use when asked to explain a P&L / income statement, a balance sheet, a cash flow statement, or to make financials understandable to a non-finance reader. Produces a plain-language walkthrough — what each section means, the line items that matter, the key ratios, and the story the numbers tell — so a non-accountant can read and act on it. Not financial advice.

When to use

  • Explain a P&L / income statement
  • Explain a balance sheet
  • Explain a cash flow statement
  • Make financials understandable to a non-finance reader
  • Walk through statement structure in plain terms
  • Identify key line items and ratios
  • Interpret the story the numbers tell

When not to use

  • Financial advice
  • Investment advice
  • Tax advice
  • Accounting advice
  • Auditing figures
  • Recommending financial decisions
  • Inventing figures
  • Computing ratios from numbers not provided
