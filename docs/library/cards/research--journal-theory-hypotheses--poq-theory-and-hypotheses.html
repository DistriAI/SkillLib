poq-theory-and-hypotheses

separate

Use when building the theoretical argument and hypotheses of a Public Opinion Quarterly (POQ) manuscript — whether the contribution is to opinion/communication theory or to survey methodology. POQ rewards explicit constructs, mechanisms, and testable hypotheses, and methods papers need a clear claim about a survey-error source. Structures the argument; it does not run analyses.

When to use

  • building theoretical argument for POQ manuscripts
  • defining explicit constructs and mechanisms
  • formulating falsifiable hypotheses
  • stating survey-error source claims for methods papers
  • distinguishing substantive effects from measurement artifacts
  • applying Total Survey Error framing

When not to use

  • running analyses
  • polishing manuscripts without theory
  • re-routing to other journals without comparison
