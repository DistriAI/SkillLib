managing-up

combined

Help users master the art of proactive communication and alignment with their managers and founders to unlock autonomy, secure resources, and drive business impact. Work more effectively with your manager — communicate, align, escalate, and get what you need. Use when asked how to manage up, work better with a boss, get buy-in from your manager, escalate without overstepping, or prepare to raise something with leadership. Produces a managing-up plan — what your manager needs and how they operate, how to frame your ask, what to bring vs. escalate, and the message.

When to use

  • proactive communication with managers
  • alignment with founders
  • unlocking autonomy
  • securing resources
  • driving business impact
  • communicating with manager
  • aligning with boss
  • escalating without overstepping

When not to use

  • politics

Skill page

Conflict

The two skills overlap in intent (improving communication and alignment with a manager) but prescribe different procedures. The Lenny's skill is a knowledge-base approach that audits communication, structures cadence, scripts difficult conversations, and maps trust using specific frameworks (Meat and Potatoes, Alignment-Confidence Matrix) and guest insights. The pm-claude-skills skill is a tactical planning tool that requires specific inputs (goal, manager profile, context) and produces a structured 5-part output (What they need, Frame the ask, Bring vs. escalate, The message, Anticipate) with quality checks and anti-patterns. Neither is a reproduction of the other; one provides the strategic 'why' and frameworks, while the other provides the tactical 'how' and output template. Combining them yields a more complete skill that can both diagnose the relationship dynamic and produce a concrete communication plan.
