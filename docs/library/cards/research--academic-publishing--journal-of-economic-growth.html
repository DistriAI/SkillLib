journal-of-economic-growth

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Economic Growth or deciding whether a growth / long-run development manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Economic Growth
  • deciding if a growth manuscript fits this venue
  • choosing among growth/development venues
  • re-framing a paper for long-run growth contribution
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • checking official submission guidelines
  • evaluating method and evidence bar
  • reviewing house style and structure

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • short-run applied micro estimates without long-run payoff
  • fragile cross-country regressions without exogenous variation
  • deep-roots correlations without demonstrated channels
  • growth-theory extensions without new mechanisms or data contact
