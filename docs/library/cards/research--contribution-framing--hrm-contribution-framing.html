hrm-contribution-framing

separate

Use when the one-sentence claim is not sharp for a Human Resource Management (Wiley "HRM") manuscript — articulating the dual contribution the journal demands: a scholarly advance AND a clear implication for workforce policy or HRM practice. Frames the claim; it does not build the theory (hrm-theory-development).

When to use

  • articulating the dual contribution
  • framing the claim
  • sharpening the scholarly advance
  • adding the practice payoff
  • calibrating the practice payoff
  • pre-empting the incremental verdict

When not to use

  • building the theory
