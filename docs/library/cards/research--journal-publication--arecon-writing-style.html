arecon-writing-style

separate

Use when revising an Annual Review of Economics (ARE) review for the ARE voice — authoritative, accessible to economists outside the subfield, signposted, with a strong opening, a forward-looking close, and a survey-style abstract. Polishes prose and structure; it does not design the framework (arecon-organizing-framework) or appraise evidence (arecon-evidence-standards).

When to use

  • revising an Annual Review of Economics review
  • polishing prose and structure
  • making the review read well
  • improving accessibility for economists outside the subfield
  • creating a strong opening arc
  • writing a forward-looking close
  • writing a survey-style abstract
  • ensuring signposted section architecture

When not to use

  • designing the framework
  • appraising evidence
