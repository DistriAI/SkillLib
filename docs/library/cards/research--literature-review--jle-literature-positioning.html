jle-literature-positioning

separate

Use when the marginal contribution of a The Journal of Law and Economics (JLE) manuscript is fuzzy or undersold and must be staked precisely against prior law-and-economics work. Positions the contribution; it does not fix the identification (jle-identification) or the prose (jle-writing-style).

When to use

  • stake marginal contribution against prior law-and-economics work
  • name contribution type (answer, legal variation, institution, mechanism, measurement)
  • identify three closest papers and their gaps
  • state one-sentence honest delta
  • position by legal source of variation
  • cite foundational JLE canon without re-deriving
  • distinguish JLE from sibling journals (JLS, JLEO, ALER)

When not to use

  • fix the identification (jle-identification)
  • fix the prose (jle-writing-style)
  • re-derive foundational results like Coase or Becker
  • conduct a literature tour with no stated marginal addition
