mksc-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Marketing Science manuscript in its literature — locating the contribution among structural and analytical modeling precedents and the relevant substantive stream (pricing, advertising/digital, channels/retail, branding, platforms, analytics), and disclosing self-overlap. Positions the paper; it does not state the headline contribution (mksc-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • Positioning a Marketing Science manuscript in its literature
  • Locating contribution among structural and analytical modeling precedents
  • Identifying relevant substantive stream (pricing, advertising, channels, branding, platforms, analytics)
  • Disclosing self-overlap with prior work
  • Mapping incumbent conversation and unresolved tension
  • Testing manuscript against quantitative marketing reviewers
  • Comparing against neighboring venues (JMR, Management Science, QME)

When not to use

  • Stating the headline contribution
  • Locking demand/supply mechanism
  • Fitting evidence
  • Defining counterfactual decision margin
