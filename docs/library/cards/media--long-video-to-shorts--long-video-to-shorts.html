long-video-to-shorts

separate

Cut one long podcast, interview, course, livestream, or other source video into social-ready shorts, reels, highlights, or clip timelines from existing project media. Use when the user asks to cut a long video into Shorts, Reels, TikToks, Xiaohongshu posts, best moments, highlights, or multiple clips.

When to use

  • cut a long video into Shorts
  • cut a long video into Reels
  • cut a long video into TikToks
  • cut a long video into Xiaohongshu posts
  • extract best moments
  • extract highlights
  • create multiple clips from one long source

When not to use

  • projects mainly multiple raw clips with no dominant long source
  • full talking-head polish request without asking for shorts, clips, highlights, or cutdowns
