nbr-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which nbr-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from fit judgment through rebuttal for 《南开管理评论》 (Nankai Business Review). Routes — does not replace — the specialized skills, and re-routes math-model or macro-policy papers to sibling journal stacks.

When to use

  • Deciding which nbr-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from fit judgment through rebuttal
  • Routing math-model or macro-policy papers to sibling journal stacks
  • Determining fit for 《南开管理评论》
  • Handling R&R external review responses
  • Sequencing theory gap, hypothesis, measurement, and analysis stages

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized nbr-* skills
  • Writing the manuscript content directly
  • Performing statistical analysis or coding
  • Generating rebuttal letters before manuscript revision
