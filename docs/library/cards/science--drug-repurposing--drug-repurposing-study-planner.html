drug-repurposing-study-planner

separate

Design evidence-discovery and validation workflows for drug repurposing studies by integrating disease mechanisms, drug-target logic, expression reversal, real-world evidence, and validation routes into a closed-loop study blueprint.

When to use

  • drug repurposing study design
  • repurposing route family identification
  • evidence chain definition
  • discovery module specification
  • validation ladder clarification
  • assumption and failure point identification
  • closed-loop repurposing research route design

When not to use

  • full protocol writing
  • fabricating drug claims
  • jumping from computational signal to therapeutic recommendation
  • target identification only
  • disease mechanism mapping only
  • expression signature comparison only
  • real-world evidence study design only
  • writing a methods section
