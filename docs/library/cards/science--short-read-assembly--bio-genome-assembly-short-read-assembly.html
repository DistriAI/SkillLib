bio-genome-assembly-short-read-assembly

separate

De novo genome assembly from Illumina short reads using SPAdes. Covers bacterial, fungal, and small eukaryotic genome assembly, as well as metagenome and transcriptome assembly modes.

When to use

  • bacterial genome assembly
  • fungal genome assembly
  • small eukaryotic genome assembly
  • metagenome assembly
  • transcriptome assembly
  • plasmid sequence extraction
  • hybrid assembly with PacBio/Nanopore

When not to use

  • large genome assembly
  • legacy small genome assembly with Velvet
