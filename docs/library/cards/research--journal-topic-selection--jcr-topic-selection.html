jcr-topic-selection

separate

Use when shaping or stress-testing a research question for the Journal of Consumer Research (JCR) — confirming it is a genuine consumer-behavior question with a conceptual contribution, and that JCR (not JCP, JMR, or JM) is the right venue.

When to use

  • shaping a research question for JCR
  • stress-testing a research question for JCR
  • confirming a question is a genuine consumer-behavior question
  • confirming a conceptual contribution
  • choosing between JCR and neighboring venues
  • testing whether work advances consumer theory
  • reframing an effect into a conceptual contribution

When not to use

  • firm-strategy questions
  • pure-modeling questions
  • managerial-strategy marketing questions
  • analytical-modeling marketing questions
  • tight cognitive/affective process with a psychology focus
  • papers with no theory advance
  • papers that are really a managerial or modeling contribution
