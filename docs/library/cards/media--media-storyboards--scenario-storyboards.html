scenario-storyboards

separate

Use when a story must unfold across a sequence of Scenario images: a comic page or strip, manga or webtoon panels, a children's storybook, a film, game, or ad storyboard, animatic pre-viz frames, or any shot list where the same characters and world must persist from panel to panel and dialogue must stay legible. Keywords: storyboard, comic, panel, sequential art, storybook, shot list, pre-viz, animatic, scene to scene, character consistency.

When to use

  • comic page or strip
  • manga or webtoon panels
  • children's storybook
  • film, game, or ad storyboard
  • animatic pre-viz frames
  • shot list with persistent characters
  • sequential art with legible dialogue

When not to use

  • single image generation
  • video generation without storyboard context
