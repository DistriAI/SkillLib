journal-of-international-business-studies

separate

Use when targeting Journal of International Business Studies (JIBS) or deciding whether an international-business / cross-border manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of International Business Studies (JIBS)
  • deciding whether an international-business / cross-border manuscript fits this venue
  • re-framing a management/economics paper so the cross-border dimension carries the contribution
  • identifying JIBS desk-reject risks
  • providing alternative journal lists for international business research

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • single-country or domestic studies with a token 'international' label
  • general strategy/OB/finance papers where the international dimension is incidental
