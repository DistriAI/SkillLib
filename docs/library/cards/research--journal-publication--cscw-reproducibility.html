cscw-reproducibility

separate

Use when strengthening the transparency of a CSCW paper — auditable qualitative analysis trails, documented trace pipelines, codebooks and instruments, and honest data-availability statements when community and participant data cannot ethically be shared.

When to use

  • strengthening transparency of a CSCW paper
  • auditable qualitative analysis trails
  • documented trace pipelines
  • codebooks and instruments
  • honest data-availability statements
  • transparency audit
  • preregistration for confirmatory quantitative strands

When not to use

  • sharing raw participant data that cannot ethically be shared
  • forcing exploratory or interpretive work into preregistration
