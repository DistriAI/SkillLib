nature-food

separate

Use when targeting Nature Food or deciding whether a food-systems manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the food-systems-significance and transdisciplinary bar, evidence and reproducibility expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a food-systems paper
  • re-framing specialist results into food-systems-significance narrative
  • choosing between Nature Food and other journals
  • Nature Food desk-reject heuristics
  • sustainable food production system-level outcomes
  • food environment and nutrition at population scale
  • environmental footprint of food analyses
  • transdisciplinary syntheses connecting production and health

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current author guidelines
  • crop/agronomy field result without systems framing
  • food composition/safety chemistry
  • agroecosystem environment focus
  • broad earth/environment OA framing
  • plant-science mechanism
