cikm-writing-style

separate

Use when revising a CIKM manuscript for the tri-community readership — writing an opening that lands with IR, data-mining, and knowledge-management reviewers simultaneously, compressing into appendix-inclusive page budgets, keeping claims inside the evidence, and maintaining double-blind and disclosure-compliant prose.

When to use

  • revising a CIKM manuscript
  • writing an opening for tri-community readership
  • compressing into appendix-inclusive page budgets
  • keeping claims inside the evidence
  • maintaining double-blind and disclosure-compliant prose
  • pinning cross-community vocabulary
  • structuring a 10-page full paper
  • writing title and abstract as routing devices

When not to use

  • single-lane papers where the boundary is not the hard part
