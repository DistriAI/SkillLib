nejm-figures-tables

separate

Use to build NEJM clinical display items correctly — Table 1 baseline characteristics by group (with standardized differences, not P values), Kaplan-Meier curves with numbers-at-risk, forest plots for subgroups and meta-analyses, and the CONSORT participant flow diagram.

When to use

  • Table 1 baseline characteristics by group
  • Kaplan-Meier curves with numbers-at-risk
  • Forest plots for subgroups and meta-analyses
  • CONSORT participant flow diagram
  • Figure specifications and display-item budget

When not to use

  • Reporting P values for baseline characteristics in randomized trials
  • Kaplan-Meier plots without numbers-at-risk rows
  • Subgroup results presented in text instead of forest plots
  • CONSORT flow diagrams with unreconciled denominators
  • Displaying identifiable patient images or PHI
