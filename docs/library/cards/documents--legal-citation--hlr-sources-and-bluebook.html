hlr-sources-and-bluebook

separate

Use when citing authority in a Harvard Law Review (HLR) piece — pinpoint citations, the authority hierarchy, and The Bluebook (which HLR co-publishes). Governs how sources are cited and weighted; it does not build the footnote apparatus end-to-end (hlr-footnotes-and-cite-check) or write the prose.

When to use

  • citing authority in a Harvard Law Review piece
  • pinpoint citations
  • authority hierarchy
  • The Bluebook formatting
  • drafting or revising footnotes that carry doctrinal weight
  • determining which authorities to cite and in what order
  • fixing inconsistent cite formatting
  • preparing manuscript for student-editor cite-check

When not to use

  • building the footnote apparatus end-to-end
  • writing the prose
