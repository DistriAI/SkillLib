Use when building the literature review for a 《数量经济技术经济研究》 (JQTE) manuscript — organizing it around the method lineage (how a quantity has been measured / a series forecast / a structure decomposed) and the China-data application gap, rather than a chronological pile of recent empirical citations. Use after the topic and contribution type are set.
