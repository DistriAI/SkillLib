jams-data-analysis

separate

Use when running and reporting the analysis for a Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science (JAMS) manuscript — selecting the estimator that matches the design (SEM/PLS, HLM, regression/econometrics, experiments, meta-analysis), reporting effect sizes and uncertainty, and translating estimates into managerial magnitudes. Executes and reports; jams-methods designs the study and jams-contribution-framing states the payoff.

When to use

  • selecting the estimator that matches the design
  • reporting effect sizes and uncertainty
  • translating estimates into managerial magnitudes
  • running and reporting analysis for JAMS manuscripts
  • matching SE clustering to sampling structure
  • reporting SEM and PLS models
  • conducting mediation and moderation analysis
  • addressing robustness threats specific to the design

When not to use

  • designing the study
  • stating the contribution payoff
