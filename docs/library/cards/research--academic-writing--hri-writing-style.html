hri-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or revising an ACM/IEEE HRI full paper's structure and prose for an interdisciplinary audience — leading with the human-robot contribution and research questions, making hypotheses and measures explicit, describing the robot embodiment, keeping claims proportional to the study within the 8-page acmart budget, and writing so the chosen track's contribution type is unmistakable.

When to use

  • drafting HRI full paper structure
  • revising HRI paper prose
  • writing for interdisciplinary audience
  • defining first-page arc
  • stating design and hypotheses
  • describing robot embodiment
  • managing 8-page acmart budget

When not to use

  • formatting violations like shrinking margins
  • system-first framing burying human question
  • undisclosed Wizard-of-Oz behavior
  • using vague liking as outcome
