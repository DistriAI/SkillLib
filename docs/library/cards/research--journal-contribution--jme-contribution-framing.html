jme-contribution-framing

separate

Use when sharpening the "what is new and why it matters for macro/policy" claim of a Journal of Monetary Economics (JME) manuscript — turning a result into a first-order monetary-economics contribution that clears a single-blind, two-referee, "up or out" process.

When to use

  • sharpening the 'what is new and why it matters for macro/policy' claim
  • turning a result into a first-order monetary-economics contribution
  • sharpening the contribution claim for JME
  • framing the paper to survive a desk screen
  • convincing two expert referees
  • justifying an R&R invitation
  • translating the result into who learns what, which mechanism changes, and which alternative explanation is ruled out
  • comparing against JIE, RED, and AEJ Macro for sibling guard

When not to use

  • giving submission-ready advice until the pack's resources/official-source-map.md has been checked
  • giving submission-ready advice until the manuscript has one concrete fix for the largest venue-specific risk
