relion

separate

Validates and executes RELION single-particle cryo-EM refinement and half-map postprocessing. Supports STAR optics/acquisition checks, particle-stack consistency, gold-standard half sets, soft-mask validation, diagnostic Fourier shell correlation, and restart guidance.

When to use

  • RELION single-particle refinement
  • STAR optics and acquisition checks
  • Particle-stack consistency validation
  • Gold-standard half-set refinement
  • Soft-mask validation
  • Diagnostic Fourier shell correlation
  • Restart guidance
  • Half-map postprocessing

When not to use

  • Motion correction
  • Particle picking
  • 2D/3D selection
  • Biological interpretation of map quality
  • Tomography
  • Helical reconstruction
  • Blush
  • Heterogeneous-state modeling
