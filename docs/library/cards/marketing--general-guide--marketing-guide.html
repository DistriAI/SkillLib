marketing-guide

separate

How every GooseWorks marketing playbook runs: connecting, picking the brand, where files go, the shared claims, money, research and schedule rules, and the index of what each playbook saves and who reads it. Load at the start of any playbook run. Not a marketer ask: for "what should I do first?" use where-to-start.

When to use

  • Connecting to GooseWorks
  • Picking the brand
  • Defining file locations
  • Managing shared claims
  • Handling money and spend gates
  • Applying research and schedule rules
  • Indexing playbook outputs

When not to use

  • Answering 'what should I do first?'
  • Direct marketing strategy advice
