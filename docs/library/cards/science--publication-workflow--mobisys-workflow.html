mobisys-workflow

separate

Use when planning a MobiSys project timeline backward from the single early-December paper deadline through registration, two-round review with an early-reject cut, the rebuttal, notification, artifact evaluation, camera-ready, and the June conference — with device-experiment lead time built in and the one-deadline-per-year risk made explicit.

When to use

  • planning a MobiSys project timeline
  • working backward from the December paper deadline
  • managing device-experiment lead time
  • coordinating registration and paper upload
  • managing two-round review and rebuttal
  • preparing artifact evaluation and camera-ready
  • managing the June conference preparation

When not to use

  • planning for MobiCom or NSDI
  • planning for journals with standing editors
  • planning for conferences with two deadlines per year
