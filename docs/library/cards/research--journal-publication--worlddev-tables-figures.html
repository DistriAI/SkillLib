worlddev-tables-figures

separate

Use when exhibits in a World Development (WD) manuscript are dense, asterisk-laden, or fail a multidisciplinary reader — including maps and qualitative displays. Makes every exhibit answer a development question; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • Tables reporting significance with asterisks
  • Coefficients in uninterpretable model units
  • Geographic studies with missing or busy maps
  • Qualitative papers lacking systematic displays
  • Exhibits that do not let readers reconstruct the argument
  • Converting log points to percent
  • Creating coding or thematic matrices
  • Designing grayscale and color-blind safe figures

When not to use

  • Inventing evidence or citations
