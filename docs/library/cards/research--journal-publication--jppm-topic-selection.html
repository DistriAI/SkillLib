jppm-topic-selection

separate

Use when testing whether a research question is policy-first enough for the Journal of Public Policy & Marketing (JPP&M), and which of its domains and paper shapes it fits, before design and data are locked. Settles fit and outlet; it does not build the theory of the policy lever (jppm-theory-development).
