jppm-topic-selection

separate

Use when testing whether a research question is policy-first enough for the Journal of Public Policy & Marketing (JPP&M), and which of its domains and paper shapes it fits, before design and data are locked. Settles fit and outlet; it does not build the theory of the policy lever (jppm-theory-development).

When to use

  • testing if a research question is policy-first enough for JPP&M
  • evaluating fit for JPP&M domains and paper shapes
  • choosing between JPP&M, JM, JCR/JCP, JCA, or policy-school outlets
  • evaluating a recently changed policy like labeling rules or privacy laws
  • assessing if a consumer finding becomes a JPP&M paper by adding policy
  • identifying the regulator, statute, or enforcement debate for a paper
  • drafting a Policy Contribution Statement as a go/no-go memo
  • checking special-issue calendars for fit

When not to use

  • building the theory of the policy lever
  • designing data collection
  • locking in research design
  • developing psychological process theories for JCP/JCR
  • firm strategy or marketing-mix effectiveness for JM/JMR
  • broad marketplace-systems critique without a policy instrument
  • household finance or consumer-affairs education focus
  • program evaluation with no marketing content
