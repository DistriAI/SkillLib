arsoc-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which arsoc-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing an invited review article from topic through revision for an Annual Review of Sociology (ARSoc) manuscript. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which arsoc-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing an invited review article
  • Managing the ARSoc manuscript lifecycle
  • Routing from topic selection to revision

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized arsoc-* skills
  • Handling primary-research papers
  • Processing unsolicited finished manuscripts
  • Managing original empirical research
