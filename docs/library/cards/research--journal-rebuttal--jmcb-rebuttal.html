jmcb-rebuttal

separate

Use when a Journal of Money, Credit and Banking (JMCB) decision letter or referee report has arrived and you need a response-letter strategy and revision plan. Turns the reports into a prioritized, point-by-point rebuttal; it does not re-run the core analysis or re-write the paper.

When to use

  • JMCB R&R or reject-and-resubmit response plan
  • Reconciling conflicting referee demands
  • Prioritizing editor's letter hierarchy
  • Point-by-point rebuttal strategy
  • Handling identification or measurement demands
  • Deciding feasibility of costly asks
  • Managing resubmission fee status

When not to use

  • Re-running the core analysis
  • Re-writing the paper
