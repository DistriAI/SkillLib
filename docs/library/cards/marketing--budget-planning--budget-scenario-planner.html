budget-scenario-planner

separate

Models what happens to your CPA, ROAS, conversion volume, and impression share when you increase or decrease budget by any amount. Uses your actual account data and historical diminishing returns patterns, not generic industry assumptions. Platform: Google and Meta.

When to use

  • Client or leadership asks to increase or cut budget
  • Quarterly planning when setting budget allocations
  • When deciding which campaigns deserve more budget
  • Before pitching a budget increase to show the projected return
