pmla-structure-and-exposition

separate

Use when organizing a PMLA (Publications of the Modern Language Association) essay so the argument unfolds clearly within the 6,000–9,000-word range. PMLA prizes a concise, readable presentation that draws out implications; the structure must carry a generalist reader through close reading and argument. Shapes the architecture; it does not write the prose.

When to use

  • Outlining the essay
  • Reorganizing a sprawling draft
  • Cutting structurally to meet word range
  • Deciding placement of thesis, readings, and stakes
  • Mapping essay moves
  • Testing manuscript for field-crossing intervention

When not to use

  • Writing the prose
  • Polishing prose
  • Re-routing to other journals
