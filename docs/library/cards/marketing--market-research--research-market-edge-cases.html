Step of the research-market playbook: rules for run edge cases such as no browser, stalled workers, earlier reports and late reads. Run research-market instead; not for direct use.

  • no browser at all

  • worker is stopped
  • facts from earlier saved research
  • saved rival that isn't one
  • earlier report on this brand exists
  • read lands after the report

When not to use

  • direct use
  • questions before the report
