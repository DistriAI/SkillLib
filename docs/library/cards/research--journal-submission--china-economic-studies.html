china-economic-studies

separate

Use when targeting 《中国经济问题》(China Economic Studies — 厦门大学主办的经济学双月刊, 不收版面费) or deciding whether a Chinese econ manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/keyword house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《中国经济问题》
  • deciding if a Chinese econ manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a generic Chinese econ manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking official submission requirements
  • assessing topic fit for Chinese economic growth, reform, and development
  • assessing topic fit for fiscal finance, industrial organization, and regional economics
  • assessing topic fit for open economy, trade investment, and cross-strait economics

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to other journals like 《会计评论》 or 《经济学（季刊）》
  • handling manuscripts that are only industry background or local cases without clear economic mechanisms
