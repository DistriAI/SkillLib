ndss-author-response

separate

Use when writing an NDSS rebuttal and working the interactive discussion phase — triaging Round-2 reviews under a short window, answering adaptive-attack and ethics objections with pointable evidence, and negotiating toward Accept or a well-scoped Minor/Major Revision.

When to use

  • writing an NDSS rebuttal
  • working the interactive discussion phase
  • triaging Round-2 reviews
  • answering adaptive-attack objections
  • answering ethics objections
  • negotiating toward Accept
  • negotiating toward Minor/Major Revision

When not to use

  • rewriting the paper's argument from scratch
  • burning word budgets on the strongest review
  • using sarcasm at a misreading
  • promising in the response what the revision cannot deliver
