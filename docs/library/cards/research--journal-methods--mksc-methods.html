mksc-methods

separate

Use when the empirical/analytical approach is the bottleneck for a Marketing Science manuscript — choosing among structural econometrics, analytical modeling, and model-disciplined causal/ML methods, and making the model estimable and identified. Designs the approach; it does not execute the estimation and counterfactuals (mksc-data-analysis).

When to use

  • Choosing between structural, analytical, or reduced-form/causal-ML approaches
  • Making a written model estimable with parameters, moments, and normalization
  • Defining specific instruments and identifying variation
  • Designing structural demand, dynamic discrete choice, or analytical game-theoretic models
  • Specifying estimators like GMM, MLE, SMM, or hierarchical Bayes
  • Planning computation including solvers, fixed points, and multiple equilibria
  • Validating counterintuitive predictions in analytical papers
  • Auditing empirical/causal lane designs using StatsPAI or Stata MCP

When not to use

  • Executing the estimation and counterfactuals
  • Structural demand and analytical modeling execution within the causal-inference toolchain
