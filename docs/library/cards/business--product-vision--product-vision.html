Skill page

Conflict

The two skills overlap in the goal of creating an inspiring, achievable product vision, but they are not interchangeable. The phuryn copy is a concise, self-contained procedure for drafting and selecting a vision statement. The RefoundAI copy is a broader, research-grounded framework that adds specific techniques (emotional metaphors, visual storyboards, cross-functional synthesis, backward planning) and a library of named frameworks (PRFAQ, 10-Star, Painted Picture) that the first copy lacks. Conversely, the phuryn copy provides a specific output format (3-5 variations) and a selection rationale step that the RefoundAI copy does not explicitly mandate. Combining them captures the full range of methods and constraints.
