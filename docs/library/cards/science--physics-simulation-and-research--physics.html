Skill page

Conflict

The two copies address completely different tasks under the same name. The first is a Unity editor skill for running physics queries (raycasts, overlaps) and configuring physics settings (gravity, materials, layer collisions). The second is a research skill that routes to guides for astrophysics, computational physics, and particle physics data analysis. They do not overlap in procedure or parameters, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
