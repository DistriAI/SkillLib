ahr-argument-development

separate

Use when turning archival findings into a sustained historical argument for The American Historical Review (AHR). The AHR rewards a clear thesis that earns its claims from evidence while remaining alert to contingency and complexity. Builds the argument and its stakes; it does not gather new sources or fabricate evidence.

When to use

  • turning archival findings into a sustained historical argument
  • stating a contestable thesis
  • making stakes explicit
  • building a chain of evidence
  • arguing against the strongest alternative
  • calibrating claims to avoid over- or under-claiming
  • addressing referee pushback regarding argument failures

When not to use

  • gathering new sources
  • fabricating evidence
