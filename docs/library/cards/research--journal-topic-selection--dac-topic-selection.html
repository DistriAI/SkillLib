dac-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether an EDA or chip-design project belongs at the ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference (DAC) and, if so, in the double-blind archival Research Manuscript track versus the industry-facing Engineering Track — or whether it should route to a sibling EDA venue (ICCAD, DATE, ASP-DAC) or a computer-architecture venue (ISCA/MICRO/HPCA), decided by contribution shape, QoR evidence maturity, and the November calendar.

When to use

  • Deciding if a project belongs at DAC
  • Choosing between Research Manuscript and Engineering Track
  • Routing to sibling EDA venues like ICCAD, DATE, or ASP-DAC
  • Routing to computer-architecture venues like ISCA, MICRO, or HPCA
  • Evaluating contribution shape and QoR evidence maturity
  • Assessing ML-for-EDA contributions
  • Evaluating hardware security contributions
  • Determining venue based on the November calendar

When not to use

  • Pure computer-architecture results with incidental EDA angle
  • Analog/RF circuit design contributions
  • Test/reliability depth contributions
  • ML papers with toy EDA wrappers
  • Device-physics contributions without automation or design-quality focus
