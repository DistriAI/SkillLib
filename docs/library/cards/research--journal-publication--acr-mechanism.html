acr-mechanism

separate

Use when the mechanism analysis for a 《会计研究》 (Accounting Research) manuscript is missing or mis-specified — the channel must be an information mechanism (reduce information asymmetry, raise disclosure quality, constrain earnings management, improve audit assurance), not a generic pricing or financing channel. Use after the main result holds.

When to use

  • Mechanism analysis for Accounting Research manuscripts
  • Information mechanism specification
  • Testing information asymmetry reduction
  • Testing disclosure quality improvement
  • Testing earnings management constraints
  • Testing audit assurance improvement
  • Cross-sectional moderation by information environment
  • Excluding alternative mechanisms like governance or financing

When not to use

  • Generic pricing channels
  • Financing channels
  • Tasks where the main result does not hold
  • Mechanisms not related to accounting information production, disclosure, or assurance
