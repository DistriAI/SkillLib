rt-venue-reframe

separate

Use after a rejection, or when switching target journals, to turn a manuscript framed for one venue into one framed for another. Produces a concrete reframing diff — introduction arc, contribution claim, evidence to add or cut, house style, format — by reading both venues' own packs. Complements rt-journal-match, which picks the venue; this one does the rewrite plan.

When to use

  • Reframing a manuscript after rejection
  • Switching target journals
  • Comparing reframe costs between two live targets
  • Re-aiming a paper from an international venue to a Chinese venue
  • Re-aiming a paper from a Chinese venue to an international venue
  • Generating a five-layer reframing diff
  • Ranking reframe changes by cost and necessity

When not to use

  • Picking the next venue
  • Scoring the reframed draft against the new venue
  • Checking mechanical submission readiness
  • Overruling the venue's own topic-selection skill
  • Overstating results or changing findings
  • Reframing from memory of a venue's reputation
