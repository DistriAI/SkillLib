soda-related-work

separate

Use when positioning a SODA (ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms) paper in the algorithms literature — tracing bound lineages across SODA/STOC/FOCS/ESA/ICALP and journals, handling arXiv-first priority culture, citing conference versus journal versions correctly, and self-citation under lightweight double-blind rules.

When to use

  • tracing bound lineages across SODA/STOC/FOCS/ESA/ICALP and journals
  • handling arXiv-first priority culture
  • citing conference versus journal versions correctly
  • self-citation under lightweight double-blind rules
  • verifying bound lineages for comparison tables
  • sweeping direct predecessors, journal consolidations, lower bounds, concurrent arXiv, and adjacent-model results
  • managing bibliography mechanics for dual-version results
  • drafting positioning prose patterns like lineage sentences and incomparability paragraphs

When not to use

  • adjudicating priority disputes in the paper
  • citing in-submission companion manuscripts that referees cannot read
  • quoting bounds from paper abstracts
  • citing arXiv identifiers for results with published versions
