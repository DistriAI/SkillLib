Conflict

Both copies describe the same end-to-end research pipeline, but they disagree on stage numbering, gate behavior, and which sub-skills are invoked. The wanshuiyin copy adds a heartbeat/watchdog cadence, a Lean formal-proof route, and many extra constants (CODE_REVIEW, BASE_REPO, COMPACT, AUTO_WRITE, VENUE, RENDER_HTML, RESUMABLE) and a resume mechanism. The brycewang copy adds an explicit 10-second auto-proceed wait, a RESEARCH_BRIEF.md input, and a different stage layout (implementation and deployment as separate stages, auto-review as Stage 4). Crowning one would hide the other's real operational steps and constraints.
