facct-author-response

separate

Use when drafting ACM FAccT author responses — the short factual-correction rebuttal after preliminary reviews, and, distinctively for the 2026-new process, the Revise-and-resubmit round where you address Area-Chair-prioritized concerns from a mixed CS+law+social-science panel, map each to a concrete change, and survive a re-review while keeping mutual anonymity.

When to use

  • drafting ACM FAccT author responses
  • short factual-correction rebuttals
  • Revise-and-resubmit responses
  • addressing Area-Chair-prioritized concerns
  • mapping concerns to concrete changes
  • maintaining mutual anonymity in responses

When not to use

  • re-arguing substantive disagreement in the short rebuttal
  • promising a new study in the short rebuttal
  • pasting new results reviewers cannot verify in the current PDF
