rand-journal-of-economics

separate

Use when targeting RAND Journal of Economics or deciding whether an industrial-organization manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting RAND Journal of Economics
  • deciding if an IO manuscript fits the venue
  • choosing among IO and applied-micro venues
  • re-framing papers to highlight IO mechanisms
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • checking official submission guidelines
  • evaluating method and evidence bar
  • reviewing house style and structure

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • general micro theory
  • trade
  • regulation-as-law
  • pure mechanism design
  • econometric advances
  • firm-level export structure
