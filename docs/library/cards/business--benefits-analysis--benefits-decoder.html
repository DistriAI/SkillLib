benefits-decoder

authentic

Decode an employment benefits package into what it's actually worth and where the fine print bites. Use when someone asks 'is this offer good', 'decode my benefits package', 'what does my equity actually mean', or 'what should I ask HR before signing'. Produces a benefit-by-benefit decode with real dollar values, ranked red flags (vesting cliffs, clawbacks, 'discretionary' bonuses, unlimited-PTO economics), and the questions to ask HR before signing.

When to use

  • Decoding employment benefits packages
  • Evaluating equity grants and vesting
  • Calculating 401k/pension match math
  • Identifying red flags like clawbacks and discretionary bonuses
  • Generating questions to ask HR before signing
  • Analyzing insurance coverage and PTO economics

When not to use

  • Providing legal or financial advice
  • Inventing benefits or terms not in documents
  • Presenting jurisdiction-dependent rules as universal
