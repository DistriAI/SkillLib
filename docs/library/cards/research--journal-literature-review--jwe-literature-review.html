jwe-literature-review

separate

Use when a 《世界经济》 (The Journal of World Economy) literature review cites only domestic empirics and fails to enter the international trade / international finance lineage. Ensures dialogue with the field's classics and frontier (gravity, heterogeneous firms, GVC, open-economy macro, capital flows), not a citation pile.

When to use

  • Literature reviews for The Journal of World Economy
  • International trade and finance lineage dialogue
  • Organizing reviews by source, progress, and position
  • Identifying classic and frontier anchors
  • Defining marginal contribution relative to international literature

When not to use

  • Reviews citing only domestic empirics
  • Citation piles without dialogue
  • Using 'no domestic research' as sole contribution
  • Citing classics without methodological relevance
