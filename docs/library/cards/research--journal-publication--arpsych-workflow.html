arpsych-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which arpsych-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing an invited review article from topic through revision for an Annual Review of Psychology (ARPsych) manuscript. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which arpsych-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing an invited review article for ARPsych
  • Routing from topic selection to revision
  • Handling ARPsych production and peer-review letters
  • Managing the lifecycle of an ARPsych manuscript

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized arpsych-* skills
  • Primary research papers
  • Self-submitted reviews
  • Stand-alone meta-analyses
  • Psychological Bulletin submissions
  • Annual Review of Clinical Psychology submissions
  • Perspectives on Psychological Science submissions
