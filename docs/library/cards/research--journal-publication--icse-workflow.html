icse-workflow

separate

Use when planning an ICSE research-track campaign end to end, covering the single-cycle calendar from the June abstract deadline through September response, October decisions, the November revision sprint, artifact evaluation, camera-ready, and the April conference, plus rerouting timelines after rejection.

When to use

  • planning an ICSE research-track campaign
  • managing the single-cycle calendar
  • preparing for the June abstract deadline
  • managing the September author response window
  • planning the November revision sprint
  • handling artifact evaluation and camera-ready
  • rerouting timelines after rejection

When not to use

  • planning for two-cycle ICSE models
  • resubmitting to a second cycle
