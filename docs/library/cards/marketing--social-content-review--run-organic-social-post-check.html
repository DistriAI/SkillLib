run-organic-social-post-check

separate

Checks a draft social post before it goes out: voice match against the account, would a person actually post it, AI-sounding phrases, claims against what the brand has signed off, banned words, disclosure and rights, trend timing, and whether a named person must approve it; rewrites weak drafts. Use alone when you already have the draft, the brand's voice and approved claims; otherwise run run-organic-social.

When to use

  • voice match against the account
  • share-worthiness and founder test
  • AI-sounding phrases
  • claims against brand sign-off
  • banned words
  • disclosure and rights
  • trend timing
  • person approval gate

When not to use

  • creating drafts from scratch
  • generating brand voice cards
  • managing social media calendars
