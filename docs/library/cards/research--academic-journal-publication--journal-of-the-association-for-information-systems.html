journal-of-the-association-for-information-systems

separate

Use when targeting Journal of the Association for Information Systems (JAIS) or deciding whether a theory-rich, methodologically broad information-systems manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of the Association for Information Systems (JAIS)
  • deciding whether a theory-rich, methodologically broad information-systems manuscript fits this venue
  • theory-development, conceptual, or methodologically diverse IS paper
  • empirical or design IS paper with a strong theoretical ambition
  • JAIS desk-reject risks
  • Basket / INFORMS alternative list
  • IS theory development and conceptual contributions
  • Behavioral IS, economics of IS, and design science

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • Behavioral/design IS for the field's premier outlet
  • Quantitative/economic IS with an INFORMS audience
  • Management-of-IS, IT business value, IS strategy
  • Broad quantitative management
  • computational/ML-for-OR with no IS theory
