transportation-research-part-b-methodological

separate

Use when targeting Transportation Research Part B (Methodological) or deciding whether a transportation manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's methodological-flagship fit, the theoretical-contribution bar, the Part B vs. Part A/C/E routing, modeling-and-proof rigor, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for Part B manuscripts
  • re-framing applied papers into methodological contributions
  • deciding among Part B, A, C, and E
  • modeling-rigor and proof expectations
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • traffic flow theory
  • network equilibrium and traffic assignment
  • transportation network design and optimization

When not to use

  • applied case studies with no methodological novelty
  • policy/behavior applications
  • emerging technologies
  • logistics/transportation economics applications
  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
