jebo-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a behavioral, experimental, or organizational question fits the Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization (JEBO) rather than a sibling outlet, and how to frame it. Sharpens scope and the behavioral hook; it does not invent evidence, editors, or citations.

When to use

  • deciding if a question fits JEBO
  • framing the behavioral hook
  • evaluating behavioral content
  • checking generality of the lesson
  • assessing method-fit
  • determining sibling journal boundaries

When not to use

  • inventing evidence
  • inventing editors
  • inventing citations
  • claiming unverified process facts
