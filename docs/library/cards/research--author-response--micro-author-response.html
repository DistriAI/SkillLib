micro-author-response

separate

Use when reviews arrive for a MICRO submission and the rebuttal/revision window opens — triaging reviewer objections by what can change a decision, answering methodology attacks with runs and configs rather than promises, keeping anonymity intact, and staying inside MICRO's professional-conduct rules for rebuttals.

When to use

  • triaging reviewer objections
  • answering methodology attacks with runs and configs
  • keeping anonymity intact
  • staying inside MICRO's professional-conduct rules
  • coordinating multi-author responses
  • prioritizing responses under length caps

When not to use

  • unsolicited chair contact to append arguments
  • promising experiments that were not run
  • introducing results without a manifest
