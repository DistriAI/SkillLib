global-change-biology

separate

Use when targeting Global Change Biology or deciding whether a biology/ecology manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the global-change-driver framing and biological-evidence bar, scale and mechanism expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a global-change ecology or biology paper
  • re-framing a local ecological, physiological, or soil/biogeochemical study around an explicit global-change driver
  • choosing between Global Change Biology, a general ecology journal, and an earth-system or biogeochemistry venue
  • checking the journal's global-change-framing requirement and biological-evidence bar
  • assessing responses of organisms, populations, communities, and ecosystems to climate change, elevated CO2, warming, drought, and shifting disturbance regimes
  • evaluating mechanistic, manipulative experiments, long-term observations, syntheses, and modeling of global-change responses
  • reviewing structure and house style for research articles, reviews, syntheses, and shorter formats
  • performing an official-submission re-check and pre-submission self-check

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current author guidelines
  • local ecology, physiology, or biogeochemistry studies with no global-change driver or framing
