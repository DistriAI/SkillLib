icdm-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or revising prose for an ICDM (IEEE International Conference on Data Mining) paper - the data-regime-first register, the named-mechanism discipline, measured-scale language instead of scalability adjectives, discovery-validity sentences, triple-blind-safe self-reference, and compression into the single 10-page IEEE all-inclusive cap.

When to use

  • drafting ICDM paper prose
  • revising ICDM paper prose
  • applying data-regime-first register
  • enforcing named-mechanism discipline
  • using measured-scale language
  • writing discovery-validity sentences
  • ensuring triple-blind-safe self-reference
  • compressing content into 10 IEEE pages

When not to use

  • tasks not involving ICDM paper drafting or revision
