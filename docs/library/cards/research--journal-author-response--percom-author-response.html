percom-author-response

separate

Use when drafting an IEEE PerCom rebuttal after being invited following the initial reviews, covering the bounded single-round reply that answers explicit reviewer questions, corrects misreadings, points to evidence already in the paper without promising new experiments, and preserves double-blindness.

When to use

  • drafting an IEEE PerCom rebuttal
  • answering explicit reviewer questions
  • correcting misreadings
  • pointing to evidence already in the paper
  • preserving double-blindness
  • triaging reviewer concerns
  • structuring the bounded reply
  • checking anonymity

When not to use

  • papers with no positive review
  • early-rejected papers
  • promising new experiments
  • journal-style revision
  • revealing author identity
  • revealing institution identity
  • revealing testbed identity
  • revealing dataset-owner identity
