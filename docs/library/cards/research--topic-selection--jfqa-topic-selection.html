jfqa-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a research question fits the Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis (JFQA) — empirical and quantitative financial economics (corporate finance, investments, capital and security markets, financial institutions, finance-relevant quantitative methods). Use before investing in a JFQA submission to test scope fit and the quantitative-evidence bar.

When to use

  • judging research question fit for JFQA
  • testing scope fit for financial economics
  • evaluating quantitative-evidence bar
  • scoring candidate questions using the fit rubric
  • assessing borderline calls from adjacent fields
  • portfolio thinking under fee structure

When not to use

  • pure macro research
  • accounting-only research
  • generic econometrics
  • descriptive industry studies without quantitative tests
  • method papers without genuine finance application
  • incremental anomalies without economic mechanism
