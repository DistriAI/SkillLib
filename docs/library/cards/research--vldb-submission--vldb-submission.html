vldb-submission

separate

Use when auditing a PVLDB submission before a monthly deadline, covering the mandatory abstract on the 25th, the CMT window, the 1st-of-month 5:00 PM Pacific cutoff, category and page-budget choice, single-blind cover-page requirements, the per-month and per-year author caps, and desk-reject hazards specific to VLDB.

When to use

  • Auditing PVLDB submissions
  • Checking abstract registration deadlines
  • Verifying category and page budgets
  • Confirming single-blind cover page requirements
  • Checking per-month and per-year author caps
  • Identifying desk-reject hazards

When not to use

  • Submitting to SIGMOD or ML venues
  • Managing non-PVLDB conference deadlines
