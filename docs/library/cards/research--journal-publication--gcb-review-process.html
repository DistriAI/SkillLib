gcb-review-process

separate

Use to understand how a Global Change Biology (GCB) manuscript is judged — high desk-rejection screening for scope and significance, expert single-anonymous peer review with 2–3 reviewers, and the decision categories. Sets expectations and informs preparation; it does not contact editors or predict outcomes.

When to use

  • Setting expectations before submitting
  • Diagnosing why a paper might be desk-rejected
  • Anticipating reviewer concerns
  • Interpreting a decision letter
  • Understanding editorial desk screening
  • Understanding expert peer review
  • Understanding decision categories

When not to use

  • Contacting editors
  • Predicting outcomes
