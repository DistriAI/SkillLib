aer-insights

separate

Use when targeting AER: Insights or deciding whether a short-format economics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting AER: Insights
  • deciding if a short-format manuscript fits
  • evaluating a single strong result
  • deciding between Insights and full AER
  • cutting a full-length manuscript to a focused short paper
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • finding full-length or field alternatives

When not to use

  • multi-essay omnibus papers
  • exhaustive field surveys
  • projects requiring many moving parts
  • replacing current official submission guidelines
