aejpol-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which aejpol-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal for an AEJ: Economic Policy manuscript. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which aejpol-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal
  • diagnosing current manuscript bottlenecks
  • switching into revision mode after a decision letter
  • routing based on paper archetype

When not to use

  • replacing specialized aejpol-* skills
  • treating AEJ: Policy like J. Public Economics
  • treating AEJ: Policy like AEJ: Applied
  • deferring replication package to acceptance
  • polishing exhibits while identification is moving
  • reporting estimates without welfare/cost-benefit reading
