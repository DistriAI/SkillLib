management-science-cn

separate

Use when targeting 《管理科学》(Management Science — 哈尔滨工业大学管理学院主办的管理科学与工程类双月刊, 1987 年创刊, 管理科学与工程学会会刊) or deciding whether a Chinese management/management-science manuscript fits this venue. 注意本刊为中文期刊《管理科学》, 与美国 INFORMS 的 Management Science 同名不同刊。Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/length house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Targeting Chinese journal 《管理科学》
  • Deciding manuscript fit for 《管理科学》
  • Rewriting general Chinese management manuscripts for 《管理科学》
  • Listing high-frequency rejection risks for 《管理科学》
  • Checking abstract and length house style for 《管理科学》

When not to use

  • US INFORMS Management Science journal
  • Macro policy effect analysis
  • Behavioral or organizational management theory construction
  • System engineering theory and practice
  • Journal of Management Sciences in China
  • Chinese Journal of Management Science
