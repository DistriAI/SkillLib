jom-rebuttal

separate

Use when planning and drafting a revision and point-by-point response letter for a Journal of Operations Management (JOM) R&R — prioritizing the Department Editor/Associate Editor's decisive concerns, resolving Empirical Research Methods method-check issues, and documenting new operations analyses across multiple rounds.

When to use

  • Planning JOM R&R revision
  • Drafting point-by-point response letter
  • Triage of DE/AE and reviewer comments
  • Resolving Empirical Research Methods issues
  • Documenting new operations analyses
  • Managing multi-round revisions

When not to use

  • Initial manuscript submission
  • Non-JOM journal submissions
  • Cosmetic edits without substantive change
  • Arguing away method-check concerns
