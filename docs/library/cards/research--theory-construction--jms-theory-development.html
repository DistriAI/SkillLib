jms-theory-development

separate

Use when the theoretical argument is the bottleneck for a Journal of Management Studies (JMS) manuscript — building a mechanism and deriving hypotheses a priori, OR abstracting grounded constructs and propositions from qualitative data. Constructs the theory both deductively and inductively; it does not run the analysis (jms-data-analysis) or write the final contribution paragraph (jms-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • building a mechanism and deriving hypotheses a priori
  • abstracting grounded constructs and propositions from qualitative data
  • constructing theory deductively
  • constructing theory inductively
  • engaging rival explanations
  • fixing deductive hypotheses before analysis
  • tracing inductive constructs to data

When not to use

  • running the analysis
  • writing the final contribution paragraph
