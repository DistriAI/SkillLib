jppm-submission

separate

Use when running the final preflight for a Journal of Public Policy & Marketing (JPP&M) submission — the two-file split, double-anonymization, 50-page cap, Policy Contribution Statement, abstract/keywords, and the SageTrack portal. Final checks only; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Running final preflight for JPP&M submission
  • Verifying 50-page all-inclusive cap
  • Assembling title page, main document, and web appendix
  • Verifying double-anonymization
  • Checking Policy Contribution Statement
  • Verifying abstract and keywords
  • Checking SageTrack portal readiness

When not to use

  • Drafting content
