asq-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a research idea fits Administrative Science Quarterly (ASQ) and has the *surprising* theoretical insight ASQ demands. Sharpens the puzzle and the contribution claim; it does not design the study or write theory sections.

When to use

  • judging whether a research idea fits ASQ
  • sharpening the puzzle
  • sharpening the contribution claim
  • choosing between ASQ and other journals
  • evaluating if an idea is a puzzle or a gap
  • drafting a one-paragraph contribution claim

When not to use

  • designing the study
  • writing theory sections
