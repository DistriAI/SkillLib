european-conference-on-information-retrieval

separate

Use when targeting European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for information retrieval.

When to use

  • targeting European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for information retrieval
  • IR methods, evaluation, search interaction, recommender systems, and retrieval applications
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • generic CS paper without naming exact subcommunity, contribution type, and proof or empirical standard
  • SIGIR for flagship IR
  • CHIIR for interactive IR
  • CIKM for broader information/knowledge management
  • ACL/EMNLP when NLP modeling is central
