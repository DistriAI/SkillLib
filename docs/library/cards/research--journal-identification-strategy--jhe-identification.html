jhe-identification

separate

Use when the identification argument is the bottleneck for a Journal of Health Economics (JHE) manuscript — quasi-experimental health-policy variation, selection into insurance/treatment, eligibility RD, or structural identification of demand/provider parameters. Stress-tests the data-to-estimand mapping to the JHE bar before exhibits are finalized; it does not write prose or build the package.

When to use

  • quasi-experimental health-policy variation
  • selection into insurance/treatment
  • eligibility RD
  • structural identification of demand/provider parameters
  • DiD / event study
  • IV / structural identification
  • stress-testing data-to-estimand mapping

When not to use

  • writing prose
  • building the package
