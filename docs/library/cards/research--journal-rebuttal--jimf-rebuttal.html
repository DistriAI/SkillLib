jimf-rebuttal

separate

Use when a Journal of International Money and Finance (JIMF) decision letter has arrived and you need a response-letter strategy for an R&R or a reject-and-resubmit. Plans the revision and response; it does not pre-empt objections before submission (jimf-referee-strategy).

When to use

  • JIMF decision letter response strategy
  • R&R or reject-and-resubmit planning
  • Reconciling divergent referee reports
  • Prioritizing must-do vs. push-back comments
  • Structuring point-by-point response letters
  • Handling JIMF-specific revision demands

When not to use

  • Pre-empting objections before submission
  • jimf-referee-strategy
