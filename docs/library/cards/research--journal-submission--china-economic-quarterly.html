china-economic-quarterly

separate

Use when targeting 《经济学(季刊)》(China Economic Quarterly, CEQ), the Peking University / CCER journal closest to international top-field economics standards in China. Apply to enforce a clean structural model or credibly identified causal design, rigorous econometric exposition, frontier-aligned framing, and CEQ formatting. Strictest on methodology among Chinese econ journals.

When to use

  • targeting 《经济学(季刊)》
  • enforcing clean structural model
  • enforcing credibly identified causal design
  • rigorous econometric exposition
  • frontier-aligned framing
  • CEQ formatting
  • comparing CEQ with 《经济研究》
  • checking modern DID compliance

When not to use

  • pure descriptive studies
  • pure policy evaluation
  • correlation studies lacking identification
  • industry background or local case studies
  • tasks for 《经济管理》
  • tasks for 《会计评论》
  • tasks for 《中国经济问题》
  • tasks for 《中国工业经济》
