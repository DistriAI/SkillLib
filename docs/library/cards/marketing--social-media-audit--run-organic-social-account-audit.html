run-organic-social-account-audit

separate

Reviews a brand's own social accounts: what each is for, the last ~30 posts ranked against the account's own median with the likely reason for top and bottom posts, cadence actually kept, best posting times, and bio and pinned-post fixes. Use alone when you already know which accounts to review and can read their posts; otherwise run run-organic-social.

When to use

  • Reviewing brand's own social accounts
  • Ranking last ~30 posts against account median
  • Analyzing cadence actually kept
  • Identifying best posting times
  • Suggesting bio and pinned-post fixes
  • Running before the first calendar
  • Light reconciliation on later runs

When not to use

  • Use when you do not know which accounts to review
  • Use when you cannot read their posts
  • Copying competitor or category references
