mobicom-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what goes in the MobiCom 12-page double-column body versus references, optional appendices, and HotCRP fields — placing channel-model derivations, full protocols, and extended measurements outside the cap, keeping the contribution self-contained, and ensuring rebuttal and revision packets stay off the page count.

When to use

  • Deciding content placement for MobiCom 12-page body
  • Placing channel-model derivations in appendices
  • Placing full protocols in appendices
  • Placing extended measurements in appendices
  • Ensuring body is self-contained for novelty and correctness
  • Auditing HotCRP submission fields
  • Checking anonymity in appendices
  • Keeping rebuttal and revision packets off page count

When not to use

  • Writing the actual paper content
  • Performing the rebuttal response
  • Managing the revision process
  • Trimming references for space or anonymity
