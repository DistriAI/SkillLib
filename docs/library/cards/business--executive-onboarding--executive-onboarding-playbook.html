executive-onboarding-playbook

separate

Plan a VP or CPO 30-60-90 day diagnostic onboarding path. Use when entering a new executive product role and avoiding premature change.

When to use

  • Structure the first 90 days of a VP or CPO transition as a diagnostic process, not an execution sprint. The single most common failure in senior product leadership transitions is acting before understanding — changing structures, replacing…

When not to use

  • 100-day plan for impressing your new boss
  • Execution sprint
  • Acting before understanding
