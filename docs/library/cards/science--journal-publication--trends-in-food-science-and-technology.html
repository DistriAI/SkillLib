trends-in-food-science-and-technology

separate

Use when targeting Trends in Food Science & Technology or deciding whether a food-science review manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's review-only fit, the critical-synthesis and comprehensiveness bar, structure expectations, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit check for a review or trend article in food science
  • re-framing primary work as a critical synthesis
  • choosing between a review venue and a primary-research food journal
  • assessing comprehensiveness and critical-synthesis bar
  • applying desk-reject heuristics
  • critical reviews across food chemistry, processing, engineering, microbiology, nutrition, safety, packaging, and sustainability
  • trend and forward-looking articles on emerging technologies, ingredients, or paradigms
  • methodological or conceptual syntheses reconciling conflicting findings

When not to use

  • primary-research manuscripts where experimental results are the contribution
  • how-to manuals without critical evaluation
  • replacing the journal's current author guidelines
