soil-biology-and-biochemistry

separate

Use when targeting Soil Biology & Biochemistry or deciding whether a soil-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the soil-process-mechanism bar, evidence and data-reporting expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a soil-process or soil-microbial paper
  • re-framing microbial-community or soil-carbon dataset around process mechanism
  • choosing between SBB, agriculture-ecosystems-and-environment, and broader biogeochemistry venue
  • SBB desk-reject heuristics and credible alternative route
  • soil organic matter formation, stabilization, and decomposition
  • soil microbial ecology and function
  • nutrient cycling and transformations
  • extracellular enzymes, microbial physiology, and carbon/nutrient-use efficiency

When not to use

  • soil survey
  • descriptive community census with no process link
  • management trial whose contribution is an agronomic outcome rather than a soil mechanism
  • agroecosystem environmental outcomes at field-landscape scale
  • crop yield/agronomy and resource-use efficiency
