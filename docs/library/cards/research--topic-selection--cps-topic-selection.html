cps-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a research question fits Comparative Political Studies (CPS) and how to frame its comparative contribution. Tests comparative leverage and travel; it does not design the study or write the paper.

When to use

  • Choosing a question or deciding if CPS is the right venue
  • Evaluating drafts that read as single-country cases with no comparative leverage
  • Addressing feedback asking why a topic is comparative or fits CPS
  • Deciding whether a single case can carry a portable theoretical claim
  • Testing comparative leverage and travel
  • Framing comparative contribution

When not to use

  • Designing the study
  • Writing the paper
  • General US political-science flagship pitches
  • IR and IR-adjacent comparative work
  • Broad UK generalist work
  • Pure-IR or pure-formal-theory questions that never compare political systems
