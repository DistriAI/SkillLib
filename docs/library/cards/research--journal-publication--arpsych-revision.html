arpsych-revision

separate

Use when responding to the Annual Review of Psychology (ARPsych) Editor's or Committee's peer-review letter on a delivered review, or to copyediting/proof queries. Handles the post-delivery revision loop; it does not run the pre-delivery preflight (arpsych-submission) or re-audit balance from scratch (arpsych-comprehensiveness-and-balance).

When to use

  • Responding to ARPsych Editor or Committee peer-review letters
  • Answering copyediting or proof queries
  • Addressing coverage gaps, balance problems, length over-run, or accuracy issues
  • Turning comments into a tracked, point-by-point revision
  • Recalibrating confidence language for replication issues
  • Verifying house-style edits and figure re-renders

When not to use

  • Running the pre-delivery preflight (arpsych-submission)
  • Re-auditing balance from scratch (arpsych-comprehensiveness-and-balance)
