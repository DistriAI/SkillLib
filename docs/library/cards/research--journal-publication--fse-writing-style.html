fse-writing-style

separate

Use when revising an ESEC/FSE paper for a practitioner-grounded software-engineering contribution on the first page, research-question contracts, a threats-to-validity section that argues rather than recites, evidence proportional to the claim, double-anonymous wording, and disciplined use of the ACM-template page budget.

When to use

  • revising an ESEC/FSE paper
  • stating a software-engineering contribution on the first page
  • defining research-question contracts
  • writing a threats-to-validity section that argues rather than recites
  • ensuring evidence is proportional to the claim
  • maintaining double-anonymous wording
  • managing the ACM-template page budget

When not to use

  • writing systems demos
  • writing ML results with an SE title
