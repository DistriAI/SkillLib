aeja-theory-model

separate

Use when an American Economic Journal: Applied Economics (AEJ: Applied) manuscript needs a model to interpret, discipline, or structure its empirical estimates — not to lead the paper. Calibrates how much theory belongs in an empirical-first journal and where it goes; it does not design the identification (aeja-identification) or build a standalone structural estimation.

When to use

  • Interpret empirical estimates
  • Discipline empirical estimates
  • Structure empirical estimates
  • Name the mechanism
  • Map reduced-form coefficient to structural parameter
  • Deliver welfare or counterfactual number
  • Discipline heterogeneity or sign predictions
  • Right-size theory for AEJ: Applied

When not to use

  • Design the identification
  • Build a standalone structural estimation
  • Lead the paper with a full structural model
