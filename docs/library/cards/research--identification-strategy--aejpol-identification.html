aejpol-identification

separate

Use when the credibility of the causal evaluation of a policy is the bottleneck for an AEJ: Economic Policy manuscript — DID/event study, IV, RDD/bunching, or RCT of a program. Stress-tests the quasi-experimental policy-evaluation design to the AEJ: Policy bar before exhibits are finalized; it does not build the welfare mapping or write exhibits.

When to use

  • DID/event study
  • IV
  • RDD/bunching
  • RCT of a program
  • Staggered policy adoption
  • Eligibility thresholds
  • Tax/benefit schedules
  • Field experiment of a program

When not to use

  • Build the welfare mapping
  • Write exhibits
