ijoc-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which ijoc-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing a manuscript from idea through rebuttal for an INFORMS Journal on Computing (IJOC) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which ijoc-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing a manuscript from idea through rebuttal
  • naming a draft's binding constraint
  • switching into revision mode after a decision letter
  • routing by paper archetype

When not to use

  • replacing specialized ijoc-* skills
  • treating IJOC like Operations Research or Management Science
  • confusing IJOC with Mathematical Programming Computation or INFORMS Journal on Optimization
  • polishing exhibits or prose while algorithm or experiments are moving
  • deferring GitHub code/data deposit to after acceptance
