jae-theory-development

separate

Use when building the economic argument and predictions for a Journal of Accounting and Economics (JAE) manuscript — grounding hypotheses in agency, information-economics, contracting, or disclosure theory so the paper applies economic theory to explain accounting phenomena rather than reporting a bare correlation.

When to use

  • building economic arguments for JAE manuscripts
  • grounding hypotheses in agency theory
  • applying information economics to accounting
  • deriving directional and falsifiable predictions
  • specifying cross-sectional variation for mechanism tests
  • distinguishing opportunism from efficient contracting
  • developing analytical or archival theory

When not to use

  • reporting bare correlations without economic logic
  • behavioral hand-waving without economic incentives
  • normative claims about standards
  • reverse-engineering predictions from data
