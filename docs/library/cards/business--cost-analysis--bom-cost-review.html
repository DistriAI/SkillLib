bom-cost-review

authentic

Review a bill of materials for cost, risk, and supply exposure — cost rollup, top-10 cost drivers, single-source and EOL risk, MOQ vs forecast mismatch, cost-down candidates, and tariff/logistics sensitivity. Use when asked to review a BOM, find cost-down opportunities, check component sourcing risk, or sanity-check BOM cost against target. Produces a structured BOM review with a cost driver Pareto, risk flags per line, and a prioritised cost-down list.

When to use

  • Review a BOM
  • Find cost-down opportunities
  • Check component sourcing risk
  • Sanity-check BOM cost against target
  • Cost rollup by commodity class
  • Identify top-10 cost drivers
  • Flag single-source and EOL risk
  • Assess tariff and logistics sensitivity

When not to use

  • Chasing pennies on passives while top-10 lines are single-sourced and EOL
  • Quoting unit prices without volume
  • Claiming cost-down savings without naming the lever and owner
  • Treating distributor stock as supply security for single-source parts
  • Ignoring custom/tooled parts in risk review
  • Fabricating costs for missing lines
