soctheory-contribution-framing

separate

Use when articulating the theoretical contribution of a Sociological Theory (ST) manuscript — naming the new way of seeing it provides and differentiating it from prior theory. Frames the contribution; it does NOT build the theory (soctheory-theory-construction) or audit its reasoning (soctheory-argument-development).

When to use

  • articulating theoretical contribution
  • naming new way of seeing
  • differentiating from prior theory
  • framing contribution as conceptual advance
  • sizing contribution strength
  • stating before and after conceptual change

When not to use

  • building the theory
  • auditing reasoning
  • testing propositions empirically
  • surveying or organizing literature
  • applying existing theory to new case without changing it
