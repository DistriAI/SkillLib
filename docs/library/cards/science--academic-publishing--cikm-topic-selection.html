cikm-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a project fits CIKM, the tri-community ACM venue spanning information retrieval, data mining, and knowledge management/databases, when weighing CIKM against SIGIR, KDD, WSDM, TheWebConf, SIGMOD/VLDB, or ISWC, and when choosing among CIKM's five tracks before writing begins.

When to use

  • deciding whether a project fits CIKM
  • weighing CIKM against SIGIR, KDD, WSDM, TheWebConf, SIGMOD/VLDB, or ISWC
  • choosing among CIKM's five tracks
  • assessing tri-community fit for information retrieval, data mining, and knowledge management
  • positioning work relative to CIKM's May deadline and November meeting
  • reframing papers to align with CIKM scope vocabulary
  • evaluating boundary papers, resource papers, and applied systems for CIKM

When not to use

  • contributions about model architecture, vision, or generic NLP with an incidental retrieval experiment
  • pure ranking-model work where SIGIR offers sharper reviewers
  • pure pattern-mining theory where KDD offers sharper reviewers
  • pure query optimization where SIGMOD/VLDB offers sharper reviewers
  • web standards, platforms, or web-scale measurement leading to TheWebConf
  • ontology languages or logic leading to ISWC/KR
  • resubmissions with untouched weaknesses from prior reviews
