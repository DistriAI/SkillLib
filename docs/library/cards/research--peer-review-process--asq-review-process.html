asq-review-process

separate

Use when understanding and navigating Administrative Science Quarterly's (ASQ) developmental, multi-round review process — decision types, reviewer/editor roles, and what each stage expects. Explains the process; it does not draft the response letter (see asq-rebuttal).

When to use

  • Setting expectations before submission
  • Interpreting a decision letter
  • Understanding ASQ's developmental review differences
  • Deciding whether an invitation to revise is worth pursuing
  • Identifying decision types and risk levels
  • Assessing feasibility of a credible revision

When not to use

  • Drafting the response letter
  • Resubmitting a previously rejected paper
