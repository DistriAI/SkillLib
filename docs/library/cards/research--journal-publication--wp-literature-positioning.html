wp-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a World Politics manuscript against the comparative-politics and international-relations literatures so it reads as a contribution that travels across cases. World Politics expects engagement with both theoretical and substantive literatures; the move is to locate the paper in a live cross-case debate, not a single-country conversation. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction and contribution paragraph
  • Addressing reviewer feedback about missing obvious work or not engaging the debate
  • Connecting single-case literature to a general cross-case debate
  • Distinguishing the contribution from the closest prior comparative or IR work
  • Building a review article that synthesizes thematically related books
  • Positioning a paper at the intersection of comparative politics and international relations

When not to use

  • Writing the full literature review
  • Designing the research methodology (hand off to wp-research-design)
  • Building the theoretical framework (hand off to wp-theory-building)
  • Handling submission anonymity issues (hand off to wp-submission)
