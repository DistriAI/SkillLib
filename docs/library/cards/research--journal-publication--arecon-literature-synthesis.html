arecon-literature-synthesis

separate

Use when systematically gathering, reading, and synthesizing a body of economic research for an Annual Review of Economics (ARE) review — coverage discipline and avoiding citation gaps. Builds the evidence corpus and synthesis notes; it does not impose the analytical spine (arecon-organizing-framework) or appraise study credibility (arecon-evidence-standards).

When to use

  • systematically gathering economic research
  • reading the field thoroughly
  • synthesizing a body of research
  • building the evidence corpus
  • creating synthesis notes
  • avoiding citation gaps
  • coverage discipline

When not to use

  • imposing the analytical spine
  • appraising study credibility
  • re-estimating primary studies
  • running new estimates
