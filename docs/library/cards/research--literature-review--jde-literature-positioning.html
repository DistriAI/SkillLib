jde-literature-positioning

separate

Use when staking a manuscript's contribution against the development-economics literature for the Journal of Development Economics (JDE). Positions the paper at the frontier; it does not write a standalone survey.

When to use

  • staking a manuscript's contribution against the development-economics literature
  • positioning the paper at the frontier
  • locating the specific development literature
  • staking the gap precisely
  • claiming the increment
  • building a three-column map of incumbent conversation, unresolved tension, and delta
  • returning a ledger of claim, evidence, risk, and manuscript location
  • comparing against sibling venues

When not to use

  • writing a standalone survey
  • surveying the field but never staking a claim
  • positioning against general economics
  • claiming novelty of setting alone as a contribution
