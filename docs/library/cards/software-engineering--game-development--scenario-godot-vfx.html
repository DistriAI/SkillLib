scenario-godot-vfx

separate

Use when making or fixing particle effects and game feel in Godot 4.7: GPUParticles3D or GPUParticles2D explosion, fireball projectile with trail, impact, sparks, smoke, shockwave, ribbon trails, sub-emitters, particle collision, color curve does nothing, particles pop out or vanish, effect too heavy on mobile, CPUParticles fallback for Compatibility or web, overdraw, hit-stop, freeze frame, screen shake, juice.

When to use

  • making particle effects
  • fixing particle effects
  • game feel implementation
  • GPUParticles3D or GPUParticles2D
  • explosion and fireball projectiles
  • impact, sparks, smoke, shockwave
  • ribbon trails and sub-emitters
  • particle collision and overdraw optimization

When not to use

  • non-Godot engines
  • Godot versions other than 4.7
