the-english-historical-review

separate

Use when targeting The English Historical Review (EHR) or deciding whether a British, European, or world history manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, its archival and empirical-rigor bar, primary-source and historiographical expectations, Chicago house style and double-blind norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • British, European, or world history manuscript fit check
  • Sharpening precise contribution and historiographical location
  • Choosing between EHR and generalist, period-thematic, or area journals
  • Assessing archival and empirical-rigor bar
  • Applying desk-reject heuristics
  • Medieval and early-modern scholarship with manuscript or charter sources
  • Religious, legal, and administrative history with documentary control
  • Studies establishing, correcting, or re-dating facts through source work

When not to use

  • Replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
  • Sweeping interpretive claims that outrun the evidence
  • Essays light on primary research
