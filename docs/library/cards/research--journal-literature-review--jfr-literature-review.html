jfr-literature-review

separate

Use when the literature review for a 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) manuscript is a citation pile rather than a dialogue — to enter the finance lineage (international + Chinese), position the contribution against canonical channel papers, and avoid missing the field's must-cite works.

When to use

  • Structuring literature reviews by source lineage
  • Positioning contributions against canonical channel papers
  • Balancing international mechanism and Chinese institutional literature
  • Distinguishing verification from contribution
  • Addressing reviewer concerns about missing foundational citations
  • Identifying gaps in mechanism, boundary, or subject

When not to use

  • Listing citations by year without lineage
  • Citing only Chinese empirical studies without international mechanism literature
  • Citing only international literature without Chinese institutional context
  • Using vague positioning statements like 'enriches related research'
  • Citing literature unrelated to the paper's identification strategy or channel
