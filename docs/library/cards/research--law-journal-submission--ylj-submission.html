ylj-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight for The Yale Law Journal (YLJ) via its own online author portal — track selection, anonymization, word/length caps, Bluebook readiness, and required materials. Final checks; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • final pre-submission preflight
  • track selection verification
  • anonymization checks
  • word and length cap verification
  • Bluebook readiness check
  • required materials verification
  • YLJ portal submission path confirmation

When not to use

  • drafting content
  • Scholastica or ExpressO submission
  • peer-review processes
