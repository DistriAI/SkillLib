pods-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing an accepted ACM PODS paper for its PACMMOD-track camera-ready, covering de-anonymization, the final acmsmall format and PACMMOD journal metadata (DOI, ORCID, CCS concepts), integrating shepherd-required revision items without overclaiming, posting and DOI-linking the arXiv full version, and confirming registration and presentation.

When to use

  • preparing accepted ACM PODS paper for camera-ready
  • de-anonymization of author block and system names
  • applying final acmsmall format
  • completing PACMMOD journal metadata
  • integrating shepherd-required revision items
  • posting and DOI-linking arXiv full version
  • confirming registration and presentation

When not to use

  • formatting pass only
  • strengthening a theorem beyond what the proofs establish
