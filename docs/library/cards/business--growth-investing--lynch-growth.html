lynch-growth

separate

Use when evaluating Peter Lynch-style growth stocks: invest in what you can understand, classify the company type, check growth runway, PEG, balance sheet, and whether the story is still improving.

When to use

  • Consumer/product-led stock discovery
  • Growth stock classification
  • PEG and growth runway analysis
  • Small/mid-cap compounder review
  • Whether a familiar product can become an investable stock
