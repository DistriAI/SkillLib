jacs-referee-strategy

separate

Use when suggesting/excluding reviewers and pre-empting likely referee objections for a Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS) manuscript. Plans the review-facing strategy — it does not run experiments or write the paper.

When to use

  • Choosing suggested and excluded reviewers
  • Anticipating and disarming objections before submission
  • Stress-testing the manuscript as a skeptical referee
  • Predicting reviewer concerns for revisions
  • Pre-empting standard JACS objections
  • Reviewer-simulation pass
  • Generating a claim-evidence-risk ledger

When not to use

  • Running experiments
  • Writing the paper
