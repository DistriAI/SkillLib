popdevr-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Population and Development Review (PDR, Wiley / Population Council) manuscript against the population-and-development literature so it reads as a broad-interest contribution. PDR readers span demography, economics, environment, and policy, so the paper must engage both the population-science conversation and the development debate it touches. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction and contribution paragraph
  • Addressing reviewer feedback about missing development literature
  • Connecting population analysis to development or policy debates
  • Distinguishing contribution from closest prior population-and-development work
  • Building a three-column map of incumbent conversation, tension, and delta
  • Comparing against sibling venues like Demography and Population Studies

When not to use

  • Writing the full literature review
  • Engaging only formal demography
  • Engaging only development economics
  • Strawmanning prior estimates or frameworks
  • Claiming 'first to study' for incremental contributions
