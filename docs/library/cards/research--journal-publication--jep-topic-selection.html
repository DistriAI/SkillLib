jep-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a topic is right for the Journal of Economic Perspectives (JEP) — broadly interesting, timely, and synthesizable for non-specialists — rather than a narrow research contribution. Judges venue fit; it does not draft the article or the proposal.

When to use

  • deciding if a topic belongs in JEP
  • judging venue fit for JEP
  • gauging if a topic cluster could anchor a symposium
  • evaluating breadth of interest for non-specialists
  • assessing timeliness and why now
  • checking synthesizability of a body of work
  • verifying accessibility ceiling for plain language

When not to use

  • drafting the article
  • drafting the proposal
  • primary research contribution
  • technical survey of record
  • op-ed or popular writing
