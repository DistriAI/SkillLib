joe-identification-strategy

separate

Use when the assumptions, regularity conditions, identification result, and asymptotic theory of a Journal of Econometrics (JoE) methodological paper are the bottleneck. Stress-tests the formal core — what is assumed, what is proved, and how general it is — before tables are drafted.

When to use

  • formal identification of estimands
  • auditing regularity conditions
  • deriving asymptotic theory
  • checking generality of results
  • proof exposition mapping
  • assumption audit tables
  • Monte Carlo confirmation of asymptotic variance

When not to use

  • applied causal design
  • full size/power design
  • drafting tables
