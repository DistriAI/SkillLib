amann-literature-synthesis

separate

Use when systematically gathering, coding, and synthesizing a management/organization literature for an Academy of Management Annals (Annals) review — the search-and-coverage methodology and the choice of narrative vs. systematic vs. bibliometric integration. Builds the corpus and synthesis notes; it does not impose the organizing spine (amann-organizing-framework) or write prose.

When to use

  • systematically gathering literature
  • coding literature
  • synthesizing management/organization literature
  • search-and-coverage methodology
  • choosing integration method
  • building coverage to saturation
  • creating coding matrix

When not to use

  • imposing the organizing spine
  • writing prose
  • running primary research
  • re-estimating or fixing primary studies
