facct-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what belongs in an ACM FAccT paper body versus its endmatter statements, appendices, and supplementary material — the 14-page acmart budget, the extra endmatter page for ethics/adverse-impacts statements, the rule that decision-critical evidence stays in the reviewed pages, mutually-anonymous supplementary material, and how to split an interdisciplinary paper between body, endmatter, and artifact.

When to use

  • FAccT paper body vs endmatter split
  • 14-page acmart budget management
  • Endmatter statements for ethics and adverse impacts
  • Mutually-anonymous supplementary material
  • Splitting interdisciplinary papers between body and artifact
  • FAccT supplementary material assembly

When not to use

  • General academic writing advice
  • Non-FAccT conference formatting
  • Code implementation details
  • Data collection methodology
