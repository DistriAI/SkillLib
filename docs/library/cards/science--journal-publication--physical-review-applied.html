physical-review-applied

separate

Use when targeting Physical Review Applied (PRApplied) or deciding whether an applied-physics manuscript bridging fundamental physics and devices, quantum technology, photonics, energy, or sensing fits this APS venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Physical Review Applied
  • deciding venue fit for applied-physics manuscripts
  • manuscripts bridging fundamental physics and devices
  • quantum technology, photonics, energy, or sensing applications
  • choosing between PRApplied, Physical Review X, and specialist venues
  • checking PRApplied application-relevance bar and evidence expectations
  • reviewing PRApplied house style and structure
  • pre-submission self-check for PRApplied

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • pure engineering venues without physics-grade rigor
  • discovery-oriented Physical Review family papers without application link
  • empirical engineering optimization without rigorous physics
