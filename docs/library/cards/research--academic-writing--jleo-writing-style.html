jleo-writing-style

separate

Use when the prose, introduction, or abstract of a Journal of Law, Economics, and Organization (JLEO) manuscript needs to land its institutional/organizational contribution for an OUP economics-of-institutions audience. Sharpens framing and exposition; it does not change the analysis or the model.

When to use

  • Sharpening framing and exposition
  • Landing institutional/organizational contribution
  • Writing for OUP economics-of-institutions audience
  • Fixing introductions that explain what was done before the institutional question
  • Fixing abstracts that read like methods summaries
  • Ensuring TCE/PPE community readers understand the conversation
  • Unburying mechanisms under notation or data description
  • Adjusting register from JLE price-theory or Org-Science behavioral voice

When not to use

  • Changing the analysis
  • Changing the model
