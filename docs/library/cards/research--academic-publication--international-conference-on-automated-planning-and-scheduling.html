international-conference-on-automated-planning-and-scheduling

separate

Use when targeting International Conference on Automated Planning and Scheduling (ICAPS) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for planning and scheduling.

When to use

  • targeting ICAPS
  • deciding if a manuscript fits ICAPS
  • conference-fit read for automated planning
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style to conference narrative
  • diagnosing evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, or rebuttal issues
  • venue-selection strategy for planning and scheduling
  • checking submission-cycle requirements
  • assessing desk-reject risks

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • generic AI venue for any algorithmic paper
  • primary data-mining or discovery contributions (use KDD)
  • hybrid AI/OR optimization (use CPAIOR)
  • multi-agent planning (use AAMAS)
