plg-sales-integration

separate

Help users transition from a pure self-serve model to a hybrid motion that uses human sales to capture large enterprise deals while maintaining a frictionless product entry point.

When to use

  • Assessing readiness to add a human sales layer
  • Defining Product Qualified Lead criteria
  • Designing a sales assist motion
  • Operationalizing data pipelines for sales visibility
  • Monitoring for enterprise triggers
  • Automating lead prioritization
  • Identifying high-intent product signals

When not to use

  • Replacing the self-serve experience with sales
  • Treating PLG as the full business model
  • Relying on traditional marketing leads instead of product engagement data
