nbr-theory-gap

separate

Use to articulate the theoretical gap and contribution for 《南开管理评论》 (Nankai Business Review) — turning a set of construct relationships into a statement of what existing management theory cannot explain and what this paper adds (concept / proposition / framework / boundary condition). Use when the contribution still reads as "we test whether X affects Y".

When to use

  • articulating theoretical gaps
  • defining theoretical contributions
  • identifying explanation gaps
  • identifying mechanism gaps
  • identifying integration or boundary gaps
  • upgrading gap strength from G0/G1 to G2/G3
  • formulating new concepts, propositions, frameworks, or boundary conditions

When not to use

  • stating that research on X and Y is insufficient
  • writing contributions as testing whether X affects Y
  • claiming contributions as enriching research or providing new perspectives
  • treating changing subjects or industries as a contribution
  • repeating validation of existing theory without theoretical dialogue
