ieeesp-related-work

separate

Use when positioning an IEEE S&P (Oakland) paper against prior literature, including coverage across the four security flagships, concurrent-work and arXiv norms, the 40% resubmission-overlap disclosure, verifying venue attribution via dblp and IEEE Xplore, and anonymity-safe self-citation.

When to use

  • positioning an IEEE S&P paper against prior literature
  • coverage across the four security flagships
  • concurrent-work and arXiv norms
  • 40% resubmission-overlap disclosure
  • verifying venue attribution via dblp and IEEE Xplore
  • anonymity-safe self-citation
  • building the positioning sections
  • auditing lane coverage and delta statements

When not to use

  • writing the main body of the paper
  • conducting the security experiment
  • formatting the paper for submission
  • managing the peer review process
