co-accessibility

separate

Infer cis-regulatory connections (peak-to-peak co-accessibility) from scATAC-seq using Cicero, ArchR getCoAccessibility, or SCENIC+. Use when linking enhancer accessibility to promoter accessibility, identifying enhancer-gene pairs from chromatin alone (without paired RNA), running gene-regulatory inference combining ATAC + RNA, or comparing predicted regulatory contacts against Hi-C/Micro-C ground truth.

When to use

  • linking enhancer accessibility to promoter accessibility
  • identifying enhancer-gene pairs from chromatin alone
  • running gene-regulatory inference combining ATAC + RNA
  • comparing predicted regulatory contacts against Hi-C/Micro-C ground truth

When not to use

  • 3D physical contacts that don't vary in accessibility
  • trans-chromosomal interactions
  • constitutive structural contacts
  • developmental contacts that opened before scATAC sample
  • hub enhancers that contact many distal targets
