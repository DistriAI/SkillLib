journal-of-clinical-investigation

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Clinical Investigation (JCI) or deciding whether a translational medicine manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Clinical Investigation (JCI)
  • deciding whether a translational medicine manuscript fits this venue
  • venue selection between JCI, Science Translational Medicine, and Nature Medicine
  • framing guidance for papers with strong human-data primary evidence
  • identifying JCI desk-reject risks
  • generating a ranked alternative-venue list
  • disease mechanism studies across all organ systems
  • translational immunology, inflammation, and autoimmunity

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • methods development per se
  • descriptive multi-omics without mechanistic follow-through
  • cataloguing associations or correlations without mechanistic insight
