jakobs-law

separate

Apply Jakob's Law — users expect your product to work like the others they already use. Use when deciding whether to innovate on a familiar pattern. For OS-mandated conventions specifically, use `platform-conventions` (ui-design).

When to use

  • deciding whether to innovate on a familiar pattern
  • identifying design conventions with strong user expectations
  • evaluating the cost of departing from conventions
  • making deliberate decisions about following or innovating on patterns
  • auditing competitors for interaction handling
  • weighting conventions by task frequency
  • flagging departures from convention in design reviews

When not to use

  • OS-mandated conventions
  • platform-specific conventions
