hri-reproducibility

separate

Use when building the openness and replicability story for an ACM/IEEE HRI full paper — sharing study materials, robot-behavior specifications, de-identified data, and analysis code; reporting Wizard-of-Oz and pre-registration; and stating honestly what can and cannot be reproduced in an embodied human-subjects study.

When to use

  • building openness and replicability story
  • sharing study materials
  • sharing robot-behavior specifications
  • sharing de-identified data
  • sharing analysis code
  • reporting Wizard-of-Oz
  • reporting pre-registration
  • stating reproducibility limits

When not to use

  • re-running embodied robot studies with real people
  • exact reproduction of human responses
