molcell-fit

separate

Use when triaging a study before any writing — stress-tests whether it clears Molecular Cell's bar (a deep molecular mechanism proven by orthogonal approaches with physiological relevance) or belongs at a sibling. Use this first to decide Molecular Cell vs Cell vs Cell Reports vs NSMB/EMBO J before investing in framing or figures.

When to use

  • Triage study before writing
  • Decide Molecular Cell vs Cell vs Cell Reports
  • Check if work clears Molecular Cell bar
  • Evaluate deep molecular mechanism
  • Assess physiological relevance
  • Choose venue among Cell Press and competitors

When not to use

  • Writing framing or figures
  • Evaluating correctness or interest alone
  • Desk-rejection decisions by editors
