second-brain-write

separate

Draft from the vault: outline from concept pages, keep citations to the user's own sources, and surface where their material disagrees. Use this skill when the user wants to write an article, post, essay or newsletter based on what they have collected, or asks what they could write about a topic. Do NOT use for research reports, for writing with no grounding in the vault, or for editing text the user wrote themselves unless asked.

When to use

  • writing an article
  • writing a post
  • writing an essay
  • writing a newsletter
  • asking what to write about a topic
  • drafting from the vault

When not to use

  • research reports
  • writing with no grounding in the vault
  • editing text the user wrote themselves unless asked
