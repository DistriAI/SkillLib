pyzhihu-cli

separate

知乎 CLI (pyzhihu-cli)：搜索、热榜、问题/回答/评论、推荐 Feed、用户资料、发想法/提问/文章、删自己的内容、点赞关注、收藏与通知。Agent 代执行 zhihu 命令，Cookie 仅存本地。

When to use

  • Zhihu search
  • Hot list retrieval
  • Question and answer management
  • User profile viewing
  • Posting ideas, questions, and articles
  • Deleting own content
  • Voting and following
  • Collections and notifications

When not to use

  • Uploading cookies to third parties
  • Accessing non-Zhihu domains
  • Operations outside the defined zhihu command scope
