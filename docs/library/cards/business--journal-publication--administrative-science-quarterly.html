administrative-science-quarterly

separate

Use when targeting Administrative Science Quarterly (ASQ) or deciding whether an organization theory / sociology-of-organizations manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Administrative Science Quarterly (ASQ)
  • deciding if organization theory manuscript fits ASQ
  • deciding if sociology-of-organizations manuscript fits ASQ
  • re-framing strong empirical org study for theoretical contribution
  • identifying ASQ desk-reject risks
  • generating alternative journal list for ASQ rejections

When not to use

  • incremental hypothesis-tweaking
  • applied results
  • purely applied or managerial-prescription paper
  • method-led work with no advance in organization theory
  • replacing current official submission guidelines
