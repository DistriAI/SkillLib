restud-identification

separate

Use when selecting, implementing, or stress-testing the causal identification strategy for an empirical The Review of Economic Studies (REStud) manuscript — DID (incl. staggered), IV (incl. weak-IV-robust inference), RDD, synthetic control, shift-share, or RCT. Apply before writing results; for theory papers route to restud-theory-model.

When to use

  • selecting causal identification strategy
  • implementing DID estimators
  • stress-testing IV inference
  • auditing RDD diagnostics
  • evaluating synthetic control designs
  • assessing shift-share identification
  • analyzing RCTs

When not to use

  • theory papers
  • restud-theory-model routing
