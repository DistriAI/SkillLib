neurips-review-process

separate

Use when explaining or diagnosing the NeurIPS main-track review process, including OpenReview, reviewer and AC roles, contribution-type review, ethics flags, reciprocal reviewing, discussion, and LLM-review policy.

When to use

  • explaining the NeurIPS main-track review process
  • diagnosing the NeurIPS main-track review process
  • identifying decision-relevant weaknesses
  • reasoning about reviewer and AC actions
  • mapping review concerns to quality, clarity, significance, ethics, reproducibility, or fit
  • predicting AC meta-review issues
  • deciding if problems are fixable by clarification, analysis, framing, or re-routing
  • prioritizing author response issues

When not to use

  • inferring acceptance odds from folklore
