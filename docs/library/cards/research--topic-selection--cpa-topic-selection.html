cpa-topic-selection

separate

Use when a 《中国行政管理》 manuscript is at the topic-selection stage and the public-administration theory frame, the research question, or the venue match are unsettled. Produces the theory-frame checklist and the research-question / contribution template; does not design the empirics.

When to use

  • topic-selection stage for 《中国行政管理》
  • settling public-administration theory frame
  • defining research question
  • venue match assessment
  • converting broad topics to researchable questions
  • theory-frame self-check
  • drafting contribution statements

When not to use

  • designing the empirics
  • manuscripts that are essentially economics
