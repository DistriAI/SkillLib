trend-discovery

combined

Use when the user wants to discover trending social topics, hashtags, sounds, posts, reels, shorts, creators, or formats in a niche. Searches public trend and discovery endpoints, ranks evidence, and turns trends into practical content angles. Discover rising category conversations, formats, sounds, questions, and creator patterns across social platforms, then separate durable demand signals from short-lived noise.

When to use

  • find trends in a niche or category
  • discover trending TikTok sounds, hashtags, creators, or videos
  • find trending Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts ideas
  • build a weekly trend brief
  • decide what content formats or angles to jump on

When not to use

  • calling something a trend from one post unless the user only asked for examples
  • ignoring region
  • recommending jumping on trends that do not fit the user's brand or audience

Skill page

Conflict

Both copies describe the same task—discovering social trends and turning them into content actions—but they are not interchangeable. The gooseworks copy is a higher-level procedure that explicitly requires a baseline comparison, classifies trends into lifecycle stages, scores brand/audience/production/risk fit, and mandates a watchlist and false-positive section. The ScrapeCreators copy is more operational: it lists concrete API endpoints, requires normalizing evidence into a structured record, clusters by topic/format/hook/sound/meme, and includes region-specific pitfalls and a specific brief template. Neither copy contains the other's full set of steps, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
