aaag-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a topic fits the Annals of the American Association of Geographers and which of its four areas to target. Tests geographic significance and area fit; it does not design the study or write the paper.

When to use

  • Choosing a project
  • Deciding if a draft fits the Annals vs. a specialty venue
  • Addressing feedback that a topic is not really geography or too narrow
  • Declaring Geographic Methods, Human, Nature-Society, Physical, or General Geography
  • Testing geographic-ness and area reach
  • Calibrating ambition between Annals and specialty venues
  • Avoiding mis-pitching to sibling journals

When not to use

  • Designing the study
  • Writing the paper
