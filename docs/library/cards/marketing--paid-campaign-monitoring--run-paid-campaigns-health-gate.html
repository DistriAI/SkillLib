run-paid-campaigns-health-gate

separate

Checks that ads are safe to spend on: conversion tracking recording, landing pages loading on mobile with the right product, price and offer, enough stock behind promoted products, no disapproved ads, and no account or payment stop. Marks each pass, fail or unknown and turns fails into fixes. Use alone when you already have the live or planned ads and their landing pages; otherwise run run-paid-campaigns.

When to use

  • checking conversion tracking recording
  • verifying landing pages load on mobile
  • checking stock behind promoted products
  • checking for disapproved ads
  • checking for account or payment stops
  • marking pass, fail, or unknown status
  • turning fails into fixes

When not to use

  • running when live or planned ads and landing pages are not already available
