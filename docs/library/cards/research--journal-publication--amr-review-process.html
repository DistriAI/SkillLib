amr-review-process

separate

Use when you need to understand the Academy of Management Review (AMR) developmental, multi-round review and interpret a decision letter focused on theoretical novelty and logical soundness. Explains the process and decision types; it does NOT draft the response document (that is amr-rebuttal).

When to use

  • interpret an AMR decision letter
  • understand developmental review
  • decide whether an R&R is worth pursuing
  • read editor's signals
  • triage reviewer comments by AMR weights
  • identify decision type
  • extract editor's stated priorities
  • map comments to specific skills

When not to use

  • draft the response document
  • amr-rebuttal
