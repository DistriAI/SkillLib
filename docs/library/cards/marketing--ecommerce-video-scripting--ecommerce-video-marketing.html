The two copies cover the same skill name but perform different tasks. The Nexscope copy is a high-level strategy skill for planning video marketing across Western platforms (Amazon, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), focusing on funnel stages, UGC, and video SEO. The anbeime copy is a detailed, step-by-step scriptwriting skill for Chinese platforms (Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Taobao), providing a 6-stage workflow, specific narrative structures (pain point -> product -> solution), and camera language instructions. Because one is a strategy planner and the other is a script generator with specific production constraints, crowning one would hide a real alternative use case.
