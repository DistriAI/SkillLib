restud-robustness

separate

Use when the main results of a The Review of Economic Studies (REStud) manuscript exist but referee-anticipating checks — robustness, heterogeneity, mechanism, placebo, alternative specifications — are missing or fragile. Hardens the result against demanding referees; does not redesign identification.

When to use

  • Specification robustness
  • Placebo / falsification tests
  • Inference robustness
  • Mechanism evidence
  • Heterogeneity analysis
  • Selection / attrition / measurement checks
  • Coefficient-stability tables
  • Stress-testing fragile assumptions

When not to use

  • Redesigning identification
  • Robustness theater
  • Data-mining subgroups
