stoc-workflow

separate

Use when planning a STOC (ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing) project calendar — backward planning to the early-November deadline, the STOC/FOCS alternating two-deadline year that theory groups schedule around, full-version and camera-ready milestones, and the June TheoryFest endpoint.

When to use

  • planning a STOC project calendar
  • backward planning to the early-November deadline
  • managing the STOC/FOCS alternating two-deadline year
  • scheduling full-version and camera-ready milestones
  • planning for the June TheoryFest endpoint
  • triaging corrections for camera-ready and full version
  • preparing TheoryFest talks
  • managing multi-author team ownership for STOC submissions

When not to use

  • planning for non-STOC/FOCS theory conferences
  • journal submission planning
  • general academic writing style without STOC context
  • managing publication costs or author fees
