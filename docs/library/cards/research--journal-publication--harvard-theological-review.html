harvard-theological-review

separate

Use when targeting Harvard Theological Review or deciding whether a religious-studies manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, its textual/philological and historical argument bar, primary-source and original-language expectations, house style and review norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Harvard Theological Review
  • deciding whether a religious-studies manuscript fits this venue
  • fit/framing check for biblical, early-Christian, Jewish-studies, theological, or religious-history manuscripts
  • tightening a reading of a religious text into a philologically and historically controlled argument
  • choosing between Harvard Theological Review and a broad religious-studies or comparative-history venue
  • understanding the journal's textual/historical-method bar and desk-reject heuristics

When not to use

  • constructive or devotional writing untethered from textual and historical evidence
  • replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
