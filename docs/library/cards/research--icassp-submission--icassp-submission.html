icassp-submission

separate

Use when auditing an ICASSP submission before the CMS portal deadline — the IEEE 4+1 page format, the single-blind rule that the author list must appear, EDICS subject selection, the Compliance-with-Ethical-Standards statement, PDF eXpress validation, the 9-papers-per-author cap, dual-submission policy, and desk-reject triggers.

When to use

  • ICASSP submission audit
  • 4+1 page format compliance
  • Single-blind author list verification
  • EDICS subject selection
  • PDF eXpress validation
  • 9-papers-per-author cap check
  • Dual-submission policy verification
  • Desk-reject trigger identification

When not to use

  • Non-ICASSP conference submissions
  • Post-deadline repairs
  • Content writing or technical review
