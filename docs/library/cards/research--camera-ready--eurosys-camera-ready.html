eurosys-camera-ready

separate

Use when converting an accepted EuroSys paper into its ACM proceedings version — flipping acmart out of review/anonymous mode, restoring authors and acknowledgments, completing ACM rights and metadata steps, attaching sysartifacts badges, and meeting the single camera-ready date that serves both the spring and fall cohorts.

When to use

  • converting accepted EuroSys paper to ACM proceedings
  • flipping acmart template from review to final mode
  • restoring authors and acknowledgments
  • completing ACM rights and metadata steps
  • attaching sysartifacts badges
  • reconciling reviewed and final versions
  • metadata coherence sweep
  • final-week camera-ready sequence

When not to use

  • initial paper submission
  • writing the paper
  • handling rejection
  • managing pre-acceptance revisions
