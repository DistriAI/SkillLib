Skill page

Conflict

The two copies share the same core structure, data sources, algorithms, and safety rules, but they disagree on critical operational details. The sickn33 copy is a heavily modified, safety-hardened version that explicitly disclaims clinical validation, adds a 'Supporting material availability' section noting missing scripts, and changes Step 6 (Risk Prediction) to require model verification before calculating probabilities. It also changes Step 9 to only save history upon user confirmation. The FreedomIntelligence copy is the 'original' or less cautious version that assumes the scripts exist, calculates 10-year probabilities directly, and automatically updates history. Because the sickn33 copy contains material constraints (missing file checks, model verification) that the other lacks, and they disagree on the execution of risk prediction and history logging, crowning one would hide a real alternative implementation.
