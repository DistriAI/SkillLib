cte-style

separate

Use when polishing the language and rhetoric of a 《财贸经济》 manuscript — eliminating empty-significance phrases, fixing the "加强完善推进" policy formula, replacing method-flexing with identifiable mechanism, and aligning tense / person / hedging with CSSCI house style. 本技能服务于《财贸经济》(Finance & Trade Economics, CTE)。

When to use

  • polishing language and rhetoric
  • eliminating empty-significance phrases
  • fixing policy formula
  • replacing method-flexing with identifiable mechanism
  • aligning tense, person, and hedging
  • reviewing manuscript for CTE submission

When not to use

  • general theoretical contributions for 《经济研究》
  • pure financial mechanisms for 《金融研究》
  • open macro or trade for 《世界经济》
