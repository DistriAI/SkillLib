mgsci-contribution-framing

separate

Use when writing the explicit contribution statement for a Management Science (INFORMS) manuscript — turning an analytical result or empirical finding into a decision-relevant insight that travels across departments, and showing why it belongs in Management Science rather than a sister INFORMS journal. It frames the "so what"; it does not run the analysis (mgsci-data-analysis) or position the literature (mgsci-literature-positioning).

When to use

  • Writing explicit contribution statements
  • Turning results into decision-relevant insights
  • Framing cross-department reach
  • Justifying fit for Management Science
  • Calibrating magnitude honestly
  • Creating decision ledgers
  • Comparing against sister journals

When not to use

  • Running the analysis
  • Positioning the literature
