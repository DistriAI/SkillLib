acmmm-reproducibility

separate

Use when strengthening the reproducibility of an ACM MM (ACM Multimedia) paper or preparing for the ACM MM Reproducibility track and ACM artifact badging — capturing environments, media/data access, seeds, and multimodal pipelines so an independent reviewer can rebuild results and reach Artifacts Evaluated or Results Reproduced badges.

When to use

  • strengthening reproducibility of ACM MM papers
  • preparing for ACM MM Reproducibility track
  • preparing for ACM artifact badging
  • capturing environments and media/data access
  • documenting multimodal pipelines
  • ensuring independent reviewers can rebuild results
  • achieving Artifacts Evaluated or Results Reproduced badges

When not to use

  • tasks not related to ACM MM reproducibility
  • non-multimedia code reproduction without media considerations
