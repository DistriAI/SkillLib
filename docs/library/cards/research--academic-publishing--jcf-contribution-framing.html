jcf-contribution-framing

separate

Use when sharpening the stated contribution of a Journal of Corporate Finance (JCF) manuscript — turning a result into a crisp "what is new and why it matters to corporate finance" claim that survives the editor's desk screen. It frames the pitch (abstract, intro, cover letter); it does not run analysis.

When to use

  • Writing the abstract's contribution sentence
  • Drafting the introduction's contribution arc
  • Drafting the cover-letter pitch
  • Deciding if the contribution clears desk-rejection
  • Grading contribution type for JCF desk screen
  • Fixing referee pushback on framing

When not to use

  • Running analysis
