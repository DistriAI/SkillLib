jmr-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing prose for a Journal of Marketing Research (JMR) manuscript — a third-person 200-word abstract, AMA author-year citations, the exact-statistics number conventions, and a front-loaded research argument that clears JMR's dual bar without drifting into managerial Journal-of-Marketing framing. Late-stage polish only.

When to use

  • Polishing JMR manuscript prose
  • Formatting 200-word third-person abstracts
  • Converting citations to AMA author-year style
  • Applying exact-statistics number conventions
  • Front-loading research arguments
  • Checking AI-content disclosure
  • Running style execution passes

When not to use

  • Polishing prose before contribution is settled
  • Managerial Journal-of-Marketing framing
  • First-person or results-teaser abstracts
  • APA-numeric citations
  • Using asterisks or 'p < .05' in prose
