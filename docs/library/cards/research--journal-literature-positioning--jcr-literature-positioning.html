jcr-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Consumer Research (JCR) manuscript — joining a live consumer-research conversation across disciplines, problematizing rather than gap-spotting, and separating the paper from JCP, JMR, JM, and disciplinary psychology/sociology outlets.

When to use

  • Positioning a JCR manuscript
  • Joining a live consumer-research conversation
  • Problematizing rather than gap-spotting
  • Separating the paper from JCP, JMR, JM, and disciplinary psychology/sociology outlets
  • Engaging base discipline canonical theory
  • Ensuring interdisciplinary legibility
  • Handling self-citation under double-anonymized review

When not to use

  • Gap-spotting introductions
  • Citing within one field only
  • Positioning as a marketing-tactics or modeling paper
  • Self-references that reveal author identity
