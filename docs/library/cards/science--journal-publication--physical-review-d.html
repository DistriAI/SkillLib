physical-review-d

separate

Use when targeting Physical Review D (PRD) or deciding whether a particles, fields, gravitation, or cosmology manuscript fits this APS archival venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Physical Review D for high-energy physics, gravitation, or cosmology manuscripts
  • determining if a result fits PRD's archival track versus PRL's broad-importance bar
  • positioning theoretical particle-physics or GR/cosmology papers within the APS Physical Review family
  • understanding PRD's scope boundaries versus Physical Review C, B, or Nature-family journals
  • checking method and evidence bars for theoretical, phenomenological, or experimental papers
  • reviewing structure and house style for PRD Letters and full Articles
  • performing official-submission re-checks and pre-submission self-checks

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • tasks not related to Physical Review D venue selection or fit
