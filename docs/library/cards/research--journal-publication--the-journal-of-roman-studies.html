the-journal-of-roman-studies

separate

Use when targeting The Journal of Roman Studies (JRS) or deciding whether a Roman history, archaeology, epigraphy, literature, or material-culture manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's Roman-world fit, the primary-evidence argument bar, epigraphic/archaeological and original-language expectations, classics house style and anonymization norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Roman history, archaeology, epigraphy, literature, or material-culture manuscript fit check
  • Re-framing descriptive or local studies into evidence-driven arguments
  • Choosing between JRS and Greek/Latin philology or specialist archaeology/epigraphy venues
  • Checking JRS primary-evidence bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • Roman history across Republic, Empire, and Late Antiquity
  • Epigraphy and documentary evidence arguments
  • Roman archaeology and material culture historical arguments
  • Numismatics and art integrated into historical or cultural arguments

When not to use

  • Descriptive catalogue with no argument
  • Thesis floating above its evidence
  • Replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
