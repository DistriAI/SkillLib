journal-of-quantitative-technological-economics

separate

Use when targeting 《数量经济技术经济研究》(The Journal of Quantitative & Technological Economics, JQTE) — the CASS journal for quantitative economics, econometric methods, technical economics, input-output, macro-econometric forecasting, and technology/innovation evaluation. Apply when the contribution is method-application or measurement (productivity, efficiency, forecasting) rather than a pure causal-policy narrative.

When to use

  • Total factor productivity and efficiency measurement
  • Macro-econometric forecasting and policy simulation
  • Input-output and CGE analysis
  • Technology and innovation evaluation
  • Application of econometric methods to Chinese data
  • Index construction and measurement sensitivity analysis

When not to use

  • Pure causal-policy narratives
  • Clean causal identification with theoretical contributions
  • Industrial policy evaluation with thick robustness
  • Mathematical models or algorithms themselves
  • Industry background or local case studies without methodological contribution
