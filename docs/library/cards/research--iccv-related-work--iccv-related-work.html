iccv-related-work

separate

Use when positioning an ICCV paper against the literature, covering the intervening-cycle problem of citing a CVPR and an ECCV that happened since the last ICCV, concurrent-preprint etiquette around the March deadline, double-blind self-reference and the do-not-cite-your-repo rule, and venue-attribution hygiene across the vision big three.

When to use

  • positioning an ICCV paper against the literature
  • sweeping intervening CVPR and ECCV cycles
  • handling concurrent preprints near the March deadline
  • managing double-blind self-reference and codebase citations
  • correcting venue attribution for vision big three papers
  • organizing related work by research questions and gaps

When not to use

  • writing related work for non-ICCV venues
  • citing fabricated references
  • citing public codebases in double-blind submissions
