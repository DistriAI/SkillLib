novelty-check

combined

Verify research idea novelty against recent literature. Use when user says "查新", "novelty check", "有没有人做过", "check novelty", or wants to verify a research idea is novel before implementing.

When to use

  • verify research idea novelty
  • check if method has been done
  • literature search for novelty
  • assess novelty of proposed method
  • identify closest prior work

When not to use

  • implementing research ideas
  • writing full research papers
  • conducting experiments
