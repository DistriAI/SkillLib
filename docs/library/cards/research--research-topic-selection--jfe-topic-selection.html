jfe-topic-selection

separate

Use when scoping or framing a topic for a Journal of Financial Economics (JFE) manuscript and you need to test fit and contribution before investing in data work. Pressure-tests the question and contribution; it does not write the literature review or design the empirics.

When to use

  • scoping or framing a topic for a JFE manuscript
  • testing fit and contribution before data work
  • pressure-testing the question and contribution
  • choosing between JFE, JF, and RFS as the target outlet
  • framing contribution for corporate finance or asset pricing

When not to use

  • writing the literature review
  • designing the empirics
