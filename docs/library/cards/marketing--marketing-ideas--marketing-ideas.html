Conflict

The copies overlap on the core task of generating marketing ideas for SaaS, but they disagree on the procedure and output. phuryn/pm-skills uses a fixed prompt to generate exactly 5 cost-effective ideas with channel, message, rationale, and cost efficiency. coreyhaines31 and LeoYeAI use a 139-idea library, ask clarifying questions, and suggest 3-5 ideas with a specific output format. sickn33 adds a mandatory Marketing Feasibility Score (MFS) calculation and a 140-idea library, requiring a different output format that includes the score and risk. Crowning one copy would hide the distinct scoring methodology and the different library sizes and output structures.
