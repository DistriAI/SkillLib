vldb-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a project belongs at VLDB and in which PVLDB category, applying the data-management-primitive test, choosing among Regular, EA&B, Scalable Data Science, and Vision papers, and routing against SIGMOD, ICDE, CIDR, EDBT, PODS, KDD, systems venues, and The VLDB Journal.

When to use

  • deciding whether a project belongs at VLDB
  • applying the data-management-primitive test
  • choosing among Regular, EA&B, Scalable Data Science, and Vision papers
  • routing against SIGMOD, ICDE, CIDR, EDBT, PODS, KDD, systems venues, and The VLDB Journal

When not to use

  • writing the paper
  • identifying nearest three prior systems
  • scanning live volume's topics-of-interest list
