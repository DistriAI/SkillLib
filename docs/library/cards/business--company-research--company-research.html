company-research

combined

Company discovery and deep research skill. Researches a company's product and ICP, discovers target companies to sell to using Browserbase Search API, deeply researches each using a Plan→Research→Synthesize pattern, and scores ICP fit — compiled into a scored research report and CSV. Supports depth modes (quick/deep/deeper) for balancing scale vs intelligence. Use when the user wants to: (1) find companies to sell to, (2) research potential customers, (3) discover companies matching an ICP, (4) build a target company list, (5) do market research on prospects. Triggers: "find companies to sell to", "company research", "find prospects", "ICP research", "target companies", "who should we sell to", "market research", "lead research", "prospect list". Create a company research brief with executive quotes, product strategy, and org context. Use when preparing for interviews, competitive analysis, partnerships, or market-entry work.

Skill page

Conflict

The two skills share the name but perform different tasks. deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills is a manual, PM-focused company profile that extracts executive quotes, product strategy, and org context from public sources for interviews, partnerships, or competitive analysis. browserbase/skills is an automated sales-discovery pipeline that uses the Browserbase Search API and a Plan→Research→Synthesize pattern to find target companies, score ICP fit, and emit a report plus CSV. They overlap only in the broad idea of researching companies; each contains material steps and constraints the other lacks, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
