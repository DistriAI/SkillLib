journal-of-consumer-research

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Consumer Research (JCR) or deciding whether a consumer-behavior manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Consumer Research
  • deciding if a consumer-behavior manuscript fits JCR
  • positioning consumer-behavior papers with theoretical advances
  • framing qualitative/CCT or economics/sociology-of-consumption papers for JCR
  • identifying JCR desk-reject risks
  • providing JCP / JMR / JM alternative lists
  • assessing method and evidence bar for JCR
  • checking official submission guidelines for JCR

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • providing submission-ready advice without checking live author instructions
