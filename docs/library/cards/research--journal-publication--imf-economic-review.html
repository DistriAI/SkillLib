imf-economic-review

separate

Use when targeting IMF Economic Review or deciding whether an international macro / finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting IMF Economic Review
  • deciding if international macro / finance manuscript fits
  • choosing among macro-finance venues
  • re-framing domestic macro or finance paper
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • checking method-and-evidence bar
  • reviewing house style
  • official-submission re-check

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • domestic monetary / business-cycle macro
  • trade and real open-economy questions
  • core finance (asset pricing / corporate / banking)
  • development-focused macro-financial questions
  • European policy debate
  • closed-economy or purely domestic paper with no international dimension
  • finance microstructure or corporate-finance paper with no macro / international angle
