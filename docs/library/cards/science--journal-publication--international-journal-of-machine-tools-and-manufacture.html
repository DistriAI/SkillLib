international-journal-of-machine-tools-and-manufacture

separate

Use when targeting the International Journal of Machine Tools and Manufacture (IJMTM) or deciding whether a precision/advanced-manufacturing manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the manufacturing-science bar combining experiment and modeling, the process-mechanics scope, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for IJMTM manuscripts
  • re-framing manufacturing papers into process-mechanics contributions
  • choosing between IJMTM and additive-manufacturing or materials venues
  • assessing IJMTM manufacturing-science rigor bar
  • applying desk-reject heuristics
  • checking machining process mechanics fit
  • checking forming process mechanics fit
  • checking machine-tool dynamics and structures fit

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
  • parameter optimization for one workpiece with no transferable manufacturing insight
  • measurement with no process understanding
