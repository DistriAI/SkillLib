aaag-review-process

separate

Use when you need to understand how the Annals of the American Association of Geographers evaluates a manuscript — double-anonymous review routed through a subject editor by area, 1-3 referees (one being the editor or designate), decision categories, and what desk-rejects a paper. Sets expectations; it does not write the paper or the response letter.

When to use

  • stress-test against likely reviewer and editor objections
  • choosing the area for the right subject editor
  • interpreting a decision letter
  • understanding double-anonymous review model
  • identifying desk-reject triggers
  • pre-empting area-specific objections

When not to use

  • writing the paper
  • writing the response letter
