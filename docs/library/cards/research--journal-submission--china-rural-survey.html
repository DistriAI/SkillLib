china-rural-survey

separate

Use when targeting 《中国农村观察》(China Rural Survey — 中国社会科学院农村发展研究所主办的三农双月刊, CN11-3586/F, 双向匿名、零审稿/版面费, 需中英文摘要) or deciding whether a Chinese 三农 manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, fee-free anonymous-review policy, abstract/keyword/citation house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《中国农村观察》
  • deciding if a Chinese 三农 manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese economics manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit and framing
  • verifying official submission requirements

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • general regional development or industrial policy without core 三农 focus
  • pure economic econometrics better suited for 《中国农村经济》
  • agricultural technical efficiency better suited for 《农业技术经济》
  • comprehensive agricultural policy better suited for 《农业经济问题》
