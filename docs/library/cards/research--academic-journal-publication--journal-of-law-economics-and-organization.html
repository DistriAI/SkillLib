journal-of-law-economics-and-organization

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Law, Economics, and Organization (JLEO) or deciding whether a manuscript at the intersection of law, economics, and organization fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Law, Economics, and Organization
  • deciding manuscript fit for law, economics, and organization
  • analyzing contracts, institutions, or organizational form with economic tools
  • re-framing positive-political-theory or organizational-economics papers
  • identifying JLEO desk-reject risks
  • checking JLEO method and evidence bar
  • reviewing JLEO house style and structure
  • verifying official submission guidelines

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • pure law, pure politics, or generic economics papers without institutions-and-organization contribution
  • empirical effects of law/regulation/antitrust/crime in the Chicago tradition
  • IO-theory of contracts/regulation/industry
  • pure microeconomic theory or mechanism
  • management/organization-theory framing
  • general-interest top-tier submissions
  • broad general field submissions
