huawen-business-writing

authentic

Use when asked to write a business email, notice, proposal or WhatsApp message in Chinese for Singapore or Malaysia, 帮我写一封新加坡的中文商务邮件, 马来西亚华文公司通知, or adapt mainland Chinese business writing for 新马 readers. Produces finished 华文 business writing in the register Singapore and Malaysian Chinese readers expect, with local terms, currency, dates and names handled correctly, and an English or Malay version where the reader needs one.

When to use

  • writing business emails in Chinese for Singapore or Malaysia
  • creating notices or proposals for local readers
  • adapting mainland Chinese business writing for 新马 readers
  • writing WhatsApp or WeChat business messages
  • producing bilingual Chinese-English or Chinese-Malay versions

When not to use

  • writing in Traditional Chinese
  • using mainland Chinese business templates
  • inventing names or titles
  • translating every English term into Chinese
