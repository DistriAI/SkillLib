rt-desk-reject-risk

separate

Use before submitting to score a manuscript against a specific venue's own desk-reject triggers and return a ranked, fixable risk report. Venue-conditional — it reads the target's documented triggers rather than applying generic advice. Run it after rt-submission-readiness clears the mechanical bar and before the paper is uploaded.

When to use

  • Scoring manuscript against venue desk-reject triggers
  • Identifying fatal, likely, and watch risks
  • Generating cost-ranked fix lists
  • Checking scope, framing, and format breaches
  • Auditing policy and evidence baseline compliance
  • Evaluating reframed drafts against new venues
  • Assessing resubmissions after previous desk rejects

When not to use

  • Checking mechanical completeness
  • Predicting editor acceptance probability
  • Providing generic advice without venue documentation
  • Inventing venue-specific triggers
  • Simulating referee reviews
  • Creating replication packages
