the-isme-journal

separate

Use when targeting The ISME Journal (ISME J) or deciding whether a microbial-ecology manuscript fits this flagship community-and-function venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting The ISME Journal
  • deciding if a microbial-ecology manuscript fits
  • choosing between ISME J, Nature Microbiology, Microbiome, and mSystems
  • assessing mechanism-and-evidence bar
  • checking multi-omics expectations
  • evaluating desk-reject criteria
  • re-framing manuscripts for ISME J

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • pure surveys
  • descriptive amplicon snapshots
  • applied or engineered-system studies without ecological insight
