jebo-literature-positioning

separate

Use when staking a Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization (JEBO) manuscript's contribution against the behavioral, experimental, and organizational literatures. Builds the "what we knew / what was missing / what this adds" frame and a replication/extension stance; it does not invent citations.

When to use

  • staking a JEBO manuscript's contribution
  • building the 'what we knew / what was missing / what this adds' frame
  • defining a replication/extension stance
  • positioning papers that replicate or extend known results
  • bridging two literatures with an explicit mechanism-level link
  • identifying specific behavioral gaps

When not to use

  • inventing citations
  • summarizing papers serially instead of building toward the gap
  • inflating novelty by ignoring the closest prior experiment
  • presenting a disguised replication as wholly new
  • positioning only within one tradition when the result must satisfy a neighboring community
