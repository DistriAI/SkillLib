cps-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Comparative Political Studies (CPS) manuscript against the comparative-politics literature and the rival explanations it must beat. Sharpens the gap and the engaged debate; it does not build the theory or design the study.

When to use

  • positioning a CPS manuscript against comparative-politics literature
  • sharpening the gap and engaged debate
  • writing or rewriting the literature/positioning section
  • writing the introduction's gap paragraph
  • addressing reviewer feedback on literature engagement
  • naming the strongest rival explanation
  • locating the paper in a specific debate

When not to use

  • building the theory
  • designing the study
