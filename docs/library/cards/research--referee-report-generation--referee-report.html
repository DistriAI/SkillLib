referee-report

separate

Generate academic referee reports for economics/finance papers, followed by a 150-word letter to the editor with recommendation (Reject / Major Revision) and Kai Wu signature. Two modes (normal / high-level), configurable number of comments; recommendation choice drives evaluation tone (Reject → negative, Major Revision → neutral). Trigger when user says "referee report" / "write referee report" / "审稿报告" / "写审稿意见" / "generate referee report" / "review this paper".

When to use

  • Generate referee reports for economics papers
  • Generate referee reports for finance papers
  • Write referee reports in English
  • Create letters to the editor with recommendations
  • Provide specific comments on empirical results
  • Evaluate identification strategies
  • Generate reports in normal or high-level modes

When not to use

  • Tasks not related to academic paper review
  • Generating reports in languages other than English
  • Providing recommendations other than Reject or Major Revision
