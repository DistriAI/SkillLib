spend-gate

separate

Shared step of the marketing playbooks (make-static-ads, research-market, run-paid-campaigns, work-with-creators): gets the human's yes before any credits are spent. Shows the price exactly as quoted, or asks for one yes to a credit ceiling for paid data reads, stays under it and logs each charge. Also covers scheduled runs. Loaded by those playbooks; not for direct use.

When to use

  • Getting human approval before credit spend
  • Managing engine-quoted work approvals
  • Handling proxy data read credit ceilings
  • Logging credit charges
  • Managing scheduled run spending limits

When not to use

  • Direct use outside of marketing playbooks
