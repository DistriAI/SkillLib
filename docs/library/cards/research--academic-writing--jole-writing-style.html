jole-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing prose, the 100-word abstract, and the introduction for a Journal of Labor Economics (JOLE) manuscript — Chicago author-date references, the 20,000-word economy, and a general labor-economics audience. Polishes exposition; it does not change results or design.

When to use

  • polishing prose
  • polishing the 100-word abstract
  • polishing the introduction
  • formatting Chicago author-date references
  • adhering to the 20,000-word economy
  • targeting a general labor-economics audience

When not to use

  • changing results
  • changing design
