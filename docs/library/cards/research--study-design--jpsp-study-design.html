jpsp-study-design

separate

Use when designing the multi-study package for a Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP) manuscript — sequencing studies, powering each one, choosing experimental / longitudinal / dyadic designs, and planning preregistration. Designs the study set; it does not collect or fabricate data.

When to use

  • Planning the sequence and roles of studies
  • Powering each study and the package
  • Choosing experimental, longitudinal, or dyadic designs
  • Deciding what to preregister
  • Designing for internal meta-analysis
  • Building in alternative-explanation tests

When not to use

  • Collecting data
  • Fabricating data
