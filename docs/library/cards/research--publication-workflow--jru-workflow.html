jru-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jru-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from question through rebuttal for a Journal of Risk and Uncertainty (JRU) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which jru-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work for JRU submission
  • diagnosing current bottleneck in JRU draft
  • switching into revision mode after JRU decision letter
  • routing by paper archetype

When not to use

  • replacing specialized jru-* skills
  • treating JRU as interchangeable with Experimental Economics
  • treating JRU as JEBO or Theory and Decision
  • polishing exhibits while parameter estimates are moving
  • letting appendix carry main text arguments
