competency-matrix

authentic

Competency matrix of expected proficiency by job title and grade, with assessment method and linked skill area. Use for role frameworks and hiring bars.

When to use

  • competency matrix
  • skills framework
  • role competency model
  • capability matrix

When not to use

  • Empty template only. It does not compute payroll, tax, leave balances or KPIs.
  • Select options are a starting set. Rename them to match how the business talks.
  • No automation, reminders or sync. Those need the integration layer.
  • Does not assess or certify competence.
  • Legal, tax and HR review is still required before this drives real decisions.
