render-3d-character-explainer

separate

Assemble an animated character-explainer video ad (~77s, 9:16, any art style: Pixar-style 3D, claymation, anime, paper-cut, felt) on an \"N types of X\" listicle spine: one recurring human protagonist plus one persona character per list item. Given per-scene clips, a scene timing table and narration, it trims, normalises, concatenates and mixes them, reusing the source ad's audio (restyle) or fresh per-scene voiceover over a music bed (original), loops a scene's keyframe when its clip failed, and burns captions last. Free, deterministic assembly (Python and ffmpeg, no paid calls); the recipe supplies the clips, keyframes, audio and captions. Use for the 3d-character-explainer format.

When to use

  • 3d-character-explainer format
  • animated character spot
  • N types of X listicle spine
  • restyle mode assembly
  • original mode assembly
  • deterministic video assembly
  • per-scene trim and normalize
  • burn captions

When not to use

  • generative work
  • paid calls
  • Nano-Banana cast anchors
  • Kling-V3 i2v clips
  • ElevenLabs VO
  • music generation
