journal-of-climate

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Climate or deciding whether a climate-dynamics or climate-variability manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the large-scale-climate-science bar, model-evaluation and statistical-rigor expectations, AMS house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a climate-dynamics or climate-variability paper
  • re-framing a regional analysis or model run into a large-scale climate-science contribution
  • choosing between Journal of Climate, a dynamics-focused AMS sibling, and a broader earth-science venue
  • checking model-evaluation and statistical-significance expectations
  • large-scale atmospheric and oceanic circulation and their role in climate
  • climate variability and modes (ENSO, NAO, monsoons, decadal variability)
  • climate change detection, attribution, and projection
  • climate modeling and model evaluation

When not to use

  • local weather case study
  • short-range forecast verification
  • impacts paper that applies climate data without contributing to climate understanding
  • replacing the journal's current author guidance
