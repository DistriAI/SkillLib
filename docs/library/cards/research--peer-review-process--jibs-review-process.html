jibs-review-process

separate

Use when setting expectations for, or interpreting, the Journal of International Business Studies (JIBS) editorial process — the desk-screen, double-blind review, area-editor routing by IB subfield, the methods-editorial standards reviewers apply, and how to read a JIBS decision letter. Explains the process; the response letter itself is drafted with jibs-rebuttal.

When to use

  • setting expectations for JIBS process
  • interpreting JIBS decision letters
  • understanding area editor routing
  • understanding methods-editorial standards
  • calibrating expectations before submission
  • interpreting revise and resubmit implications

When not to use

  • drafting the response letter
