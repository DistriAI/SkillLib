jais-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight of a Journal of the Association for Information Systems (JAIS) manuscript — ManuscriptCentral portal, double-blind anonymization, the correct manuscript category and its length budget, the required SEM correlation/covariance matrix, the cover-letter elements (Senior Editor nomination, two suggested reviewers), and APA-6 formatting. Verifies readiness to submit; it does not polish prose (jais-writing-style) or interpret decisions (jais-review-process).

When to use

  • final pre-submission preflight
  • ManuscriptCentral portal check
  • double-blind anonymization verification
  • manuscript category and length budget check
  • SEM correlation/covariance matrix requirement
  • cover-letter elements verification
  • APA-6 formatting check

When not to use

  • polishing prose
  • interpreting decisions
