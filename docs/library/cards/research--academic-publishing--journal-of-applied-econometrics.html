journal-of-applied-econometrics

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Applied Econometrics (JAE) or deciding whether an applied-econometric manuscript that brings methods to a substantive economic question fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Applied Econometrics
  • deciding venue fit for applied-econometric manuscripts
  • re-framing data-rich empirical papers
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • checking method-and-evidence bar
  • reviewing house style and structure
  • verifying official submission requirements
  • suggesting alternative venues

When not to use

  • pure econometric theory with no application
  • applications with sloppy econometrics
  • replacing current official submission guidelines
