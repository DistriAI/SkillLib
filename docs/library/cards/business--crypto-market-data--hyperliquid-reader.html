hyperliquid-reader

separate

Read Hyperliquid (app.hyperliquid.xyz) perp and spot market data through opencli and Hyperliquid's public info API: mark, oracle, and mid prices, 24h change, funding rates (hourly and annualized APR), open interest, volume, the L2 order book, OHLCV candles, funding history, and cross-venue funding comparisons (Hyperliquid vs Binance vs Bybit) for funding arbitrage. Use this skill whenever the user mentions Hyperliquid (HL, HL DEX, hyperliquid.xyz) or asks about its perps, spot pairs such as PURR, funding, open interest, order book, or candles. Read-only market data: no account, order, or transfer operations.

When to use

  • Hyperliquid perp market data
  • Hyperliquid spot market data
  • Mark, oracle, and mid prices
  • 24h change and volume
  • Funding rates (hourly and annualized APR)
  • Open interest
  • L2 order book
  • OHLCV candles and funding history

When not to use

  • Account operations
  • Order placement
  • Order modification
  • Order cancellation
  • Fund transfers
  • Wallet-signed actions
