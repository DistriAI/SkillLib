Analyze stocks and cryptocurrencies using Yahoo Finance data. Supports portfolio management, watchlists with alerts, dividend analysis, 8-dimension stock scoring, viral trend detection (Hot Scanner), and rumor/early signal detection. Use for stock analysis, portfolio tracking, earnings reactions, crypto monitoring, trending stocks, or finding rumors before they hit mainstream. 财务和投资分析师在进行个股研究时，当需要深度研判买卖点与趋势，使用本技能可实时获取行情，计算MACD/RSI等技术指标，精准识别缺口与支撑压力位，智能预测未来3天走势并输出专业操作建议，大幅提升投研决策效率。

When to use

  • stock analysis
  • portfolio tracking
  • earnings reactions
  • crypto monitoring
  • trending stocks
  • finding rumors before they hit mainstream
  • dividend analysis
  • watchlists with alerts

When not to use

  • Yahoo Finance may lag 15-20 minutes
  • Short interest lags ~2 weeks (FINRA)
  • Insider trades lag 2-3 days (SEC filing)
  • US markets only (non-US incomplete)
  • Breaking news: 1h cache, keyword-based

Skill page

Conflict

The two skills share the name and the broad goal of stock analysis, but they are materially different procedures. The anbeime/skill copy is a China/A-share focused technical analysis workflow that fetches data via a local script, computes MA/MACD/RSI, identifies gaps and support/resistance, predicts a 3-day move, and outputs buy/sell advice with risk disclaimers. The LeoYeAI/openclaw-master-skills copy is a US/crypto focused multi-tool suite built on Yahoo Finance that performs 8-dimension scoring, portfolio management, watchlists/alerts, dividend analysis, hot/trend scanning, and rumor/early-signal detection. Neither copy is a shortened or wrapped version of the other; crowning one would hide a real alternative workflow and a different set of capabilities.
