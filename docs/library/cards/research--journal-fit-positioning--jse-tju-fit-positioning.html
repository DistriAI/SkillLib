jse-tju-fit-positioning

separate

Use when an author is deciding whether a systems, management-science, operations-research, engineering, control, or empirical manuscript genuinely fits 《系统工程学报》 (Journal of Systems Engineering, Tianjin University), or when a concrete rerouting decision is needed among Systems Engineering Theory and Practice, Journal of Management Sciences in China, Chinese Journal of Management Science, Systems Engineering and Electronics, Control and Decision, and Operations Research and Management Science.

When to use

  • Deciding fit for systems, management-science, or OR manuscripts
  • Rerouting decisions among seven specific journals
  • Evaluating system object, mechanism, and method increment
  • Identifying primary deliverable of a paper
  • Performing deletion tests for core contribution

When not to use

  • Providing admission probability predictions
  • Using unverified acceptance rates or fees
  • Comparing journals solely by impact factor
  • Treating heuristic routing as editorial rules
