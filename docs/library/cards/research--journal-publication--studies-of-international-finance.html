studies-of-international-finance

separate

Use when targeting 《国际金融研究》(Studies of International Finance — 中国银行股份有限公司与中国国际金融学会主办、1985 年创刊的国际金融月刊) or deciding whether a Chinese finance manuscript fits this top-tier venue. Encodes the journal's global-finance fit, framing, abstract/keyword house style, official chinaifs.org.cn submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《国际金融研究》
  • deciding if a Chinese finance manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese economics manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit for international finance topics
  • verifying submission requirements via chinaifs.org.cn

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • manuscripts where finance is only one mechanism among others
  • pure domestic credit or corporate finance empirical studies without international perspective
  • open trade or global value chain topics
  • domestic bank credit and monetary policy topics
  • general empirical studies for university finance economics
