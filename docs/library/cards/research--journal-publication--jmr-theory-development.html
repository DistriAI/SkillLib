jmr-theory-development

separate

Use when building the conceptual core of a Journal of Marketing Research (JMR) manuscript — deriving behavioral hypotheses with a testable process mechanism, or specifying the economic primitives and behavioral assumptions of a structural/econometric model. Adapts theory to JMR's two dominant genres without inflating into a managerial Journal-of-Marketing narrative.

When to use

  • Deriving behavioral hypotheses with a testable process mechanism
  • Specifying economic primitives and behavioral assumptions of a structural/econometric model
  • Building theory for JMR behavioral papers
  • Building theory for JMR modeling/econometric papers
  • Testing whether the manuscript addresses marketing reviewers
  • Separating construct, mechanism, scope condition, and testable implication

When not to use

  • Inflating into a managerial Journal-of-Marketing narrative
  • Presenting post hoc patterns as a priori behavioral hypotheses
  • Black-box effects with no tested process in a behavioral paper
  • Structural models whose assumptions and identification are never stated
  • Over-claiming generalizability beyond what the mechanism or model supports
