restud-topic-selection

separate

Use when evaluating whether a research idea clears the REStud top-5 bar, or when sharpening a fuzzy idea into one clean original contribution for a The Review of Economic Studies (REStud) manuscript. Diagnoses fit and contribution; does not draft prose or design empirics.

When to use

  • Evaluating if a research idea clears the REStud top-5 bar
  • Sharpening a fuzzy idea into one clean original contribution
  • Diagnosing prior desk rejections related to topic fit
  • Assessing if a paper is REStud-level or field-journal level
  • Compressing a contribution into a single sentence
  • Auditing novelty against existing literature
  • Checking if a contribution has general interest

When not to use

  • Drafting prose
  • Designing empirics
