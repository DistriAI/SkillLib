make-video-ads

separate

Use when the marketer says: make me a video ad, a 30-second ad for paid social, a Reel / TikTok / Shorts ad, a UGC-style video made with AI (no real creator), a product demo video, remake this ad I like for my brand, cut our best organic video into an ad, more like my winning video with new hooks, my video ad got rejected — fix it. Delivers a produced, ready-to-post video ad grounded in a researched angle: one message, a hook for the first two seconds, every claim traced to the brand's signed-off claims, no generated person posing as a customer, the exact words and the price approved before anything renders (one video, or one plus two hook variants), an honest review of the result, priced fixes only on a yes, and launch copy and notes for the ads team. Works in chat apps. Never publishes or spends ad budget.   • Organic posting calendars and phone-shot organic scripts — **run-organic-social**. Real creators filming for the brand — **work-with-creators**.
  • Researching angles — **research-competitors** / **research-market**. Approving claims — **learn-my-brand**.
