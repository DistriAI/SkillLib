jbv-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing the prose of a Journal of Business Venturing (JBV) manuscript — front-loading the entrepreneurial phenomenon, writing for a multidisciplinary audience, honoring JBV's openness to narrative/interpretive voice, and applying Elsevier reference conventions and a ≤250-word abstract. Late-stage polish; do not use as a substitute for a real entrepreneurship-theory contribution (jbv-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • polishing JBV manuscript prose
  • front-loading entrepreneurial phenomenon
  • writing for multidisciplinary audience
  • tightening abstract, intro, and discussion
  • applying Elsevier reference conventions
  • ensuring abstract is under 250 words
  • matching voice to deductive or narrative mode
  • executing a style review pass

When not to use

  • substitute for real entrepreneurship-theory contribution
  • substitute for jbv-contribution-framing
  • giving submission-ready advice before checking official-source-map.md
  • polishing format if sibling journal owns the contribution
