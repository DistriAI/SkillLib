loadpage

separate

Remove signs of AI-generated writing from text. Use when editing or reviewing text to make it sound more natural and human-written. Based on Wikipedia's comprehensive "Signs of AI writing" guide. Detects and fixes patterns including: inflated symbolism, promotional language, superficial -ing analyses, vague attributions, em dash overuse, rule of three, AI vocabulary words, negative parallelisms, and excessive conjunctive phrases.

When to use

  • editing text to sound natural
  • reviewing text for AI patterns
  • removing AI-generated writing signs
  • fixing inflated symbolism
  • fixing promotional language
  • fixing superficial -ing analyses
  • fixing vague attributions
  • fixing em dash overuse
