socs-problematic

separate

Use to build a sociological problem consciousness (问题意识) for 《社会学研究》 (Sociological Studies) — turning a variable relationship or a pile of fieldwork into a question about how a social mechanism, structure, or process works. Use when the manuscript still reads as "X affects Y" without a sociological stake.

When to use

  • Turning variable relationships into sociological questions
  • Elevating fieldwork findings to structural or process-based problems
  • Identifying social mechanisms behind empirical anomalies
  • Refining research questions for Sociological Studies
  • Distinguishing sociological stakes from statistical effects

When not to use

  • Writing policy recommendations
  • Defining variable net effects
  • Using grand terms without specific mechanism confusion
