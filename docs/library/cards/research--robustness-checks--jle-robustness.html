jle-robustness

separate

Use when a The Journal of Law and Economics (JLE) manuscript's headline estimate must be shown to survive specification, sample, jurisdiction, and inference choices before submission or in an R&R. Builds the robustness suite a law-and-economics referee expects; it does not establish the primary identification (jle-identification) or format the exhibits (jle-tables-figures).
