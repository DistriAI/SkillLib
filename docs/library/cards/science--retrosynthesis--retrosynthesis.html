retrosynthesis

separate

Performs retrosynthetic planning using AiZynthFinder (template-based MCTS), maintained or version-pinned template-free models, ASKCOS, and emerging RetroSynFormer with explicit handling of route scoring, configurable MCTS rewards, building-block availability, and forward-prediction checks. Use when assessing synthetic feasibility of generated or selected molecules, planning multi-step syntheses, building synthesis-aware design pipelines, or screening libraries for retro-route feasibility.

When to use

  • assessing synthetic feasibility of generated or selected molecules
  • planning multi-step syntheses
  • building synthesis-aware design pipelines
  • screening libraries for retro-route feasibility
  • validating generated routes via forward prediction
  • cost-aware synthesis planning
  • disconnection-aware design
  • patent-aware route planning

When not to use

  • experimental validation of synthesis routes
  • forward reaction enumeration
  • generative molecular design without feasibility checks
