quality-assurance-auditor

authentic

Perform the final submission-level audit of mathematical-modeling workflow integrity, evidence quality, anti-fabrication, paper coherence, figures, references, and contest readiness after consistency and completeness audits pass.

When to use

  • final submission-level audit
  • workflow integrity audit
  • evidence integrity audit
  • method quality audit
  • paper quality audit
  • presentation audit
  • contest readiness check

When not to use

  • partial audits
  • repairing issues inside QA
  • using artifact count as quality proxy
  • hiding uncertainty
  • downgrading blockers silently
