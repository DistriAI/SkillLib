orgsci-data-analysis

separate

Use when analyzing and reporting results for an Organization Science manuscript across its eclectic methods — establishing trustworthiness for qualitative work, the right estimator for quantitative/multilevel data, transparency for simulation/formal models, and mechanism-supported inference where causal identification is impossible. Executes and reports the analysis; it does not design the study (orgsci-methods) or frame the contribution (orgsci-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • analyzing and reporting results for Organization Science manuscripts
  • establishing trustworthiness for qualitative work
  • selecting estimators for quantitative and multilevel data
  • ensuring transparency for simulation and formal models
  • supporting inference with mechanism evidence when causal identification is impossible
  • executing statistical analysis batteries
  • auditing unit, comparison, uncertainty, and reproducibility
  • generating decision ledgers for manuscript patches

When not to use

  • designing the study
  • framing the contribution
  • simulation work execution
  • qualitative work execution
