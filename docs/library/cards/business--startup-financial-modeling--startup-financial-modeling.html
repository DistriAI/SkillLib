Conflict

The two copies are nearly identical, but they disagree on the runway formula and on how to label the three scenarios. The first copy uses a simpler burn-rate formula and labels scenarios P10/P50/P90; the second copy uses a net-cash-burn formula with an explicit positive-burn guard and explicitly forbids P10/P50/P90 labels unless a probabilistic model justifies them. Crowning one copy would hide a real alternative, so the combined instruction should preserve both the detailed modeling steps and the conflicting guidance under a Conflicts heading.
