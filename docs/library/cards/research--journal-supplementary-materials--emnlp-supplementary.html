emnlp-supplementary

separate

Use when dividing an EMNLP paper between content pages, uncounted Limitations and ethics sections, appendices, and ACL Rolling Review supplementary uploads — deciding what reviewers are owed in the body, what belongs in appendix prompts-and-tables territory, and what ships as anonymous code and data archives.

When to use

  • dividing EMNLP paper content
  • deciding placement of Limitations and ethics sections
  • structuring appendices for prompts and tables
  • preparing anonymous supplementary uploads
  • splitting long and short papers under page caps
  • checking anonymity in supplementary archives

When not to use

  • writing the core methodology
  • conducting the experiments
  • generating the annotated corpus
