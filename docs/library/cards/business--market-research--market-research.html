market-research

combined

Industry and market research. Market sizing, structure, competition, regulation, supply chains, pricing, M&A. Produces a single compact, source-cited research report with Porter's Five Forces, HHI / CR4, PESTLE, and trade flows. Supports UK, US, EU, Australia, and global scope, with multi-geography comparison. Manages the founder's market understanding — running initial market research, updating findings, and answering questions about market size, customer segments, buying behavior, pricing benchmarks, and industry trends. Use when the conversation touches market size (TAM/SAM/SOM), who the buyers are and how they make decisions, what people typically pay, industry tailwinds or headwinds, or when the founder wants to understand the broader landscape their idea sits in.

When to use

  • Market sizing
  • Market structure
  • Competition analysis
  • Regulation analysis
  • Supply chains
  • Pricing analysis
  • M&A analysis
  • Multi-geography comparison

Skill page

Conflict

The three copies are not reproductions of one procedure. davepoon/buildwithclaude is a conversational founder workflow that reads/writes a local startup/market-research.md file, infers intent, and decides between inline WebSearch/WebFetch and dispatching a web-researcher subagent. gooseworks-ai/goose-skills is an API-driven workflow that requires Gooseworks credentials and calls Orthogonal/Fiber/Apollo endpoints to find companies, details, and decision makers. brycewang-stanford/Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills is a structured econstack report generator with CLI preambles, parameter database checks, learnings capture, safety hooks, geography/depth/focus/format arguments, and a fixed analytical framework (Porter, PESTLE, HHI/CR4, trade flows). Each has material steps and constraints the others lack, and they disagree on the core mechanism (local file workflow vs. external API calls vs. local CLI report pipeline), so crowning one would hide real alternatives.
