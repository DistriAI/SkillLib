car-methods

separate

Use when the research design and identification are the bottleneck for a Contemporary Accounting Research (CAR) manuscript — choosing and defending an archival, experimental, analytical, field, or survey design and, for human-participant work, meeting CAR's mandatory ethics-approval verification. Designs the study; it does not run the estimation (car-data-analysis).

When to use

  • Choosing and defending research design
  • Archival, experimental, analytical, field, or survey design
  • Meeting CAR's mandatory ethics-approval verification
  • Designing the study
  • Matching design to the question
  • Designing against threats CAR reviewers probe
  • Securing ethics-approval verification
  • Preparing full research instrument

When not to use

  • Running the estimation
