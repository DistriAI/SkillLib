Use when targeting International Symposium on Experimental Robotics (ISER) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for experimental robotics.

When to use

  • targeting International Symposium on Experimental Robotics (ISER)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for experimental robotics
  • manuscript in experimental robotics needs a conference-fit read
  • re-framed from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • ISRR should read as agenda-setting robotics research
  • CoRL as robot learning
  • ICRA/IROS as broader robotics conference targets
  • Route broad robotics theory and long-horizon research vision to ISRR
  • flagship robot systems to RSS/ICRA/IROS
  • human-interaction claims to HRI/RO-MAN when people are central
