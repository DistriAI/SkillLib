Conflict

The LeoYeAI copies (openclaw-marketing-skills and openclaw-master-skills) are nearly identical, providing a detailed, step-by-step guide for form optimization (field-by-field, layout, error handling). The sickn33 copy (agentic-awesome-skills) introduces a distinct, mandatory diagnostic framework (the 'Form Health & Friction Index') with specific scoring weights and health bands that the LeoYeAI copies lack. Conversely, the LeoYeAI copies contain specific implementation details (e.g., 44px tap targets, specific button placement rules, progressive commitment patterns) that are absent or less detailed in the sickn33 copy. Because the sickn33 copy adds a unique diagnostic phase and the LeoYeAI copies add unique implementation constraints, crowning one would hide a real alternative approach (diagnostic-first vs. implementation-first).
