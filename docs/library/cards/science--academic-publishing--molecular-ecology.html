molecular-ecology

separate

Use when targeting Molecular Ecology (Mol Ecol) or deciding whether a manuscript applying molecular approaches to ecological, evolutionary, or conservation questions fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Molecular Ecology
  • deciding manuscript fit for Molecular Ecology
  • population genomics and landscape genetics
  • phylogeography
  • molecular adaptation and selection
  • environmental DNA and metabarcoding
  • host-microbe and symbiosis ecology
  • conservation genomics

When not to use

  • marker-development notes
  • methods without a biological question
  • descriptive genotyping without ecological inference
