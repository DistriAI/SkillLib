Use when targeting IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for robotics flagship.

When to use

  • targeting IROS
  • deciding if a manuscript fits IROS
  • conference fit read
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style to conference narrative
  • evidence-gap diagnosis
  • anonymity diagnosis
  • artifact diagnosis
  • rebuttal diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • routing to ICRA
  • routing to RSS
  • routing to HRI
  • routing to ISER
