amanthro-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning an American Anthropologist (AA) manuscript against the literature so it reads as a four-field contribution. AA readers span sociocultural, archaeology, biological, and linguistic anthropology, so the paper must engage the conversations they expect and decenter a US/Anglophone-only canon. Stakes the contribution and citational politics; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Positioning an AA manuscript against the literature
  • Drafting or revising the introduction and contribution paragraph
  • Engaging live anthropological conversations across subfields
  • Decentering the US/Anglophone canon
  • Distinguishing contribution from closest prior studies
  • Practicing citational politics to include marginalized voices

When not to use

  • Writing the literature review
  • Research design
  • Submission process
