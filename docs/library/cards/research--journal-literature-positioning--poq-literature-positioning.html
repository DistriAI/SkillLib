poq-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Public Opinion Quarterly (POQ) manuscript against the literature so it reads as a contribution to public-opinion or survey-methodology scholarship. POQ readers span opinion theory, communication, political behavior, and survey methods, so the paper must engage the right literatures and show the methodological lineage. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction and contribution paragraph
  • Addressing reviewer feedback about missing obvious work or survey-methods literature
  • Distinguishing contribution from closest prior papers
  • Engaging substantive and methodological literatures simultaneously
  • Naming the gap precisely in opinion or survey science
  • Pre-empting obvious objections regarding coverage, nonresponse, or mode
  • Building a three-column map of incumbent conversation, tension, and delta
  • Comparing against sibling venues like Political Analysis or Journal of Politics

When not to use

  • Writing the full literature review
  • Self-citation that breaks anonymity
  • Claiming 'first to study' for incremental contributions
  • Strawmanning prior work or hiding the closest competitor paper
