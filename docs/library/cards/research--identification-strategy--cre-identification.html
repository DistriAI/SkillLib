cre-identification

separate

Use when the empirical identification strategy is the bottleneck for a 《中国农村经济》 manuscript — micro-household / village-level quasi-experimental designs (DID, IV, RDD, PSM). Stress-tests the design and the rural-specific endogeneity before drafting tables.

When to use

  • micro-household / village-level quasi-experimental designs
  • DID, IV, RDD, PSM
  • rural-specific endogeneity
  • staggered DID
  • instrumental variables
  • regression discontinuity
  • propensity score matching
  • structural estimation

When not to use

  • pure descriptive statistics + OLS
  • self-selected behavior without endogeneity treatment
  • TWFE + staggered without heterogeneity discussion
  • PSM without unobservable selectivity discussion
  • IV without exclusion argument
  • RDD without density test
