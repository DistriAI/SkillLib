jmsc-behavioral-om

separate

Use when taking the behavioral operations (行为运作) path of a 《管理科学学报》 (Journal of Management Sciences in China) manuscript — designing controlled experiments, manipulation checks, and a behavioral model that identifies the bias parameter, so the contribution is a model that explains behavior, not just an effect. Use when human decisions are the object of study instead of pure analytical modeling; use when reviewers ask where the identifiable behavioral parameter is.

When to use

  • Designing controlled experiments for behavioral operations
  • Identifying behavioral parameters from experimental data
  • Conducting manipulation checks
  • Comparing behavioral models to rational benchmarks
  • Addressing reviewer concerns about behavioral model identification
  • Calibrating behavioral models with experimental data

When not to use

  • Pure analytical modeling without human decision data
  • Optimization, algorithms, or numerical proofs
  • Pure psychology experiments without operations decisions
  • Hypothetical surveys without incentive compatibility
  • Reporting effects without a behavioral model
