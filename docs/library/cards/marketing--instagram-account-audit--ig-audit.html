ig-audit

separate

Post-mortem on what the user has already posted - which reels actually worked, why, and what to stop making. Use when the user pastes their Instagram insights or past posts and asks "what's working", "why did this flop", "read my analytics", "audit my content", or wants to know what to do more of.

When to use

  • audit my content
  • read my analytics
  • what's working
  • why did this flop
  • analyze past posts
  • determine what to do more of

When not to use

  • planning future content
  • creating new reels
  • optimizing profile
