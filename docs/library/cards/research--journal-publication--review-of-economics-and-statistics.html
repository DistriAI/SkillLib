review-of-economics-and-statistics

separate

Use when targeting Review of Economics and Statistics (REStat) or deciding whether an applied econometrics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Review of Economics and Statistics
  • deciding if applied econometrics manuscript fits REStat
  • assessing fit for focused empirical papers
  • evaluating papers with careful identification or measurement
  • checking REStat desk-reject risks
  • re-framing papers for REStat
  • verifying official submission guidelines
  • assessing method and evidence bar for REStat

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • general-interest empirical importance targeting AER or QJE
  • applied-micro design as headline targeting AEJ Applied
  • empirical macro targeting AEJ Macro
  • genuine new estimator with full theory targeting Econometrica
