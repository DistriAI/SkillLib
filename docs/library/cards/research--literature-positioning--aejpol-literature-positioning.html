aejpol-literature-positioning

separate

Use when placing the contribution against the policy-evaluation literature and sharpening it as a policy answer for an AEJ: Economic Policy manuscript. Stakes the contribution and the policy-relevant "so what"; it does not write the full introduction or design the identification.

When to use

  • placing contribution against policy-evaluation literature
  • sharpening contribution as a policy answer
  • stating the open policy question
  • framing contribution as a sharper policy answer
  • identifying credibility or welfare upgrades
  • mapping referee pushback to fixes
  • framing contribution bullets for AEJ: Policy

When not to use

  • writing the full introduction
  • designing the identification
