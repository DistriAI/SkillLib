nous

separate

Use when targeting Noûs or deciding whether an analytic philosophy manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit as one of the big-three generalist venues, the argument-rigor and originality bar, dialectical-engagement expectations, house style and double-blind norms, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for Noûs
  • tightening argument for top generalist bar
  • deciding among big-three generalist venues
  • Noûs originality/rigor bar
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • house style and double-blind norms
  • official-submission re-check

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current submission guidelines
  • competent survey
  • incremental tweak to a familiar move
  • paper that sidesteps its best objections
