msom-methods

separate

Use when matching the method and identification to the operations problem for a Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM) manuscript — selecting the analytical model class and solution approach, or the empirical identification strategy. Designs the study; msom-data-analysis executes and reports it.

When to use

  • selecting analytical model class
  • selecting solution approach
  • selecting empirical identification strategy
  • matching method to operations decision
  • designing M&SOM study
  • justifying method class by operational decision
  • planning numerical study for analytical models
  • addressing endogenous operational choices in empirical studies

When not to use

  • executing and reporting the study
  • analytical queueing work
  • optimization work
  • method-first theory for Operations Research
  • broader OR/MS reach for Management Science
  • wider OM readership for Production and Operations Management
