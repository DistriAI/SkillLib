lancet-ethics

separate

Use to satisfy The Lancet's clinical-ethics and research-integrity requirements — ethics-committee approval and informed consent under the Declaration of Helsinki, the Declaration of interests, ICMJE author contributions, the role-of-the-funding-source statement, the data-sharing statement, patient and public involvement, and SAGER sex/gender plus equity reporting.

When to use

  • Preparing ethics, contributions, conflicts, funding, and data-sharing statements
  • Addressing missing Declaration of interests or role-of-the-funding-source statement
  • Adding missing sex/gender or race/ethnicity reporting
  • Resolving reviewer or editor flags on consent, integrity, or funder-influence

When not to use

  • Omitting the role-of-the-funding-source statement
  • Leaving the data sharing statement blank for a clinical trial
  • Reporting a mixed-sex study without sex/gender analysis
