jqte-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jqte-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from fit positioning through rebuttal for 《数量经济技术经济研究》 (The Journal of Quantitative & Technological Economics, JQTE). Routes — does not replace — the specialized skills, and cross-routes to other journals when the contribution is clean causal, industrial-policy, or pure modeling.

When to use

  • Deciding which jqte-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from fit positioning through rebuttal
  • Routing for JQTE submissions
  • Cross-routing to other journals for clean causal, industrial-policy, or pure modeling contributions
  • Mapping rejection signals to root cause skills
  • Determining contribution type (measurement, forecasting, method application, causal)

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized jqte-* skills
  • Handling clean causal + theory contributions (route to economic-research/china-economic-quarterly)
  • Handling industrial policy empirical + thick robustness (route to china-industrial-economics)
  • Handling mathematical models/algorithms as the main contribution (route to journal-of-management-sciences-china)
  • Generating rebuttal responses before manuscript revision is complete
  • Applying causal journal placebo/parallel trends routines as JQTE robustness
