CRISPResso2 for analyzing CRISPR gene editing outcomes. Quantifies indels, HDR efficiency, and generates comprehensive editing reports. Use when analyzing amplicon sequencing data from CRISPR editing experiments to assess editing efficiency.

When to use

  • analyzing amplicon sequencing data from CRISPR editing experiments
  • assessing editing efficiency
  • quantifying indels
  • quantifying HDR efficiency
  • generating comprehensive editing reports
  • batch analysis of multiple CRISPR editing samples
  • pool analysis of multiple guides
  • WGS analysis of off-target editing
