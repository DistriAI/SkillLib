jpsp-review-process

separate

Use to understand how a Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP) manuscript is judged — per-section editors, masked review, the theoretical-innovation gate, central vs. peripheral limitations, and decision categories. Sets expectations; it does not predict any specific editor's or reviewer's verdict.

When to use

  • Anticipating how the section will evaluate the paper before submitting
  • Interpreting a decision letter's framing
  • Deciding whether a prospective design should be a Registered Report
  • Understanding the theoretical-innovation gate
  • Categorizing central vs. peripheral limitations
  • Understanding decision categories like Accept with revision or R&R

When not to use

  • Predicting any specific editor's or reviewer's verdict
