notes-humanizer

authentic

Strips AI writing patterns from text and rewrites it to sound genuinely human by removing statistical defaults and injecting the specific signals that human writers produce. Use when a draft reads as AI-generated, over-polished, or rhythmically uniform — including blog posts, emails, LinkedIn posts, or any prose that needs to sound like a real person wrote it. Produces a pattern audit, side-by-side comparison, itemised change log, and clean rewritten output ready to paste.
