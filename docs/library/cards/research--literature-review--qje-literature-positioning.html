qje-literature-positioning

separate

Use when staking a Quarterly Journal of Economics (QJE) manuscript's contribution against the frontier — what it builds on, what it overturns, and why it belongs in a general-interest journal. Positions the contribution; it does not write the full literature survey.

When to use

  • staking a QJE manuscript's contribution against the frontier
  • positioning the contribution in the introduction
  • identifying what the paper builds on and overturns
  • determining why the paper belongs in a general-interest journal
  • naming the frontier papers
  • stating the delta
  • bounding the claim

When not to use

  • writing the full literature survey
  • creating a standalone Literature Review section
