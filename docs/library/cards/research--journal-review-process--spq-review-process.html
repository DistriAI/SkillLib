spq-review-process

separate

Use to understand how Social Psychology Quarterly (SPQ) evaluates a manuscript — masked (blinded) review, the blinding check that temporarily rejects under-blinded papers, what reviewers from different sociological-social-psychology traditions weigh, and the reject / revise-and-resubmit / accept decision pattern. Sets expectations and shapes the paper to survive review; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • stress-test fit and blinding before submitting
  • interpreting a decision letter
  • understanding what reviewers from different traditions weigh
  • deciding between Article or Note
  • shaping the paper to pass review
  • running a review-risk pass
  • comparing against sibling journals

When not to use

  • contacting editors
  • submitting individual-cognition papers
  • submitting macro-sociology papers without social-psychological mechanism
  • expecting open data to improve acceptance odds
