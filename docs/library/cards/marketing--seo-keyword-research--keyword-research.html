Ryze-AI-Adgent is tool-specific, requiring the Ryze MCP connector and Google Ads/DataForSEO calls, and mandates CPC in the output. nowork-studio is a thin pointer to an external WORKFLOW.md and explicitly defers calendar work to a different skill. Crowning one copy would hide real alternative procedures and tooling paths.
