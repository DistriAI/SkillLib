Use when targeting Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres (JGR-Atmospheres) or deciding whether a full-length atmospheric-science manuscript fits this AGU venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting JGR-Atmospheres for full-length atmospheric-science studies
  • deciding if a manuscript fits JGR-Atmospheres
  • choosing between JGR-Atmospheres, GRL, or ACP
  • assessing fit for atmospheric composition, aerosols, clouds, dynamics, or radiation
  • evaluating method and evidence bar for JGR-Atmospheres
  • checking JGR-Atmospheres house style and structure
  • reviewing official-submission checklist for JGR-Atmospheres
  • assessing desk-reject heuristics for JGR-Atmospheres

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • short-format letters
  • broad readership publications
