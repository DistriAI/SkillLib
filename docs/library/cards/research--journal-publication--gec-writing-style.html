gec-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing the prose of a Global Environmental Change (GEC) manuscript so it reads for an interdisciplinary, policy-relevant audience within the word and abstract caps. GEC values clarity and rigor that travel across disciplines. Polishes the writing; it does not draft the analysis.

When to use

  • Drafting the introduction, abstract, or discussion
  • Polishing prose for interdisciplinary readability
  • Cutting to meet word caps
  • Writing the abstract and plain-language framing
  • Front-loading the contribution
  • Defining terms consistently
  • Minimizing discipline-specific jargon
  • Connecting to scale and stakes

When not to use

  • Drafting the analysis
