joe-rebuttal

separate

Use after a Journal of Econometrics (JoE) revision request to plan the response letter and revised manuscript — handling technical referee objections on assumptions, asymptotics, and Monte Carlo, and resubmitting through the live official route (note the fee can re-trigger on resubmissions over one year old).

When to use

  • Planning JoE response letter
  • Handling technical referee objections
  • Addressing assumption challenges
  • Fixing asymptotic proof gaps
  • Improving Monte Carlo simulations
  • Managing JoE resubmission logistics
  • Checking resubmission fee re-trigger

When not to use

  • Initial submission to JoE
  • Rhetorical responses without evidence
  • Drafting response before revised manuscript exists
  • Ignoring specific referee reports
