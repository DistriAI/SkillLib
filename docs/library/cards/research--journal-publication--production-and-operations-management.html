production-and-operations-management

separate

Use when targeting Production and Operations Management (POM) or deciding whether a broad operations-management analytical or empirical manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Production and Operations Management
  • deciding if a broad operations-management manuscript fits POM
  • analytical or empirical OM papers
  • sustainability, healthcare, or interface operations
  • operations-marketing or operations-finance interface papers
  • assessing POM desk-reject risks
  • finding credible OM, MS, or OR alternatives

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • pure OR methodology papers with no OM payoff
  • non-OM papers framed as operations
  • analytical OM theory for an INFORMS audience
  • survey-based or behavioral empirical OM
  • broad management insight across subfields
  • OR methodology or optimization for its own sake
