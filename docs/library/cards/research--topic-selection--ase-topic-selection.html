ase-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a software-engineering project belongs at ASE (IEEE/ACM Automated Software Engineering) or should be routed to ICSE, ESEC/FSE, ISSTA, a PL venue, or an SE journal, and when distinguishing ASE by its automation-centric contribution shape, its tool/technique emphasis, and its AI4SE/SE4AI scope.

When to use

  • deciding if a project belongs at ASE
  • routing to ICSE, ESEC/FSE, ISSTA, PL venues, or SE journals
  • distinguishing ASE by automation-centric contribution shape
  • distinguishing ASE by tool/technique emphasis
  • distinguishing ASE by AI4SE/SE4AI scope
  • applying model-swap and re-label tests
  • selecting co-located tracks like Research Papers or NIER
  • performing cheap reconnaissance before committing

When not to use

  • broad empirical or foundational SE contributions where the lesson is a finding
  • depth in testing theory, program analysis foundations, or fault detection
  • program-language or compiler advances
  • new model or architecture contributions where the SE task is a benchmark
  • studies too long or nuanced for the page budget
