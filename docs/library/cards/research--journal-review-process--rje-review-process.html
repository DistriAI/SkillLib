rje-review-process

separate

Use to understand and plan around The RAND Journal of Economics (RJE) review pipeline — the upfront editor screen (possible desk reject), peer review by two anonymous referees, and a final read by the handling Editor. Sets expectations and shapes a submission that clears the first screen.

When to use

  • understand RJE review pipeline
  • plan around editor screen
  • decide how to pitch cover letter
  • front-load contribution
  • gauge desk-reject risk
  • calibrate submission before fee
  • anticipate screen on auction paper
  • understand referee and editor expectations

When not to use

  • general-micro or macro questions
  • assuming double-blind review
  • treating RJE like a general-interest journal
  • defensive or sprawling cover letters
