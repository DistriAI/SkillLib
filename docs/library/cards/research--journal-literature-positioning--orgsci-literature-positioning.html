orgsci-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning an Organization Science manuscript in the literature — identifying the organization-theory conversation it joins, problematizing rather than gap-spotting, and integrating across the disciplines (organization theory, strategy, sociology, economics, psychology) the journal draws on.

When to use

  • Positioning an Organization Science manuscript
  • Identifying the organization-theory conversation
  • Problematizing rather than gap-spotting
  • Integrating across organization theory, strategy, sociology, economics, and psychology
  • Building a three-column map of incumbent conversation, unresolved tension, and manuscript delta
  • Comparing against AMJ, ASQ, and Management Science
  • Checking resources/official-source-map.md for upload-week rules

When not to use

  • Summarizing everything and arguing nothing
  • Gap-spotting with no challenged assumption
  • Ignoring the sibling literature at the other level of analysis
  • Citing a discipline's theory without translating it to an organizational problem
