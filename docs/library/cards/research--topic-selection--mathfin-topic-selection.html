mathfin-topic-selection

separate

Use when choosing or sharpening a problem for Mathematical Finance (Wiley) — tests whether the question is a methodologically novel, rigorously tractable contribution to financial modelling (stochastic analysis, pricing, risk, portfolio, microstructure) rather than a routine computational application to data.

When to use

  • Choosing a problem for Mathematical Finance
  • Sharpening a financial-mathematics idea
  • Testing if a result clears the journal bar
  • Choosing between incremental extension and novel contribution
  • Evaluating methodological novelty
  • Assessing tractability of a theorem
  • Verifying fit for stochastic analysis tradition

When not to use

  • Routine computation on financial data
  • Purely empirical or econometric finance results
  • Incremental special cases of known theorems
  • Models proposed without proofs of formal properties
