accounting-organizations-and-society

separate

Use when targeting Accounting, Organizations and Society (AOS) or deciding whether an accounting manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Accounting, Organizations and Society (AOS)
  • deciding whether an accounting manuscript fits this venue
  • positioning qualitative, historical, field-based, or behavioral papers on accounting-in-context
  • framing accounting papers drawing on sociology, organization theory, or critical theory
  • identifying AOS desk-reject risks
  • providing credible CAR / TAR alternative lists
  • contrasting with economics-based venues

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • economics-based archival capital-markets papers
  • purely technical, capital-markets, or positive-economics papers with no social/organizational engagement
  • organization-theory-core work without an accounting focus
  • economics-core work
