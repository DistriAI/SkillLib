jams-contribution-framing

separate

Use when stating the contribution of a Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science (JAMS) manuscript — discharging JAMS's dual mandate of a theoretical AND a managerial contribution and defending against "incremental." Frames the contribution; it does not run the analysis (jams-data-analysis) or polish the prose (jams-writing-style).

When to use

  • Stating the contribution of a JAMS manuscript
  • Discharging JAMS's dual mandate of theoretical and managerial contribution
  • Defending against 'incremental' or 'new-context replication' critiques
  • Drafting explicit contribution sentences for the intro and discussion
  • Diagnosing the type of theoretical contribution
  • Quantifying managerial stakes in real units

When not to use

  • Running the analysis
  • Polishing the prose
