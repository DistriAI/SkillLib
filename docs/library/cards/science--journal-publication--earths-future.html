earths-future

separate

Use when targeting Earth's Future (Earth's Future) or deciding whether an Anthropocene, Earth-system-futures, or sustainability manuscript fits this AGU open-access venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Earth-system futures and projections
  • Human-Earth interactions
  • Sustainability and the Anthropocene
  • Risk, vulnerability, and resilience
  • Solutions-relevant analysis
  • Cross-cutting interdisciplinary studies
  • Venue selection between Earth's Future and One Earth
  • Desk-reject criteria evaluation

When not to use

  • Narrow disciplinary results without Earth-system futures engagement
  • Single-discipline geoscience topics not serving interdisciplinary needs
