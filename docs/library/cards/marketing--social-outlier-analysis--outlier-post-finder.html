outlier-post-finder

combined

Use when the user wants to find posts, videos, reels, shorts, tweets, or social content that overperformed versus a creator, brand, or competitor baseline. Finds outliers, explains why they worked, extracts hooks and formats, and produces a practical swipe file. Find posts that materially outperform an account's normal baseline and explain the hook, topic, format, timing, and audience response patterns worth testing.

When to use

  • find outlier posts, viral posts, top posts, best reels, best shorts, best TikToks, or best tweets
  • analyze why a creator's content is working
  • find competitor posts worth copying or learning from
  • build a swipe file from high-performing social posts
  • compare performance across a creator's recent posts

When not to use

  • raw endpoint lookup only

Skill page

Conflict

The ScrapeCreators copy is the more complete, operational procedure: it defines the data layer, per-platform endpoints, normalization rules, explicit lift formulas, outlier thresholds, enrichment steps, and a concrete report template. The gooseworks copy adds constraints and cautions the ScrapeCreators copy does not state, such as keeping format differences visible rather than blending them, explicitly flagging deleted posts, giveaways, paid boosts, and platform mismatches as limitations, and requiring brand-specific experiments in the output. Because each copy has material steps or constraints the other lacks, crowning one would hide a real alternative.
