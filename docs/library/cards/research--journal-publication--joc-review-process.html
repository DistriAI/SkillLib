joc-review-process

separate

Use to understand how the Journal of Communication (JoC) evaluates a manuscript — double-anonymous review, editorial screening for fit, external review by two or three referees, decision categories, and editor discretion. Sets expectations and shapes the paper to survive review; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • stress-test fit and anonymization before submitting
  • deciding if contribution is significant enough for JoC
  • interpreting a decision letter
  • understanding what reviewers are instructed to weigh
  • pre-empting failure modes
  • shaping the paper to pass review
  • executing a review-risk pass
  • comparing against sibling journals

When not to use

  • contacting editors
  • providing submission-ready advice without checking official source map
  • polishing format if a sibling journal owns the contribution
