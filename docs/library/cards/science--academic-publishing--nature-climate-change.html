Use when targeting Nature Climate Change or deciding whether a climate science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Nature Climate Change
  • deciding if a climate science manuscript fits the venue
  • choosing between Nature Climate Change, Nature Geoscience, and Nature Sustainability
  • understanding scope boundaries
  • applying desk-reject heuristics
  • finding credible re-routing options
  • assessing fit for physical science, impacts, mitigation, adaptation, or policy dimensions

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
