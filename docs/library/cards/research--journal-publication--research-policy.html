research-policy

separate

Use when targeting Research Policy or deciding whether an innovation-studies / science-technology-policy manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Research Policy
  • deciding if innovation-studies manuscript fits venue
  • studying R&D, technological change, innovation systems
  • studying science policy, IP, or organization of innovation
  • finding interdisciplinary policy-aware home for innovation papers
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • re-framing for STI-policy relevance

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • general firm-strategy or management papers without innovation-studies contribution
  • patent/bibliometric analysis with no theoretical contribution
  • policy advocacy unsupported by credible evidence
  • narrow disciplinary studies not speaking to interdisciplinary innovation field
