Step of the work-with-creators playbook: builds and screens a long list of creators from fans, category creators and search, and ranks finalists to vet next. Run work-with-creators instead; not for direct use.

When to use

  • building a long list of creators
  • screening creators from organic posts
  • searching for category creators
  • ranking finalists for vetting
  • identifying creators from brand mentions
  • screening public profiles
  • marking vetting depth for finalists

When not to use

  • direct use
  • ranking on follower count
