imfer-rebuttal

separate

Use when an IMF Economic Review (IMFER) R&R or decision letter arrived and you need a response-letter strategy for a dual academic/policy referee panel. Plans and drafts the response; it returns to imfer-submission for the resubmission preflight and does not re-run the original identification (imfer-identification).

When to use

  • IMFER R&R response strategy
  • Reading IMFER decision letters
  • Addressing dual academic and policy referees
  • Deciding what to concede or push back on
  • Drafting point-by-point replies
  • Re-scoping policy implications
  • Handling referee disagreements

When not to use

  • Resubmission preflight
  • Re-running original identification
