jacs-au

separate

Use when targeting JACS Au or deciding whether a chemistry manuscript fits this gold open-access, broad, high-quality sister journal of JACS. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting JACS Au
  • deciding if a chemistry manuscript fits JACS Au
  • choosing between JACS Au, JACS, and ACS Central Science
  • assessing JACS Au quality bar and breadth
  • checking JACS Au open-access expectations
  • evaluating desk-reject criteria for JACS Au
  • assessing fit for broad, high-quality chemistry advances
  • re-framing manuscripts for JACS Au

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • lower-tier outlet for JACS rejects
  • incremental or narrowly specialized work
