sensys-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-upload audit of a SenSys submission — confirming the right per-edition HotCRP site and which of the two deadlines is open, the AoE cutoff in local time, the 12/6-page double-column cap, the double-blind sweep including hardware and deployment leaks, and the resubmission-with-revision contract at the second deadline.

When to use

  • final pre-upload audit
  • confirming per-edition HotCRP site
  • checking open deadline status
  • verifying AoE cutoff in local time
  • checking 12/6-page double-column cap
  • double-blind sweep for hardware and deployment leaks
  • verifying resubmission-with-revision contract

When not to use

  • writing the paper content
  • designing experiments
  • selecting reviewers
