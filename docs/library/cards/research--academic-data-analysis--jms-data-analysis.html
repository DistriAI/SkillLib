jms-data-analysis

separate

Use when the execution and credibility of the analysis is the bottleneck for a Journal of Management Studies (JMS) manuscript — regression/SEM and robustness for quantitative work, OR coding, abduction, and trustworthiness for qualitative work. Runs and defends the analysis; it does not design the study (jms-methods) or build exhibits (jms-tables-figures).

When to use

  • regression/SEM and robustness for quantitative work
  • coding, abduction, and trustworthiness for qualitative work
  • defending analysis against endogeneity or robustness questions
  • auditing the path from data to constructs in qualitative analysis
  • testing indirect effects with bootstrapped confidence intervals
  • plotting interactions and reporting simple slopes
  • running identification strategies and robustness batteries
  • making abductive logic and coding transparency explicit

When not to use

  • designing the study
  • building exhibits
