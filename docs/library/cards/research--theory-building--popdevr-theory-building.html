popdevr-theory-building

separate

Use when building the argument of a Population and Development Review (PDR, Wiley / Population Council) manuscript into a population-and-development contribution — whether the work is an empirical study, a formal/conceptual model, or a synthetic essay that reorganizes a debate. PDR rewards a clear mechanism, a sharpened estimate, or a framework that travels, over a bare correlation. Structures the argument; it does not run analyses.

When to use

  • building the argument of a PDR manuscript
  • structuring empirical population-development studies
  • structuring formal or conceptual models
  • structuring synthetic or conceptual essays
  • defining mechanisms and scope conditions
  • addressing referee pushback on theory

When not to use

  • running analyses
