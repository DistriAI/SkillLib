cte-topic-selection

separate

Use when a 《财贸经济》 manuscript is at the topic-selection stage and the fit to fiscal / tax / finance / trade / consumption scope, the theoretical grounding, the marginal-contribution sentences, or the venue match are unsettled. Produces the contribution-sentence template and the scope-fit checklist. 本技能服务于《财贸经济》(Finance & Trade Economics, CTE)。

When to use

  • topic-selection stage for 《财贸经济》
  • checking fit to fiscal, tax, finance, trade, or consumption scope
  • evaluating theoretical grounding
  • drafting marginal-contribution sentences
  • determining venue match

When not to use

  • pure asset pricing or corporate finance topics
  • international trade or open macro topics
  • general theoretical contributions without specific fiscal/finance focus
