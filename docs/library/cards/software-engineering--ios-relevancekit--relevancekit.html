relevancekit

separate

Increase widget visibility on Apple Watch using RelevanceKit. Use when providing contextual relevance signals for watchOS widgets, declaring time-based or location-based relevance, combining multiple relevance providers, helping the system surface the right widget at the right time on watchOS 26, or routing mixed RelevanceKit/WidgetKit/HealthKit/MapKit Smart Stack scope.

When to use

  • providing contextual relevance signals for watchOS widgets
  • declaring time-based or location-based relevance
  • combining multiple relevance providers
  • helping the system surface the right widget at the right time on watchOS 26
  • routing mixed RelevanceKit/WidgetKit/HealthKit/MapKit Smart Stack scope
  • implementing timeline provider relevance
  • building relevant widgets with RelevanceConfiguration
  • managing permissions for location, fitness, and sleep clues

When not to use

  • using RelevanceKit on platforms other than watchOS
  • making strong RelevanceKit availability or behavior claims without re-checking Apple documentation
