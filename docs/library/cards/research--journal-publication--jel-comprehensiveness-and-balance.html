jel-comprehensiveness-and-balance

separate

Use when calibrating completeness vs. selectivity and ensuring fairness across schools, authors, and controversies in a Journal of Economic Literature (JEL) survey — including not over-promoting the author's own work. Audits coverage and even-handedness; it does not design the framework (jel-organizing-framework) or write prose (jel-writing-style).

When to use

  • calibrating completeness vs. selectivity
  • ensuring fairness across schools, authors, and controversies
  • auditing coverage and even-handedness
  • tiering the corpus
  • handling live controversies
  • auditing self-citation

When not to use

  • designing the framework
  • writing prose
