phg-literature-positioning

separate

Use when mapping and entering a large, contested literature for a Progress in Human Geography (PiHG) review essay — defining the corpus, its camps and debates, and where your intervention sits. Positions the essay in the field; it does not build the synthetic argument (see phg-argument-synthesis).

When to use

  • Assembling and bounding the literature
  • Mapping who argues what
  • Defining the corpus
  • Mapping camps and debates
  • Locating the entry point
  • Identifying silences and talking-past

When not to use

  • Building the synthetic argument
