Use when targeting Food Chemistry or deciding whether a food-chemistry manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the analytical-rigor and chemical-mechanism bar, method-validation expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a food-chemistry paper
  • re-framing compositional or bioactive-compound results
  • choosing between Food Chemistry, food-technology, and analytical-chemistry venues
  • Food Chemistry method-validation bar
  • desk-reject heuristics
  • food composition and structure
  • food analysis and authentication
  • food safety chemistry

When not to use

  • descriptive screening study with poorly validated methods
  • no quantification
  • no chemical-mechanism interpretation
  • replacing the journal's current author guidelines
