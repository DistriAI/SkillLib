icassp-experiments

separate

Use when designing or auditing ICASSP experiments across signal-processing modalities — matching the metric to the task law (WER, SI-SDR, PESQ/STOI, EER/minDCF, PSNR/SSIM, BER, RMSE), anchoring baselines to current strong methods and standard corpora, sweeping the operating condition, and reporting spread over runs within the four-page limit.

When to use

  • designing ICASSP experiments
  • auditing ICASSP experiments
  • matching metric to task
  • anchoring baselines to current strong methods
  • sweeping operating conditions
  • reporting spread over runs
  • ablation design
  • experiment readiness assessment

When not to use

  • topping a leaderboard by any margin
  • using novel or convenient metrics
  • using weak or dated references
  • reporting single-condition numbers without sweep
