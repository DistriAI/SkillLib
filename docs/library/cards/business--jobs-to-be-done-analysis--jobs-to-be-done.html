Conflict

The three copies overlap on the core JTBD concept (functional, emotional, and social dimensions) but each contains material steps and constraints the others lack. wondelai adds a scoring rubric, the four Forces of Progress (Push, Pull, Anxiety, Habit), and copy patterns. deanpeters adds a structured Pains and Gains taxonomy, anti-patterns, and a step-by-step application process. Owl-Listener adds a specific 8-stage job lifecycle (define, locate, prepare, etc.) and outcome expectations. Because no single copy contains all these distinct frameworks (Forces, Pains/Gains, Job Stages), crowning one would hide real alternatives.
