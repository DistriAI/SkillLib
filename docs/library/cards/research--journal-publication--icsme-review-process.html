icsme-review-process

separate

Use when reasoning about how an IEEE ICSME research submission is evaluated, covering double-anonymous review, the early-decision cut that issues Accept/Reject before the rebuttal, the "Response Recommended" author-response period, the single-round accept/reject model with no Major Revision, and how ICSME's process differs from FSE's journal-style round and ICSE's cycles.

When to use

  • reasoning about ICSME evaluation
  • understanding double-anonymous review
  • interpreting early-decision cut
  • planning for Response Recommended period
  • comparing ICSME to FSE and ICSE
  • reading review packets
  • identifying author leverage points

When not to use

  • planning for Major Revision rounds
  • submitting to FSE or ICSE
  • appealing after rejection
  • promising new experiments in rebuttal
