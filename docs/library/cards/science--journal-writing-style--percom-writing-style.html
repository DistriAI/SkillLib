percom-writing-style

separate

Use when revising an IEEE PerCom paper for a pervasive-computing contribution on the first page, cross-subject claims stated precisely, a limitations section that argues rather than recites, evidence proportional to the claim, double-blind wording, and disciplined use of the tight IEEEtran 9-page budget.

When to use

  • revising an IEEE PerCom paper
  • stating pervasive-computing contribution on first page
  • ensuring cross-subject claims are precise
  • arguing limitations rather than reciting them
  • matching evidence to claim proportionality
  • maintaining double-blind wording
  • managing the tight IEEEtran 9-page budget

When not to use

  • formatting afterthoughts
  • shrinking evaluation or limitations to fit page count
  • touching the IEEEtran template
