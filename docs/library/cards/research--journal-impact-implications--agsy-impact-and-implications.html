agsy-impact-and-implications

separate

Use when articulating why an Agricultural Systems (AgSy) result matters — its relevance for farm design, management, decision support, or policy. AgSy values systems analysis that informs a decision, so this is what separates an AgSy paper from a methods demo. It frames implications honestly within the model's scope; it does not over-claim or invent impact.

When to use

  • Writing discussion, implications, or conclusion sections
  • Framing recommendations for farmers, advisers, or policymakers
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on academic tone or over-claims
  • Connecting systems results to decision relevance
  • Scoping claims to modelled system boundaries and conditions

When not to use

  • Inventing impact or over-claiming policy reach
  • Recommending a single optimal option without trade-offs
  • Generalizing beyond modelled system, scales, or conditions
  • Making confident policy claims from high-uncertainty outputs
