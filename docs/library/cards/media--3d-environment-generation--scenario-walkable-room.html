scenario-walkable-room

separate

Use when one interior photo, uploaded or generated on Scenario, should become a room a visitor walks into in a web browser and plays with: props lifted out of the photo and rebuilt as 3D objects to grab, throw, and hear land, inside a Gaussian splat world, or restyled variants of one room. Keywords: image-blaster, walkable room, photo to 3D room, clean plate, object cut-out, splat world, physics props, grab and throw, impact sounds, heightfield collision.
