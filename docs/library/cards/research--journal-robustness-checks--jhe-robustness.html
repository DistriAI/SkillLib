jhe-robustness

separate

Use when a Journal of Health Economics (JHE) result must be shown stable to specification, sample, inference, and mechanism threats before submission or in response to referees. Organizes robustness by identifying threat; it does not establish the design (jhe-identification) or build exhibits.

When to use

  • showing result stability to specification threats
  • addressing sample window sensitivity
  • handling inference threats with few clusters
  • separating mechanism from competing explanations
  • mapping robustness checks to specific threats
  • reporting point-estimate stability
  • handling skewed or zero-inflated health spending
  • adjusting for multiple outcomes or subgroups

When not to use

  • establishing the design (jhe-identification)
  • building exhibits
