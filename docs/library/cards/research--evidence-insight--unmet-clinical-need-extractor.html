unmet-clinical-need-extractor

separate

Extracts concrete unmet clinical needs from guidelines, reviews, real-world studies, and clinical-practice evidence. Use this skill when a user wants to turn broad medical research value into specific clinical pain points such as weak early detection, poor risk stratification, treatment-response heterogeneity, monitoring gaps, diagnostic delay, undertreatment, overtreatment, or implementation failure. Always ground unmet-need claims in retrieved evidence and distinguish true care gaps from generic statements of importance.

When to use

  • identifying unmet clinical needs in a disease area
  • locating where current care fails or underperforms
  • classifying unmet needs by type and strength
  • distinguishing true clinical pain points from generic importance language
  • translating unmet-need maps into research-value framing
  • identifying needs across screening, diagnosis, treatment, and monitoring
  • prioritizing the most clinically meaningful need

When not to use

  • patient-specific treatment recommendations
  • broad disease summaries without a request to identify unmet need
  • product positioning or investment advice unrelated to clinical unmet need
  • unsupported claims that a disease area has huge unmet need
  • calling every disease burden statement an unmet clinical need
  • confusing scientific curiosity with clinically meaningful pain points
  • treating biomarker enthusiasm as proof of unmet need
  • presenting vague better outcomes are needed language as a specific need map
