Use when targeting Energy & Environmental Science (EES, RSC) or deciding whether an energy/environment manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Energy & Environmental Science (EES, RSC)
  • deciding whether an energy/environment manuscript fits this venue
  • energy materials/device paper with clear environmental co-benefit
  • green chemistry, CO₂ capture, or water-treatment paper connected to energy systems
  • comparing EES, Joule, and Nature Energy
  • understanding EES's distinct scope and evidence bar

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
