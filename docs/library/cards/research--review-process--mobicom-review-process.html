mobicom-review-process

separate

Use when reasoning about where a MobiCom paper sits in the two-round review process — the early-reject cut after round one, the released reviews and rebuttal window after round two, the accept / one-shot revision / reject outcome space, reviewer continuity across a revision, and how the rolling-deadline structure shapes what to do next.

When to use

  • reasoning about MobiCom review process stages
  • understanding early-reject cut after round one
  • planning for released reviews and rebuttal window
  • evaluating accept, one-shot revision, or reject outcomes
  • assessing reviewer continuity across revisions
  • determining next steps based on rolling-deadline structure

When not to use

  • writing camera-ready artifacts
  • drafting specific author responses
  • planning specific experiments for revisions
