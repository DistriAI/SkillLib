osdi-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or revising an OSDI paper's prose and structure — building the pain → abstraction → mechanism → measurement narrative, fitting it into the 12-page reviewed body with no appendix escape hatch, and writing for a PC told to down-rank padded papers and given no rebuttal to clarify with.

When to use

  • drafting OSDI paper prose
  • revising OSDI paper structure
  • building pain to abstraction narrative
  • fitting content into 12-page body
  • writing for OSDI program committee
  • structuring abstract in seven sentences
  • executing revision passes

When not to use

  • using appendices in submission
  • padding papers to fill 12 pages
  • using geometry or font tricks to save space
