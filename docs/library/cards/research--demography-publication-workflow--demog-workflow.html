demog-workflow

separate

Use as the entry point for any Demography (PAA / Duke University Press) manuscript. Routes to the right Demography sub-skill based on where you are in the lifecycle and which article type (Research Article, Research Note, Commentary) fits. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new Demography paper
  • Deciding which article type fits
  • Routing to the right Demography sub-skill
  • Handling decision letters
  • Confirming population-science question fit

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Treating Demography like a generic applied-stats venue
  • Forcing one demographic method when another is required
  • Calling the editorial-management fee an open-access charge
