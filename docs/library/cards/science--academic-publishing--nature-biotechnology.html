nature-biotechnology

separate

Use when targeting Nature Biotechnology or deciding whether a biotechnology/enabling-methods manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Nature Biotechnology
  • deciding if a biotechnology manuscript fits the venue
  • assessing venue fit against Nature Methods, Nature Medicine, or Cell Chemical Biology
  • assessing significance of a biotechnological platform with in vivo proof-of-concept
  • identifying desk-reject risks and re-routing options

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
