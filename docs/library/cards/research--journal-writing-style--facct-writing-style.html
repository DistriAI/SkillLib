facct-writing-style

separate

Use when revising an ACM FAccT paper so that a fairness/accountability/transparency contribution is legible on the first page to a mixed CS+law+social-science reviewer pool, claims are scoped to who is affected, limitations and harms are argued rather than recited, the interdisciplinary framing is native, and the acmart page budget and endmatter statements are used well.

When to use

  • Revising ACM FAccT papers
  • Making FAccT contributions legible to mixed reviewers
  • Scoping claims to specific populations
  • Arguing limitations and adverse impacts
  • Integrating interdisciplinary framing
  • Managing acmart page budget
  • Ensuring mutual anonymity in submissions

When not to use

  • Writing non-FAccT papers
  • General academic writing advice
  • Technical model implementation
  • Legal advice
  • Social science methodology design
