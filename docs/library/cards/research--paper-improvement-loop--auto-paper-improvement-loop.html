Conflict

Both copies describe the same two-round review→fix→recompile loop, but they disagree on the reviewer model (gpt-6-astra vs gpt-5.4) and on a core loop mechanic: the wanshuiyin copy mandates a fresh reviewer thread each round (REVIEWER_BIAS_GUARD, no codex-reply) and adds style-ref/edit-whitelist options, while the brycewang copy explicitly uses codex-reply with the saved threadId in Round 2 and adds state-persistence recovery. Crowning one would hide a real alternative procedure.
