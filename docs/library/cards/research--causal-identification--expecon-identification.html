expecon-identification

separate

Use when the credibility of an Experimental Economics (ExpEcon) manuscript rests on experimental control — incentive compatibility, randomization, the no-deception gate, and clean treatment contrasts. Stress-tests design-based identification before exhibits are finalized; it does not draft prose.

When to use

  • verifying incentive compatibility
  • checking the no-deception gate
  • auditing randomization and control
  • stress-testing treatment contrasts
  • identifying session-level confounds
  • checking comprehension and order effects
  • defining the estimand and inference unit

When not to use

  • drafting prose
