dbs-slowisfast

separate

dontbesilent 慢就是快。帮创业者找到看起来更慢但长期更快的方法，用摩擦建造资产。 触发方式：/dbs-slowisfast、/慢就是快、「有没有更慢的方法」「我是不是太快了」 Slow-is-fast diagnosis. Help entrepreneurs find seemingly slower methods that build assets through friction. Trigger: /dbs-slowisfast, "is there a slower way", "am I going too fast"

When to use

  • Diagnosing current methods for friction and asset creation
  • Recommending slower alternatives for judgment-intensive tasks
  • Auditing whether a method produces compounding assets
  • Identifying which parts of a workflow should remain fast

When not to use

  • Tasks that are mechanical or execution-based
  • Rationalizing inaction or procrastination as 'slow is fast'
  • General life advice or motivational speaking
  • Automating all processes without distinction
