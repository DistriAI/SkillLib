car-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Contemporary Accounting Research (CAR) manuscript within the accounting literature — identifying the conversation it joins, distinguishing it from closely related accounting/finance/economics work, and disclosing overlap with the authors' own related papers as CAR's author declaration requires.

When to use

  • Positioning a CAR manuscript within the accounting literature
  • Identifying the accounting conversation the paper joins
  • Distinguishing the paper from closely related accounting, finance, or economics work
  • Disclosing overlap with the authors' own related papers
  • Defending the accounting locus of the contribution
  • Mapping incumbent conversation and unresolved tension
  • Testing whether the manuscript addresses accounting reviewers
  • Comparing against neighboring outlets like TAR, JAR, and JAE

When not to use

  • Gap-spotting without joining a debate
  • Citation dumping without explaining contributions
  • Hidden overlap with authors' concurrent papers
