evidence-level-ranker

separate

Ranks papers by evidence family, methodological quality tier, validation depth, and claim discipline; assigns anchor, context-setting, mechanistic support, or caution citation roles; prevents prestige-based or design-label-based ranking errors.

When to use

  • rank papers by evidence strength
  • assign citation priority
  • compare reviews and observational studies
  • identify anchor citations
  • prioritize citations for manuscripts
  • sort studies by methodological strength

When not to use

  • write a literature review
  • retrieve papers
  • summarize clinical guidelines
  • make treatment recommendations
  • clinical decision-making
