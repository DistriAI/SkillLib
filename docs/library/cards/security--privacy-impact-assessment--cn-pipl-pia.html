cn-pipl-pia

authentic

Use when asked 个人信息保护影响评估怎么做, 写一份 PIA, 处理敏感个人信息要评估吗, 自动化决策评估, PIPL 第五十五条, or assess a processing activity under China's Personal Information Protection Law. Produces a personal information protection impact assessment (个人信息保护影响评估) covering the PIPL Article 55 triggers, the Article 56 questions (lawful, legitimate and necessary; impact and risk; adequacy of protection), a risk register with mitigations and owners, and a record kept for at least three years.

When to use

  • assess a processing activity under PIPL
  • write a PIA
  • assess sensitive personal information processing
  • assess automated decision-making
  • assess cross-border data transfer
  • assess entrusting or sharing data
  • assess publication of personal information

When not to use

  • legal advice
  • assessing after launch
