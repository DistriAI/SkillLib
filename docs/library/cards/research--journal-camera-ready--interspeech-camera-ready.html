interspeech-camera-ready

separate

Use when an INTERSPEECH paper is accepted and the camera-ready, registration, and presentation chain begins — de-anonymizing for the ISCA Archive, restoring acknowledgments on the references page, meeting the author-registration requirement for proceedings inclusion, DOI metadata, and oral/poster preparation for the conference week.

When to use

  • De-anonymizing accepted papers
  • Restoring acknowledgments and funding
  • Author registration for proceedings inclusion
  • ISCA Archive metadata hygiene
  • DOI metadata management
  • Oral and poster presentation preparation
  • Camera-ready PDF validation

When not to use

  • New experiments that alter claims
  • Changing title or author list without chair approval
