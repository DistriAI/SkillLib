Audit figures, tables, captions, cross-references, and statistical notes.

When to use

  • Auditing figures and tables
  • Checking captions and notes
  • Verifying cross-references
  • Checking statistical notes
  • Auditing SI and replication linkage
  • Checking accessibility and production quality

When not to use

  • Figure design and production guidance during drafting
  • Table design
  • Visual hallucination or inferring exact values by eyeballing plots
