icalp-writing-style

separate

Use when shaping the exposition of an ICALP (EATCS) theory paper — stating the model before the theorem, putting a legible theorem statement and its improvement over prior bounds on the first page, structuring the 15-page body around the argument while full proofs live in the appendix/full version, and meeting the correctness-and-clarity bar of Track A and Track B referees.

When to use

  • stating the model before the theorem
  • putting a legible theorem statement on the first page
  • structuring the 15-page body around the argument
  • meeting the correctness-and-clarity bar of Track A and Track B referees
  • positioning against a named prior bound
  • naming the barrier and the technique
  • pairing an algorithm with a matching lower bound

When not to use

  • experimental-algorithms voice
  • benchmark framing
  • tasks without a theorem behind them
