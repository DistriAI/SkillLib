misq-theory-development

separate

Use when building the theoretical engine of a MIS Quarterly manuscript — a behavioral mechanism and hypotheses, a design theory and testable design propositions for a design-science artifact, an economic mechanism for an economics-of-IS study, or an organizational process theory. Adapts the form of "theory" to the paper's IS tradition; does not run the analysis (misq-data-analysis) or frame the contribution (misq-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • building the theoretical engine of a MIS Quarterly manuscript
  • developing a behavioral mechanism and hypotheses
  • developing a design theory and testable design propositions
  • developing an economic mechanism for an economics-of-IS study
  • developing an organizational process theory
  • deriving hypotheses or design propositions before looking at results
  • making boundary conditions explicit

When not to use

  • running the analysis
  • framing the contribution
