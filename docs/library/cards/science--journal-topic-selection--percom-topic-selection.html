percom-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a pervasive-computing project belongs at IEEE PerCom or should be routed to ACM UbiComp/IMWUT, MobiCom, MobiSys, SenSys, or IPSN, and when distinguishing PerCom's human-centric ubicomp focus from networked-sensor-systems venues by contribution shape, the human-centricity test, and the IEEE-conference vs. IMWUT-journal model.

When to use

  • deciding venue for pervasive-computing projects
  • distinguishing PerCom from UbiComp/IMWUT
  • routing to MobiCom, MobiSys, SenSys, or IPSN
  • applying human-centricity and model-swap tests
  • evaluating contribution shapes for PerCom
  • comparing IEEE conference vs IMWUT journal models
  • checking evidence maturity for submission

When not to use

  • drafting the paper
  • writing skills
  • percom-workflow execution
