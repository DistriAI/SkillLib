chinese-journal-of-management-science

separate

Use when targeting 《中国管理科学》(Chinese Journal of Management Science — 中国优选法统筹法与经济数学研究会与中科院科技战略咨询研究院主办的管理科学与工程类月刊, 中科院主管) or deciding whether a Chinese management-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/citation house style, fee note, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《中国管理科学》
  • deciding if a manuscript fits the venue
  • rewriting a manuscript for the journal's narrative
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking abstract and citation house style
  • verifying official submission requirements

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to other journals like 《中国软科学》 or 《管理学报》
  • manuscripts with only regression or questionnaire without model contribution
  • macro policy effect analysis without management science model
