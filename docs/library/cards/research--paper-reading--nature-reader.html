nature-reader

separate

Create source-grounded Chinese-English paper readers with aligned text, figures, tables, and equations. Use for 全文翻译、中英文对照、论文精读 or source-linked questions about a paper; respect a requested excerpt or question without generating a full reader.

When to use

  • full-paper translation
  • Chinese-English comparison
  • paper close reading
  • source-linked questions
  • excerpt translation
  • bilingual reader creation

When not to use

  • generating a full reader for a simple question
  • requiring a full source map before answering a question
  • summary mode
