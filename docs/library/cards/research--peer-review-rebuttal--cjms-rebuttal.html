cjms-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to referee reports from 《中国管理科学》 (Chinese Journal of Management Science) — classifying comments, planning supplementary experiments or derivations, and drafting the point-by-point 修改说明. Works after the manuscript has actually been revised; it never fabricates changes.

When to use

  • Classifying referee comments
  • Planning supplementary experiments or derivations
  • Drafting point-by-point 修改说明
  • Handling conflicting referee opinions
  • Formatting rebuttal responses
  • Managing revision deadlines and uploads

When not to use

  • Fabricating changes
  • Responding before manuscript revision is complete
  • Guessing reviewer identity to tailor responses
  • Ignoring unfavorable experimental results
