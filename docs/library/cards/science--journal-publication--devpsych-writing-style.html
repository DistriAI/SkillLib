devpsych-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing a Developmental Psychology (APA) manuscript to fit its house style — APA 7th edition, JARS-compliant reporting, the manuscript length tier (brief report to multi-study/longitudinal report), the abstract, and the required Public Significance Statement. Tightens prose and format; it does not invent content.

When to use

  • Drafting Introduction/Discussion
  • Final polish
  • Sizing manuscript to length tier
  • Writing abstract
  • Writing Public Significance Statement
  • Aligning statistics to JARS
  • Aligning citations to APA 7th edition

When not to use

  • Inventing content
