dac-review-process

separate

Use when reasoning about how an ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference (DAC) Research Manuscript is evaluated, covering double-blind TPC review, the novelty-plus-QoR decision criteria, program-committee discussion, the accept/reject (no major-revision) outcome, the ~20-25% selectivity, and how DAC's industry-facing, single-shot process differs from the architecture venues' rebuttal-and-revision cycles.

When to use

  • reasoning about DAC Research Manuscript evaluation
  • understanding double-blind TPC review
  • analyzing novelty-plus-QoR decision criteria
  • modeling program-committee discussion
  • interpreting accept/reject outcomes
  • comparing DAC process to architecture and software venues
  • identifying author leverage points in DAC submission

When not to use

  • tasks not related to DAC Research Manuscript review
  • venues with standing author rebuttal periods
  • journal-style Major Revision processes
  • non-EDA research evaluation
