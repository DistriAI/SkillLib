jet-review-process

separate

Use when interpreting the Journal of Economic Theory (JET) review pipeline — editor desk-screening, single-anonymized refereeing, at least two reviewers, editor decisions, and the generative-AI bar on referees.

When to use

  • understanding JET evaluation of theory submissions
  • deciding how defensively to write proofs
  • reading decisions against JET's actual process
  • identifying desk-reject patterns for theory submissions
  • performing a self-referee pass before submitting

When not to use

  • double-blind review processes
  • pure-mathematics outlet submissions
  • survey or synthesis without a new result
