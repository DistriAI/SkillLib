jru-rebuttal

separate

Use when a Journal of Risk and Uncertainty (JRU) decision letter has arrived and you need a response-letter strategy. Triages referee reports into a revision plan and reply; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • JRU decision letter response strategy
  • Triaging referee reports into a revision plan
  • Reconciling disagreeing referees
  • Correcting referee misreadings
  • Scoping feasible requested analyses
  • Building a point-by-point response letter
  • Handling recurring JRU specialist objections
  • Structuring a response document for editors

When not to use

  • Inventing evidence
  • Inventing citations
  • Performing actual robustness or identification work
  • Arguing with referees instead of fixing or conceding
