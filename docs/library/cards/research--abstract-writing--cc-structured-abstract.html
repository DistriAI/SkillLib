cc-structured-abstract

separate

Use when writing the Cancer Cell (Cell Press) front matter — the Summary, eTOC blurb, Highlights, and graphical abstract. It crafts these short items for accuracy and impact; it does not write Results or calibrate statistics.

When to use

  • Writing the Cancer Cell Summary
  • Creating Highlights
  • Writing the eTOC blurb
  • Building or refining the graphical abstract
  • Second-pass capability check for abstracts

When not to use

  • Writing Results
  • Calibrating statistics
