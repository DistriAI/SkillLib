nature-electronics

separate

Use when targeting Nature Electronics or deciding whether an electronic-devices / circuits / systems manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the Nature-style significance and field-advancing bar, device-and-system measurement rigor, house style, the device-vs-materials-vs-systems routing, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • Nature Electronics fit and framing check
  • Re-framing metric-improvement reports into field-advancing narratives
  • Choosing between Nature Electronics and other venues
  • Checking Nature Electronics significance bar
  • Applying desk-reject heuristics
  • Device physics and emerging devices with new mechanisms
  • Integrated circuits and computing hardware with broad significance
  • System-level demonstrations bridging device physics to integrated capability

When not to use

  • Incremental device metric improvements without conceptual advance
  • Process tweaks with no broader consequence
  • Simulation-only device claims
  • Replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
