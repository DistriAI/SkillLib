molcell-writing

separate

Use to structure a Molecular Cell Article and hold its length — Summary, Introduction, Results, Discussion, STAR Methods, figure legends, references — with the ~7,000-word main-text and ~7-display-item budgets, and Results subheadings that carry the single-mechanism argument.

When to use

  • structuring a Molecular Cell Article
  • holding main text length to ~7,000 words
  • managing ~7 display item budgets
  • writing Results subheadings for single-mechanism argument
  • formatting STAR Methods
  • writing concise Discussion sections
  • structuring Short Articles to ~4,000 words

When not to use

  • writing free-text Methods sections
  • re-listing results in the Discussion
  • ordering Results by technique chronology
  • padding main text with arc-irrelevant experiments
