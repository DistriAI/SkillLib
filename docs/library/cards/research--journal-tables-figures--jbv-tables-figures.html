jbv-tables-figures

separate

Use when building or cleaning the exhibits for a Journal of Business Venturing (JBV) manuscript — venture descriptive/correlation tables, survival curves, interaction plots, process models, and qualitative data structures in Elsevier house style. Late-stage polish; do not invoke while the entrepreneurial mechanism or identification is still unsettled (use jbv-theory-development / jbv-data-analysis first).

When to use

  • building or cleaning JBV exhibits
  • venture descriptive/correlation tables
  • survival curves
  • interaction plots
  • process models
  • qualitative data structures
  • Elsevier house style formatting
  • late-stage polish of exhibits

When not to use

  • when entrepreneurial mechanism is unsettled
  • when identification is unsettled
  • jbv-theory-development
  • jbv-data-analysis
