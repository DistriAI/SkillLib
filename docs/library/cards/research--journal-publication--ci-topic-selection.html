ci-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a project fits Critical Inquiry (CI) and which format to target. CI is the leading interdisciplinary journal of criticism and theory in the arts and humanities, associated with no single school and tied to no single discipline; the test is a theoretical intervention with stakes beyond one field, not subfield novelty or a competent close reading alone. Helps frame the project; it does not gather sources.

When to use

  • Choosing among possible projects or framings for a CI essay
  • Deciding between a full Article and a Critical Response
  • Pressure-testing fit before investing in a project
  • Evaluating if a project is theory or just an able reading
  • Assessing interdisciplinary stakes for a theoretical intervention

When not to use

  • Gathering sources
  • Writing the full argument
  • Summarizing a published CI piece
  • Contesting one prior CI essay as a stretched Article
