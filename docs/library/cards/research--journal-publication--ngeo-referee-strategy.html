ngeo-referee-strategy

separate

Use when choosing suggested and excluded referees for a Nature Geoscience submission and pre-empting the objections Earth-science referees are likely to raise. Plans referee handling; does not write the manuscript or the rebuttal.

When to use

  • Choosing suggested referees
  • Choosing excluded referees
  • Anticipating referee objections
  • Coordinating cover-letter referee section
  • Rethinking reviewers after a decline

When not to use

  • Writing the manuscript
  • Writing the rebuttal
