jimf-empirical-design

separate

Use when the cross-country/time-series data construction, sample, frequency, or measurement of a Journal of International Money and Finance (JIMF) manuscript is the bottleneck. Builds the international dataset and measurement; it does not establish causality (jimf-identification) or run robustness (jimf-robustness).
