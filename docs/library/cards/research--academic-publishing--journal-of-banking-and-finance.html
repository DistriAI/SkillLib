journal-of-banking-and-finance

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Banking and Finance (JBF) or deciding whether an applied banking / finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Banking and Finance
  • deciding if applied banking/finance manuscript fits JBF
  • banking, risk, regulation, credit, markets, asset pricing, corporate finance, international finance
  • assessing method and evidence bar for broad field journal
  • checking house style and structure
  • identifying desk-reject risks
  • re-routing to alternative venues

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • elite-tier or top-3 venue submissions
  • specialist banking/intermediation mechanism papers
  • corporate-finance core papers
  • microstructure papers
  • international finance papers
  • empirical-methods focus papers
  • macro/monetary papers
