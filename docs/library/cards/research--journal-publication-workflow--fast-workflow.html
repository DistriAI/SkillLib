fast-workflow

separate

Use when planning a USENIX FAST project timeline from venue fit through choosing a Spring or Fall deadline, double-blind submission, the author-response period, shepherding or a one-shot revision, artifact evaluation, and the open-access camera-ready, with backward-planning offsets tuned to storage evaluation and honest handling of the two-deadline cycle.

When to use

  • planning a USENIX FAST project timeline
  • choosing a Spring or Fall deadline
  • double-blind submission
  • author-response period
  • shepherding or a one-shot revision
  • artifact evaluation
  • open-access camera-ready
  • backward-planning offsets tuned to storage evaluation

When not to use

  • planning for a plain accept/reject conference
  • planning for a conference with a standing editor-in-chief
  • planning for a conference with an article-processing charge
