jet-identification-strategy

separate

Use when hardening the assumptions, results, and proof architecture of a Journal of Economic Theory (JET) paper — the theory analogue of an identification strategy. Make every assumption explicit and load-bearing, architect the proof so two expert referees can verify it, and defend the generality/tractability trade-off; for JET, "identification" means assumption sets and proof exposition, not a causal design.

When to use

  • hardening assumptions, results, and proof architecture
  • making assumptions explicit and load-bearing
  • architecting proofs for expert verification
  • defending generality/tractability trade-off
  • addressing necessity of assumptions with counterexamples
  • structuring proof spines with lemmas and roadmaps
  • separating maintained structure, economic assumptions, and technical regularity

When not to use

  • causal design identification
  • data design identification
