agricultural-and-forest-meteorology

separate

Use when targeting Agricultural and Forest Meteorology or deciding whether a land–atmosphere manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the land–atmosphere-process and flux-measurement bar, micrometeorological evidence expectations, Elsevier house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a land–atmosphere paper
  • re-framing crop, forest, or ecosystem study around energy/water/carbon exchange
  • choosing between Agricultural and Forest Meteorology and other venues
  • flux-measurement and micrometeorological evidence expectations
  • land–atmosphere exchange of energy, water, CO2, CH4, N2O
  • micrometeorology and surface-layer processes
  • eddy-covariance, chamber, and remote-sensing flux measurement
  • phenology and its climatic drivers

When not to use

  • pure crop-agronomy study with no atmospheric coupling
  • pure climate-modeling paper with no surface-process component
