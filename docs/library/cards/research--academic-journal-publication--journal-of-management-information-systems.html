journal-of-management-information-systems

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Management Information Systems (JMIS) or deciding whether an information-systems management / IT-business-value / IS-strategy manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Management Information Systems (JMIS)
  • deciding whether an IS management manuscript fits JMIS
  • evaluating IT-business-value or IS-strategy manuscripts
  • checking JMIS desk-reject risks
  • finding Basket or INFORMS alternative venues
  • assessing managerial relevance of IS research

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • technical or analytics papers with no IS theoretical contribution
  • papers that are really CS, operations, or general management
  • behavioral papers with weak constructs or unaddressed common-method bias
  • econometric papers with endogeneity and no credible source of variation
