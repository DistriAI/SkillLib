jeem-literature-positioning

separate

Use when the contribution of a Journal of Environmental Economics and Management (JEEM) manuscript relative to the environmental-economics frontier and its sibling journals is fuzzy or undersold. Stakes the claim against the field; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • Positioning JEEM manuscript contributions
  • Distinguishing JEEM from sibling journals
  • Identifying environmental-economic contributions
  • Avoiding incremental green replication traps
  • Building literature positioning arguments
  • Naming specific prior papers to argue with
  • Stating marginal contribution to environmental economics

When not to use

  • Inventing evidence
  • Inventing citations
  • General applied micro positioning
  • Biophysical accounting
  • Climate-science or ecology literature positioning
