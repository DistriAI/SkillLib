industrial-economics-research

separate

Use when targeting 《产业经济研究》(Industrial Economics Research — 江苏省教育厅主管、南京财经大学主办的产业经济专业双月刊, 2002 年创刊, CSSCI) or deciding whether a Chinese industrial-econ manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/keyword house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《产业经济研究》
  • deciding if a Chinese industrial-econ manuscript fits this venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese economics manuscript for this journal
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks
  • checking journal fit for industrial organization topics
  • checking journal fit for competition policy topics
  • checking journal fit for market structure topics
  • checking journal fit for enterprise innovation and productivity

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to 《工程管理科技前沿》
  • submitting to 《治理研究》
  • submitting to 《经济问题探索》
  • submitting to 《国际经济评论》
  • submitting to china-industrial-economics
  • submitting to business-management-journal
