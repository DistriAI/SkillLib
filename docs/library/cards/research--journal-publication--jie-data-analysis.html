jie-data-analysis

separate

Use when building or auditing the empirical analysis for a Journal of International Economics (JIE) manuscript — constructing bilateral trade panels, estimating PPML gravity, running open-economy panels and local projections, calibrating/estimating structural models, and producing the robustness suite international-economics referees expect. Strengthens the analysis; it does not fabricate results.

When to use

  • constructing bilateral trade panels
  • estimating PPML gravity
  • running open-economy panels and local projections
  • calibrating/estimating structural models
  • producing the robustness suite
  • assembling cross-country macro/finance panels
  • choosing gravity estimator and fixed-effect structure
  • disciplining structural open-economy or trade models

When not to use

  • fabricating results
