arsoc-literature-synthesis

separate

Use when systematically gathering, reading, and synthesizing a large body of sociological research for an Annual Review of Sociology (ARSoc) review — coverage discipline and avoiding citation gaps across theoretical traditions. Builds the evidence corpus and synthesis notes; it does not impose the analytical spine (arsoc-organizing-framework) or write prose (arsoc-writing-style).

When to use

  • gathering sociological research
  • reading a subfield thoroughly
  • synthesizing a large body of research
  • avoiding citation gaps
  • building the evidence corpus
  • creating synthesis notes
  • weighing and comparing findings
  • reconciling study findings

When not to use

  • imposing the analytical spine
  • writing prose
  • re-analyzing primary studies
  • correcting primary studies
  • re-estimating primary studies
