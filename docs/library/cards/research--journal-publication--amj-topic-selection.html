amj-topic-selection

separate

Use when scoping or sanity-checking a research question for an Academy of Management Journal (AMJ) manuscript — fit, theory-drivenness, and feasibility. Decides whether the idea can earn a theoretical contribution; it does not build the theory itself (use amj-theory-development) or position the literature (amj-literature-positioning).

When to use

  • scoping a research question for AMJ
  • sanity-checking AMJ fit
  • evaluating theory-drivenness
  • assessing feasibility of empirical design
  • choosing divisional home
  • pressure-testing fit before data collection

When not to use

  • building the theory itself
  • positioning the literature
  • purely conceptual ideas
  • pure methods or measurement papers
