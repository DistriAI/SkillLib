cte-literature-review

separate

Use when drafting or revising the literature-review section for a 《财贸经济》 manuscript, when the Chinese / English reference balance is off, or when canonical fiscal / financial / trade theory references are missing. 本技能服务于《财贸经济》(Finance & Trade Economics, CTE)。

When to use

  • drafting literature-review section for 《财贸经济》
  • revising literature-review section for 《财贸经济》
  • balancing Chinese and English references
  • adding canonical fiscal, financial, or trade theory references
  • ensuring dialogue-style literature review
  • checking reference ratios and recency

When not to use

  • writing policy background introductions instead of academic dialogue
  • listing references by author order without thematic grouping
  • citing only international literature without domestic Chinese finance and trade authorities
