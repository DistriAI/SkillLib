Remove signs of AI-generated writing from academic medical papers. Use when editing or reviewing manuscripts to make them sound more natural and professionally written. Based on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" guide, adapted for medical literature.

When to use

  • editing academic medical papers
  • reviewing manuscripts
  • removing AI-generated text patterns
  • making text sound natural
  • improving professional writing style

When not to use

  • inventing unsupported factual claims
  • inserting figures or statistics from model knowledge
  • removing intentional blank lines at section boundaries
  • merging short claim sentences to satisfy rhythm checks
