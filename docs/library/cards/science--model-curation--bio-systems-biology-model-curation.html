bio-systems-biology-model-curation

Validate, gap-fill, and curate genome-scale metabolic models using memote for quality scores and COBRApy for manual curation. Ensure models meet SBML standards and produce biologically meaningful predictions.

When to use

  • improving draft models
  • preparing models for publication
  • validating genome-scale metabolic models
  • gap-filling metabolic models
  • curating metabolic models
  • checking mass and charge balance
  • fixing gene-protein-reaction rules
  • identifying dead-end metabolites
