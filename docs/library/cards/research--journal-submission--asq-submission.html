asq-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight for an Administrative Science Quarterly (ASQ) manuscript — fit, anonymization, format, exhibits, references, and the editorial-system submission. Checks readiness; it does not write or revise the manuscript.

When to use

  • ASQ manuscript pre-submission preflight
  • Checking ASQ fit and theoretical contribution
  • Verifying ASQ anonymization and double-blind compliance
  • Checking ASQ format and length requirements
  • Reviewing ASQ exhibits and evidence completeness
  • Reconciling ASQ references and citations
  • Preparing ASQ editorial system metadata and cover letter

When not to use

  • Writing or revising the manuscript
  • Resubmitting papers previously rejected by ASQ
  • Submitting thin-theory, sophisticated-method papers
