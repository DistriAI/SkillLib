iros-submission

separate

Use when auditing an IROS paper for the PaperPlaza upload — author PINs and metadata, the content-page limit with references counted in, IEEE two-column format, the 2026 double-anonymous rule, the 60-second/10 MB video attachment and its own deadline, dual-submission traps shared with ICRA and RA-L, and return-without-review triggers.

When to use

  • Auditing IROS paper for PaperPlaza upload
  • Checking author PINs and metadata
  • Verifying content-page limit with references
  • Checking IEEE two-column format
  • Auditing 2026 double-anonymous rule
  • Verifying 60-second/10 MB video attachment
  • Checking dual-submission traps with ICRA and RA-L
  • Identifying return-without-review triggers

When not to use

  • Submitting to ICRA
  • Submitting to RA-L
  • Submitting to CASE
