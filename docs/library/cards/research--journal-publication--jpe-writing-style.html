jpe-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing the prose of a Journal of Political Economy (JPE) manuscript into its spare, analytical, economics-first register. Tightens voice, structure, and citation/format house style; it does not fix the economic argument itself (see jpe-theory-model / jpe-identification).

When to use

  • Polishing JPE manuscript prose
  • Tightening voice and structure
  • Fixing citation and format house style
  • Late polish stage editing
  • Converting to Chicago author-date citations
  • Removing throat-clearing and hedging
  • Ensuring economics-first writing

When not to use

  • Fixing the economic argument
  • jpe-theory-model tasks
  • jpe-identification tasks
