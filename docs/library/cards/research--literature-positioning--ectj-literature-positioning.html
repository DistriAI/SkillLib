ectj-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a The Econometrics Journal (EctJ) paper against econometric theory, applied econometrics, statistics, and machine-learning literatures while keeping the leading-case contribution compact under the roughly 20-page RES format, including the frontier-versus-use citation split.

When to use

  • Positioning EctJ papers against econometric theory
  • Differentiating from applied econometrics and statistics
  • Structuring frontier-versus-use citation splits
  • Compressing related work for the 20-page RES format
  • Identifying the closest econometric object
  • Drafting worked delta statements
  • Sweeping cemmap working papers for nearest neighbors

When not to use

  • Creating broad methods surveys
  • Writing sprawling related-work sections
  • Citations that do not define incumbent methods or prove leading-case value
