jbf-review-process

separate

Use when you need to understand the Journal of Banking & Finance (JBF) editorial pipeline — the desk screen, double-anonymized refereeing, the role of the USD 350 fee in reviewer rewards, and what to expect between submission and decision. Sets expectations; pair with jbf-rebuttal once you receive an R&R.

When to use

  • understand JBF editorial pipeline
  • interpret Editorial Manager status changes
  • understand desk screen process
  • understand double-anonymized refereeing
  • understand USD 350 fee role
  • assess desk screen readiness
  • read referee report coverage areas

When not to use

  • writing rebuttal responses
  • selecting specific topics
  • designing identification strategies
