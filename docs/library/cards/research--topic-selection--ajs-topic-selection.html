ajs-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a sociology project fits the American Journal of Sociology (AJS) and how to frame it. AJS is the discipline's oldest generalist journal, with a premium on theoretical ambition and dialogue with current sociology; the test is general significance plus a portable theoretical payoff, not subfield novelty alone. Helps frame the question; it does not collect data.

When to use

  • Choosing among possible projects or framings for an AJS submission
  • Deciding whether the contribution warrants a full article or a Comment
  • Weighing AJS against ASR or a field journal
  • Pressure-testing fit before investing in a project
  • Framing questions to meet AJS theoretical ambition standards

When not to use

  • Collecting data
  • Opinion pieces or current-events takes
  • Papers that are not original sociological research in dialogue with the field
