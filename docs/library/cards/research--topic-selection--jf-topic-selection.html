jf-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a research question is general-interest and economically significant enough for The Journal of Finance (JF), and when sharpening the framing. Tests fit and contribution; it does not design the empirics.

When to use

  • judging whether a research question is general-interest
  • judging economic significance for JF
  • sharpening the framing
  • deciding whether a question is JF-level
  • judging the reach of a contribution
  • choosing among JF, JFE, and RFS

When not to use

  • designing the empirics
