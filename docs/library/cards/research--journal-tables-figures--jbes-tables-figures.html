jbes-tables-figures

separate

Use when designing the simulation tables and figures for a Journal of Business & Economic Statistics (JBES) methods paper so size, power, coverage, and the empirical results are legible to both statisticians and applied economists. Improves exhibits; it does not change results.

When to use

  • Designing simulation tables
  • Designing figures for JBES methods papers
  • Improving exhibit legibility
  • Formatting Monte Carlo exhibits
  • Formatting empirical exhibits
  • Writing self-contained exhibit notes
  • Generating exhibits from fitted results

When not to use

  • Changing statistical results
  • Retyping numbers manually
