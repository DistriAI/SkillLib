ppopp-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or tightening a PPoPP paper's prose and structure, covering the two-column acmart sigplan layout, the 10-page text+figures budget, stating the concurrency-correctness and scalability claim up front, presenting speedup curves and core sweeps, and the parallel-programming conventions PPoPP reviewers expect.

When to use

  • drafting PPoPP paper prose
  • tightening PPoPP paper structure
  • formatting two-column acmart sigplan layout
  • managing 10-page text and figures budget
  • stating concurrency-correctness and scalability claims
  • presenting speedup curves and core sweeps
  • applying parallel-programming conventions

When not to use

  • padding body with artifact material
  • shrinking the template to recover space
  • using et al. in references
  • hiding saturation points behind truncated x-axes
  • conflating strong and weak scaling
