Conflict

The three copies are not simple reproductions of one another. The gooseworks-ai and sickn33 copies are nearly identical rule-file routers for Remotion domain knowledge, but the remotion-dev copy is a higher-level workflow router that adds project setup, Studio preview, rendering, SaaS, and upgrade procedures that the other two lack. Conversely, the rule-file lists in the first two contain specific topics (e.g., light-leaks, maps, voiceover, parameters, subtitles, ffmpeg, audio-visualization) that the remotion-dev copy does not enumerate. Crowning one would hide the other's real alternative steps.
