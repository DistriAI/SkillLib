discovery-eyes

authentic

Read your team's messages the way opposing counsel would in litigation discovery — prevention training that makes communication hygiene visceral. Use when asked how would our Slack look in discovery, train my team on communication hygiene, review this thread like a plaintiff's lawyer, or what shouldn't we put in writing. Produces the highlighted-exhibit reading of sample messages, the patterns that create legal risk, and a debrief with the write-it-this-way rules — strictly for prevention, never for concealment.

When to use

  • prevention training on communication hygiene
  • reviewing sample messages like a plaintiff's lawyer
  • identifying patterns that create legal risk
  • providing write-it-this-way rules
  • analyzing how Slack or threads look in discovery
  • training teams on what not to put in writing

When not to use

  • assisting with deleting or concealing records
  • evading preservation obligations
  • performing legal analysis
  • reading real named individuals' messages punitively
  • training people to stop reporting problems
  • sanitizing messages into meaninglessness
