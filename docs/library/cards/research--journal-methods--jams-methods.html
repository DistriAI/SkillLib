jams-methods

separate

Use when matching the research design to the claim for a Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science (JAMS) manuscript — construct validity and measurement, survey/SEM design, secondary-data identification, experiments, or meta-analysis. Designs the study and stress-tests validity; jams-data-analysis executes and reports the estimates.
