journal-of-high-energy-physics

separate

Use when targeting Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) or deciding whether a high-energy physics manuscript fits this author-formatted, open-access SISSA/Springer venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP)
  • deciding whether a high-energy physics manuscript fits JHEP
  • choosing between JHEP, Physical Review D, and Physical Review Letters
  • JHEP author-formatting and open-access conventions
  • desk-reject criteria for JHEP
  • formal theory, quantum field theory, and particle phenomenology papers
  • collider and experimental HEP interpretation papers
  • holography and gravity papers connected to fundamental interactions

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • papers aimed at a broad-physics audience rather than specialist HEP community
  • papers too short or lacking technical detail for JHEP's completeness bar
