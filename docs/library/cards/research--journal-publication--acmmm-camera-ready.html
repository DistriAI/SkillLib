acmmm-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing the ACM MM (ACM Multimedia) camera-ready version of record — de-anonymizing safely, completing the ACM rights form and CCS concepts, meeting ACM sigconf requirements, releasing code/data/media artifacts and any earned reproducibility badge, registering, and planning the oral/poster presentation in Rio.

When to use

  • de-anonymizing the reviewed paper
  • completing the ACM rights form
  • adding CCS concepts and keywords
  • meeting ACM sigconf requirements
  • releasing code, data, and media artifacts
  • including earned reproducibility badges
  • registering for the conference
  • planning oral or poster presentations
