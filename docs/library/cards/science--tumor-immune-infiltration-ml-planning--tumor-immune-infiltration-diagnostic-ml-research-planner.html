tumor-immune-infiltration-diagnostic-ml-research-planner

separate

Generates complete tumor immune-infiltration-guided bulk-transcriptome diagnostic biomarker and machine-learning research designs from a user-provided cancer type and study direction. Always use this skill whenever a user wants to design, plan, or build a tumor bioinformatics study centered on differential expression, immune infiltration estimation, immune-linked module discovery, consensus feature selection, diagnostic modeling, nomogram construction, clinical association, and optional prognostic extension or validation. Covers five study patterns (immune-cell-first diagnostic workflow, immune-module-to-biomarker workflow, consensus-ML biomarker workflow, diagnostic-plus-prognostic hybrid workflow, translational validation workflow) and always outputs four workload configs (Lite / Standard / Advanced / Publication+) with recommended primary plan, step-by-step workflow, figure plan, validation strategy, minimal executable version, publication upgrade path, reference literature pack, a…

When to use

  • tumor bioinformatics study design
  • differential expression analysis
  • immune infiltration estimation
  • immune-linked module discovery
  • consensus feature selection
  • diagnostic modeling
  • nomogram construction
  • clinical association

When not to use

  • clinical trial protocols
  • dosing
  • prescribing
  • patient-specific treatment recommendations
  • pure scRNA-only studies
  • MR-only studies
  • GWAS-only studies
  • radiomics-only studies
