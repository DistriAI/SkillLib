jacs-results-framing

separate

Use when the chemical advance and its significance are not yet sharp for a Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS) manuscript. Shapes the narrative and claims — it does not generate new data or fix characterization gaps.

When to use

  • Shaping narrative and claims
  • Framing the chemical advance
  • Calibrating novelty claims
  • Title and abstract discipline
  • Framing broad interest
  • Reviewing claim-evidence-risk ledger

When not to use

  • Generating new data
  • Fixing characterization gaps
