xyfcli-order-guide

separate

肥料订单下单与客户/供应商管理技能。使用 xyfcli CLI 执行：(1) 下单/订货/采购肥料；(2) 查询产品信息/价格/库存；(3) 查询客户/供应商/经销商信息；(4) 解析订货单图片。触发词：下单、订货、买肥料、查产品、查客户、供应商信息、产品列表、客户编码。

When to use

  • Placing fertilizer orders
  • Querying product information, prices, and inventory
  • Querying customer, supplier, and dealer information
  • Parsing order images via OCR
  • Managing customer delivery addresses
  • Querying delivery bases and stock
