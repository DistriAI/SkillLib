jmr-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jmr-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal for a Journal of Marketing Research (JMR) manuscript. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills, and classifies the paper as behavioral, modeling/econometric, or a methods contribution.

When to use

  • deciding which jmr-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal
  • classifying paper genre as behavioral, modeling/econometric, or methods
  • assessing if the dual bar of rigor and substance is met
  • planning response to a JMR decision letter

When not to use

  • replacing specialized jmr-* skills
  • treating JMR as single-genre
  • running polish skills before the dual bar is plausibly met
  • drafting a rebuttal response before the manuscript is revised
