humrel-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a question belongs in Human Relations (HR) and how to frame it for the journal's interdisciplinary work-and-society audience. Tests fit and sharpens the question; it does not develop the theory or invent evidence.

When to use

  • deciding whether a question belongs in Human Relations
  • framing a question for the journal's interdisciplinary work-and-society audience
  • testing fit for Human Relations
  • sharpening the question
  • distinguishing HR from sibling journals
  • identifying the relational/social core
  • declaring a theoretical stake
  • assessing interdisciplinary reach

When not to use

  • developing the theory
  • inventing evidence
