academic-paper-reviewer

combined

Multi-perspective academic paper review with dynamic reviewer personas. Runs a 5-seat, role-separated review panel (Journal-Fit Reviewer + 3 peer-review roles + Devil's Advocate) with field-specific expertise; role separation is not a claim of independent error processes. Supports full review, re-review (verification), quick assessment, methodology focus, Socratic guided, and calibration modes. Skill page

Conflict

The two copies describe the same multi-perspective academic paper review workflow but disagree on the reviewer panel composition and include materially different operational content. The Imbad0202 copy (v1.11.1) uses a Journal-Fit Reviewer plus 3 peer reviewers plus a Devil's Advocate, adds a calibration mode, and contains a detailed routing/escape-hatch protocol. The brycewang-stanford copy (v1.4) uses an Editor-in-Chief plus 3 peer reviewers plus a Devil's Advocate, omits calibration, and provides a Quick Start, trigger/non-trigger table, and a 7-agent orchestration diagram. Crowning one would hide the alternative panel structure and the distinct routing or quick-start guidance.
