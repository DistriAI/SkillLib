ecommerce-product-montage

separate

Assemble product footage, UGC, and b-roll into a conversion-oriented montage with a hook → pain → demo → proof → CTA rhythm. Use for 带货混剪, 产品混剪, 商品短视频, 种草视频, ecommerce montage, product reel, UGC cutdown, or when the user wants selling footage cut into a short that converts.

When to use

  • 带货混剪
  • 产品混剪
  • 商品短视频
  • 种草视频
  • ecommerce montage
  • product reel
  • UGC cutdown
  • selling footage cut into a short that converts

When not to use

  • writing ad copy
  • re-explaining music tooling
  • beat-driven placement
  • many clips with no selling intent
  • N distinct selling variants from one pool
