jfi-topic-selection

separate

Use when working on Journal of Financial Intermediation (JFI) manuscripts to test whether the paper is centrally about banking and financial intermediation — whether an intermediary mechanism is load-bearing, how it differs from a Journal of Banking and Finance fit, and whether to lead with theory or empirics — before investing in the draft.

When to use

  • Deciding whether a project fits the Journal of Financial Intermediation
  • Choosing whether the paper's spine is a model or an empirical design
  • Testing if an intermediary or financial-institution mechanism is first-order
  • Differentiating fit from the Journal of Banking and Finance
  • Rescuing borderline verdicts by finding intermediation predictions
  • Determining if a paper is theory-led or empirics-led

When not to use

  • Pure asset-pricing results
  • Corporate-finance studies where banks are just a control
  • Macro papers with no institutional channel
  • Broad empirical banking without testing an intermediation mechanism
  • Institutional documentation or cross-country performance without mechanism testing
