rfs-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight for a The Review of Financial Studies (RFS) manuscript — the SFS Editorial Express portal, cover letter, formatting, Internet Appendix, submission fee, and SFS norms. Checks readiness; does NOT write the paper or the rebuttal.

When to use

  • final pre-submission preflight
  • SFS Editorial Express portal check
  • cover letter review
  • formatting compliance
  • Internet Appendix preparation
  • submission fee verification
  • SFS norms check

When not to use

  • writing the paper
  • writing the rebuttal
