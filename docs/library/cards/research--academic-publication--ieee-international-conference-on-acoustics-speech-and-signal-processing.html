ieee-international-conference-on-acoustics-speech-and-signal-processing

separate

Use when targeting IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for signal processing.

When to use

  • targeting ICASSP
  • deciding if a manuscript fits ICASSP
  • conference-fit read for signal processing
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style to conference narrative
  • evidence-gap diagnosis
  • anonymity diagnosis
  • artifact diagnosis
  • rebuttal diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • manuscripts mainly in planning
  • manuscripts mainly in NLP
  • manuscripts mainly in information retrieval
  • manuscripts mainly in knowledge capture
