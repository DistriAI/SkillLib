Conflict

The two copies implement the same review-fix-rereview loop but disagree on the stop condition, reviewer backend, and state persistence. The wanshuiyin copy is the more evolved procedure (Copilot-native reviewer, explicit AND stop condition, difficulty levels, output directory, HTML rendering, and a warning against external timers), while the brycewang copy adds a concrete REVIEW_STATE.json persistence and resume protocol that the wanshuiyin copy does not contain. Crowning one would hide a real alternative: the brycewang copy's state recovery is a material step absent from the other, and the wanshuiyin copy's reviewer routing and stop-condition correction are absent from the other.
