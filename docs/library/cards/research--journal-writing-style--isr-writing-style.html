isr-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing the prose, structure, abstract, and notation of an Information Systems Research (ISR) manuscript — front-loading the IS contribution, writing for a sociotechnical and intradisciplinary readership, keeping the 300-word abstract and double-anonymized text, and following INFORMS author-date style. Late-stage polish; do not invoke while the contribution or identification/proof is still unsettled.

When to use

  • polishing prose, structure, abstract, and notation
  • front-loading the IS contribution
  • writing for a sociotechnical and intradisciplinary readership
  • keeping the 300-word abstract and double-anonymized text
  • following INFORMS author-date style
  • final pass before isr-submission

When not to use

  • use while the contribution or identification/proof is still unsettled
