competitor-research

combined

Research competitor products, launch history, and acquisition evidence from public sources or supplied materials. Use when evaluating growth claims, comparing competitors, or choosing growth experiments. Produces a cited brief that separates observations, attributed claims, estimates, and hypotheses. Research competitors - products, pricing, team, funding, and strategy

When to use

  • evaluating growth claims
  • comparing competitors
  • choosing growth experiments
  • researching launch history
  • analyzing acquisition evidence
  • identifying transferable strategies

When not to use

  • installing connectors
  • creating accounts
  • submitting directory listings
  • contacting founders
  • incurring paid research charges
  • promising complete growth history for every domain

Conflict

The three copies define 'competitor research' in fundamentally different ways. The first is an SEO/content-focused procedure using tools like Ahrefs and Semrush to find keyword and backlink gaps. The second is a specific API-driven workflow for the 'gooseworks' platform to scrape company data. The third is a general business intelligence procedure focused on evidence-based growth analysis and hypothesis testing. Because they cover different domains (SEO vs. API scraping vs. Business Strategy) and use different tools and methodologies, no single copy is the 'original' that the others reproduce; they are distinct approaches to a broad task name.
