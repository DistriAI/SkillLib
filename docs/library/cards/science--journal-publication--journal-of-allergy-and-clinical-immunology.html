journal-of-allergy-and-clinical-immunology

separate

Use when targeting The Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology (JACI) or deciding whether an allergy/asthma/clinical-immunology study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the clinical-and-mechanistic immunology evidence bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, AAAAI/Elsevier house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • JACI fit/framing check
  • Re-framing studies around allergic-disease mechanisms
  • Choosing between JACI and other venues
  • Reporting-guideline and registration expectations
  • Desk-reject heuristics
  • Asthma and allergic airway disease studies
  • Food, drug, and venom allergy studies
  • Translational immunology of allergic disease

When not to use

  • Clinical advice
  • Regulatory advice
  • Purely descriptive case series
  • Bench studies with no allergic-disease anchor
