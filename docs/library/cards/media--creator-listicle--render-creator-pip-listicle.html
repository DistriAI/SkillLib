render-creator-pip-listicle

separate

Assemble a creator picture-in-picture product-listicle ad from a config. The creator stays full-frame for every beat (voice and lips generated together), and each product beat carries three overlays for the whole beat: a title pill, the product demo in a rounded picture-in-picture window (the brand's muted UGC clip, or its UI still or screen recording), and a product card with thumbnail, number, category and name. Hook and CTA beats show the creator with the title pill only. It builds one overlay image per beat, composites it on the creator clip, concatenates the beats and burns timed captions last. Free, deterministic assembly; the creator anchor and talking clips come from create-image-fal and create-video-fal. Use for the creator-pip-listicle format.

When to use

  • creator picture-in-picture product listicle ads
  • assembling listicle ads from config
  • compositing overlays on creator clips
  • building per-beat overlay PNGs
  • concatenating video beats
  • burning timed captions

When not to use

  • generating creator anchor images
  • generating talking clips
  • paid generation steps
  • separate voiceover generation
  • lip-sync passes
