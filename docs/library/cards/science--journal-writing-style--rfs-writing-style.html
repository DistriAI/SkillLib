rfs-writing-style

separate

Use as a late-stage polish when prose, contribution framing, or the abstract/introduction is the bottleneck for a The Review of Financial Studies (RFS) manuscript. Sharpens exposition; does NOT change the empirics, identification, or results.

When to use

  • Late-stage polish of prose
  • Contribution framing
  • Abstract and introduction refinement
  • Final exposition pass
  • Sharpening exposition

When not to use

  • Changing empirics
  • Changing identification
  • Changing results
  • Polishing prose around an unsettled result
