sci-framing

separate

Use when locking the conceptual advance and the "why now" before drafting for Science (AAAS) — converts a correct result into a Science-shaped narrative that leads with the broad advance rather than the background, and seeds the title, abstract, and cover letter.

When to use

  • Locking the conceptual advance
  • Defining the 'why now'
  • Converting results into Science-shaped narrative
  • Seeding the title
  • Seeding the abstract
  • Seeding the cover letter
  • Reframing lab-report style manuscripts
  • Creating a one-sentence advance

When not to use

  • Manufacturing claims not supported by data
  • Method-led framing for methods journals
  • Tasks where the work is not Science-timely
