jhe-theory-model

separate

Use when a Journal of Health Economics (JHE) manuscript needs a model — insurance demand/selection, provider behavior under a payment rule, or a health-production/human-capital frame — to interpret estimates or run a counterfactual. Disciplines the conceptual frame; it does not establish identification (jhe-identification) or write prose.

When to use

  • interpreting reduced-form health-policy estimates
  • insurance demand and selection modeling
  • provider behavior under payment rules
  • health-production or human-capital framing
  • running counterfactuals for coverage or price changes
  • disciplining the conceptual frame of a JHE manuscript

When not to use

  • establishing identification
  • writing prose
