Use when targeting Molecular Biology and Evolution (MBE) or deciding whether a molecular evolution manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Molecular Biology and Evolution (MBE)
  • deciding whether a molecular evolution manuscript fits this venue
  • molecular evolution, phylogenetics, or population-genetics study
  • rigorous molecular-evolutionary framing
  • new statistical or computational method for phylogenetic inference
  • selection detection or population-genetic analysis
  • choosing between MBE, Genome Biology, and Nature Ecology & Evolution
  • integrating molecular evolution with ecology or genomics

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
