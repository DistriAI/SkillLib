red-topic-selection

separate

Use when testing whether a research question fits the Review of Economic Dynamics (RED) — a journal whose scope is defined by method and lens (dynamic, quantitative economics) rather than subfield. Helps decide if the paper studies a genuinely dynamic mechanism through a dynamic model, and whether it belongs at RED versus a generalist or a specialist outlet.

When to use

  • Deciding whether a paper is a RED paper
  • Evaluating dynamic-macro ideas for general-interest or field journals
  • Assessing if a question is too static, empirical, or applied-micro for RED
  • Scoring fit for dynamic economics contributions
  • Determining if a dynamic model carries the argument

When not to use

  • Mostly reduced-form policy estimate with no dynamic model
  • Pure econometric method with no dynamic-economics object
  • Broad macro paper focused on policy relevance rather than dynamic mechanism
  • Calibration exercise with no new mechanism, method, or disciplined moment
