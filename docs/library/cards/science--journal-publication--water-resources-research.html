water-resources-research

separate

Use when targeting Water Resources Research (WRR) or deciding whether a hydrology / water-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the quantitative water-science contribution bar, data-and-code deposition expectations, AGU house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a hydrology or water-science paper
  • re-framing basin-specific modeling or monitoring study
  • choosing between WRR, journal-of-hydrology, and broader earth-science venue
  • WRR's quantitative-contribution bar and AGU data-deposition expectations
  • surface-water and groundwater hydrology
  • hydrologic modeling, data assimilation, uncertainty quantification
  • hydroclimatology, snow/ice hydrology, land–atmosphere water exchange
  • socio-hydrology, water-resources systems, human–water interactions

When not to use

  • applied case study with no methodological or conceptual contribution
  • pure water-engineering design
  • water-policy essay
  • chemistry paper with no water-science contribution
  • carbon/nutrient biogeochemical cycling focus
  • climate-dynamics framing dominant
  • land–atmosphere flux / agro-meteorology
  • new documented hydrologic dataset as the product
