hire-or-pass

authentic

Turn a hiring debrief into a typed decision — hire, hire at a different level, get more signal, or pass — from rubric evidence only, with probabilities and confidence. Use when asked should we hire this candidate, run the debrief, hire or no-hire, or when interviewers disagree and the manager has to call it. Produces the evidence state per competency, the four-way decision with probabilities, the act-or-hold verdict, and when holding, the exact competency to test and who tests it. Decision support for a human decision — a person owns the hire.

When to use

  • hiring debrief
  • hire or no-hire decision
  • resolving interviewer disagreement
  • manager decision support
  • generating signal plan for untested competencies

When not to use

  • making the final hiring decision
  • replacing human accountability
  • using overall thumbs or vibes
  • deciding based on protected characteristics
