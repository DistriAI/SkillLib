sw-chinese-opera-banqiang-cases

separate

Worked full-length scripts of banqiang-system Chinese opera with their creation records (板腔体全本语料库). Primary texts; 翁偶虹《锁麟囊》 (the revised score edition, 65 arias, 15 self-account-vs-score checks), 汪曾祺等《沙家浜》 (the 1965 text, not the 1970 one; 36 arias, a 12-row two-version diff), 田汉《白蛇传》 (the 1955 book, not the web revision; 16 scenes, 王瑶卿说腔五条), 杨兰春《朝阳沟》 (1978 revival, 50 arias, 口语入唱, per-scene sung/spoken ratios), 魏明伦《潘金莲》 (跨时空, missing pages), 罗怀臻's 越剧《李清照》《真假驸马》, 淮剧《金龙与蜉蝣》, 甬剧《典妻》; plus index tables of 翁偶虹's 60 创作记 and 范钧宏's seven 改编札记. Use when you want to see how a 唱工戏 lays out its 板式, need real scene counts, aria counts and 辙口 to imitate, want the road from raw material to 审腔, are writing a 现代戏 or colloquial lyrics, or compare one author across two声腔 systems. Method is in sw-chinese-opera-banqiang; this skill holds the worked scripts and their creation records.

When to use

  • banqiang-system Chinese opera scripts
  • creation records and revision notes
  • scene and aria count analysis
  • colloquial lyrics in modern opera
  • comparing authors across vocal systems
  • layout of banqiang patterns
  • road from raw material to vocal review

When not to use

  • general opera writing methods
  • qupai-system opera scripts
