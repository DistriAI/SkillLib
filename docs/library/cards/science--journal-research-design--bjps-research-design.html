bjps-research-design

separate

Use when defending the research design of a British Journal of Political Science (BJPS) manuscript — causal identification for quantitative work, case selection and process tracing for qualitative work, experimental and survey-experimental design, or formal-empirical linkage. BJPS judges each tradition on its own terms. Strengthens the design; it does not write code.

When to use

  • defending research design for BJPS manuscripts
  • causal identification for quantitative work
  • case selection and process tracing for qualitative work
  • experimental and survey-experimental design
  • formal-empirical linkage
  • specifying identification, case selection, or experimental design
  • preparing a pre-analysis plan
  • justifying why the design adjudicates the rival account

When not to use

  • writing code
