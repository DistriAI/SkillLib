jfe-empirical-design

separate

Use when settling the measurement and estimation choices of a Journal of Financial Economics (JFE) manuscript — factor construction, portfolio sorts, Fama-MacBeth/GMM, standard-error clustering, and multiple-testing discipline. Covers the design/estimator layer; for causal identification of corporate-finance effects use jfe-identification.

When to use

  • factor construction
  • portfolio sorts
  • Fama-MacBeth/GMM estimation
  • standard-error clustering
  • multiple-testing discipline
  • asset-pricing predictor out-of-sample testing
  • corporate finance variable definition and fixed effects
  • economic magnitude reporting

When not to use

  • causal identification of corporate-finance effects
