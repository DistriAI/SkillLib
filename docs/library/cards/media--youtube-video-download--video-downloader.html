video-downloader

combined

Download YouTube videos with customizable quality and format options. Use this skill when the user asks to download, save, or grab YouTube videos. Supports various quality settings (best, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p), multiple formats (mp4, webm, mkv), and audio-only downloads as MP3. Downloads videos from YouTube and other platforms for offline viewing, editing, or archival. Handles various formats and quality options.

When to use

  • Downloading YouTube videos for offline viewing
  • Saving educational content for reference
  • Archiving important videos
  • Getting video files for editing or repurposing
  • Downloading your own content from platforms
  • Saving conference talks or webinars

When not to use

  • Redistribute copyrighted content
  • Download videos without permission

Skill page

Conflict

The davepoon, ComposioHQ, and composio-community copies are near-identical procedural skills that wrap a specific scripts/download_video.py CLI with quality, format, audio-only, and output flags. The Prat011 copy is a different, higher-level skill that describes natural-language usage, batch/playlist downloads, metadata preservation, and copyright guidance, but it does not provide the concrete CLI procedure. Because the copies overlap but each has material steps or constraints the others lack, and they disagree on playlist support and yt-dlp installation behavior, crowning one would hide a real alternative.
