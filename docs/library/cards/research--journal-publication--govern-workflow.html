govern-workflow

separate

Use when starting or navigating any Governance: An International Journal of Policy, Administration, and Institutions manuscript and you need the entry point that routes to the right govern- sub-skill based on lifecycle stage and the comparative/institutional fit of the paper. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new Governance paper
  • Navigating mid-project skill selection
  • Checking if a paper is in scope
  • Routing after a decision letter
  • Confirming comparative/institutional contribution

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Corporate governance topics
  • Literature reviews
  • Pure bibliometric studies
  • Single-country administrative descriptions without comparative contribution
