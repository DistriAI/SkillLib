rss-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a robotics project fits RSS (Robotics: Science and Systems), the single-track selective venue whose bar is a scientific claim about robotics, and when routing instead toward ICRA, IROS, CoRL, HRI, WAFR, T-RO, or IJRR based on claim type, evidence shape, and audience.

When to use

  • judging whether a robotics project fits RSS
  • routing projects toward ICRA, IROS, CoRL, HRI, WAFR, T-RO, or IJRR
  • evaluating claim type, evidence shape, and audience
  • running the three-part fit probe
  • applying the claim taxonomy
  • weighing signals before committing the January slot

When not to use

  • writing the paper
  • full calendar argument for deadline geometry
