mathfin-review-process

separate

Use when navigating the Mathematical Finance (Wiley) review process — single-blind editor screening, associate-editor handling, and referees who verify proofs line by line. Explains what reviewers at this theory venue check first, common desk-screen failure patterns for theorem-first papers, and how to read review-stage signals.

When to use

  • Understanding editor and referee lens before submission
  • Interpreting review-stage status after submission
  • Assessing readiness of theorem-first papers
  • Identifying desk-screen failure patterns
  • Preparing a reviewer packet
  • Reading decision vocabulary and signals

When not to use

  • Naming the current editor without checking live pages
  • Relying on folklore about average handling times
  • Submitting the reviewer packet verbatim
