jom-methods

separate

Use when choosing and defending the research design for a Journal of Operations Management (JOM) empirical study — matching survey, archival/secondary, field, case, experimental, or intervention-based research to the operations question, anticipating the Empirical Research Methods Department's method check, and keeping the design observation-grounded rather than analytical.
