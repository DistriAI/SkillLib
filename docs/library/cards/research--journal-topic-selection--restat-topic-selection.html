restat-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether an applied-economics project fits The Review of Economics and Statistics (REStat) rather than AER / AEJ:Applied / J. Econometrics / a field journal, and sharpening the question to REStat's empirical-and-measurement bar. Decides venue fit and frames the question; it does not run the analysis.

When to use

  • deciding venue fit for applied-economics projects
  • sharpening questions to REStat's empirical-and-measurement bar
  • framing measurement or identification contributions
  • distinguishing REStat from AER, AEJ:Applied, J. Econometrics, and field journals
  • deciding between Article and Short Paper formats

When not to use

  • running the analysis
  • developing new estimators with proofs
  • field-internal contributions
  • agenda-setting or general-interest papers
  • method-first contributions
