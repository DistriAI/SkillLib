genome-biology

separate

Use when targeting Genome Biology or deciding whether a genomics or computational biology manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Genome Biology
  • deciding if a genomics manuscript fits the venue
  • new genomics method or software tool with biological validation
  • large-scale functional genomics or epigenomics studies
  • benchmark studies of existing tools
  • single-cell genomics methods
  • comparative or population genomics with analytical advance
  • desk-reject risks and alternative routing

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
