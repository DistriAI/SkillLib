ptj-macro-trend

separate

Use when evaluating Paul Tudor Jones-style macro trend trades: major turning points, trend confirmation, risk control, stop discipline, liquidity, and not losing big.

When to use

  • Macro trend and turning-point analysis
  • Equity index, rates, FX, commodity setup review
  • Crash-risk and risk-control discussion
  • Whether to press or cut a macro trade

When not to use

  • Averaging losers
  • Risking a large loss on a macro opinion
  • Fighting the tape without a defined stop
