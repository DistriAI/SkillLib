Conflict

The copies overlap on the core idea of exploring intent before building, but they disagree on the procedure and contain material steps the others lack. obra/superpowers is a conversational discovery skill that explicitly allows skipping questions, uses open-ended questions, and includes visual mockups and spikes. foryourhealth111-pixel and LeoYeAI are near-identical procedural skills that require a hard gate, a fixed checklist, a design doc at a specific path, and a handoff to writing-plans. sickn33 adds mandatory non-functional requirements, an understanding lock, and a decision log. Crowning one would hide real alternatives.
