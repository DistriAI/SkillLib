stoc-reproducibility

separate

Use when hardening a STOC (ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing) paper so its results can be independently checked — proof completeness across the extended-abstract/full-version split, single-source builds that prevent statement drift between the two documents, and determinism for any computation a claim relies on.

When to use

  • hardening STOC paper reproducibility
  • ensuring proof completeness across extended-abstract and full-version
  • preventing statement drift between documents
  • implementing single-source builds for theorem statements
  • auditing checkability before submission
  • ensuring determinism for machine-checked claims
  • verifying external theorem applicability
  • managing statement freezes and verification workflows

When not to use

  • packaging mechanics for artifact evaluation
  • deciding when to include experiments
