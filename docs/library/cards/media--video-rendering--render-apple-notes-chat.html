render-apple-notes-chat

separate

Assemble an Apple Notes list video ad from a note + end-card JSON — a frame-accurate fake iPhone screen recording of a short list being typed into Apple Notes (character by character, key pops, blinking caret, the note scrolling up as it fills) crossfaded into a checklist end card (the note's picks as ticked rows, real product photos, logo, CTA, in the brand's colours and fonts), with an optional music bed. FREE assembly (Playwright + ffmpeg); the recipe supplies the per-brand note + end card and gates the paid music call to its own capability. The Apple Notes sibling of render-imessage-chat and render-chatgpt-chat. Use for the apple-notes format.
