pmla-review-process

separate

Use to understand how PMLA (Publications of the Modern Language Association) evaluates an essay — anonymous (blind) review with at least two reviewers, the Editorial Board's final decision, the Advisory Committee's reports, MLA-membership eligibility, and how anonymity is maintained. Sets expectations and shapes the essay to survive review; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • stress-test the essay against PMLA's standards
  • understanding what reviewers and the Editorial Board weigh
  • interpreting a decision letter and setting expectations
  • confirming eligibility
  • review-risk pass for PMLA
  • shaping the essay to pass

When not to use

  • contacting editors
