jep-evidence-without-equations

separate

Use when presenting empirical or theoretical evidence credibly in a Journal of Economic Perspectives (JEP) article with minimal math — intuition, examples, well-chosen numbers, and honest caveats. Handles how evidence is conveyed in prose; defer figures/tables to jep-exhibits-for-general-readers and fairness across studies to jep-balance-and-objectivity.

When to use

  • presenting empirical evidence without regression tables
  • conveying theoretical evidence with minimal math
  • translating coefficients into plain units
  • characterizing the body of evidence
  • stating caveats and external validity
  • anchoring magnitudes with benchmarks

When not to use

  • creating figures or tables
  • ensuring fairness across studies
  • displaying estimation equations
  • using asterisks for significance
