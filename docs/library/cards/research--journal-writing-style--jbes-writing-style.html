jbes-writing-style

separate

Use when polishing prose, theorem statements, the abstract, and the introduction for a Journal of Business & Economic Statistics (JBES) methods paper so the methodological contribution and its empirical relevance both land. Polishes exposition; it does not change theory or results.

When to use

  • polishing prose
  • polishing theorem statements
  • polishing the abstract
  • polishing the introduction
  • JBES methods paper writing
  • making methodological contribution legible
  • ensuring empirical relevance is front and center

When not to use

  • changing theory
  • changing results
