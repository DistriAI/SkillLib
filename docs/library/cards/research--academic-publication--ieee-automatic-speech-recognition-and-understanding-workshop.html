ieee-automatic-speech-recognition-and-understanding-workshop

separate

Use when targeting IEEE Automatic Speech Recognition and Understanding Workshop (ASRU) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for speech recognition.

When to use

  • targeting IEEE ASRU
  • deciding if a manuscript fits ASRU
  • conference-fit read for automatic speech recognition
  • re-framing journal or arXiv style into CS conference narrative
  • diagnosing evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, or rebuttal issues
  • submission-cycle checks for ASRU
  • desk-reject risk assessment for speech recognition

When not to use

  • substitute for current year's CFP
  • substitute for author kit
  • substitute for ethics policy
  • substitute for submission portal
  • broader speech processing (route to INTERSPEECH)
  • signal processing (route to ICASSP)
  • spoken-language technology (route to SLT)
  • language modeling core contribution (route to ACL/EMNLP)
