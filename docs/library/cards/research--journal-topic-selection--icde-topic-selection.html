icde-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a project is a strong IEEE ICDE fit, routing among the three database venues from ICDE's seat (SIGMOD's quarterly rounds vs PVLDB's monthly deadlines vs ICDE's two rounds), choosing ICDE research vs industry track, applying the data-management-primitive test, and comparing with TKDE, EDBT, or systems venues.

When to use

  • deciding ICDE fit
  • routing among ICDE, SIGMOD, and PVLDB
  • choosing ICDE research vs industry track
  • applying the data-management-primitive test
  • comparing with TKDE, EDBT, or systems venues

When not to use

  • writing the paper
  • ML model contributions without a DB primitive
  • pure theory results with no system or workload
