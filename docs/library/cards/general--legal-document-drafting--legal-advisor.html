Conflict

The two copies share the same core focus areas, approach, key regulations, and output structure, but they disagree on scope and safety constraints. The rmyndharis copy is a broad drafting template that proactively generates complete legal documents. The sickn33 copy is a stricter, risk-aware workflow that requires verified facts, primary sources, and explicit limitations on what a draft can do (e.g., it does not implement consent or security). Crowning one would hide the other's material constraints and alternative approach to legal drafting.
