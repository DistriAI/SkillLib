wacv-submission

separate

Use when auditing a WACV submission before an OpenReview round deadline, covering the Round 1 vs Round 2 choice, the Applications vs Algorithms track field, the 8-page-including-figures limit, double-blind anonymity and OpenReview-profile requirements, the anonymized supplement, dual submission, and desk-reject triage.

When to use

  • WACV submission audit
  • Round 1 vs Round 2 choice
  • Applications vs Algorithms track selection
  • 8-page limit compliance
  • Double-blind anonymity check
  • OpenReview profile validation
  • Anonymized supplement review
  • Desk-reject triage

When not to use

  • Writing the paper content
  • Choosing a different conference
  • Handling post-deadline revisions
