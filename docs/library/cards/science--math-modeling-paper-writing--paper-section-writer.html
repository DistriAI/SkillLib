paper-section-writer

authentic

Draft submission-ready mathematical-modeling paper sections from the approved solution package, frozen numbers, human decision ledger, and verified figures without searching scattered exploratory outputs or inventing interpretation.

When to use

  • Drafting method descriptions
  • Drafting results sections
  • Building claim maps
  • Transcribing human-owned content
  • Formatting paper sections

When not to use

  • Searching scattered exploratory outputs
  • Inventing interpretation
  • Hunting through raw experiment folders
  • Originating method choice rationale
  • Fabricating citations
  • Creating new decision artifacts
