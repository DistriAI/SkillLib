bjps-review-process

separate

Use to understand how the British Journal of Political Science (BJPS) evaluates a manuscript — double-blind review with at least two referees, the desk screen, decision categories, and what reviewers are asked to weigh. Sets expectations and shapes the paper to survive review; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • stress-test against desk-screen grounds
  • interpreting a decision letter
  • understanding what reviewers are instructed to weigh
  • deciding whether the paper's general interest is strong enough
  • shaping the paper to pass review
  • calibrating to referee traditions

When not to use

  • contacting editors
