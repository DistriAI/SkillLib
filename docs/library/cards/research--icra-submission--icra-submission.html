icra-submission

separate

Use when auditing an ICRA paper for upload — PaperPlaza account and metadata, the 6-page-plus-2-reference-page limit, IEEE two-column template compliance, the double-anonymous rule introduced in the 2026 cycle, video-attachment specs (180 s, 20 MB), dual-submission conflicts with RA-L, and return-without-review triggers.

When to use

  • auditing ICRA paper for upload
  • checking PaperPlaza account and metadata
  • verifying 6-page-plus-2-reference-page limit
  • checking IEEE two-column template compliance
  • verifying double-anonymous rule
  • checking video-attachment specs
  • checking dual-submission conflicts with RA-L
  • identifying return-without-review triggers

When not to use

  • writing the ICRA paper
  • submitting to IROS or RA-L directly
  • managing post-deadline revisions
  • handling IEEE Xplore record updates
