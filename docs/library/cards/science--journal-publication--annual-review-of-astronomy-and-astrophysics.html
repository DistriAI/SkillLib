annual-review-of-astronomy-and-astrophysics

separate

Use when targeting Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics (ARA&A) or deciding whether a review proposal fits this invited, comprehensive, very-high-impact venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • scoping and framing a comprehensive review for ARA&A
  • clarifying the invited-review model
  • choosing between ARA&A and other review venues
  • assessing topic fit for comprehensive critical reviews
  • applying ARA&A house style and structure expectations
  • re-checking official submission guidelines

When not to use

  • submitting unsolicited primary research
  • presenting new primary results as a research paper
  • replacing the journal's current official guidelines
