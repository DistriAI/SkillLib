ecj-submission

separate

Use when running the final pre-submission preflight for a The Economic Journal (EJ) manuscript via Editorial Express — single PDF format, author-date references, single-blind review, JEL codes and keywords, full-length vs. short-paper choice, submission-fee awareness, funding disclosure, and the RES/EJ data-and-code policy. Final mechanical gate; it does not improve the economic argument.

When to use

  • final pre-submission preflight
  • The Economic Journal manuscript
  • Editorial Express portal requirements
  • single PDF format check
  • author-date references check
  • single-blind review confirmation
  • JEL codes and keywords entry
  • full-length vs. short-paper choice

When not to use

  • improving the economic argument
  • double-blind journal submissions
  • ScholarOne portal submissions
