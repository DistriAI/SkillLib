amr-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which amr-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing a theory-building manuscript from theoretical-puzzle framing through developmental-review revision for an Academy of Management Review (AMR) submission. Routes — does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which amr-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing a theory-building manuscript
  • locating manuscript bottlenecks
  • managing AMR developmental review revision
  • routing between specialized AMR skills

When not to use

  • replacing specialized amr-* skills
  • empirical hypothesis-testing
  • manuscripts with datasets or results sections
  • AMJ, ASQ, or SMJ submissions
