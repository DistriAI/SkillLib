jde-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a research question fits the Journal of Development Economics (JDE) — a first-order question about low- and middle-income economies and the process of economic development. Tests topic fit before identification or drafting begins.

When to use

  • Judging if a research question fits JDE
  • Testing topic fit before identification or drafting
  • Evaluating development question stakes
  • Deciding between JDE and general-interest or regional journals
  • Deciding on prospective pre-results-reviewed design
  • Scoring manuscript on venue fit and novelty
  • Comparing against sibling venues like World Development or JPubE

When not to use

  • Identification strategy
  • Drafting
  • Literature positioning
  • General micro results with incidental developing-country settings
  • Descriptive correlations without welfare or policy stakes
  • Prestige-only targeting without genuine fit
