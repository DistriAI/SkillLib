commres-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Communication Research (CR) manuscript against the literature so it reads as a cumulative, theory-testing contribution to communication science. CR reviewers expect engagement with the empirical communication literature and the rival accounts your hypotheses adjudicate. Stakes the contribution; it does not write the lit review.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the introduction
  • Revising the present study or hypotheses paragraph
  • Distinguishing hypotheses from closest prior empirical papers
  • Clarifying contribution to communication theory
  • Engaging the empirical debate and rival accounts
  • Building to explicit numbered hypotheses
  • Naming the gap precisely
  • Positioning against rival accounts

When not to use

  • Writing the lit review
  • Providing submission-ready advice without checking official-source-map.md
  • Polishing format if a sibling venue owns the contribution
