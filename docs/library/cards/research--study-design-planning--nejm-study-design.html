nejm-study-design

separate

Use to confirm study-design rigor and the mandatory prospective trial registration, protocol, and statistical analysis plan before writing up a clinical study for NEJM. Surfaces registration problems early — they cannot be fixed retroactively.

When to use

  • confirming prospective trial registration
  • verifying protocol and statistical analysis plan
  • stating design rigor for Methods
  • naming and justifying observational study design
  • checking randomization and blinding
  • verifying primary endpoint alignment
  • assessing sample size and power
  • identifying protocol or SAP deviations

When not to use

  • writing up an unregistered trial as an Original Article without flagging
  • presenting a post-hoc analysis plan as pre-specified
  • reporting a primary outcome that differs from the registered one without disclosure
  • treating per-protocol as the primary analysis for a superiority trial
