journal-of-experimental-medicine

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Experimental Medicine (JEM) or deciding whether an immunology or experimental medicine manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Experimental Medicine
  • deciding if immunology manuscript fits JEM
  • venue-selection for mechanistic immunology
  • re-framing experimental medicine manuscripts
  • assessing JEM desk-reject risks
  • calibrating hematopoiesis or lymphocyte biology studies
  • checking JEM house style and structure
  • pre-submission self-check for JEM

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • purely descriptive clinical characterization
  • pharmacological-only perturbation studies
  • large-scale omics surveys without mechanistic proof
