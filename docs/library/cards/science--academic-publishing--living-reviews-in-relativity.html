living-reviews-in-relativity

separate

Use when targeting Living Reviews in Relativity (LRR) or deciding whether an invited, continuously updated authoritative review in relativity and gravitation fits this very-high-impact Springer venue that publishes no primary research. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • comprehensive, maintainable review of a relativity/gravitation topic
  • authoritative living survey committed to updating over years
  • choosing between LRR, Reviews of Modern Physics, and topical-review format
  • LRR invitation model, comprehensiveness and maintenance expectations
  • desk-reject criteria before drafting
  • cosmology and relativistic astrophysics surveys
  • gravitational-wave science reviews
  • numerical relativity and approximation methods references

When not to use

  • primary research
  • tasks not related to Living Reviews in Relativity venue selection
