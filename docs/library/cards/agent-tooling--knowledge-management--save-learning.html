save-learning

separate

Shared step of the marketing playbooks (make-static-ads, analyze-performance, email-lifecycle, write-outreach): saves a short brand rule or finding in the human's own words, one idea per rule, retires the playbook's own older rules it replaces, and reads the result back to confirm. Loaded by those playbooks; not for direct use.

When to use

  • Saving brand rules
  • Saving findings
  • Retiring older rules
  • Reading back saved learnings
  • Saving measured readouts
  • Saving brand facts

When not to use

  • Direct use
  • Saving long evidence
  • Saving tables
  • Saving reports
  • Writing without human's words
