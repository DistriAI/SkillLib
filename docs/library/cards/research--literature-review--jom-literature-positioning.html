jom-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Operations Management (JOM) manuscript within the operations and supply chain management conversation — joining a live OM/SCM debate, distinguishing the paper from analytical OM work, and aligning the framing with the target Department's mission.

When to use

  • Positioning a JOM manuscript within the OM/SCM conversation
  • Joining a live OM/SCM debate
  • Distinguishing the paper from analytical OM work
  • Aligning framing with the target Department's mission
  • Problematizing a shared OM/SCM assumption
  • Mapping the conversation with anchor works and tensions
  • Calibrating against the empirical-OM identity

When not to use

  • Publishing purely analytical models or optimization techniques
  • Citation dumps with no argumentative thread
  • Positioning against analytical OM as if the paper were a model
  • Ignoring the target Department's literature
  • Claiming novelty without naming what the field currently believes
  • Competing on modeling sophistication rather than observed evidence
