popl-review-process

separate

Use when interpreting where a POPL submission stands — the full double-blind pipeline from the July HotCRP deadline through reviews, the optional author response, October conditional-acceptance decisions, the mandatory revision gate, Distinguished Paper selection, and publication as PACMPL Issue POPL in January.

When to use

  • Interpreting POPL submission status
  • Understanding the POPL double-blind pipeline
  • Reviewing POPL decision outcomes
  • Handling POPL revision gates
  • Understanding Distinguished Paper selection
  • Tracking POPL publication timeline

When not to use

  • Selecting POPL topics
  • Writing POPL papers
  • Managing non-POPL conference submissions
