cell-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which cell-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing a manuscript from significance test through reviewer rebuttal for Cell (Cell Press). Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which cell-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing a manuscript from significance test through reviewer rebuttal
  • diagnosing the current bottleneck of a draft
  • switching into rebuttal mode after reviews arrive
  • routing between experiments, writing, and revision

When not to use

  • replacing specialized cell-* skills
  • targeting Molecular Cell
  • targeting Cell Reports
  • targeting Cell Reports Medicine
  • targeting Developmental Cell
  • targeting Nature or Science without conversion
