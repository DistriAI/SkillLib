proceedings-of-the-ieee

separate

Use when targeting Proceedings of the IEEE or deciding whether an electrical-and-computer-engineering review manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the comprehensive-and-balanced synthesis bar, the no-primary-results expectation, the invited-vs-unsolicited and tutorial-vs-survey framing, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for tutorials, surveys, or scoped reviews
  • re-framing primary results into comprehensive synthesis
  • weighing unsolicited submission against invited contribution
  • breadth-and-balance bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • authoritative tutorials for non-specialists
  • comprehensive surveys organizing and critically comparing literature
  • cross-cutting syntheses at convergence of subfields
  • scoped reviews of emerging technology with societal impact

When not to use

  • primary-research paper reporting new experimental or theoretical results
  • narrow contributions
  • opinion pieces masquerading as reviews
  • surveys that simply tabulate one group's prior work
  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
