ectheory-literature-positioning

separate

Use to stake an Econometric Theory (ET) contribution against the existing theory frontier — which assumptions you weaken, which limit result you generalize, what was previously unproven — using APA author-date citations, not a standalone literature survey.

When to use

  • Staking an Econometric Theory contribution against the existing theory frontier
  • Identifying which assumptions are weakened or generalized
  • Showing regularity conditions are weaker than prior work
  • Setting up citation apparatus in ET's APA author-date style
  • Locating a theorem on the limit-theory lattice
  • Building a three-column map of incumbent conversation, unresolved tension, and delta
  • Comparing against sibling venues like Journal of Econometrics or Econometrica

When not to use

  • Standalone literature survey
  • Vague novelty without stated change in assumptions or scope
  • Overclaiming priority where a known result already covers the case
  • Mixed or numeric citation styles
  • Submission-ready advice before checking official source maps
