aerj-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing the prose of an American Educational Research Journal (AERJ) manuscript. AERJ is read across the whole field, follows APA 7th-edition style, and expects a 100–120-word abstract within a maximum 50-page manuscript. Improves clarity and compliance; it does not ghostwrite the paper.

When to use

  • Drafting or tightening any section
  • Bringing the manuscript into APA 7th-edition compliance
  • Writing or trimming the 100–120-word abstract
  • Cutting to fit the length limit while protecting the argument

When not to use

  • Ghostwriting the paper
