world-development

separate

Use when targeting World Development or deciding whether a development-studies manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting World Development
  • deciding if a development-studies manuscript fits
  • re-framing a general applied or qualitative manuscript
  • assessing desk-reject risks
  • checking method-and-evidence bar
  • reviewing house style and structure
  • verifying official submission guidelines

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • purely technical econometrics or theory papers with no development substance
  • high-income-country papers with no global-development angle
