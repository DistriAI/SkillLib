emnlp-camera-ready

separate

Use when converting an accepted EMNLP paper — Main or Findings — into its ACL Anthology camera-ready form: de-anonymization, integrating response-phase commitments, Limitations and checklist-appendix handling, Anthology metadata hygiene, artifact link restoration, and Budapest presentation and registration logistics.

When to use

  • converting accepted EMNLP papers to camera-ready
  • de-anonymization of papers
  • integrating response-phase commitments
  • Limitations and checklist-appendix handling
  • Anthology metadata hygiene
  • artifact link restoration
  • Budapest presentation and registration logistics

When not to use

  • ARR submission phase
  • pre-acceptance drafting
