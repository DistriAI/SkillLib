Daily cash, bank and digital day book: opening and closing balances per book, in/out movements, debit/credit presentation and reconciliation status. Use for daily bookkeeping.

When to use

  • daily cash and bank book
  • cash book and bank book
  • daily receipts and payments register
  • end-of-day balance check
  • petty cash day sheet
