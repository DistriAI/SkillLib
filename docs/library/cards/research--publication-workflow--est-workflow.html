est-workflow

separate

Use as the entry point for any Environmental Science & Technology (ES&T) manuscript. Routes to the right ES&T sub-skill based on where you are in the lifecycle and which article type (Research Article, Critical Review, Feature, Perspective, Policy Analysis, or the rapid companion ES&T Letters) fits. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new ES&T paper
  • Deciding which article type fits
  • Routing mid-project to the next skill
  • Handling decision letters
  • Determining environmental significance
  • Routing urgent results to ES&T Letters

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Treating ES&T like a pure-chemistry journal
  • Choosing ES&T Letters for non-urgent work
  • Choosing ES&T for results suited to specialist venues
