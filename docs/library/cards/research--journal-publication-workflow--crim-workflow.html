crim-workflow

separate

Use as the entry point for any Criminology (ASC / Wiley) manuscript. Routes to the right crim sub-skill based on where you are in the lifecycle and whether the paper is a full Article or a Research Note. Criminology is theory-forward and interdisciplinary, so the router first checks that the paper makes a criminological contribution, not just reports a crime correlation. It dispatches; it does not draft content.

When to use

  • Starting a new Criminology paper
  • Deciding between Article and Research Note
  • Routing mid-project to the next skill
  • Handling decision letters
  • Checking theoretical contribution
  • Triage of reviewer complaints

When not to use

  • Drafting content
  • Bare crime-data correlation without theoretical payoff
  • Forcing quantitative templates on qualitative work
