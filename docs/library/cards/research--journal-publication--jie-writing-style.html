jie-writing-style

separate

Use to align prose, abstract, and structure with Journal of International Economics (JIE) house norms — the 150-word factual abstract, 1-7 keywords, balancing theory and empirics, and writing for an audience split between international trade and international macro/finance. Shapes style; it does not generate results.

When to use

  • Drafting or tightening the abstract, introduction, or section structure for JIE
  • Balancing theory and empirics in the manuscript
  • Pitching trade or open-economy results to JIE's mixed readership
  • Reviewing abstract length and citation usage
  • Checking keyword and JEL code requirements
  • Executing a style execution pass for JIE

When not to use

  • Generating results
  • Public-finance policy contributions
  • Development settings
  • Monetary macro emphasis
