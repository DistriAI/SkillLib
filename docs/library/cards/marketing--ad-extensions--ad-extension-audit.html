ad-extension-audit

separate

Reviews all your Google Ads extensions — sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, call extensions, image extensions, price extensions — across every campaign. Flags what's missing, what's underperforming, what's outdated, and writes replacements based on your best performing ads and landing pages. Platform: Google.

When to use

  • When setting up new campaigns to ensure full extension coverage from day one
  • Quarterly extension reviews to catch outdated copy and missed opportunities
  • After website changes or new product launches that make existing extensions stale
  • When CTR is lower than expected and you want to improve ad real estate in search results
