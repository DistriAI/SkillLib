orgsci-methods

separate

Use when choosing and defending the research design for an Organization Science manuscript — matching one of the journal's eclectic methods (qualitative/inductive, quantitative/archival, experimental, computational/simulation, formal-analytical) to the question and level of analysis, and justifying design without requiring causal identification.

When to use

  • Choosing research design for Organization Science
  • Defending method fit to theoretical contribution
  • Justifying mixed methods combinations
  • Matching method to level of analysis
  • Auditing empirical causal designs
  • Checking design transparency and trustworthiness
  • Comparing against neighboring journals

When not to use

  • Simulation work
  • Qualitative work execution
  • Tasks not related to Organization Science manuscripts
