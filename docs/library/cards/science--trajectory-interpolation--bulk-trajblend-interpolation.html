bulk-trajblend-interpolation

separate

Extend scRNA-seq developmental trajectories with BulkTrajBlend by generating intermediate cells from bulk RNA-seq, training beta-VAE and GNN models, and interpolating missing states.

When to use

  • bridge gaps in single-cell developmental trajectories
  • deconvolve bulk samples
  • identify overlapping communities with a GNN
  • interpolate interrupted cell states
  • train beta-VAE and GNN models
  • generate synthetic cells
  • analyse trajectories with pseudotime and PAGA
