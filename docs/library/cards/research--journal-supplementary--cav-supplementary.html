cav-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what belongs in a CAV (Computer Aided Verification) paper body versus its optional appendix and artifact, covering the LNCS page limits, the rule that reviewers are not obliged to read the appendix so decision-critical content stays in the body, where full proofs and benchmark tables live, and double-anonymous supplementary material for the anonymized categories.

When to use

  • deciding what belongs in a CAV paper body versus appendix
  • checking LNCS page limits for CAV papers
  • ensuring decision-critical content stays in the body
  • placing full proofs and benchmark tables in appendix or artifact
  • managing double-anonymous supplementary material
  • verifying anonymity of appendix and review-time artifact
  • checking appendix architecture and references
  • splitting technique-plus-tool papers

When not to use

  • writing the main theorem or technique
  • conducting benchmark experiments
  • building the tool or solver
  • generating proof certificates
