jams-literature-positioning

separate

Use when staking a Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science (JAMS) manuscript's contribution against the marketing literature — locating it in the right stream, defending against "incremental / new-context," and citing the substantive canon the editor and reviewers expect. Positions the contribution; it does not state it in one sentence (jams-contribution-framing) or build the theory (jams-theory-development).

When to use

  • staking a JAMS manuscript's contribution against marketing literature
  • locating the paper in the right marketing-science stream
  • defending against 'incremental / new-context' challenges
  • citing the substantive canon expected by editors
  • identifying theoretical, empirical, and managerial gaps
  • building a contribution/comparison table
  • positioning by archetype (survey, secondary-data, experimental, meta-analytic)
  • reading the stream's current editorial signals

When not to use

  • stating the contribution in one sentence
  • building the theory
