commres-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a communication project fits Communication Research (CR) and is shaped as quantitative, theory-testing social science. CR rewards a clear communication-science question with a testable mechanism, not platform novelty or description. Helps frame the question; it does not collect data.

When to use

  • Choosing among possible projects or framings for a CR submission
  • Pressure-testing fit before investing
  • Deciding whether CR or a sibling is the right home
  • Sharpening a vague effect into a hypothesis-testing communication-science question
  • Reframing descriptive or qualitative ideas for a quantitative venue

When not to use

  • Collecting data
  • Qualitative or purely interpretive designs
  • Platform novelty or description without a testable mechanism
