jfm-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Journal of Financial Markets (JFM) manuscript against the microstructure frontier and its broad-finance siblings — staking a contribution the markets-literature insiders will recognize. Positions; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • Positioning JFM manuscripts against microstructure frontier
  • Differentiating from JF, JFE, RFS, and JFQA
  • Anchoring theory lineage (Kyle, Glosten-Milgrom, Easley-O'Hara)
  • Naming closest prior work and identifying gaps
  • Stating marginal contribution in trading-process terms
  • Pre-empting incremental charges
  • Mapping literature by microstructure sub-field
  • Writing the contribution paragraph

When not to use

  • Inventing evidence
  • Inventing citations
