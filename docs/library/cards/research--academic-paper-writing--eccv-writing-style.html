eccv-writing-style

separate

Use when writing or revising an ECCV paper in the Springer LNCS single-column format — spending the 14-page budget (figures and tables included), LNCS-specific layout craft, first-page architecture for a mixed European panel, numbered-citation hygiene, and claims phrased to survive a one-page rebuttal.

When to use

  • writing or revising an ECCV paper
  • spending the 14-page budget
  • LNCS-specific layout craft
  • first-page architecture for a mixed European panel
  • numbered-citation hygiene
  • claims phrased to survive a one-page rebuttal
  • drafting or compressing an ECCV manuscript

When not to use

  • two-column CVF page format
