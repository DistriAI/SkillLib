qje-identification

separate

Use when the causal identification strategy is the bottleneck for a Quarterly Journal of Economics (QJE) manuscript — RCT, staggered DID, IV, RDD, event study. Stress-tests the design to the QJE general-interest bar before tables are drafted.

When to use

  • RCT identification
  • staggered DID
  • IV
  • RDD
  • event study
  • novel descriptive / measurement paper
  • causal identification strategy bottleneck

When not to use

  • narrow within-field estimate
  • technique for its own sake
