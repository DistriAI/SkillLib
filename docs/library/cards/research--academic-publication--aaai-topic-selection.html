aaai-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a project is a strong AAAI submission across its broad AI scope, should be reframed or routed to a dedicated track such as AI for Social Impact or AI Alignment, or should instead go to IJCAI, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AISTATS, UAI, ACL, CVPR, KDD, CHI, ICRA, or another specialist venue.

When to use

  • deciding if a project is a strong AAAI submission
  • reframing projects for AAAI
  • routing to AI for Social Impact or AI Alignment tracks
  • routing to IJCAI, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AISTATS, UAI, ACL, CVPR, KDD, CHI, or ICRA
  • evaluating broad AI scope contributions
  • assessing general AI claims vs local application results
  • checking responsible treatment of ethics, safety, privacy, fairness, or social impact
  • verifying reproducibility paths for checklist scrutiny

When not to use

  • pure application deployment with little AI insight
  • benchmark bump without mechanism, analysis, or robust comparison
  • closed system with no reviewable evidence
  • papers better framed as statistics, NLP, vision, HCI, robotics, or systems for a specialist venue
  • policy-sensitive claims with thin ethics or stakeholder analysis
