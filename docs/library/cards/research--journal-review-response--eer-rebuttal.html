eer-rebuttal

separate

Use when a European Economic Review (EER) decision letter arrives (R&R or reject-with-appeal grounds) and a response strategy and letter are needed. Plans the revision and drafts the point-by-point response; it does not run new estimation or rewrite the whole paper.

When to use

  • EER R&R revision plan
  • EER response letter drafting
  • Triage of conflicting referee reports
  • Formal appeal strategy for EER reject
  • Point-by-point response to referee comments

When not to use

  • Running new estimation
  • Rewriting the whole paper
