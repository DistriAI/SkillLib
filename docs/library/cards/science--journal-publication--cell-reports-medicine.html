cell-reports-medicine

separate

Use when targeting Cell Reports Medicine (Cell Rep Med) or deciding whether a translational or clinical manuscript with mechanistic depth fits this open-access Cell Press venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Cell Reports Medicine
  • deciding if translational or clinical manuscript fits
  • manuscripts with mechanistic depth
  • choosing between Cell Reports Medicine and nature-medicine
  • choosing between Cell Reports Medicine and science-translational-medicine
  • high-quality open-access Cell Press venue selection
  • journal scope and evidence bar assessment
  • desk-reject criteria evaluation

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
