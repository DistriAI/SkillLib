edbt-supplementary

separate

Use when deciding what belongs in an EDBT paper body versus its artifact and appendices, covering the per-shape page budget with unlimited references, the rule that decision-critical evidence stays inside the reviewed pages, and how to split a database-systems paper between body and reproducible package.

When to use

  • deciding what belongs in an EDBT paper body versus its artifact and appendices
  • covering the per-shape page budget with unlimited references
  • applying the rule that decision-critical evidence stays inside the reviewed pages
  • splitting a database-systems paper between body and reproducible package
  • assembling EDBT supplementary material
  • checking reproducibility-forward supplementary rules
  • structuring in-body appendices
  • verifying partition of decision-critical evidence

When not to use

  • writing the full text of the paper
  • designing the database system itself
  • performing the experimental evaluation
  • managing the submission portal
