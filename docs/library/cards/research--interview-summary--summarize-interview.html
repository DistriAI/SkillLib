summarize-interview

combined

Turn one interview transcript into themes, supporting quotes, and action items. Use immediately after a session. For synthesising many sessions at once, use `affinity-diagram`. Summarize a customer interview transcript into a structured template with JTBD, satisfaction signals, and action items. Use when processing interview recordings or transcripts, synthesizing discovery interviews, or creating interview summaries.

When to use

  • summarizing one interview transcript
  • processing interview recordings or transcripts
  • synthesizing discovery interviews
  • creating interview summaries
  • identifying jobs-to-be-done
  • extracting pain points and workarounds
  • listing action items

When not to use

  • synthesising many sessions at once

Skill page

Conflict

Both copies summarize a single interview transcript, but they use different output structures and constraints. The phuryn copy is JTBD-centric with a fixed template (Current Solution, What They Like, Problems, Key Insights, Action Items) and a plain-language requirement. The Owl-Listener copy is theme-centric with participant profile, 3-5 themes, pain points with severity, workarounds, delighters, 5-8 verbatim quotes, surprises, and a confidence rating for each insight. Neither is a strict reproduction of the other, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
