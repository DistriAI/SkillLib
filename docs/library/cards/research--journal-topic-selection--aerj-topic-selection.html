aerj-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a topic fits the American Educational Research Journal (AERJ), now an integrated field-wide journal, and whether the manuscript's dominant education-research lens is clear. AERJ rewards broad significance to education research, not subfield-only description. Tests fit; it does not generate research questions.

When to use

  • Choosing or sharpening the question for an AERJ paper
  • Assessing if a topic is big enough for a flagship general journal
  • Naming the dominant education-research lens
  • Weighing AERJ vs a specialty outlet
  • Testing broad significance and field-wide stakes
  • Evaluating conceptual contribution and equity context

When not to use

  • Generating research questions
  • Subfield-only description without broader significance
  • Choosing a lens based on method rather than topic
  • Chasing trendiness over a durable conceptual question
