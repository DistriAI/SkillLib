psychrev-literature-positioning

separate

Use when locating a Psychological Review manuscript within its theoretical conversation — naming the theories it extends and, above all, the rival models it must beat or subsume. Positions the theory; it does NOT build the model (psychrev-theory-construction) or run the head-to-head prediction contrast (psychrev-argument-development).

When to use

  • locating a manuscript within its theoretical conversation
  • naming theories the manuscript extends
  • identifying rival models to beat or subsume
  • building a rival map for diagnostic phenomena
  • selecting a positioning stance (subsumption, adjudication, extension)
  • steelmanning rival theories at their current strongest form

When not to use

  • building the model
  • running the head-to-head prediction contrast
  • topic-level literature review with no specific rival models named
  • claiming false novelty when a standing model applies
