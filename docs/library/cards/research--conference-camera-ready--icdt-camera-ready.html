icdt-camera-ready

separate

Use when preparing the final LIPIcs camera-ready for an accepted ICDT (International Conference on Database Theory) paper — the lipics-v2021 final document, mandatory ACM CCS concepts and keywords, author ORCIDs, de-anonymization, complete proofs and the full-version link, CC-BY licensing, and passing the Dagstuhl/DROPS production checks that mint the DOI.

When to use

  • preparing final LIPIcs camera-ready
  • de-anonymizing accepted ICDT paper
  • adding mandatory ACM CCS concepts and keywords
  • restoring author ORCIDs and affiliations
  • linking full-version arXiv proofs
  • ensuring CC-BY licensing compliance
  • passing Dagstuhl/DROPS production checks

When not to use

  • ACM/IEEE camera-ready preparation
  • anonymous submission preparation
