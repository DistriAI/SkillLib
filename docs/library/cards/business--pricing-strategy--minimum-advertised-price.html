AI-powered skill for creating Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) policies, monitoring violations, and managing dealer compliance across e-commerce channels.

When to use

  • create MAP policies
  • monitor violations across channels
  • manage dealer compliance
  • design enforcement workflows
  • generate pricing frameworks
  • provide data-driven recommendations

When not to use

  • Requires your specific business data for accurate recommendations
  • Market benchmarks are based on US/EU data — adjust for other regions
  • Recommendations should be validated against your platform's current policies
  • Does not replace dedicated monitoring SaaS tools — designs the strategy and framework
