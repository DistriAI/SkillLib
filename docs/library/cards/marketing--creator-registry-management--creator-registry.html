creator-registry

separate

Use when the user asks "what did we pay this creator last time" or to "update the creator roster"; curates creator identity, rate, rights, exclusivity, compliance-event, and performance facts through the append-only creators event stream. Not for scoring fit — use fit-scorer; not for reviewing content — use creator-content-auditor. 创作者档案/达人名册

When to use

  • consolidating creator roster facts
  • querying creator roster facts
  • accepting pending creator proposals
  • deduplicating handles
  • recording closed-cycle rates
  • recording rights and exclusivity
  • recording compliance events
  • recording performance baselines

When not to use

  • scoring fit
  • reviewing content
  • calculating STAR score
  • judging compliance
  • choosing partners
