content-repurposer

combined

Transform one long-form piece into multiple platform-specific content derivatives including LinkedIn posts, tweet threads, email snippets, ad hooks, and video scripts while maintaining voice consistency. Use when given a blog post, article, or pillar content to atomize across channels. Platform: Google and Meta. Convierte un contenido en un pack completo multiplataforma — hilo de X/Twitter, post de LinkedIn, sección de newsletter, carrusel de Instagram y script de video de formato corto — cada uno reescrito nativamente para su plataforma, sin copiar y pegar. Úsalo cuando te pidan reutilizar contenido, atomizar un blog o vídeo, convertir una idea en muchos posts u obtener más rendimiento de un contenido. Produce borradores listos para publicar por plataforma con hooks, formato y CTAs ajustados a cada una.

When to use

  • Transforming long-form content into derivatives
  • Creating LinkedIn posts
  • Creating tweet threads
  • Creating email snippets
  • Creating ad hooks
  • Creating video scripts
  • Atomizing pillar content
  • Maintaining voice consistency

Skill page

Conflict

The two copies overlap in the core task (atomizing one source into platform-specific derivatives) but disagree on the target platforms and the required deliverables. The first copy targets LinkedIn, Twitter/X, email, ad hooks, and short video, with a specific 1-pillar-to-8-derivatives ratio and a detailed output template. The second copy targets X/Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletter, Instagram/LinkedIn carousel, and short video, and adds a scoring rubric, a publishing cadence plan, and a reference to an automation tool. Because each copy contains material steps and constraints the other lacks, crowning one would hide a real alternative.
