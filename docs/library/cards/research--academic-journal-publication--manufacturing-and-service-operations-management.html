manufacturing-and-service-operations-management

separate

Use when targeting Manufacturing and Service Operations Management (M&SOM) or deciding whether an operations-management theory / modeling / empirical manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting M&SOM
  • deciding if an OM manuscript fits M&SOM
  • re-framing modeling papers for operational insight
  • identifying M&SOM desk-reject risks
  • checking M&SOM method and evidence bar
  • reviewing M&SOM house style and structure
  • re-checking official M&SOM submission guidelines
  • suggesting alternative OM/OR/MS venues

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • pure OR methodology without OM payoff
  • broad management papers not OM-centered
  • technical novelty without operations payoff
