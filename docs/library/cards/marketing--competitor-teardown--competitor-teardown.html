competitor-teardown

combined

Systematic competitive analysis covering positioning, messaging hierarchy, objection handling, and CTA strategy from landing page URLs or screenshots. Use when pasting a competitor URL, uploading competitor screenshots, requesting positioning analysis, or needing to understand competitive messaging and differentiation strategies. Platform: Google and Meta. Produce un análisis competitivo estructurado para cualquier producto o mercado. Úsalo cuando se te pida un análisis competitivo, desglose competitivo, comparación de mercado, SWOT o mapa de posicionamiento. Genera un desglose estructurado con mapa de posicionamiento, comparativa de características, brechas de mensajería y recomendaciones estratégicas. Para un documento completo de landscape con matriz de características y análisis de victorias/derrotas usa competitive-analysis en su lugar.

When to use

  • positioning analysis
  • messaging hierarchy mapping
  • CTA strategy analysis
  • competitive differentiation strategies
  • landing page analysis
  • value proposition extraction
  • scoring competitor pages
  • identifying strengths and weaknesses

Skill page

Conflict

The two copies perform different teardown tasks. The first is a single-landing-page conversion audit (URL/screenshot input, 100-point rubric, messaging hierarchy, CTA/trust signals, red flags, and a fixed output template). The second is a multi-competitor market landscape analysis (2–5 competitors, positioning map, feature comparison table, SWOT, 0–40 rubric, and sourcing/confidence labels). They overlap in competitive messaging analysis but each contains material steps and constraints the other lacks, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
