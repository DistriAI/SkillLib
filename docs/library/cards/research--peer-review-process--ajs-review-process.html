ajs-review-process

separate

Use to understand how the American Journal of Sociology (AJS) evaluates a manuscript — its distinctive student-run, double-blind review out of the University of Chicago Department of Sociology, the "preject" screen, reviewer assignment by a Manuscript Assignment Board, and decision categories. Sets expectations and shapes the paper to survive review; it does not contact editors.

When to use

  • Stress-test the manuscript against the AJS process
  • Interpreting a decision letter
  • Understanding the 'preject' screen
  • Understanding reviewer assignment by a Manuscript Assignment Board
  • Shaping the paper to pass review
  • Calibrating expectations for AJS decisions

When not to use

  • Contacting editors
