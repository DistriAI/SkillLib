outcomes-ledger

separate

Shared step of the marketing playbooks (research-audience, write-outreach, work-with-creators): keeps the brand's record of the prospects or creators that were handed over. Tracks a status for each person, permanent opt-outs, re-check dates and dated lessons, which the next run reads. Loaded by those playbooks; not for direct use.

When to use

  • tracking prospect and creator status
  • managing permanent opt-outs and suppressions
  • recording dated lessons from outreach
  • updating ledger rows with outcomes
  • flagging stale rows for re-checking

When not to use

  • direct use by humans
  • contacting anyone
  • requiring outcomes
