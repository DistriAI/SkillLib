Conflict

Both copies define a structured interview script with warm-up, core exploration, and wrap-up, but they diverge on structure, duration, and guardrails. The phuryn copy is anchored in The Mom Test and JTBD with a specific note-taking template and willingness-to-pay probes. The Owl-Listener copy is anchored in Portigal's funnel approach, adds a specific scenarios section, facilitator notes, and a leading-question guardrail table. Neither is a strict reproduction of the other, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
