jpsp-open-science-and-transparency

separate

Use when meeting the Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP) transparency requirements — TOP Guidelines at Level 2, JARS reporting, data/code/materials disclosure to a trusted repository, preregistration status, and Registered Reports. Guides compliance; it does not create data or post your files for you.

When to use

  • Preparing transparency disclosures
  • Posting data/code/materials to a trusted repository
  • Deciding preregistration or Registered Report status
  • Writing data-availability statements
  • Writing preregistration statements
  • Completing JARS reporting tables

When not to use

  • Creating data
  • Posting files to repositories
  • Claiming or designing for open-science badges
