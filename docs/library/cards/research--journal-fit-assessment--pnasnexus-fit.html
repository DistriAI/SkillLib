pnasnexus-fit

separate

Use before any writing — as the first gate — to stress-test whether a result fits PNAS Nexus — high-quality, broadly significant, multidisciplinary research across biological/health/medical, physical sciences & engineering, and social & political sciences — and to make the realistic call between PNAS Nexus, flagship PNAS, and a field journal, including the PNAS-to-PNAS-Nexus transfer route.

When to use

  • Deciding if PNAS Nexus is the right venue before drafting
  • Judging whether to accept a PNAS Nexus transfer after a PNAS decline
  • Choosing between flagship PNAS, PNAS Nexus, and a field journal
  • Evaluating multi/interdisciplinary work that does not fit a single field journal
  • Assessing cross-disciplinary significance for a general scientific audience

When not to use

  • Writing or drafting the manuscript prose
  • Fixing unsound science or technical merit issues
  • Contributing papers as an NAS member to PNAS Nexus
