Use when targeting CA: A Cancer Journal for Clinicians (CA Cancer J Clin) or deciding whether an oncology manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting CA: A Cancer Journal for Clinicians
  • deciding whether an oncology manuscript fits this venue
  • assessing fit for invited reviews or clinical updates
  • understanding CA's editorial DNA
  • re-routing mis-targeted primary-research cancer manuscripts
  • evaluating Cancer Statistics series fit
  • checking method and evidence bar
  • reviewing house style and structure

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • submitting unsolicited primary research manuscripts
  • mechanistic laboratory research
  • primary clinical-trial data without a commissioned editorial framework
