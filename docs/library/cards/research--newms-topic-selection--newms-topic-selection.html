newms-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a topic fits New Media & Society (NM&S) and how to frame it for an interdisciplinary digital-media-and-society readership. Tests fit and stakes; it does not design the study or write the paper.

When to use

  • Deciding whether an idea belongs at NM&S vs. a sibling venue
  • Framing a study of one platform so it speaks to digital media and society broadly
  • Addressing feedback that work is too niche, just a case study, or atheoretical
  • Testing if a topic clears the four-part fit test
  • Evaluating if a contribution is a portable concept rather than a platform report
  • Assessing interdisciplinary reach and timely but not ephemeral claims

When not to use

  • Designing the study
  • Writing the paper
  • Quantitative US communication-science effects
  • Narrowly about information systems, policy, or ICT mechanics
  • Social-media-platform-specific empirical studies with little reach
  • Technical methods or systems
  • Pure platform walkthroughs or feature audits
  • Chasing a viral moment with no durable mechanism
