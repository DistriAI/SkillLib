annual-review-of-ecology-evolution-and-systematics

separate

Use when targeting Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics (AREES) or deciding whether a proposed synthesis fits this invited-review-only venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics
  • deciding whether a proposed synthesis fits AREES
  • choosing between AREES, Trends in Ecology & Evolution, and Biological Reviews
  • understanding AREES invited-review conventions
  • understanding AREES breadth expectations
  • understanding AREES desk-reject criteria
  • comprehensive syntheses across ecology, evolution, or systematics
  • conceptual and methodological reviews recasting field questions

When not to use

  • primary research
  • meta-analyses-as-primary-studies
  • opinion pieces
