govern-literature-positioning

separate

Use when situating a Governance: An International Journal of Policy, Administration, and Institutions manuscript in the comparative governance / public-policy / public-administration / institutions literatures and distinguishing it from sibling-journal audiences. Positions the contribution; it does not build the theory.

When to use

  • Drafting or revising the framing / literature section
  • Addressing reviewer comments about not engaging the comparative literature
  • Determining which scholarly conversation and journal audience to join
  • Establishing the gap the paper fills
  • Situating the gap in comparative governance, public policy, public administration, or institutions
  • Distinguishing the contribution from sibling-journal audiences
  • Applying the gap stress test to determine venue fit

When not to use

  • Building the theory
  • Reframing topic selection if the paper fits a sibling journal better
