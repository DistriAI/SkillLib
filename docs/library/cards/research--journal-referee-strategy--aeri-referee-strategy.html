aeri-referee-strategy

separate

Use when calibrating expectations for the fast, decisive American Economic Review: Insights (AER: Insights) review process — conditional-accept-or-reject decisions, the single-round model, and pre-empting the objections a short paper cannot afford. Explains the process and stress-tests the paper; it does not write the response letter (see aeri-rebuttal).

When to use

  • Understanding how AER: Insights decides
  • Deciding if a paper can survive without traditional R&R
  • Anticipating objections that would sink a short paper
  • Choosing between AER: Insights and journals with multiple rounds
  • Stress-testing the paper design
  • Pre-empting objections before submission

When not to use

  • Writing the response letter
