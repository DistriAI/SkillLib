msom-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM) manuscript within the operations-management literature — engaging the canonical OM streams (inventory, queueing, supply-chain contracting, revenue management, service operations, empirical OM), selecting the target editorial Department, and stating the operational conversation the paper joins.

When to use

  • Positioning M&SOM manuscripts within OM literature
  • Engaging canonical OM streams
  • Selecting target editorial Department
  • Distinguishing contribution from close OM antecedents
  • Stating the operational conversation the paper joins
  • Building a three-column map of incumbent conversation, tension, and delta
  • Comparing against sibling venues like Management Science or P&OM
  • Generating decision ledger rows for manuscript editing

When not to use

  • Generic gap-spotting with no engaged OM stream
  • Citing across all six departments causing ambiguous fit
  • Differentiating only by dataset or industry
  • Ignoring the closest analytical or empirical antecedent
