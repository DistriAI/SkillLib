jebo-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jebo-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal for a Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization (JEBO) submission. Routes by paper archetype — experiment, observational/behavioral empirics, behavioral theory, or agent-based simulation — but does not replace the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which jebo-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal
  • Diagnosing a draft's current bottleneck
  • Switching into revision mode after a JEBO decision letter
  • Routing by paper archetype (experiment, observational, theory, simulation)

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized jebo-* skills
  • Treating JEBO as interchangeable with Experimental Economics (ESA)
