sensys-experiments

separate

Use when designing or auditing a SenSys evaluation — energy and low-power measurement with a named instrument, real-testbed and deployment realism, honest sensor ground truth, on-device latency and memory, and same-hardware baselines, so the evidence meets SenSys's built-and-measured bar rather than a simulation or offline-benchmark one.

When to use

  • designing a SenSys evaluation
  • auditing a SenSys evaluation
  • energy and low-power measurement
  • real-testbed and deployment realism
  • honest sensor ground truth
  • on-device latency and memory
  • same-hardware baselines

When not to use

  • simulation-only claims
  • offline-benchmark claims
  • single-run bench results called a deployment
