er-introduction

separate

Use when drafting or revising the 引言 (introduction) of an 《经济研究》 manuscript — when the opening starts from a remote macro backdrop, when contributions read like "丰富了相关研究", or when the introduction fails to spoil the core finding. 本技能服务于《经济研究》(Economic Research Journal, ERJ)。

When to use

  • Drafting the introduction of an 《经济研究》 manuscript
  • Revising the introduction of an 《经济研究》 manuscript
  • Structuring the introduction into a five-paragraph funnel
  • Writing a compelling first sentence or hook
  • Formulating marginal contributions relative to specific literature
  • Ensuring the introduction spoils the core finding

When not to use

  • Writing the abstract
  • Writing the literature review
  • Writing the methodology section
  • Writing the conclusion section
