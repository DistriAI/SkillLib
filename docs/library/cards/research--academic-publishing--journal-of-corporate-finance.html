journal-of-corporate-finance

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Corporate Finance (JCF) or deciding whether a corporate-finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Corporate Finance
  • deciding if a corporate-finance manuscript fits JCF
  • re-framing general finance or governance papers as firm-financial-decision contributions
  • assessing JCF desk-reject risks
  • identifying credible top-3 or elite-field alternatives
  • evaluating corporate governance, M&A, capital structure, and payout topics
  • checking method and evidence bar for credible identification
  • reviewing house style and structure for JCF submissions

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • papers that are really asset pricing, banking, or accounting framed as corporate finance
  • governance-as-accounting or disclosure topics
  • economics of the firm topics
  • bank-centric financing or intermediation topics
  • broad applied or large volume topics
  • practitioner-relevant topics
