expecon-writing-style

separate

Use when the prose of an Experimental Economics (ExpEcon) manuscript buries the design or the result, or when the intro/abstract do not land for an experimentalist reader. Polishes structure and voice; it does not change the analysis.

When to use

  • Polishing ExpEcon manuscript structure
  • Improving abstract clarity with treatment contrast and headline effect
  • Refining Design/Procedures sections for reproducibility
  • Adjusting voice for procedural transparency
  • Quantifying results in subjects' units
  • Ensuring explicit no-deception statements
  • Calibrating external validity claims

When not to use

  • Changing the analysis
  • Writing behavioral essays (JEBO style)
  • Writing theory papers with demos (GEB style)
