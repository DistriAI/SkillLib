stoc-author-response

separate

Use when planning author-side communication for a STOC (ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing) submission, where no standing rebuttal phase exists — covering pre-emptive writing that answers objections in advance, chair-mediated technical clarifications if the PC asks, and the FOCS resubmission letter after a rejection.

When to use

  • planning author-side communication for STOC submission
  • pre-emptive writing answering objections in advance
  • chair-mediated technical clarifications
  • FOCS resubmission letter after rejection
  • building an objection ledger
  • decoding theory reviews
  • writing a resubmission memo

When not to use

  • emailing PC members or the chair to argue a decision
  • posting a public rebuttal thread attacking anonymous reviewers
  • leaving known weaknesses unaddressed unless asked
