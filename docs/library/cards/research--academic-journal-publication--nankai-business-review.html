nankai-business-review

separate

Use when targeting 《南开管理评论》(Nankai Business Review) — a top Chinese business-school journal for strategy, organizational behavior, HRM, marketing, and corporate governance. Apply when a paper builds management theory grounded in the China context, uses survey/SEM or multi-case methods, and needs theoretical contribution framed for an OB/strategy audience rather than econometric policy evaluation.

When to use

  • organizational behavior and human resources
  • strategy and entrepreneurship
  • marketing and consumer behavior
  • corporate governance from a management perspective
  • survey-based empirical studies using SEM or HLM
  • multi-case or grounded theory qualitative research
  • construct relationship modeling with mediation or moderation
  • management theory construction in China context

When not to use

  • macro policy effect evaluation
  • econometric policy evaluation
  • mathematical models with theorems or algorithms
  • capital market empirical corporate governance
  • pure financial or economic policy analysis
  • industrial or public management policy studies
