jppm-rebuttal

separate

Use when executing a Journal of Public Policy & Marketing (JPP&M) revise-and-resubmit — the response letter, converting reviewer demands into analyses or studies, and defending policy claims without slipping into advocacy. Drafts the rebuttal; letter-reading and feasibility calls are jppm-review-process.

When to use

  • Drafting the JPP&M response letter
  • Converting reviewer demands into analyses or studies
  • Defending policy claims without slipping into advocacy
  • Handling identification critiques of DiD/RDD
  • Resolving conflicts between methods and policy reviewers
  • Pushing back productively on comments

When not to use

  • Letter-reading
  • Feasibility calls
