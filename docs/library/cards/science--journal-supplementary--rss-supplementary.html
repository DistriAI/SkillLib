rss-supplementary

separate

Use when assembling RSS (Robotics: Science and Systems) supplementary material under the separate one-week-later deadline — deciding what leaves the self-contained main PDF, cutting robot video that functions as evidence rather than promotion, anonymizing footage and archives, and packaging appendices and trial logs.

When to use

  • deciding what leaves the self-contained main PDF
  • cutting robot video that functions as evidence
  • anonymizing footage and archives
  • packaging appendices and trial logs
  • drafting video storyboards
  • checking archive hygiene
  • mirroring main text section order in appendices

When not to use

  • writing the main PDF
  • submitting the paper before the supplement deadline
