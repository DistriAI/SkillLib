ectheory-workflow

separate

Use as the router for an Econometric Theory (ET) manuscript — decides which ectheory-* skill to invoke next across the theorem-proof lifecycle, from topic selection through single-anonymous review and rebuttal. Start here when unsure where you are.

When to use

  • Starting a new theorem-proof paper aimed at Econometric Theory
  • Deciding whether the paper is a full Article or a short Miscellanea
  • Routing to specific ectheory skills based on manuscript symptoms
  • Preflight and review-process skill selection before submission
  • Diagnosing bottlenecks in assumptions, proofs, simulations, or framing

When not to use

  • Empirical design tasks
  • Polishing format before math is sound
  • Tasks for Journal of Econometrics, Quantitative Economics, or Econometrica
