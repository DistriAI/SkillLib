govern-rebuttal

separate

Use when writing the response to a Governance: An International Journal of Policy, Administration, and Institutions revise-and-resubmit. The response must convert each of the ≥ 2 referees and the editor, answering comparative-reviewer concerns (case selection, generalizability across countries, measurement of institutions, alternative institutional explanations) point by point. Structures the response letter; it does not fabricate new results.

When to use

  • writing the response to a Governance R&R
  • planning the revision and response letter
  • reconciling demands from disagreeing referees
  • writing the cover memo to the editor
  • addressing case selection concerns
  • addressing generalizability across countries
  • addressing measurement of institutions
  • addressing alternative institutional explanations

When not to use

  • fabricating new results
