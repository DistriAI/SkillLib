jama-cover-letter

separate

Use when drafting the editor-facing cover letter that pitches a JAMA manuscript's general medical importance and confirms compliance. Frames the pitch and declarations; it does NOT write the manuscript or the abstract.

When to use

  • Drafting editor-facing cover letter
  • Pitching general medical importance
  • Confirming originality and registration
  • Stating design and reporting standard
  • Declaring IRB approval and consent
  • Affirming ICMJE authorship criteria
  • Disclosing conflicts and funding
  • Stating intended article type

When not to use

  • Writing the manuscript
  • Writing the abstract
  • Naming a specific editor-in-chief from memory
  • Rehashing the abstract
  • Overclaiming without support
  • Omitting registration or disclosure affirmations
  • Creating multi-page letters padded with background
  • Framing importance only for one subspecialty
