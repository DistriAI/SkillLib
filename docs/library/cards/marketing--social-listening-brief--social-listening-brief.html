social-listening-brief

combined

Use when the user wants a social listening report about what people are saying about a brand, person, product, topic, category, or niche across public social platforms. Produces cited themes, sentiment caveats, notable posts, and recommended actions. Produce a decision-ready brief of current brand, product, category, and competitor conversations across social platforms, including sentiment drivers, questions, risks, and growth opportunities.

When to use

  • what are people saying about X?
  • monitor this brand/topic/category
  • find complaints or praise about a product
  • summarize recent social discussion
  • compare sentiment across Reddit, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, or Threads

When not to use

  • exhaustive social monitoring
  • averaging sentiment across very different communities without caveats
  • using engagement as a proxy for truth
  • inferring sensitive personal traits
