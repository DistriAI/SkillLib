business-design

separate

Read financials, map competitive landscapes, and argue design decisions in the language of value. Use when defending design to commercial stakeholders. For the live negotiation itself, use `design-negotiation` (designer-toolkit).

When to use

  • Reading a P&L as a designer
  • Mapping competitive landscapes
  • Defending design in business language
  • Aligning design work to KPIs
  • Translating design thinking to business thinking
  • Identifying revenue and cost drivers
  • Framing design decisions as bets
  • Separating taste from evidence

When not to use

  • Live negotiation
