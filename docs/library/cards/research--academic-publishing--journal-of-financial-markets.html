journal-of-financial-markets

separate

Use when targeting Journal of Financial Markets (JFM) or deciding whether a market-microstructure / trading manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Journal of Financial Markets
  • deciding if a market-microstructure manuscript fits
  • re-framing general asset-pricing papers around microstructure
  • identifying JFM desk-reject risks
  • checking JFM house style and structure
  • evaluating method and evidence bar for microstructure

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • general asset pricing without microstructure mechanism
  • descriptive studies with no mechanism or design question
  • high-frequency analysis mishandling microstructure noise
