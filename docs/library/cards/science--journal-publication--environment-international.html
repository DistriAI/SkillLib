environment-international

separate

Use when targeting Environment International or deciding whether an environmental-exposure/health manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the population-relevant exposure-health evidence bar, exposome/contaminant expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for an environmental-exposure or environmental-health paper
  • re-framing exposure-assessment, contaminant, or exposome studies toward population-relevant health implication
  • deciding between Environment International and Environmental Health Perspectives or pure environmental-science venues
  • checking exposure–health evidence bar and desk-reject heuristics
  • human exposure assessment to chemical, physical, and biological agents
  • exposome and mixtures high-dimensional exposure characterization
  • environmental epidemiology linking exposures to health outcomes
  • emerging and legacy contaminants with measured exposure and health or risk endpoint

When not to use

  • purely environmental-monitoring paper with no health relevance
  • clinical study with no environmental exposure
  • replacing the journal's current author guidelines
