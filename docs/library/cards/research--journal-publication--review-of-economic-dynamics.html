review-of-economic-dynamics

separate

Use when targeting Review of Economic Dynamics (RED) or deciding whether a quantitative-macro / dynamic-economics manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Review of Economic Dynamics
  • deciding if quantitative-macro manuscript fits RED
  • re-framing applied-macro paper as dynamic-equilibrium insight
  • identifying RED desk-reject risks
  • finding credible quantitative-macro alternative venues

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • general-interest macro at top-5 journals
  • long-run growth and development dynamics
  • monetary/financial-macro
  • broad European general-economics framing
  • pure dynamic theory
