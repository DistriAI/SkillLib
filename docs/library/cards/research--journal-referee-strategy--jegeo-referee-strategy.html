jegeo-referee-strategy

separate

Use when anticipating referee objections for a Journal of Economic Geography (JEG) manuscript, where the panel typically pairs an economist with a geographer. Maps likely cross-disciplinary objections to fixes; it does not invent results.

When to use

  • pre-empting objections from a two-community panel
  • armoring the side a hostile referee will attack
  • deciding which robustness work to do now vs. holding for R&R
  • choosing which reviewers to suggest or oppose
  • framing the cover letter to balance the panel

When not to use

  • inventing results
