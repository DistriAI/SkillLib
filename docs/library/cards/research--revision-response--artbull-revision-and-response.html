artbull-revision-and-response

separate

Use when revising an Art Bulletin manuscript after a decision and writing the response to referees — converting double-blind reviewers without diluting the art-historical contribution, while keeping anonymity and the image/permissions plan on track. Structures the response; it does not fabricate new evidence.

When to use

  • Planning the revision and response letter after an R&R decision
  • Reconciling demands from disagreeing referees
  • Writing the cover note to the editor summarizing the revision
  • Structuring point-by-point responses to referee comments
  • Managing image permissions for new figures during revision

When not to use

  • Fabricating new evidence
  • Ignoring or quietly merging away a comment
  • Capitulating to requests that break the argument
  • Adding a new figure without clearing its permissions
  • Letting the revised manuscript lose anonymity
