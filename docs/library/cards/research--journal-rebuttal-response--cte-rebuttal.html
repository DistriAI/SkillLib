cte-rebuttal

separate

Use when responding to reviewer reports for a 《财贸经济》 R&R. Produces the point-by-point response letter and aligned manuscript edits. 本技能服务于《财贸经济》(Finance & Trade Economics, CTE)。

When to use

  • Responding to reviewer reports for 《财贸经济》 R&R
  • Producing point-by-point response letter
  • Producing aligned manuscript edits
  • Handling policy endogeneity and reverse causality
  • Addressing mechanism identification
  • Responding to heterogeneity dimensions
  • Reviewing editorial and committee opinions

When not to use

  • Tasks not related to 《财贸经济》 R&R
  • Initial manuscript submission without R&R
  • General academic writing without specific reviewer feedback
