kdd-author-response

separate

Use when drafting KDD rebuttals on OpenReview, where authors answer each review under a no-hyperlink rule, cannot upload revisions, and write for the area chair who recommends Accept, Reject, or Resubmit. Covers evidence-anchoring without links, per-reviewer triage, and turning a likely Resubmit into next-cycle material at ACM SIGKDD.

When to use

  • drafting KDD rebuttals on OpenReview
  • answering each review under a no-hyperlink rule
  • evidence-anchoring without links
  • per-reviewer triage
  • turning a likely Resubmit into next-cycle material
  • responding to area chair recommendations
  • handling factual misreads, missing baselines, scale skepticism, and scope objections
  • conceding points for resubmission

When not to use

  • uploading revisions
  • introducing new repositories, demos, or anonymous sites
  • using hyperlinks in rebuttals
  • promising camera-ready experiments not yet run
  • revealing identity or company names in Research Track responses
