crispr-offtarget-predictor

separate

Predicts potential off-target sites for a given sgRNA sequence using mismatch analysis. It helps researchers assess the specificity of their CRISPR design.

When to use

  • Designing new CRISPR experiments.
  • Validating sgRNA specificity before synthesis.
  • Analyzing potential safety risks in gene editing protocols.
