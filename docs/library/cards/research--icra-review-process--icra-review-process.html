icra-review-process

separate

Use when explaining or strategizing around ICRA's review pipeline — the RAS Conference Editorial Board of Editors and Associate Editors, reviewer recruitment through PaperPlaza, the absence of an author rebuttal in the classic ICRA flow, the January accept/reject decision, and how this differs from RA-L's revise-capable journal review.

When to use

  • Explaining ICRA review pipeline
  • Strategizing around ICRA review process
  • Understanding RAS Conference Editorial Board structure
  • Analyzing ICRA reviewer recruitment
  • Comparing ICRA and RA-L review processes
  • Planning for lack of author rebuttal
  • Calibrating ICRA acceptance expectations
  • Reading ICRA decision packets

When not to use

  • Writing ICRA papers
  • Formatting ICRA camera-ready submissions
  • Selecting ICRA topics
  • Creating ICRA supplementary materials
