jfr-fit-positioning

separate

Use to judge whether a manuscript fits 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) and to settle the line — macro-finance vs micro corporate-finance — re-routing to industrial-economics or accounting journals when the paper is not really a finance-mechanism paper.

When to use

  • judge manuscript fit for Journal of Financial Research
  • settle macro-finance vs micro corporate-finance line
  • re-route to industrial-economics or accounting journals
  • determine if paper is a finance-mechanism paper
  • assess if dependent variable is a financial variable
  • check if story relies on Chinese financial institutions
  • evaluate single-line narrative consistency
  • provide re-submission suggestions

When not to use

  • topics where finance is only a control variable
  • papers focused on industrial upgrading or regional economy
  • papers focused on accounting information quality or auditing
  • papers focused on management theory or strategy
  • papers with mixed macro-transmission and corporate governance narratives
  • general empirical papers without specific financial mechanisms
