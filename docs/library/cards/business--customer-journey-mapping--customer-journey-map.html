combined

Create a customer journey map across stages, touchpoints, actions, emotions, and metrics. Use when diagnosing a broken experience or aligning a team on the full customer flow. Construye un mapa de recorrido del cliente para un producto, servicio o experiencia. Úsalo cuando se te pida mapear un recorrido del cliente, crear un viaje del usuario, documentar puntos de contacto y puntos de fricción, o diseñar un mapa de experiencia. Produce un mapa de recorrido completo con etapas, puntos de contacto, emociones, puntos de fricción y oportunidades priorizadas.

Skill page

Conflict

The three copies overlap on the core task (mapping stages, touchpoints, emotions, pain points, and opportunities) but each contains material steps or constraints the others lack. phuryn uses a 7-stage model (Awareness through Advocacy) and explicitly asks for critical moments (Aha, Moments of Truth, Churn triggers) plus a prioritized improvement list. deanpeters uses a 5-stage model (Awareness through Loyalty) and adds KPIs, business goals, teams involved, prerequisites, and anti-patterns. mohitagw15856 uses a 6-stage model (Conciencia through Defensa) and adds a strict output template, a current-vs-future state distinction, and a rule to mark assumptions. Because the stage models and required artifacts disagree, crowning one copy would hide real alternatives.
