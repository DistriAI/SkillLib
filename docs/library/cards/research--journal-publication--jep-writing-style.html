jep-writing-style

separate

Use when revising sentence-level voice and the abstract/opening for a Journal of Economic Perspectives (JEP) article — engaging, clear, authoritative-but-readable, minimal notation. Polishes prose and front matter; defer structure to jep-narrative-arc and evidence framing to jep-evidence-without-equations.

When to use

  • revising sentence-level voice
  • revising the abstract
  • revising the opening
  • polishing prose
  • polishing front matter
  • removing notation from body text
  • improving readability

When not to use

  • structure
  • evidence framing
  • narrative arc
  • balance and objectivity
