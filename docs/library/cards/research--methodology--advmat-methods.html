advmat-methods

separate

Use when deciding what characterization and experimental detail an Advanced Materials manuscript must show to prove its structure–property claim, and what belongs in the Experimental Section and Supporting Information. Partitions and stress-tests methods; does not design figures or run analysis.

When to use

  • deciding characterization detail for Advanced Materials manuscripts
  • partitioning content between Experimental Section and Supporting Information
  • stress-testing methods for structure-property claims
  • evaluating multi-technique triangulation
  • checking benchmarking against state-of-the-art
  • assessing reproducibility and statistics

When not to use

  • designing figures
  • running analysis
