chemical-reviews

separate

Use when targeting Chemical Reviews (Chem Rev) or deciding whether a chemistry review manuscript fits this ACS venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. NOTE: Chemical Reviews is a largely INVITED venue; unsolicited primary-data manuscripts will be desk-rejected.

When to use

  • targeting Chemical Reviews
  • deciding if a chemistry review fits Chem Rev
  • evaluating comprehensive review against Chem Rev standard
  • understanding why primary data should be redirected
  • preparing unsolicited review proposal
  • assessing fit for interdisciplinary chemistry reviews

When not to use

  • primary research papers
  • progress reports on a single group's work
  • short thematic reviews
  • opinion pieces
  • replacing current official submission guidelines
