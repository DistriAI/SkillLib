Conflict

The copies overlap heavily on the 'Operational Arsenal' (probes, regexes, wordlists) but diverge on scope and safety. SnailSploit/Claude-Red is a broad methodology skill covering people, social media, and crypto, lacking the specific technical probes of the others. uphiago/recon-skills and elementalsouls/Claude-OSINT are technical recon arsenals, but elementalsouls includes a much larger secret-pattern catalog (80 vs 48) and mobile/APK analysis. sickn33/agentic-awesome-skills is a mirror of the uphiago/elementalsouls content but adds a mandatory 'confirmation gate' for authorization that the others lack. Because the SnailSploit copy covers a different domain (human/OSINT) and the sickn33 copy adds a critical safety constraint not present in the others, no single copy is the 'authentic' procedure for all.
