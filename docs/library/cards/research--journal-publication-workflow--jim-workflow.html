jim-workflow

separate

Use when starting or unblocking a Journal of International Marketing (JIM) manuscript and you need to pick the right jim-* sub-skill for the current stage, from first idea through the response letter. It routes to the specialist skills; it does not do their work.

When to use

  • Starting a JIM manuscript
  • Unblocking a JIM manuscript
  • Picking the right jim-* sub-skill
  • Sequencing revision work after a decision letter
  • Debating JIM vs. other journals
  • Routing questions about adding countries

When not to use

  • Doing the work of specialist skills
  • Single-country papers without theoretical international dimension
