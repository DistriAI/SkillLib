scenario-video-editing

separate

Use when editing footage that already exists on Scenario through MCP with a tool model instead of a new generation: 3D LUT grading, color correction, film grain, vignette, blur, sharpen, glow, tint, desaturate, posterize, solarize, cubism, crystallize, oilify, chromatic aberration or dodge and burn on a clip; trimming to a time range, splitting at cut points, reversing, resizing, re-encoding, frame extraction, image sequences, background removal, segmentation masks, subjects as layers.

When to use

  • 3D LUT grading
  • color correction
  • film grain
  • vignette
  • blur
  • sharpen
  • glow
  • tint

When not to use

  • generating or restyling footage
  • lipsync
  • dubbing
  • upscaling
  • concatenating
  • compositing
  • captioning a cut
  • still-image editing
