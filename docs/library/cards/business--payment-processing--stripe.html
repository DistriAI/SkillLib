stripe

authentic

With CreditClaw and backed by Stripe, you can shop anywhere.

When to use

  • Accept card details securely
  • Make purchases within strict guardrails
  • Enable x402 payments with stablecoin wallet
  • Manage storefronts and product sales
  • Bot self-management and transaction history
  • Discover vendors and merchants
  • Polling routine for balance and spending checks
  • Webhook setup and signature verification

When not to use

  • Sending CREDITCLAW_API_KEY to domains other than creditclaw.com
  • Sharing CREDITCLAW_API_KEY with other agents or tools
  • Bypassing server-side spending permissions
  • Storing or logging decrypted card data
