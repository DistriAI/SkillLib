meeting-minutes-taker

separate

Transforms raw meeting transcripts into structured, evidence-based meeting minutes — merges multiple versions without losing content, reviews existing minutes against the transcript for missing items, and identifies anonymous speakers (Speaker 1/2/3, 发言人1). Triggers on 会议纪要, 会议记录, 整理纪要, 妙记转纪要, "write meeting minutes", "merge these minutes". Not for fixing ASR/STT errors first (use transcript-fixer).

When to use

  • Transform raw meeting transcripts into structured minutes
  • Merge multiple versions of minutes without losing content
  • Review existing minutes against transcript for missing items
  • Identify anonymous speakers (Speaker 1/2/3, 发言人1)
  • Write meeting minutes
  • Merge these minutes
  • 整理纪要
  • 妙记转纪要

When not to use

  • Fixing ASR/STT errors first
