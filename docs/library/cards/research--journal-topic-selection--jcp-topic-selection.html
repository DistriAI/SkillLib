jcp-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether a consumer phenomenon belongs at the Journal of Consumer Psychology (JCP) versus a sibling, and which JCP manuscript type fits, before theory and studies are locked. Sets scope and outlet fit; it does not build the mechanism (jcp-theory-development).

When to use

  • deciding whether a consumer phenomenon belongs at JCP
  • choosing between JCP, JCR, JMR, Marketing Science, or Psychological Science
  • determining which JCP manuscript type fits
  • evaluating if a finding is psychological-process enough for JCP
  • setting scope and outlet fit before theory and studies are locked

When not to use

  • building the mechanism
  • jcp-theory-development
