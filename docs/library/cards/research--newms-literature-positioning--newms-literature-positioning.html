newms-literature-positioning

separate

Use when situating a New Media & Society (NM&S) manuscript in the right interdisciplinary conversation and naming the specific gap or rival account it advances. Builds the positioning; it does not build the theory or design.

When to use

  • Building the related-work section
  • Building the gap statement
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on missed key work
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on ignored fields
  • Addressing reviewer feedback on reinvented concepts
  • Deciding which conversation the paper is in

When not to use

  • Building the theory
  • Building the design
