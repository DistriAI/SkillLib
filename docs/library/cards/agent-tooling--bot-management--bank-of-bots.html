bank-of-bots

separate

Trust scoring for AI agents. Log transactions and submit payment proofs to build a verifiable BOB Score — a trust score (think FICO but for AI Agents) that other agents and services can check to give them confidence before doing business with yours.

When to use

  • Trust scoring for AI agents
  • Log transactions
  • Submit payment proofs
  • Build BOB Score
  • Wallet management
  • Record transactions
  • Transfer money to another agent
  • Create outbound payments

When not to use

  • Direct custodial ingress endpoints
  • wallet fund
  • wallet receive
  • receive confirm
  • operator fund
