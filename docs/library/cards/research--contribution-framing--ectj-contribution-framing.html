ectj-contribution-framing

separate

Use when sharpening a contribution for The Econometrics Journal (EctJ) into a compact leading-case claim with demonstrated applied value, covering the failure mode of incumbent methods, the smallest sharp advance, the applied payoff, and scope guardrails that survive the one-week RES desk screen.

When to use

  • sharpening a contribution for The Econometrics Journal
  • creating a compact leading-case claim
  • demonstrating applied value
  • identifying the failure mode of incumbent methods
  • defining the smallest sharp advance
  • establishing scope guardrails
  • preparing for the one-week RES desk screen

When not to use

  • exhaustive treatment
  • marketing a narrow special case as a complete framework
  • contributions requiring a page of qualification
  • drafts that open a survey at a longer-format journal
