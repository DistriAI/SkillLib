organization-science

separate

Use when targeting Organization Science (OrgSci) or deciding whether an organization theory / behavior / innovation manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Organization Science
  • deciding venue fit for organization theory manuscripts
  • framing organization behavior or innovation papers
  • checking method-and-evidence bar
  • reviewing house style
  • official-submission re-check
  • identifying desk-reject heuristics
  • re-routing to alternative journals

When not to use

  • replacing current official submission guidelines
  • operations/optimization papers with no organizational-theory payoff
  • pure analytics papers with no organizational-theory payoff
