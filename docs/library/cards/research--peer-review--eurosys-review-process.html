eurosys-review-process

separate

Use when reasoning about how EuroSys decides papers — the two independent yearly rounds feeding one April conference, HotCRP double-blind reviewing, the accept/revise/reject outcome triple, the same-season resubmission ban after rejection, and community structures like the Shadow PC that shape who reviews.

When to use

  • modeling EuroSys decision machinery
  • understanding two yearly rounds feeding one conference
  • analyzing HotCRP double-blind reviewing
  • interpreting accept/revise/reject outcomes
  • understanding same-season resubmission bans
  • analyzing Shadow PC community structures
  • forecasting review outcomes
  • extracting structural insights from reviews

When not to use

  • writing the paper itself
  • conducting experiments
  • managing HotCRP instance settings
  • scheduling public talks during review
