scenario-gemini-image

separate

Use when generating or editing images with Google's Gemini image models (Nano Banana) on Scenario via MCP: text-to-image, natural-language instruction editing, identity locking or style transfer from reference images, multi-image fusion, pulling stills from a video clip, Google Search grounding, thinking level tuning, 512 to 4K output, or choosing between Flash, Pro, and Lite. Keywords: Gemini 3.1 Flash, Gemini 3.0 Pro, Gemini 3.1 Lite, Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro, txt2img, img2img.

When to use

  • text-to-image generation
  • natural-language instruction editing
  • identity locking from reference images
  • style transfer from reference images
  • multi-image fusion
  • pulling stills from a video clip
  • Google Search grounding
  • choosing between Flash, Pro, and Lite models

When not to use

  • Gemini video generation
  • Gemini speech models
  • mask painting
  • seed-based reproduction
  • negative-prompt usage
