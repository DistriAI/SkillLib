orgsci-topic-selection

separate

Use when choosing or sharpening a research question for an Organization Science manuscript — testing whether it is a theory-driven question about organizations spanning micro to macro, and whether Organization Science (rather than ASQ, AMJ, Management Science, or a field journal) is the right venue.

When to use

  • Choosing a research question for Organization Science
  • Sharpening a research question
  • Testing venue fit for Organization Science
  • Evaluating if a question is theory-driven
  • Comparing Organization Science against ASQ, AMJ, or Management Science
  • Assessing contribution sources
  • Determining level of analysis
  • Running a fit pass for Organization Science

When not to use

  • Tasks not involving Organization Science manuscript preparation
  • Questions without an organization-theory engine
  • Forcing causal-identification framing unsupported by data
  • Pitching pure novelty without a contribution
