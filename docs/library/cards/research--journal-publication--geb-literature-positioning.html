geb-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning a game-theory manuscript against the existing literature for Games and Economic Behavior (GEB) — staking what is new relative to known solution concepts, theorems, mechanisms, and prior experiments. Sharpens the contribution; it is not a standalone survey.

When to use

  • Positioning a game-theory manuscript for GEB
  • Staking contribution relative to known solution concepts
  • Naming specific prior theorems or mechanisms
  • Pre-empting special-case objections
  • Distinguishing prior conference versions
  • Citing cross-field results in CS, biology, or political science
  • Testing manuscript against GEB positioning bar
  • Generating a decision ledger for manuscript edits

When not to use

  • Standalone survey
  • Explaining game theory to outsiders
  • Polishing without re-routing if a neighboring outlet has a stronger audience claim
