aaai-related-work

separate

Use when positioning an AAAI paper's novelty against archival work, contemporaneous arXiv or workshop papers, and AAAI/IJCAI/NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR neighbors across the broad AI scope, while staying inside AAAI's dual-submission and AI-as-source policy constraints and writing a related-work section legible to non-specialist reviewers.

When to use

  • positioning AAAI paper novelty
  • comparing against archival AI papers
  • comparing against contemporaneous arXiv or workshop papers
  • differentiating from AAAI/IJCAI/NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR neighbors
  • writing related-work section for non-specialist reviewers
  • checking dual-submission policy constraints
  • verifying citations against real sources

When not to use

  • submitting substantially similar work to multiple archival venues at the same time
  • using AI systems as citable scientific sources
