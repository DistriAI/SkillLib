science-advances

separate

Use when targeting Science Advances (Sci Adv) or deciding whether a multidisciplinary open-access manuscript fits this AAAS venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting Science Advances
  • deciding if a manuscript fits Science Advances
  • re-routing from Science to Science Advances
  • distinguishing Science Advances from Nature Communications and PNAS
  • checking method and evidence bar
  • checking house style and structure
  • official-submission re-check
  • desk-reject heuristics

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
