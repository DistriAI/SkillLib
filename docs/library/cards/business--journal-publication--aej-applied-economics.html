aej-applied-economics

separate

Use when targeting American Economic Journal: Applied Economics or deciding whether an applied micro manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, method-and-evidence bar, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting American Economic Journal: Applied Economics
  • deciding whether an applied micro manuscript fits this venue
  • evaluating fit for labor, development, health, or education papers
  • assessing method-and-evidence bar for credible causal identification
  • checking house style and structure for AEJ: Applied
  • identifying desk-reject risks
  • re-routing to alternative journals like AER or field-specific journals

When not to use

  • replacing the journal's current official submission guidelines
  • heavy structural modeling
  • general-interest work suitable for AER
  • policy-evaluation framing suitable for AEJ: Economic Policy
