Use when targeting Conference on Health, Inference, and Learning (CHIL) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for AI for health.

When to use

  • targeting Conference on Health, Inference, and Learning (CHIL)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for AI for health
  • machine learning for health, clinical inference, health systems
  • fairness, robustness, and deployment constraints
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • generic CS paper without naming the exact subcommunity, contribution type, and proof or empirical standard
  • optimization, scheduling, or operations-research papers just because they use medical examples
  • central contribution is solver design, resource allocation, or constraint modeling
  • general machine-learning or data-mining contribution is strong without the clinical setting
