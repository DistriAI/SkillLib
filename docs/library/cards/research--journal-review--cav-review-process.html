cav-review-process

separate

Use when reasoning about how a CAV (Computer Aided Verification) submission is evaluated, covering the two-stage reviewing process (two reviews then an early-reject filter, then two more reviews with a rebuttal), the partial double-anonymity by category, the accept/reject outcome, the optional non-conditional artifact evaluation, and how CAV differs from TACAS and FMCAD.

When to use

  • reasoning about CAV submission evaluation
  • modeling the two-stage reviewing process
  • understanding partial double-anonymity by category
  • interpreting accept/reject outcomes
  • understanding optional non-conditional artifact evaluation
  • comparing CAV with TACAS and FMCAD

When not to use

  • resubmitting unchanged after early reject
  • treating stage 1 as a formality
  • treating the rebuttal as a debate
  • assuming artifact evaluation gates acceptance
  • projecting TACAS or FMCAD process onto CAV
