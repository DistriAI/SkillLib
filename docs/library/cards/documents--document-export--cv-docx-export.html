cv-docx-export

authentic

Turn a finished CV into a parsing-safe Word file ready to upload: one column, real heading styles, no tables, headers or images, the right paper size, and correct fonts for Chinese, Japanese or Korean text. Use when asked to give me my CV as a Word file, export my CV to docx, make an ATS-safe CV file, or produce my 简历 as a Word document. Produces the .docx file, a text read-back proving it parses, and the upload file name.
