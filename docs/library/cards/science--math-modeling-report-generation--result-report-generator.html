result-report-generator

authentic

Summarize modeling experiment evidence, compare the approved main method with a usable baseline, surface fallback triggers, and produce a decision-point or final report without creating routine per-round prose.

When to use

  • Turn saved experiment artifacts into compact evidence. Do not treat ordinary successful runs as requiring a long report, and do not choose the winning method.

When not to use

  • Creating routine per-round prose
  • Choosing the winning method
  • Generating paper prose
  • Creating result-report-generator_modeler_decision.md
  • Archiving from an AI suggestion alone
  • Treating diagnostic reference as usable baseline
