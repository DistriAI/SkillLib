ngeo-cover-letter

separate

Use when writing the cover letter for a Nature Geoscience submission, which must convince an in-house editor that the work is a broad-interest Earth-system advance appropriate for a diverse readership. Drafts the editorial case; does not edit the manuscript itself.

When to use

  • Writing cover letter for Nature Geoscience submission
  • Drafting editorial case for in-house editor
  • Reframing after decline without review on interest grounds
  • Noting suggested or excluded referees

When not to use

  • Editing the manuscript itself
  • Writing abstracts or summaries
  • Overselling significance or impact
