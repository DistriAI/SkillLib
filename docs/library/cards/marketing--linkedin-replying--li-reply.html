li-reply

separate

Handle the replies under the user's own LinkedIn posts - draft answers to every comment, sorted by which ones are worth answering. Use when the user pastes the comments on their post, says "reply to these", "handle my comments", "someone said X on my post", or is dealing with a critic or a lead in the comments.

When to use

  • drafting answers to comments on own posts
  • sorting comments by worth answering
  • handling critics in comments
  • responding to leads in comments
  • replying to peers and supporters
  • triaging noise and spam

When not to use

  • scraping threads with a browser tool
  • posting replies automatically
  • replying to pitches in comments
