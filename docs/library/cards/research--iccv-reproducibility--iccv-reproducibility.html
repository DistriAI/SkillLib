iccv-reproducibility

separate

Use when hardening the reproducibility story of an ICCV paper, covering full recipe disclosure without a mandated compute form, protocol pinning for foundation-model and zero-shot evaluations, seed and variance honesty at vision training scale, and writing results that stay checkable across the two-year gap to the next ICCV.

When to use

  • hardening the reproducibility story of an ICCV paper
  • full recipe disclosure without a mandated compute form
  • protocol pinning for foundation-model and zero-shot evaluations
  • seed and variance honesty at vision training scale
  • writing results that stay checkable across the two-year gap
  • compute disclosure without a form
  • determinism paragraph writing
  • withheld test sets and server etiquette

When not to use

  • CVPR paper reproducibility
  • reproducibility checklist verification at check time
