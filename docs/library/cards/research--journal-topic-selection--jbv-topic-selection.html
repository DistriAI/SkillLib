jbv-topic-selection

separate

Use when shaping or stress-testing a research question for the Journal of Business Venturing (JBV) — confirming the entrepreneurial phenomenon is central, picking a multidisciplinary lens, and checking fit before investing in theory or data. Defines the question; it does not build the mechanism (jbv-theory-development) or frame the contribution (jbv-contribution-framing).

When to use

  • shaping a research question for JBV
  • stress-testing a research question for JBV
  • confirming the entrepreneurial phenomenon is central
  • picking a multidisciplinary lens
  • checking fit before investing in theory or data
  • deciding if an idea belongs in JBV
  • identifying the primary disciplinary lens
  • triaging venue routing for entrepreneurship papers

When not to use

  • building the mechanism
  • framing the contribution
