jfr-heterogeneity

separate

Use when the heterogeneity analysis of a 《金融研究》 (Journal of Financial Research) manuscript is too coarse or atheoretical — to cut along finance-relevant dimensions (ownership, bank type, market segment, financing constraint, region) that the mechanism predicts, not arbitrary splits.

When to use

  • heterogeneity analysis of a 《金融研究》 manuscript
  • cutting along finance-relevant dimensions
  • ownership, bank type, market segment, financing constraint, region
  • dimensions predicted by the mechanism
  • group coefficient difference tests
  • aligning heterogeneity with mechanism conclusions

When not to use

  • arbitrary splits
  • data fishing
  • splits unrelated to the mechanism
  • stacking splits without theoretical basis
  • claiming heterogeneity based only on individual group significance
