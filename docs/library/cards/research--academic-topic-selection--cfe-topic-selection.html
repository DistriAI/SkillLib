cfe-topic-selection

separate

Use when a Journal-of-Finance-and-Economics manuscript is at the topic-selection stage and the theoretical grounding, China-reality fit, marginal-contribution sentences, or venue match are unsettled. Produces the contribution-sentence template and the topic-fit checklist.

When to use

  • topic-selection stage
  • theoretical grounding
  • China-reality fit
  • marginal-contribution sentences
  • venue match
  • contribution-sentence template
  • topic-fit checklist

When not to use

  • pure theory or structural estimation
  • management or case studies
  • operational policy implications
