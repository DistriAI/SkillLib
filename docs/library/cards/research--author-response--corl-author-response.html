corl-author-response

separate

Use when drafting the CoRL rebuttal — a single-page PDF due days after reviews are released, aimed at reviewers, the Area Chair, and the discussion window that follows. Covers triage against the first-round gate, one-page layout economy, presenting new numbers compactly, and tone for an exchange that becomes public on acceptance.

When to use

  • drafting the CoRL rebuttal
  • triage against the first-round gate
  • one-page layout economy
  • presenting new numbers compactly
  • tone for an exchange that becomes public on acceptance
  • answering objections by type
  • managing the discussion window
  • final checklist for submission

When not to use

  • re-explaining the paper
  • quoting long review passages back
  • answering every minor point at equal depth
  • promising future work as a substitute for a number
