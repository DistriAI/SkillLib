qe-literature-positioning

separate

Use to stake a Quantitative Economics (QE) manuscript's contribution against the frontier — what quantitative gap it closes relative to prior empirical, structural, and methodological work, and against its Econometric Society siblings. Positions the paper; it does not write a standalone literature survey.

When to use

  • Staking a QE manuscript's contribution against the frontier
  • Identifying the quantitative gap closed relative to prior work
  • Distinguishing the paper from Econometric Society siblings
  • Positioning the paper without writing a standalone literature survey
  • Naming load-bearing predecessors and the precise margin of departure
  • Distinguishing the closest competing paper
  • Identifying the empirical, structural, or methodological strand of the contribution
  • Addressing referee pushback regarding novelty and journal fit

When not to use

  • Writing a standalone literature survey
  • Writing a literature-dump section that lists everything and positions nothing
  • Positioning a method-first contribution as QE when Econometrica is the real home
  • Citing only working papers when published or stable-archive versions exist
