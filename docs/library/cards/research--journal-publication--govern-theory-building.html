govern-theory-building

separate

Use when building the argument and theoretical contribution of a Governance: An International Journal of Policy, Administration, and Institutions manuscript — to theories of the state, institutions, the policy process, bureaucracy, governance networks, and accountability, with portability across cases and disciplined concept formation. Builds the argument; it does not test it.

When to use

  • Stating the core argument, mechanism, and scope conditions
  • Defining or refining a concept for comparative use
  • Specifying what the theory predicts that rivals do not
  • Building portable arguments with explicit scope conditions
  • Applying Sartori-style discipline to concept formation
  • Identifying contribution types like new mechanisms or reconceptualizations
  • Writing the strongest-rival test sentence

When not to use

  • Testing the theory
  • Running the empirics
