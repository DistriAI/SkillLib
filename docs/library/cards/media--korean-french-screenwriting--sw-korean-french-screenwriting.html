sw-korean-french-screenwriting

separate

Korean and French screenwriting practice (韩国/法国编剧方法) — from the international masterclass; Oh Seung-uk (Christmas in August, The Shameless; field research in disguise, "write the emotion", first act and last act first, spaces as skeleton, reading to hostile listeners, surviving producers), Choi Seok-hwan (King and the Clown; genre as promise to the audience, "story = deepening crisis + intensifying desire", two reversals and bittersweet endings, the producer/director/writer trio), and Frédéric Auburtin (Paris je t'aime; dialogue last, Italian-style table reads, "12 lines not 13", dignity for characters, originals born from commissions, editing by emotion). Use when working in a Korean-style commercial drama or genre film, a French auteur/commission context, when planning field research, when a producer relationship is the problem, or when you need the Korean "three live together" development model or the French "emotion first, structure second" editing approach.
