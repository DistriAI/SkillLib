amanthro-topic-selection

separate

Use when deciding whether an anthropology project fits American Anthropologist (AA) and which section to target. AA is the four-field AAA flagship, so the test is significance across anthropology (sociocultural, archaeology, biological, linguistic), not single-subfield novelty. Helps frame the question and pick a section; it does not collect data or conduct fieldwork.

When to use

  • Choosing among possible projects or framings for an AA submission
  • Addressing feedback that a paper feels too narrow or subfield-only
  • Deciding among Research Article and shorter/engaged sections
  • Pressure-testing fit and section before investing in a project
  • Framing questions for American Anthropologist submissions

When not to use

  • Collecting data
  • Conducting fieldwork
