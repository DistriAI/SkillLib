Step of the plan-campaign playbook: marks each candidate channel Run, Test, Later or No with a brand-fact reason and founder hours. Run plan-campaign instead; not for direct use.

When to use

  • Marking candidate channels Run, Test, Later, or No
  • Assigning brand-fact reasons to channel decisions
  • Calculating founder hours per leg
  • Determining execution per leg (GooseWorks levers or human hands)
  • Writing the Channel decisions section of the brief

When not to use

  • Direct use outside the plan-campaign playbook
