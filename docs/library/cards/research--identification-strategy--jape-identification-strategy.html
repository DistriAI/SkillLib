jape-identification-strategy

separate

Use when designing or defending the empirical identification of a Journal of Applied Econometrics (JAE) manuscript — a credible strategy applied to real data, with assumptions stated, tested, and reproducible. Covers time-series, panel, IV, and quasi-experimental designs typical of applied econometrics, tying every claim to depositable evidence.

When to use

  • Choosing or defending the empirical design for a JAE paper
  • Mapping identifying assumptions to depositable tests
  • Time-series and macro-econometrics identification
  • Panel and dynamic panel GMM diagnostics
  • IV and GMM weak-instrument robustness
  • Quasi-experimental designs like DID, event study, and RDD
  • Forecasting and structural identification
  • Reproducibility and archive artifact mapping

When not to use

  • Theoretical identification proofs
  • Tasks not involving real data applications
  • Non-JAE journal specific formatting
