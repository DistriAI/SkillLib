jfe-robustness

separate

Use when a Journal of Financial Economics (JFE) result needs the exhaustive robustness battery the journal is known for — alternative measures, subsamples, specifications, and falsification. Builds the stress-test plan; it does not choose the core estimator (jfe-empirical-design) or the identification design (jfe-identification).

When to use

  • alternative measures
  • alternative specifications
  • alternative samples
  • alternative inference
  • falsification and placebos
  • ruling out alternative explanations
  • main-text vs. Internet Appendix split

When not to use

  • choosing the core estimator
  • choosing the identification design
