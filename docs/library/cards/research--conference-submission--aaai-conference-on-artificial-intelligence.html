aaai-conference-on-artificial-intelligence

separate

Use when targeting AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for AI/ML flagship.

When to use

  • targeting AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for AI/ML flagship
  • broad artificial intelligence across reasoning, learning, search, knowledge, agents, and applications
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • manuscripts where the central contribution is not genuinely in AI/ML flagship
  • narrow ML method papers where ML reviewers are primary (route to ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR)
  • European AI fit (route to ECAI)
  • international AI flagship cycle/community fit (route to IJCAI)
