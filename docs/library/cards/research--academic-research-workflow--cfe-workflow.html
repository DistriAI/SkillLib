cfe-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which cfe-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal for 《财经研究》 (Journal of Finance and Economics). Routes — does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which cfe-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work from topic selection through rebuttal
  • judging current bottleneck from a draft
  • switching to rebuttal mode after receiving review comments
  • managing workflow state when switching between empirical, writing, and response tasks

When not to use

  • replacing specialized cfe-* skills
  • managing pure economics theory or structural estimation manuscripts
  • managing management, case study, or practical policy manuscripts
