jcr-methods

separate

Use when choosing or stress-testing the research design for a Journal of Consumer Research (JCR) manuscript — multi-study behavioral experiments, interpretive Consumer Culture Theory (CCT) fieldwork, mixed designs, or a Registered Report — so the evidence matches the conceptual claim. Designs the studies; it does not analyze them (jcr-data-analysis).
