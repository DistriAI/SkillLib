jetson-video-benchmark

separate

Use when measuring Jetson Video Codec SDK or PyNvVideoCodec encode/decode throughput, comparing presets or surfaces, testing codec-worker capacity with authenticated samples and user media, or producing a clearly labeled documentation-derived planning estimate when representative media is absent. Also use for a video request limited to PSNR or SSIM, to apply the terminal scope response.
