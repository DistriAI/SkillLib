expecon-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which expecon-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from design through rebuttal for an Experimental Economics (ExpEcon) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which expecon-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing manuscript work from design through rebuttal
  • Routing specialized skills for Experimental Economics submissions
  • Identifying the next step when a draft's binding constraint is unclear
  • Weighing Experimental Economics against JEBO, GEB, AEJ: Micro, or JESA
  • Switching into revision mode after a decision letter

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized expecon-* skills
  • Treating Experimental Economics like JEBO, GEB, AEJ: Micro, or JESA
  • Polishing prose or exhibits while treatment contrast or power justification is moving
  • Letting an appendix carry comprehension checks, balance, or instructions
