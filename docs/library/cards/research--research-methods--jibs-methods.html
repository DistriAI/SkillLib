jibs-methods

separate

Use when designing the empirical study for a Journal of International Business Studies (JIBS) manuscript — matching a cross-country and/or multilevel design to the question, planning for measurement equivalence, common-method variance, and endogeneity before data collection. Designs the study; it does not run the estimation (jibs-data-analysis) or build the mechanism (jibs-theory-development).

When to use

  • designing empirical studies for JIBS manuscripts
  • matching cross-country or multilevel designs to questions
  • planning for measurement equivalence
  • planning for common-method variance
  • planning for endogeneity
  • choosing designs for cross-border or multi-country questions
  • structuring nested data for multilevel analysis

When not to use

  • running the estimation
  • building the mechanism
  • jibs-data-analysis
  • jibs-theory-development
