american-journal-of-gastroenterology

separate

Use when targeting The American Journal of Gastroenterology or deciding whether a clinical GI/hepatology study fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the practice-relevant clinical-evidence bar, reporting-guideline and registration requirements, ACG house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics. Venue-fit aid only, not clinical advice.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for clinical GI, hepatology, endoscopy, or outcomes studies
  • re-framing GI studies around practice-relevant questions
  • choosing between the Red Journal, basic GI-science, and other venues
  • reporting-guideline, registration, and desk-reject expectations
  • clinical trials and comparative-effectiveness studies in luminal GI disease
  • endoscopy outcomes, quality metrics, and colorectal-cancer screening
  • clinical hepatology and pancreaticobiliary disease with practice-relevant endpoints
  • diagnostic, prognostic, and biomarker studies with clinically meaningful endpoints

When not to use

  • clinical or regulatory advice
  • mechanistic bench studies without a clinical anchor
  • underpowered single-center descriptive series with no practice implication
  • replacing the journal's current instructions for authors
