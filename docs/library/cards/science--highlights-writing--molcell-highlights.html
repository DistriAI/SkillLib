molcell-highlights

separate

Use to produce the three signature Cell Press front-matter artifacts for Molecular Cell — Highlights (3–4 bullets, each ≤85 characters), the eTOC/In Brief blurb (~50 words, third-person), and the Graphical Abstract (single-panel visual summary of the mechanism). Late-stage polish.

When to use

  • Producing Molecular Cell Highlights
  • Writing eTOC/In Brief blurb
  • Designing Graphical Abstract
  • Late-stage polish of front-matter artifacts

When not to use

  • Writing full sentences for Highlights
  • Writing eTOC blurb in first person
  • Creating multi-panel Graphical Abstracts
  • Copying Summary into eTOC blurb
