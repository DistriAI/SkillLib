jimf-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jimf-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing a Journal of International Money and Finance (JIMF) manuscript from topic through rebuttal. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • Deciding which jimf-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • Sequencing a JIMF manuscript from topic through rebuttal
  • Identifying the binding constraint of a draft
  • Comparing JIMF with sibling journals for fit
  • Routing based on paper archetype
  • Checking stage gates before advancing

When not to use

  • Replacing specialized jimf-* skills
  • Performing the actual writing or analysis tasks
