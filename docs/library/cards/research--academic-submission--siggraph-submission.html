siggraph-submission

separate

Use when auditing an ACM SIGGRAPH / SIGGRAPH Asia Technical Papers submission for Linklings readiness, covering the two-step form-then-upload deadline, the acmart (>=2.16) format and the 7-page conference-track budget, the dual-track vs Journal-only choice, the mandatory representative image and supplemental video, and desk-risk triage before the UTC cutoff.

When to use

  • auditing SIGGRAPH submission
  • checking Linklings readiness
  • verifying two-step deadline compliance
  • validating acmart format version
  • checking 7-page conference-track budget
  • reviewing dual-track vs Journal-only choice
  • verifying representative image and supplemental video
  • triaging desk-rejection risks

When not to use

  • writing the paper content
  • managing the review process
  • handling post-acceptance publication
