govern-writing-style

separate

Use when drafting or polishing a Governance: An International Journal of Policy, Administration, and Institutions manuscript so it reads for a comparative/international public-administration and political-science audience, front-loads the contribution, and fits the caps (≤ 9,000 words excluding citations; ~150-word abstract). Tightens prose and framing; it does not invent content.

When to use

  • Drafting the introduction
  • Framing the contribution
  • Final polish
  • Cutting text to fit word cap
  • Writing the ~150-word abstract
  • Aligning citations and format to journal guidelines

When not to use

  • Inventing content
  • Generating claims
