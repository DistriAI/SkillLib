stoc-related-work

separate

Use when positioning a STOC (ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing) paper against the theory literature — tracing bound-improvement lineages, citing conference/journal/preprint version pairs correctly, handling concurrent arXiv and ECCC work, and keeping self-citation double-blind-safe.

When to use

  • tracing bound-improvement lineages
  • citing conference/journal/preprint version pairs
  • handling concurrent arXiv and ECCC work
  • keeping self-citation double-blind-safe
  • positioning against barrier literature
  • comparing neighboring models
  • building honest comparison tables
  • performing deadline-week literature sweeps

When not to use

  • writing the main body of the paper
  • conducting experiments
  • formatting the final camera-ready PDF
  • responding to reviewer objections
