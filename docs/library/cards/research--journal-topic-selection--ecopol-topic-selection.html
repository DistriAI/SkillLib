ecopol-topic-selection

separate

Use when judging whether a topic is a timely, important policy question the Economic Policy (EP) Panel would commission and want to debate. Tests Panel-worthiness and policy timeliness; it does not invent evidence or citations.

When to use

  • deciding whether a question is worth pitching to Economic Policy
  • judging if a topic is an EP paper or a field-journal paper
  • identifying the decision a policymaker faces
  • assessing if a topic is a live European or global policy issue
  • determining which discussants would find the topic worth their time
  • sharpening a weak topic into a Panel-worthy question

When not to use

  • inventing evidence or citations
  • submitting unsolicited papers
  • inventing editor names, conference dates, or fees
