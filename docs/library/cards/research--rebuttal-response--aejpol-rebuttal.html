aejpol-rebuttal

separate

Use when an AEJ: Economic Policy decision letter (R&R or reject-and-resubmit) has arrived and a response-letter strategy and revision plan are needed. Structures the response to referees and editor with policy-relevance front and center; it does not run new estimation or redesign identification from scratch.

When to use

  • Planning response to AEJ: Policy R&R or major-revision letters
  • Prioritizing conflicting referee reports based on editor emphasis
  • Converting referee reports into a revision plan and point-by-point letter
  • Drafting resubmission cover notes addressing all comments
  • Structuring response to strengthen policy contribution and welfare reading

When not to use

  • Running new estimation
  • Redesigning identification from scratch
