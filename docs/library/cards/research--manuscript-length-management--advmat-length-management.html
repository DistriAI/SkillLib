advmat-length-management

separate

Use when an Advanced Materials manuscript must be fit to its article-type format — Communication versus Research Article — where word count and display items are budgeted together against a typeset page limit. Manages the length budget; does not restructure the science or polish prose line by line.

When to use

  • Choosing between Communication and Research Article
  • Fitting manuscript to typeset page limit
  • Trading display items against text
  • Condensing to Communication or expanding to Article
  • Trimming hype, redundant prose, and secondary data
  • Merging or moving figures to SI
  • Pruning references
  • Fit-checking typeset page count

When not to use

  • Restructuring the science
  • Polishing prose line by line
  • Trimming decisive characterization
  • Trimming benchmarking
  • Trimming headline metric statistics
  • Shrinking fonts or figures below legibility
