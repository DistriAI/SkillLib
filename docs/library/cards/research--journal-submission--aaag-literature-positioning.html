aaag-literature-positioning

separate

Use when positioning an Annals of the American Association of Geographers manuscript in the literature — engaging geographic scholarship across the relevant area and the cross-cutting debates Annals readers expect. Frames the gap and the conversation; it does not design the study.

When to use

  • Writing or revising the introduction/related-work
  • Framing the gap
  • Deciding which conversations to foreground
  • Positioning within the area
  • Connecting to cross-cutting geographic concerns
  • Engaging geographic theory
  • Mapping the field into positions

When not to use

  • Designing the study
