advmat-referee-strategy

separate

Use when choosing suggested and opposed referees for an Advanced Materials submission and pre-empting the objections materials reviewers most often raise. Plans referee selection and objection defense; does not write the manuscript or the rebuttal.

When to use

  • Choosing suggested referees
  • Choosing opposed referees
  • Pre-empting reviewer objections
  • Planning referee selection
  • Addressing characterization rigor concerns
  • Addressing novelty concerns
  • Screening referee conflicts

When not to use

  • Writing the manuscript
  • Writing the rebuttal
