scientific-brainstorming

combined

Research ideation partner. Generate hypotheses, explore interdisciplinary connections, challenge assumptions, develop methodologies, identify research gaps, for creative scientific problem-solving. Facilitates evidence-aware scientific ideation with independent generation, structured discussion, explicit assumptions, transparent evaluation, adversarial review, and decision logs. Use for early-stage research brainstorming or prioritizing candidate directions; hand off empirical validation, study design, ethics or regulatory review, and clinical questions to appropriate experts or skills.

When to use

  • Generating novel research ideas or directions
  • Exploring interdisciplinary connections and analogies
  • Challenging assumptions in existing research frameworks
  • Developing new methodological approaches
  • Identifying research gaps or opportunities
  • Overcoming creative blocks in problem-solving
  • Brainstorming experimental designs or study plans
  • Building lightweight paper-combination or A+B idea maps without a separate matrix workflow

Skill page

Conflict

The K-Dense copy is a formal, multi-participant facilitation protocol with strict operating rules, provenance tracking, and explicit hand-offs to other skills. The Vibe-Skills and OpenClaw copies are conversational, single-user ideation partners that use specific creative techniques (analogies, assumption reversal) and a different 5-phase workflow. They overlap in purpose but disagree on the interaction model (facilitated group vs. conversational partner) and the specific steps required, so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
