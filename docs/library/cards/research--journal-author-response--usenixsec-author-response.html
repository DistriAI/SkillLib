usenixsec-author-response

separate

Use when reviews arrive from a USENIX Security Symposium cycle and the authors must respond — writing the rebuttal that survives online PC discussion, handling required ethics-content revisions flagged mid-review, and working with a shepherd after an "Accepted on Shepherd Approval" decision.

When to use

  • writing the rebuttal that survives online PC discussion
  • handling required ethics-content revisions flagged mid-review
  • working with a shepherd after an "Accepted on Shepherd Approval" decision
  • sorting reviews before writing
  • drafting rules that fit this venue
  • converting decision letter into a change matrix
  • delivering edits inside the two-week window
  • tracking edits against camera-ready deadline

When not to use

  • appeal lane inside the cycle
  • revised resubmission
  • usenixsec-review-process
  • usenixsec-camera-ready
