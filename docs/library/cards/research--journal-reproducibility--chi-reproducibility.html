chi-reproducibility

separate

Use when strengthening research transparency for an ACM CHI paper — protocols, instruments, codebooks, analysis scripts, preregistration, and data availability under human-subjects constraints — so methods survive the ADR-Method screening and others can actually build on the work.

When to use

  • Strengthening research transparency for ACM CHI papers
  • Documenting protocols, instruments, and codebooks
  • Managing analysis scripts and preregistration
  • Handling data availability under human-subjects constraints
  • Preparing availability statements for review and camera-ready
  • Verifying reproducibility claims and de-identification

When not to use

  • Tasks not related to ACM CHI paper transparency
  • Non-human-subjects research reproducibility
