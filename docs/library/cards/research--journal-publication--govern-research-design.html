govern-research-design

separate

Use when defending the research design of a Governance: An International Journal of Policy, Administration, and Institutions manuscript — comparative/causal designs for governance & institutions, qualitative & comparative-historical case logic, mixed methods, and the measurement of governance/institutions. Governance judges each tradition on its own terms. Strengthens the design; it does not write code.

When to use

  • Specifying identification, case selection, or comparative design
  • Defending causal claims, case choice, or country selection
  • Choosing and defending governance/institutions measures
  • Justifying design adjudication of rival institutional accounts
  • Comparative and causal designs for governance reforms
  • Qualitative and comparative-historical case logic
  • Mixed methods integration logic
  • Measuring governance and institutions with caveats

When not to use

  • Writing code
  • Conceptual contribution (non-empirical design)
