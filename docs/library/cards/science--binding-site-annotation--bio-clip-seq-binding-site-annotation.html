bio-clip-seq-binding-site-annotation

separate

Annotate CLIP-seq binding sites to genomic features including 3'UTR, 5'UTR, CDS, introns, and ncRNAs. Use when characterizing where an RBP binds in transcripts.

When to use

  • Annotate CLIP-seq binding sites
  • Characterize RBP binding locations
  • Annotate peaks to 3'UTR
  • Annotate peaks to 5'UTR
  • Annotate peaks to CDS
  • Annotate peaks to introns
  • Annotate peaks to ncRNAs
