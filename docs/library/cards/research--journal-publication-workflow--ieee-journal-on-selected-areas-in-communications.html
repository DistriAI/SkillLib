ieee-journal-on-selected-areas-in-communications

separate

Use when targeting IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications (JSAC) or deciding whether a communications-and-networking manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's call-driven fit, the high-impact-and-topical bar, the route-by-active-call logic, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • checking fit against an active or upcoming special-issue call
  • re-framing results to foreground impact and timeliness
  • choosing between JSAC and broad-scope transactions
  • applying call-matching logic and desk-reject heuristics
  • next-generation wireless and cellular systems
  • network architecture, protocols, and virtualization
  • security, privacy, and trust in communication systems
  • machine-learning-for-communications and emerging paradigms

When not to use

  • routine, incremental results that do not advance a call's frontier
  • isolated PHY tweaks for systems-and-networks calls
  • generic ML contributions without communications-systems advance
  • replacing the journal's current official author guidelines
