devils-advocate

combined

Challenge slide design with 5-7 pedagogical questions. Checks ordering, prerequisites, and cognitive load. 结构化压力测试一个论点（devil's advocate / 魔鬼代言人）：把论点拆成显性断言与隐性假设（A1/A2 编号）， 逐条在用户给定的证据材料里检索带出处的反方论证，标 High/Medium/Low 风险等级，补基础比率外部视角， 最后给出「可监测的证伪信号」清单；输出结构化 findings + 人读叙事双层结果。找不到反证时如实声明「未找到」， 绝不编造。复刻自 LinqAlpha（对冲基金 AI 产品）公开的 Devil's Advocate 架构，叠加 CIA 结构化分析技术与 RAND 假设规划。当用户要「压力测试 / 唱反调 / 找盲点 / 拆假设 / 挑战这个论点 / bear case / 红队这个方案 / 上投委会前过一遍 / pressure test my thesis」，或拿着投资论点、IC memo、研究结论、商业决策要人挑刺时， 用本 skill——即使用户没说出"魔鬼代言人"这个词。

When to use

  • Challenge slide design
  • Check ordering, prerequisites, and cognitive load
  • Structured pressure test of an argument
  • Decompose explicit assertions and implicit assumptions
  • Retrieve counter-arguments from evidence materials
  • Identify falsifiable signals
  • Red team investment theses or business decisions

When not to use

  • Inventing counter-evidence when none is found
  • Free-form debate without evidence anchoring

Skill page

Conflict

The two copies share the name but perform different tasks. The daymade copy is a structured, evidence-anchored stress test of an investment or business thesis (assumption decomposition, counter-evidence retrieval, risk flags, falsification signals, JSON + narrative outputs). The brycewang copy is a pedagogical review of a slide deck (ordering, prerequisites, cognitive load, notation conflicts, 5-7 challenges). They overlap only in the general idea of 'challenging a work product,' so crowning one would hide a real alternative.
