Use when targeting Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI) or deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks, rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for AI statistics.

When to use

  • targeting Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI)
  • deciding whether a computer-science manuscript fits this venue
  • conference fit, framing, evidence bar, submission-cycle checks
  • rebuttal posture, and desk-reject risks for AI statistics
  • probabilistic reasoning, graphical models, causal inference
  • decision making under uncertainty, and calibrated ML
  • re-framing from journal style or arXiv style into a selective CS conference narrative
  • evidence-gap, anonymity, artifact, rebuttal, or re-routing diagnosis

When not to use

  • substitute for the current year's CFP, author kit, ethics policy, or submission portal
  • manuscripts where uncertainty is present but not the central representation or decision object
  • general ML method breadth
  • learning-theory guarantees
  • symbolic reasoning
  • applied venues when the uncertainty method is mostly a domain evaluation wrapper
