nature-plants

separate

Use when targeting Nature Plants or deciding whether a plant-science manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, the broad-significance and Nature-framing bar, evidence and reproducibility expectations, house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • fit/framing check for a plant-science paper
  • re-framing specialist results into broad-significance narrative
  • choosing between Nature Plants and sibling journals
  • desk-reject heuristics and alternative routes
  • molecular and cellular plant biology with broad significance
  • plant development, physiology, and stress responses
  • plant genetics, genomics, and evolution
  • crop science and agricultural plant biology

When not to use

  • technically sound but incremental specialist result with no broad significance
  • mechanism asserted from a single correlative experiment
  • single-condition, single-genotype, or unreplicated phenotyping
  • descriptive catalogues with no functional validation
  • framing pitched only to one subfield
