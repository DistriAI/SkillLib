Conflict

The three copies share the same core workflow (check for existing context, auto-draft or interview, save to .agents/product-marketing-context.md), but they disagree on the document schema. The LeoYeAI/openclaw-marketing-skills copy uses an 8-section structure (Product Overview, Target Audience, Positioning & Differentiation, Value Proposition, Pricing, Competitive Landscape, Voice & Messaging, Customer Language). The other two copies use a 12-section structure (Product Overview, Target Audience, Personas, Problems & Pain Points, Competitive Landscape, Differentiation, Objections & Anti-Personas, Switching Dynamics, Customer Language, Brand Voice, Proof Points, Goals). The 12-section version also includes a 'When to Use' section, a check for the legacy .claude/ path, and a specific output template with tables for Personas and Objections. Because the section lists are materially different and the output templates conflict, crowning one copy would hide a real alternative structure.
