hri-author-response

separate

Use when writing the single ACM/IEEE HRI full-paper rebuttal that responds to three external reviewers and two area chairs — triaging study-design and statistics critiques, correcting misreadings, promising only what a camera-ready can deliver, staying anonymous and within the length limit, and giving the online reviewer discussion reasons to raise scores.

When to use

  • Writing HRI full-paper rebuttal
  • Responding to three external reviewers and two area chairs
  • Triaging study-design and statistics critiques
  • Correcting reviewer misreadings
  • Promising camera-ready deliverables
  • Maintaining anonymity in rebuttal
  • Staying within rebuttal length limit
  • Preparing for online reviewer discussion

When not to use

  • Journal revise-and-resubmit
  • Running a new study before deadline
  • In-cycle major-revision round
  • Promising new data collection
  • Promising uncomputed results
  • Breaking anonymity
  • Introducing brand-new claims or results
  • Conceding central contribution
