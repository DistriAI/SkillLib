orgstud-literature-positioning

separate

Use when staking the contribution of an Organization Studies (OS) manuscript against the specific theoretical conversation it joins — what the field knows, what it misses, and the precise gap this paper closes. Positions the argument; it does not build the theory (see orgstud-theory-development) or run analysis.

When to use

  • staking contribution against theoretical conversation
  • identifying specific OS conversation and canonical anchors
  • synthesizing literature into a tension or unresolved problem
  • differentiating framing from sibling outlets like ASQ or JMS
  • positioning argument to earn right to make theoretical claim

When not to use

  • building the theory
  • running analysis
  • creating a neutral map or catalogue of prior work
  • gap-spotting by setting or population only
