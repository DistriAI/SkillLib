papertrade

separate

Trade Papertrade's synthetic BTC and ETH perps (HyperEVM, priced from Hyperliquid) from a Bankr wallet with REAL USDC, and earn USDC from the PAPER that losing trades mint. Open a long or short with a market order at up to 1000x, close, set, edit or remove a take-profit and stop-loss that Bankr runs (on the open or later), fund from USDC or another token in one preview, withdraw, and check positions, balance, deposit status and queued payouts. Bankr stakes the user's PAPER automatically so it earns a share of Papertrade's USDC, and scheduled automations can auto-stake and claim or run a hedged long+short PAPER farm. Use when the user names Papertrade or PAPER, asks about their Papertrade positions, take-profit or stop-loss, balance, deposits, payouts or PAPER staking, or asks how Papertrade works or what its risks are. When to use

  • Open long or short BTC/ETH perps
  • Close positions
  • Set, edit, or remove take-profit and stop-loss
  • Fund from USDC or other tokens
  • Withdraw balance
  • Check positions, balance, deposits, and payouts
  • Stake and claim PAPER
  • Run automated PAPER farming

When not to use

  • Practice or simulated paper trading
  • Hyperliquid's own perps
  • Limit orders
  • Partial-close orders
  • Add-margin orders
