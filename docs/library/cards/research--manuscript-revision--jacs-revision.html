jacs-revision

separate

Use when a decision letter/referee reports arrive for a Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS) manuscript, to plan experiments and draft the point-by-point response. Structures the revision and rebuttal — it does not fabricate data or guarantee acceptance.

When to use

  • triage referee comments into experiments vs edits vs rebuttals
  • draft the point-by-point response letter
  • decide whether to appeal or resubmit elsewhere
  • plan new experiments for rigor-critical comments
  • create a JACS revision ledger
  • structure the response letter with opening and point-by-point sections

When not to use

  • fabricate data
  • guarantee acceptance
