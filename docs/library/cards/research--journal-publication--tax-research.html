tax-research

separate

Use when targeting 《税务研究》(Taxation Research — 中国税务杂志社主办的税务学术月刊, 在线投约稿系统 tg.ctax.org.cn) or deciding whether a Chinese tax/public-finance manuscript fits this venue. Encodes the journal's fit, framing, abstract/keyword/citation house style, official-submission re-check, and desk-reject heuristics.

When to use

  • targeting 《税务研究》 or Taxation Research
  • deciding if a Chinese tax/public-finance manuscript fits the venue
  • rewriting a general Chinese tax/finance manuscript for 《税务研究》
  • listing high-frequency rejection risks and alternative submission directions
  • checking journal fit, framing, and house style
  • verifying official submission requirements

When not to use

  • replacing the latest official submission guidelines
  • submitting to journals where finance/tax is not the main problem
  • using second-hand submission website formats
