tacas-workflow

separate

Use when planning a TACAS (ETAPS) project timeline from category choice and venue fit through the EasyChair paper deadline, the mandatory tool-paper artifact deadline, the rebuttal window, notification, the voluntary post-acceptance artifact round, LNCS camera-ready, and presentation, with backward-planning offsets that account for TACAS's single annual round and its parallel paper+artifact review.

When to use

  • planning a TACAS project timeline
  • backward-planning from the EasyChair paper deadline
  • managing mandatory tool-paper artifact deadlines
  • coordinating parallel paper and artifact review
  • preparing LNCS camera-ready and presentation
  • assigning owners for artifact and paper tasks

When not to use

  • planning for CAV, VMCAI, or FMCAD
  • planning for ESOP's two review rounds
  • managing author-facing APCs
  • planning for conferences with standing editor-in-chief
