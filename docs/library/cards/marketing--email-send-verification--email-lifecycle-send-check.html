email-lifecycle-send-check

separate

Checks a drafted email flow or broadcast before it is loaded: flow logic and exits, overlaps, offer depth and codes, stock, claims, consent and legal footer, SMS rules, rendering, voice and AI-copy tells, and the goal metric. Returns a pass / fixed / open-question table. Use alone when you already have the finished draft and the brand's product and policy facts; otherwise run email-lifecycle.

When to use

  • flow logic and exits
  • overlaps
  • offer depth and codes
  • stock
  • claims
  • consent and legal footer
  • SMS rules
  • rendering

When not to use

  • judging claims itself
