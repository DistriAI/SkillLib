epsl-reporting-and-reproducibility

separate

Use when assembling the supplementary material, data-availability statement, and FAIR data deposit for an Earth and Planetary Science Letters (EPSL) manuscript. EPSL follows Elsevier's research-data policies and the community expects deposition in discipline repositories — EarthChem, PANGAEA, IRIS/EarthScope, MagIC, NASA PDS, Zenodo. It guides reporting and deposit; it does not generate the data.

When to use

  • Assembling supplementary text, tables, and figures
  • Writing the data-availability statement
  • Choosing repositories for data deposition
  • Depositing geochemical tables, waveforms, paleomagnetic data, model code, or mission-derived products
  • Ensuring readers can re-derive every figure and headline number

When not to use

  • Generating the data
