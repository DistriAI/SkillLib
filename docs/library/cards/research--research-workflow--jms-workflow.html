jms-workflow

separate

Use when deciding which jms-* sub-skill to invoke next, or when sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal for a Journal of Management Studies (JMS) submission. Routes — it does not replace — the specialized skills.

When to use

  • deciding which jms-* sub-skill to invoke next
  • sequencing manuscript work from topic through rebuttal
  • diagnosing current bottleneck in a JMS-bound paper
  • switching into revision mode after a decision letter
  • routing based on paper archetype

When not to use

  • replacing specialized jms-* skills
  • treating JMS like AMJ, Organization Studies, Journal of Management, or SMJ
